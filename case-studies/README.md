# Use case studies

Case studies investigate whether an extracted instrument can contribute to a further mathematical result. Their status is recorded separately from the extraction and review status of their inputs.

| Study | Extracted input | Status |
|---|---|---|
| [CS-001: Signed restriction and fixed-depth circuit lower bounds](CS-001-signed-restriction/README.md) | MI-000083: clause-count-independent signed restriction bound from family 112 | [Public preprint with proofs](https://zenodo.org/records/23429046); same polynomial-time language at every fixed depth |
| [CS-002: Stationary descent and its information cost](CS-002-stationary-descent/README.md) | MI-000098: strict-descent obstruction; MI-000014: entropy comparison | [Public preprint with proofs](https://zenodo.org/records/23408425); historical priority of the finite formula unresolved |
| [CS-003: Quantum decoding and sharp subset bounds](CS-003-quantum-subset/README.md) | MI-000329: constrained spectral optimization; MI-000787: exact Bernstein positivity certificates | [Public preprint with proofs](https://zenodo.org/records/23441704); sharp bounds on specified pure- and mixed-state classes; unrestricted sharp mixed-state problem open |

Further studies can be added with the [case-study template](../templates/case-study.md). A proposed application should identify the target problem and the missing argument; a completed study should supply its theorem, hypotheses, proof or evidence, and public citation.
