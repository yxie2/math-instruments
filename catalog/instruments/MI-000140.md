# Diagonal spectral energy supports signs with bounded adaptive shifts

[Family 097](../families/openai-math-097.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000092.md) · [Batch B004](../../batches/B004/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let K be nonempty, bounded, open, convex and symmetric about zero in $\mathbb R^m$. For positive diagonal Q let $\lambda_Q(K)$ be the first Dirichlet Rayleigh infimum $\inf\int\langle Q\nabla g,\nabla g\rangle/\int g^2$. Assume

$$\lambda_Q(K)\le\kappa^2\operatorname{tr}Q\quad\text{for every such Q},\qquad\kappa=1/100.$$

For distinct coordinate indices $j_1,\ldots,j_s$ and arbitrary functions $q_i:\mathbb R^{i-1}\to[-1/4,1/4]$, signs exist for which the successive coordinate assignments $x_{j_i}=q_i(x_{j_1},\ldots,x_{j_{i-1}})+\epsilon_i$, with all unassigned coordinates zero, end at a point in K.

The energy hypothesis first produces one normalized nonnegative f supported in K with $\int|\partial_i f|^2\le\kappa^2$ for every i. Convexity of coordinate-energy upper bounds follows from mixing densities and taking square roots; separation uses every positive diagonal Q. A lifted Steiner construction then supplies a domain $T_e(K)$ with the same spectral bounds such that every y in it, for every |q|<=1/4, has one of $y+(q\pm1)e$ in K. Its height estimate is $(\ell/2)(1-2\ell\kappa)=19/16>1$ for $\ell=5/2$. Backward domain transforms followed by forward sign choices accommodate the adaptive shifts.

## Role in the source argument

Allows each coefficient to contain a small causal correction as well as a sign. This extra freedom is exactly what the contraction-canceling predictor needs.

## Antecedents and comparison

[Guo–Fang–Lu](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11189v1) supply the density and lifting framework; [Akbas–Sra](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.27172v2) use quadratic Dirichlet energy and lifted symmetrization, and [Bandeira](https://randomstrasse101.math.ethz.ch/posts/komlos/) gives a related spectral formulation. The source explicitly credits these ingredients. The bounded adaptive-shift conclusion is the extracted extension. The cited Akbas–Sra version is dated September 29; the source’s filename date alone is not a priority chronology.

## Checks and limitations

Read the common-witness separation and shifted-section argument in full, including the all-Q quantifier and Jensen step. Checked the rational height margin and interval condition ensuring one shifted signed step belongs to the original open fiber.

The result is existential and gives neither an online strategy nor a method to compute the backward domains. Testing Q=I alone is insufficient for the simultaneous coordinate energies. The coordinate directions must be distinct, and the shift bound is part of the hypothesis.

## Possible applications

Rounding coefficient sequences with bounded causal corrections while retaining one final membership constraint in a convex body.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
