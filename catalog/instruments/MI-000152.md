# Converging conditional Gaussian projection norms preserve locality of bounded tests

[Family 233](../families/openai-math-233.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000098.md) · [Batch B006](../../batches/B006/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Fix buffers $K\Subset B\Subset D$, with B a regular polygon. Let cable GFFs in admissible approximations of D have full harmonic-extension Green forms converging to $G_D$. The local sigma-field $\mathcal G_n(B)$ contains the entire cable restriction on the complete cells of B, including perimeter bridges. After conditioning, extend the zero-clamped residual by zero on those cables and then use the original ambient extension. The source identifies its covariance limit as $G_{D\setminus\overline B}$.

For any bounded local tests $0\le T_n\le1$ supported in K and any joint limit $(\widehat\Phi_n,T_n)\Rightarrow(\Phi,T)$,

$$\mathbb E[T\mid\Phi]=\mathbb E[T\mid\mathcal G(B)],\qquad\mathcal G(B)=\sigma\{\Phi(f):f\in C_c^\infty(B)\}.$$

The mechanism is more general than the cable application. For a finite vector of full-field pairings F_n, let $Q_n=\mathbb E[F_n\mid\mathcal G_n(B)]$. Its covariance is the total covariance minus the clamped residual covariance. Approximate Q_n by projection onto r interior smooth pairings, denoted $Q_n^{(r)}$. Convergence of both covariance forms and density of the interior coordinates give

$$\lim_{r\to\infty}\limsup_{n\to\infty}\mathbb E|Q_n-Q_n^{(r)}|^2=0.$$

Gaussian conditioning expresses conditional expectations of bounded Lipschitz cylinder functions as convolution with the residual Gaussian. The displayed approximation makes them uniformly approximable in L1 by continuous functions of finitely many interior pairings. Multiplication by the arbitrary bounded T_n preserves that error. Passing to the limit and a monotone class gives conditional locality without continuity of T_n.

## Role in the source argument

Prevents information outside the buffer from entering the conditional limit of a local path indicator when ordinary weak convergence of the field is insufficient.

## Antecedents and comparison

Gaussian projection and domain Markov decomposition are classical. The clamped covariance convergence invokes [Aru, Lupu and Sepulveda’s metric-graph/local-set results](https://arxiv.org/abs/1805.09204v1). The source’s selected argument uses convergence of projection norms to handle arbitrary bounded local tests; priority for this exact buffered formulation remains unverified.

## Checks and limitations

Read the projection approximation and conditional factorization proof. Finite Gaussian linear-algebra examples test residual/projection covariance identities. A disappearing-coordinate negative control illustrates why field convergence alone cannot justify conditional locality.

The clamped residual covariance convergence is essential. Deleting perimeter edges is not the same operation as keeping their zero trace before ambient extension. The selected Gaussian calculation does not prove metric-graph Green convergence for an arbitrary approximation.

## Possible applications

Passing discontinuous local events through Gaussian-field limits while preserving conditional locality in a fixed interior buffer.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
