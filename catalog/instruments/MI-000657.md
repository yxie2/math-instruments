# Solve a compatible polynomial lift without dividing by coefficients

[Family 049](../families/openai-math-049.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000426.md) · [Batch B069](../../batches/B069/README.md)

## Mathematical content

In any commutative ring B, suppose x^2+y^3=hs and (h,y)=B. Set S(h,U,V,W)=2U^3V+3U^4W+h(V^2-3U^2W^2)+h^2W^3. Then B[U,V,W]/(U^3+hV-x,-U^2+hW-y,S-s) is B[T].

Choose alpha h+beta y=1 and write G=V+UW. The equations become hG-yU=x, hW=a=y+U^2 and y^2W=b=s-hG^2+2UyG. Their compatibility y^2a=hb follows from the cusp relation. With r=alpha(1+beta y), q=beta^2 one has rh+qy^2=1. Therefore W0=ra+qb solves both equations and their generated ideal is (W-W0), including in the presence of zero divisors.

The remaining equation is parametrized by U=hT-beta x, G=yT+alpha x, with inverse T=alpha U+beta G. Recover W=W0 and V=G-UW. Every step is a polynomial identity; neither h nor y is inverted.

## Role in the source argument

Produces a polynomial parameter on an affine-space fiber whose original equations do not visibly permit elimination.

## Antecedents and comparison

The mechanism is classical Bezout/unimodular-row elimination applied to a particular cusp presentation. The source-specific formulas are fully explicit; no general new lifting principle is claimed.

## Checks and limitations

Universal symbolic identities and exhaustive small residue-ring examples, including nonunits and zero divisors, check compatibility, solutions and both parameter maps.

This identifies the stated quotient under its hypotheses; it does not verify the separate ambient noncoordinate obstruction.

## Possible applications

Constructing polynomial trivializations that remain valid over nonreduced or reducible bases.
