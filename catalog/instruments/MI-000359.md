# Certify twisted second-homology vanishing by an exact Fox minor

[Family 321](../families/openai-math-321.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000198.md) · [Batch B031](../../batches/B031/README.md)

## Mathematical content

For the presentation with relations x1 a1 x1^{-1}=a1^4, x2 a2 x2^{-1}=a2^3, x1=a2^{13}, and s x2 s^{-1}=a1^5, choose a primitive cube root zeta. The assignment (x1,a1,x2,a2,s)=(-1,zeta,zeta^2,-1,1) respects the relations and also x1^2=x2^3=1.

Apply the Fox product and inverse rules to the four relator words. With edges in the displayed generator order, the evaluated boundary vectors are the rows of

$$D=\begin{pmatrix}1-\zeta&-2&0&0&0\\0&0&2&\zeta^2-1&0\\1&0&0&-1&0\\0&-(1+\zeta)&1&0&1-\zeta^2\end{pmatrix}.$$

The minor in columns x1,a1,x2,s is -4(1-zeta^2), which is nonzero. Hence C_2(B;L_rho)->C_1(B;L_rho) is injective and H_2(B;L_rho)=0. The finite plus construction preserves this vanishing for X. The calculation also checks the chain identity D(rho(g)-1)_g=0. All arithmetic can be done in Q[zeta]/(zeta^2+zeta+1), avoiding numerical decisions about a complex determinant.

Supplies the explicit local-system homology input for the source tower obstruction.

## Antecedents and comparison

Fox differentiation and character specialization are standard. This card records a reproducible instance and its small determinant certificate; the matrix alone does not prove a D(2) counterexample.

## Checks and limitations

The program independently differentiates all four words, reproduces all 20 entries, verifies the chain identity and computes the nonzero minor exactly as -4*z-8 in the cyclotomic quotient.

The separate tower theorem that would turn this homology input into a dimensional obstruction has not been verified.

## Possible applications

Exact local-system homology calculations for finite presentations and plus constructions.
