# Reference-state rounding on question-support components

[Family 277](../families/openai-math-277.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000085.md) · [Batch B002](../../batches/B002/README.md)

## Mathematical content

For a finite question law $\mu$, form the graph on positive-probability pairs $H=\{(x,y):\mu(x,y)>0\}$, joining pairs sharing either local question. Let $H_j$ be its components, $\mu_j=\mu(\cdot\mid H_j)$, and

$$\kappa_\mu=8\max_j\frac{|H_j|-1}{\min_{z\in H_j}\mu_j(z)}.$$

A singleton contributes zero. With the tested-set notation of the conditioning transfer, let $m=k-|C|$, $p=\Pr(E_C)>0$, and $\tau=(\log(1/p)+|C|\log d)/m$. The source bounds the conditional remaining success by

$$\frac1m\sum_{i\notin C}\Pr(I_i=1\mid E_C)
\le v+\sqrt{\frac{\log(1/p)}{2m}}+4\sqrt{\kappa_\mu\tau}+2\kappa_\mu\tau.$$

The exposure argument supplies common vectors with Alice-local and Bob-local approximations. Adjacent support pairs share an approximation. A path to a fixed component root, followed by weighted Cauchy--Schwarz, bounds the total squared replacement error by $\kappa_\mu p\tau$. The root vectors define question-independent states for the component games. Relative entropy and Pinsker control the change in component frequencies after conditioning.

Using $T=\delta^2/(1024(1+\kappa_\mu))$ in the transfer yields rate $\delta^3/[16384(1+\kappa_\mu)(1+\log d)]$.

## Role in the source argument

Handles zero entries and disconnected question support without changing the original game. Each player can identify its support component from the local question, so component strategies combine by tensoring near-optimal finite-dimensional strategies.

## Antecedents and comparison

[Dinur--Harsha--Venkat--Yuen](https://arxiv.org/abs/1610.08349v2) uses connection graphs in classical multiplayer repetition. [Chailloux--Scarpa, corrected version](https://arxiv.org/abs/1310.7787v3) is a related distribution-dependent entangled repetition result. The source credits its common resolvent purification to the earlier OpenAI report. The candidate combination is the quantum component-root replacement with the explicit threshold constant, not graph paths or Pinsker inequality.

## Checks and limitations

Checked local identifiability of components, the path-length and minimum-mass estimate, and 1/32+1/8+1/512=81/512<1/4 in the final budget choice.

The constant depends on the smallest positive conditional mass in each component and may be large. The full conditioning theorem depends on the preceding exposure lemma. This is a fixed-distribution bound, not a universal cubic rate independent of the question law.

## Possible applications

Potential use: replace input-dependent quantum representatives by one reference per locally identifiable component when adjacent representatives share a controlled local approximation.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
