# Cancel synthetic characters without division and retain the FFT output order

[Family 109](../families/openai-math-109.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000211.md) · [Batch B035](../../batches/B035/README.md)

## Mathematical content

In R_r=C[y]/(y^r+1), with r a power of two, multiplication by y^a is an exact signed permutation of the r coefficients and an isometry for their maximum norm. For a power of two t dividing 2r, set omega=y^{2r/t}. If t does not divide j, write j=2^v j_0 with j_0 odd. Shifting the sum index by t/2^{v+1} multiplies sum_{k=0}^{t-1}omega^{jk} by -1, so that sum vanishes. This avoids division by omega^j-1 in a ring with zero divisors.

Define F_t^+ and F_t^- with both sums normalized by 1/t. Character cancellation gives F_t^+F_t^-=I/t, and both maps are contractions. Decimation in frequency using the half-Hadamard butterfly writes frequency j at its bit-reversed address B_t(j). Reversing the recursion, with inverse monomial twists but the same half-Hadamard, consumes that layout and computes F_t^+B_t^{-1}.

Thus the actual procedures are B_t F_t^- and F_t^+ B_t^{-1}. A pointwise product between equally permuted arrays retains the same layout, so convolution can avoid materializing a separate frequency permutation.

The source identifies this as the Nussbaumer-Quandalle polynomial-transform method used in [Harvey and van der Hoeven 2021](https://annals.math.princeton.edu/2021/193-2/p04). The extraction emphasizes zero-divisor-safe cancellation and the normalization/layout contract.

## Checks and limitations

Forty-eight exact rational transform models compare recursive stored order with direct transforms and check the I/t composite; all relevant character sums are checked.

No simultaneous butterfly-layer speedup, truncation error budget or final multiplication running time is certified.
