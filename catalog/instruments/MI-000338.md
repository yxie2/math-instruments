# Realize a small arbitrary curvature function through a scalar ODE

[Family 334](../families/openai-math-334.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000188.md) · [Batch B029](../../batches/B029/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Given smooth K_0(X,Y) on (-1,1)^2 with sup norm at most 10^{-3}, solve f_XX=-K_0 f, f(0,Y)=1 and f_X(0,Y)=0. Smooth dependence on Y gives a smooth function. The integral equation gives F<=1+10^{-3}F on each segment from 0 to X, hence F<=1000/999 and f>=998/999>0.

For the metric g=dX^2+f(X,Y)^2 dY^2, direct curvature computation gives K_g=-f_XX/f=K_0. The formula allows arbitrary Y-dependence; no separation of variables is assumed. Its metric eigenvalues stay uniformly positive and bounded.

If every derivative of K_0 vanishes at the origin, then every derivative of f-1 does too. The initial data settle X-orders zero and one. Every higher X-order follows by differentiating f_XX=-K_0 f: each Leibniz term contains a derivative of K_0 at the origin. Thus the resulting metric has the Euclidean Taylor jet there.

## Role in the source argument

Builds a smooth positive background metric carrying the source prescribed sign-changing curvature arrangement.

## Antecedents and comparison

The construction f_XX+Kf=0 with these initial data and metric dX^2+f^2dY^2 appears in [Khuri 2009, page 453](https://www.math.stonybrook.edu/~khuri/Khuri_AIMS_Darboux.pdf). The source adds a convenient uniform smallness bound and uses flat jets in its assembly.

## Checks and limitations

Symbolic Christoffel and curvature calculations retain arbitrary Y-dependence. Nine formal ODE models verify the flat-jet recurrence; the positivity bound is exact rational arithmetic.

This constructs metrics with prescribed curvature, not metrics without local immersions. The latter requires the separate unaudited local obstruction.

## Possible applications

Encoding curvature patterns in a positive metric while controlling normalization and Taylor data.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
