# Replace several product comparison laws by the actual marginals of one retained core

[Family 078](../families/openai-math-078.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000201.md) · [Batch B032](../../batches/B032/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let nu be a finite-space subprobability of mass m>0. For tuples of observations (F_l1,...,F_lk_l), suppose nu_{tuple}<=A_l product_j mu_lj, where the reference marginals mu_lj are probabilities. Put q=sum_l k_l, counting repeated observations with different reference laws separately.

For 0<eta<m/q, repeatedly delete all points with a factor value v whenever its current positive marginal is less than eta mu_lj(v). Once deleted, that factor value never reappears. Charging each pair ((l,j),v) once bounds total loss by q eta. The procedure terminates, leaving nu' of mass M>=m-q eta.

Every surviving value satisfies mu_lj(v)<=M rho_{F_lj}(v)/eta for rho=nu'/M. Since restriction preserves each old joint upper bound, substitution gives simultaneously

$$\rho_{F_{l1},...,F_{lk_l}}\le A_lM^{k_l-1}\eta^{-k_l}\prod_j\rho_{F_{lj}}.$$

Taking eta=m/(2q) retains at least half the mass. Support counts cannot increase, while any old marginal upper bound gains exactly the normalization factor M^{-1}.

## Role in the source argument

Makes all later comparisons use the same retained law and its actual marginals, avoiding mismatched independent reference measures.

## Antecedents and comparison

This is a finite peeling construction. Its explicit constant and separate charging of repeated observations are the reusable details.

## Checks and limitations

One hundred exact rational models with two simultaneous product comparisons and repeated observations remove 150 factor values and verify the retained mass and all resulting inequalities.

The original joint comparison constants and the geometric meaning of the observations are inputs.

## Possible applications

Consistent product domination under several overlapping observations and repeated restrictions.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
