# Prune commuting reflections using probabilities in the current pruned state

[Family 274](../families/openai-math-274.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000310.md) · [Batch B055](../../batches/B055/README.md)

## Mathematical content

At each layer of disjoint nonempty reflection gates I-2P_g on m qubits, evaluate q_g=||P_g psi||^2 in the exact state psi produced by the already pruned prefix. Retain gates with q_g>=m^(-6). Each deleted gate changes this state by less than 2m^(-3). Other gates in the layer commute with P_g and preserve that norm, so deleting at most m gates costs at most 2m^(-2). Across t layers, unitary telescoping gives ||U psi_0-W psi_0||<=2t m^(-2). This is an existence construction; computing the exact probabilities need not be efficient.

The source also proves regularity conditionally on an earlier uniform overlap-tail estimate for the pruned prefix: q_B ||1_(H_B>=m^h)psi||<=exp(-m^p) for every fixed 0<p<h<1 and product test B. For a retained gate, dividing by q_g gives arbitrarily subpolynomial mean count. For at most m^b retained gates, their total mean is at most m^(b+epsilon). Markov puts half the mass on outcomes of weight at most R=2m^(b+epsilon). There are at most (floor R+1)m^(floor R) such outcomes, even if they are correlated. One has probability at least exp(-m^(b+2epsilon)) eventually.

Recenter the product test at this outcome. Its count differs from the old count by at most R, so a high old count implies a high recentered count. The uniform overlap-tail estimate, with intermediate exponents larger than b+2epsilon, absorbs the reciprocal outcome probability. This closes regularity of the next retained layer. The tail-transport premise is not supplied by the deletion telescope alone.

## Role in the source argument

Separates a small output-vector error from the conditional regularity bootstrap used in the source parity argument.

## Antecedents and comparison

[Nadimpalli-Parham-Vasconcelos-Yuen, On the Pauli Spectrum of QAC0](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2311.09631), Lemma 23 and Claim 24, use a gate-deletion telescope controlled by gate width in normalized Frobenius norm. Claim 24 and its complete proof on PDF page 25 were read. The selected source instead controls one evolving input state by actual projection probabilities; its regularity step needs additional tail information.

## Checks and limitations

Small circuits compare original and adaptively pruned outputs against the cumulative probability bound. Correlated outcome distributions test the heavy-atom counting and Hamming recentering step.

The tests do not establish uniform tail transport, efficient classical pruning or the claimed parity lower bound.

## Possible applications

Approximating one circuit trajectory by removing commuting reflections that rarely act on its current state.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
