# Two trees separate individual multiple hits from shared one-hit quotas

[Family 118](../families/openai-math-118.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000112.md) · [Batch B010](../../batches/B010/README.md)

## Mathematical content

For every integer d>=1 there are two finite rooted trees with all leaves at the same depth L and positive integer quotas p_1,...,p_L. Each tree separately has a marking using at most p_l vertices at depth l and hitting every root-to-leaf path at least d times. Yet any pair of markings sharing the quotas, with their total at each depth at most p_l, leaves some path in one tree completely unhit. Marks are on positive depths; the root is not an available universal mark.

Build an auxiliary colored tree in 2d-1 phases. If the current leaves u1,...,uN are all at depth h, add N levels with p_{h+i}=2^{i-1}. At u_i attach 2^i disjoint spines of length i, half with red and half with blue endpoints. For i<N, extend each endpoint through 1+sum_{j>i}2^{j-1} disjoint chains of length N-i. Every new leaf has depth h+N.

An unhit u_i has an unhit spine since all earlier phase quotas sum to 2^i-1. That endpoint has an unhit continuation because its chain count exceeds all remaining phase quotas. Inductively an unhit path survives every phase under any quota-feasible marking. Each auxiliary root-to-leaf path has 2d-1 colored endpoints, so at least d have one color.

In two copies retain all complete paths having at least d red or at least d blue endpoints, respectively. Do not contract unary vertices: depth quotas must remain aligned. Mark the appropriate color in each copy; there are at most p_l such vertices at each depth. Any shared one-hit marking projects by union to a quota-feasible marking hitting all auxiliary paths, a contradiction.

## Role in the source argument

Creates incompatible resource demands whose individual fractional or separated solutions remain cheap in the bin-packing reduction.

## Antecedents and comparison

The source explicitly builds on the disjoint-branch idea in [Chalermsook-Chuzhoy, Resource Minimization for Fire Containment](https://doi.org/10.1137/1.9781611973075.108), Section 6. The additional selected feature is two-color path pruning with separate d-hit markings and a shared one-hit obstruction. The full antecedent PDF was not retrieved during this batch; this specific attribution follows the source.

## Checks and limitations

Read the complete phase and pruning argument. Finite quota calculations and small phase trees check the unhit-branch mechanism; the d=1 two-tree instance is exhaustively enumerated. These checks do not construct the enormous general d trees.

The construction is finite but grows rapidly with d. Its use in a polynomial reduction keeps d fixed before input size grows. Roots cannot be marked, and preserving depths during pruning is essential.

## Possible applications

Separating shared and individual budgets in hierarchical covering, scheduling and integrality-gap constructions.
