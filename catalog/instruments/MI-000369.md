# Pay for shrinking domains with a quadratic holomorphic splitting recurrence

[Family 042](../families/openai-math-042.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000203.md) · [Batch B033](../../batches/B033/README.md)

## Mathematical content

On buffered overlaps, let gamma(v)=v+c(v) be a small transition on a fixed auxiliary fiber ball. A fixed linear dbar solution splits c=b-a, with size and fiber derivative bounds C h^{-p}e when e bounds c and h is the base-domain loss. The new transition is (id+b)^{-1} gamma (id+a)=id+d. Its equation is

$$d=c(v+a)-c(v)-\{b(v+d)-b(v)\},$$

so a fiber contraction gives size O(h^{-p}e^2), after adjusting the fixed exponent and constant. With h_i=h_0 2^{-i}, write A=C h_0^{-p}. The recurrence e_{i+1}<=A 2^{pi}e_i^2 becomes E_{i+1}<=E_i^2 under E_i=A 2^{p(i+1)}e_i. If E_0<1/2, this beats every fixed geometric derivative loss, so the corrections and derivatives are summable on the remaining domain.

Composing the limiting corrections glues the sprays. Translating small fiber corrections into small target displacement requires a fixed spray on the controlled side or a separately uniform modulus of continuity.

## Role in the source argument

Makes the gluing iteration quantitative while retaining a positive domain margin.

## Antecedents and comparison

[Forstneric 2013, Lemma 3.8 and Section 3.3](https://users.fmf.uni-lj.si/forstneric/papers/2013AFST.pdf) supplies the classical additive and nonlinear splitting background. Newton loss budgets are standard; no new general splitting theorem is claimed here.

## Checks and limitations

Exact second-order cancellation in a scalar nonlinear transition and 36 exact rescaled recurrence steps.

No dbar estimate, spray existence or uniform modulus for a changing spray is computationally certified.

## Possible applications

Analytic gluing procedures in which polynomial losses accompany geometric domain shrinkage.
