# Delocalized exterior minors and spectral damping give a quadratic short-interval bound

[Family 226](../families/openai-math-226.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000097.md) · [Batch B006](../../batches/B006/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let Q be an orthogonal one-particle mode matrix on n sites with $|Q_{ir}|\le C/\sqrt n$, let S be a set of sites, and put $P_S=Q^T\mathbf1_SQ$. The two-replica operator that deletes two occupied sites in the first exterior factor and creates them in the second has mode-pair coefficient

$$\sum_{i<j\in S}\det Q_{\{i,j\},R}\det Q_{\{i,j\},T}=\det(P_S)_{R,T}$$

for increasing two-element mode sets R,T. This is Cauchy-Binet. Each coefficient has absolute value at most $2C^4(|S|/n)^2$; the wedge signs do not alter that bound.

Suppose normalized transfers on each side are diagonal in compatible mode bases with nonnegative weights $d_-(\mu),d_+(\nu)$ and bounded sums. Then the sandwiched insertion satisfies

$$\|D_+\mathcal O_SD_-\|\le2C^4(|S|/n)^2\Bigl(\sum_\nu d_+(\nu)\Bigr)\Bigl(\sum_\mu d_-(\mu)\Bigr).$$

The source supplies those state sums for odd width $n=2p+1$. With $u_l=\operatorname{arsinh}(\cos(\pi l/(n+1)))$, single-particle singular values are $e^{u_l},1,e^{-u_l}$. Relative to the filled expanding modes,

$$\sum_Jw_t(J)=2\prod_{l=1}^p(1+e^{-tu_l})^2\le C_\eta\quad(t\ge\eta n).$$

The factor two retains the zero mode. Thus positive macroscopic transfer distances before and after the insertion convert mode delocalization into a quadratic bound uniform in n. The source’s marked-cap norm and partition comparisons then give the same order for the normalized observable expectation.

## Role in the source argument

Supplies the small expectation needed to exclude signed crossing anomalies on short transverse intervals. The subsequent positivity argument turns it into a probability bound.

## Antecedents and comparison

The transfer-matrix method is due to [Lieb](https://collaborate.princeton.edu/en/publications/solution-of-the-dimer-problem-by-the-transfer-matrix-method/); open-boundary spectral sectors are developed by [Rasmussen and Ruelle](https://arxiv.org/abs/1207.0385). Cauchy-Binet and exterior powers are classical. The extraction concerns the insertion after propagation on both sides and its use with a separate planar positivity construction.

## Checks and limitations

Checked the minor identity, uniqueness of the removed/added pair for a matrix entry, the zero-mode factor and the entrywise absolute-sum norm estimate. Finite sine-mode and exterior-state computations test these identities and bounds.

An entrywise minor bound alone does not imply a dimension-uniform operator bound. Summable damped state weights and compatible bases are essential. This calculation does not establish the source’s cap comparison or full curve tightness.

## Possible applications

Localizing multi-particle observables in transfer systems when delocalized modes and a positive propagation gap make occupation-state sums uniformly finite.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
