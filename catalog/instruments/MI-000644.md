# Detect numerical triviality from one ample intersection for a nef surface class

[Family 035](../families/openai-math-035.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000413.md) · [Batch B067](../../batches/B067/README.md)

## Mathematical content

On an integral projective surface T, let H be ample Cartier and P nef rational Cartier. If P.H=0, then P is numerically trivial. For any integral curve C, a sufficiently large multiple of H has an effective Cartier member containing C. Its effective one-cycle has a positive coefficient at C and nonnegative P-degree on every component. The total degree is zero, forcing P.C=0. A nonzero effective Cartier divisor has positive H-degree by the ample degree of each curve component.

Consequently, if N is a nonzero effective nef Cartier divisor on a projective threefold and N².H=0 for ample H, then N restricted to each prime surface component T of N is numerically trivial. The positive-coefficient sum of the nonnegative numbers N.H.T is zero, and the surface criterion applies to each restriction. This argument uses ampleness and effective cycles and does not require a smooth surface or a Hodge-index theorem.

## Role in the source argument

Turns a numerical-dimension-one intersection identity into exact numerical restrictions on every boundary component.

## Antecedents and comparison

This is elementary positivity of nef divisors and sufficiently positive ample systems, valid in any characteristic under the stated Cartier assumptions. No abundance conclusion follows without the source remaining arguments.

## Checks and limitations

Exact toric surface intersection models and nonnegative effective-cycle sums check the zero-detection step. Non-nef classes orthogonal to an ample class provide a negative control.

Toric models do not verify the source boundary preparation, torsion or abundance theorem.

## Possible applications

Reducing a vanishing global intersection to componentwise numerical triviality.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
