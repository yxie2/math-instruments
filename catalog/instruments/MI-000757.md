# Extend a heat-kernel sandwich estimate to every positive real trace power

[Family 267](../families/openai-math-267.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000505.md) · [Batch B081](../../batches/B081/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let A=sum_i a(x_i), with bounded Lipschitz a, and let H have kinetic coefficients c_i<=c_1 and a commuting multiplication potential, on a form domain preserved by multiplication by exp(uA). For delta>0 put X=exp(A)exp(-delta H)exp(A) and H_tilde=H-sum_i[2a(x_i)/delta+c_1|grad a(x_i)|^2]. Under the source compactness and trace hypotheses,
Tr X^q <= Tr exp(-q delta H_tilde) for EVERY real q>0.

Set U(s)=exp(u(s)A)exp(-sH)exp(A), u(s)=-1+2s/delta. Then U(0)=I and U(delta)=X. The form product rule gives one half of the derivative of ||U(s)f||^2 at most -q_tilde[U(s)f]: the mixed kinetic term is imaginary and the real correction is u(s)^2 sum c_i|grad a|^2. This uses the form domain; multiplication need not preserve the operator domain.

Apply the energy estimate to exterior powers of the WHOLE Hilbert space. This is a singular-value argument, not a change of particle statistics. For the decreasing eigenvalues x_i of X and increasing eigenvalues E_i of H_tilde it yields sum_(i<=k)log x_i<=-delta sum_(i<=k)E_i for every k. Consequently sum_i(log x_i-h)_+<=sum_i(-delta E_i-h)_+. Integrate against q^2 exp(qh) dh, using exp(qu)=integral q^2 exp(qh)(u-h)_+ dh, to obtain the trace inequality for arbitrary positive q, without integer replication.

## Role in the source argument

Avoiding an integer-power restriction in a positive heat-kernel comparison.

## Antecedents and comparison

The differential energy and exterior-power route is classical log-majorization machinery. The source cites a ground-state companion for the sandwich and supplies a self-contained proof here; that internal citation is not independent novelty evidence.

## Checks and limitations

A finite graph analogue uses its exact cosh edge correction, rather than importing the continuum gradient-square term. It checks the energy matrix inequality, all leading log-eigenvalue sums and fractional as well as integer trace powers.

The graph test checks the finite mechanism, not the continuum Lipschitz product rule, trace-class limits or the source condensation theorem.

## Possible applications

Avoiding an integer-power restriction in a positive heat-kernel comparison.
