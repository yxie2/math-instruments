# Budget countably many deep relators with weighted Golod-Shafarevich

[Family 046](../families/openai-math-046.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000424.md) · [Batch B069](../../batches/B069/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Give the free pro-2 generators positive integer Magnus weights w_i. For relators r_lambda suppose nu(r_lambda)>=v_lambda>=1, only finitely many bounds lie below each fixed degree, and the series sum t^v_lambda converges at some 0<t<1. If 1-sum_i t^w_i+sum_lambda t^v_lambda<0, the quotient by their closed normal closure is infinite.

In the completed noncommutative algebra quotient let C(n) be the dimension modulo degree n and set C(n)=0 for n<=0. First-letter decomposition and relator derivatives give C(n)-sum_i C(n-w_i)+sum_lambda C(n-v_lambda)>=1. Closure does not enlarge a finite-dimensional truncated span. A finite group quotient of order m would force C(n)<=m. Multiplication by t^(n-1) and summation would then make the negative budget times a positive convergent series at least 1/(1-t), a contradiction.

The companion substitution lemma provides degree bounds: if an automorphism changes generator i only in degrees >=w_i+a, then alpha-id sends filtration n into n+a. Each substituted monomial error replaces at least one factor by an error of a extra degrees; completeness extends this to series. Degree-raising operator substitutions are likewise well defined in each finite truncation.

## Role in the source argument

Separates finitely many expensive relations from an arbitrarily deep convergent tail of additional relations.

## Checks and limitations

Exact noncommutative substitutions check degree gains. A monomial algebra checks the cumulative inequality, and rational geometric-series budgets check convergence and strict negativity.

Finite truncations do not verify all source geometric relators or prove their proposed group realization.
