# Partial stub pairings have exact low-rank resolvent updates and centered pair cancellation

[Family 178](../families/openai-math-178.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000134.md) · [Batch B015](../../batches/B015/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Give n vertices d stubs each. For l unresolved stubs S, let C_S be their incidence matrix, t=C_S 1, Xi=C_S(I-J_l/l), and D=Xi Xi^T=diag(t)-tt^T/l. If A is the adjacency matrix of resolved pairs, with a loop counted twice on the diagonal, set A_e=A+tt^T/l. For sigma=plus or minus one, z=h^(-1)+(d-1)h and m_h=h/(1-h^2), put M=zI-sigma A_e-hD and W=M^(-1)/m_h on the constant-orthogonal vertex space when M is positive.

For distinct u,v in S and l>2, put U=[xi_u,xi_v], G0=I-J2/l, B=G0^(-1)=I+J2/(l-2), and H0=-sigma h F B, where F swaps two coordinates. Resolving this pair gives

$$D'=D-UBU^T,\quad A_e'=A_e+UFBU^T,\quad M'=M+U(hI-\sigma F)BU^T.$$

If G=U^T WU, Dp=G-G0 and H=(H0^(-1)+Dp)^(-1), Woodbury gives W'=W-WUHU^TW whenever the inverses exist.

Let C be full stub incidence and P exchange resolved partners and average unresolved stubs. Then P^3=P. With Q0=I and Qj=CP[(C^TC-I)P]^(j-1)C^T, write D_e=CP^2C^T=dI-D. One has Q1=A_e, Q2=A_e^2-D_e and Qj=A_e Q_(j-1)-(D_e-I)Q_(j-2) for j>=3. Thus W=sum_(j>=0)(sigma h)^j Qj as a formal series on the contrast space.

For scalar arrays a,c and an ordered distinct pair uniform in I subset S, |I|=l-k>=2,

$$\frac{\mathbb E[a_p^TH_0c_p]}{-2\sigma h}=\frac{(1+1/(l-2))(\sum_Ia)(\sum_Ic)-k\sum_Iac/(l-2)}{|I|(|I|-1)}.$$

It vanishes for centered arrays when I=S. Deleting possible endpoints introduces the displayed diagonal correction, so centering on the smaller set alone does not restore exact cancellation.

## Antecedents and comparison

[Foata-Zeilberger, Section 6](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/9806037v1) provides the classical Ihara-Bass matrix-polynomial context. Woodbury inversion is established and is explicitly credited by the source. The unresolved averaging block and its precise update and cancellation formulas are the selected interface.

## Checks and limitations

Read the recurrence, direct matrix expansion, inverse update and pair average. Finite partial configurations check all three update matrices, recurrence coefficients, contrast-space inverses and restricted-pair expectations, including repeated vertex incidences.

The l=2 final pairing needs a separate formula. Positivity or existence of the updated inverse is a hypothesis of the inverse calculation; these identities alone do not maintain it through a full construction. The formal series is not asserted to converge for all tracked h.

## Possible applications

Analyzing sequential regular-graph pairings through exact matrix changes rather than treating each exposure as an independent edge perturbation.
