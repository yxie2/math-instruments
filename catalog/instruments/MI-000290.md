# Exponential-clock thinning cancels its own inverse-probability cost

[Family 083](../families/openai-math-083.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000164.md) · [Batch B023](../../batches/B023/README.md)

## Mathematical content

On a finite conflict graph, assign independent exponential clocks of positive rates n_c and retain c if its clock precedes every neighboring clock. Retained vertices are pairwise nonadjacent and

$$\pi_c=\frac{n_c}{n_c+\sum_{c'\sim c}n_{c'}}.$$

If m_c bounds the whole competing rate, then pi_c>=n_c/m_c. Indicators need not be independent. Multiplying a retained contribution by pi_c^(-1) reconstructs it in expectation, while its expected rth-power cost is pi_c^(1-r).

Let 0<=mu<1, r=1/(1-mu), r<=p, l_c>=0 with sum l_c<=1, and |lambda_c|=(n_c/m_c)^mu. Suppose the source's separated-family Lp estimate applies to each retained sum. For F=sum l_c^(1-mu)lambda_c U_c X_c, use the unbiased retained sum with coefficients divided by pi_c. Minkowski followed by concave Jensen bounds its Lp norm by

$$\left(\sum_c\pi_c^{1-r}l_c|\lambda_c|^r\|X_c\|^r\right)^{1/r}\le\left(\sum_c l_c\|X_c\|^p\right)^{1/p}.$$

The cancellation is exact: pi_c^(1-r)|lambda_c|^r=((n_c/m_c)/pi_c)^(r-1)<=1, and r(1-mu)=1 turns the mass power into l_c. Missing mass can be put at a zero vector for the last weighted Lr-to-Lp step.

In the paper, conflicts mean child intervals lie too close, the clocks are fixed at one snapshot, and band-limited probabilities supply the separated-family estimate. Counting retained children and verifying their carrier-frequency separation are additional hypotheses, not consequences of unbiased sampling.

## Role in the source argument

Transfers an estimate for separated child sets to the full weighted family without paying for each competing neighbor separately.

## Checks and limitations

Read the full clock and averaging arguments. Exact rational race-order probabilities are enumerated in 32 graphs, totaling 6,960 orders. Numerical r=p=2 Hilbert models verify unbiased reconstruction and the compensated bound; 181 indicator pairs are explicitly dependent.

The program does not test the source higher-p Fourier estimate or geometric snapshot hypotheses. Removing the compensating coefficient can fail even for a complete conflict graph with equal rates and identical vectors.

## Possible applications

Extending estimates available only on separated or conflict-free subfamilies to weighted overlapping data.
