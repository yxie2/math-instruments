# Compare projected mass with integer counts using determinant and tilt errors

[Family 346](../families/openai-math-346.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000376.md) · [Batch B063](../../batches/B063/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let P be orthogonal projection onto an m-plane, decomposed relative to an m-dimensional reference tangent plane and its normal complement. Put B=I-P_hh and e=tr B=tr P_vv. Then 0<=B<=I and P_hv P_vh=B-B², so |P_vh a|²<=B[a,a]. For nearest-point projection onto a smooth reference submanifold, J_z=I-A_z is the tubular tangential factor and

J_pi=sqrt(det P_hh)/det J_z.

With the tube uniformly small, the source obtains |J_pi-1|<=C(e+lambda), where lambda=|z|²+|H_S|²+|grad-perp H_S|² uses physical units. The key expansions are 1-sqrt(product(1-b_i))<=sum b_i=e, det J_z=1-<H_S,z>+O(|z|²), and sqrt(det(I-B))=1-e/2+O(e²). In particular, for small e, 2(1-J_pi)-e<=C e²+C(|z|²+|H_S|²).

For an integral rectifiable measure mu, the area formula yields pi_#(J_pi mu)=N vol_S with integer N counting nonzero-Jacobian preimages with multiplicity. Thus |pi_#mu-N vol_S| is bounded by the pushforward of |1-J_pi|mu. A deficient projection has e>=1 and is paid for by this error. Its pushforward need not be absolutely continuous.

## Role in the source argument

Separates integer sheet counts from geometric projection defects without discarding degenerate pieces.

## Antecedents and comparison

[Simon, Introduction to Geometric Measure Theory](https://web.stanford.edu/class/math285/ts-gmt.pdf), Chapter 3, equations (2.3)-(2.5), gives the Jacobian and weighted rectifiable area formula. Printed pages 76-77 were inspected visually because text extraction was corrupted. The determinant estimates are elementary consequences, not new area formulas.

## Checks and limitations

Random orthogonal tangent projections, curved tubular factors and a rank-deficient control check the determinant, block identity and second-order tilt behavior. Linear graph sheets verify integer multiplicity.

Finite matrices and linear sheets do not verify stationary first-variation estimates, signed excess or almost-everywhere regularity.

## Possible applications

Transferring geometric mass to an integer-valued base count while retaining singular projection errors.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
