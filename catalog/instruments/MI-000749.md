# Compare ordered marginals using the actual prefix distribution

[Family 238](../families/openai-math-238.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000501.md) · [Batch B080](../../batches/B080/README.md)

## Mathematical content

For any law of a distinct-position tuple (Z_1,...,Z_k), its TV distance from uniform injections is at most the sum over j of E TV(law(Z_j|Z_<j), uniform on unused positions). Every expectation uses the actual prefix distribution.

Compare the actual j-tuple to its actual (j-1)-prefix extended by a uniform available position; their distance equals the jth summand. Extending the uniform prefix by the same kernel gives uniform injections and cannot increase TV. Triangle inequality and induction prove the bound. Refining the conditioning can only increase expected TV if the new sigma-field contains the prefix, since the comparison law is already prefix-measurable.

Thus exposed-path conditional flows can upper-bound endpoint tuple distances. If w_j is the centered conditional law with m_j free positions, its contribution is at most (1/2)sqrt(m_j E||w_j||_2^2). An average energy estimate over input lists yields an average marginal estimate over those lists. It does not assert the same bound for every fixed input list, and a further argument is required to control the entire permutation.

## Role in the source argument

Converting conditional one-coordinate information into a joint law while preserving the averaging convention.

## Antecedents and comparison

[Morris, Rogaway and Stegers, author conference text](https://www.cs.ucdavis.edu/~rogaway/papers/thorp.pdf), Appendix A, Lemma 2 on PDF page 18, was read in full. It gives the established sequential TV comparison for general tuple laws. The injection version and refinement step are its direct specializations.

## Checks and limitations

Exact finite injection laws check each actual-prefix summand, the total TV bound and an auxiliary-information refinement. Dependent tuples with uniform individual marginals demonstrate why separate one-card marginals are insufficient.

These checks do not verify the subsequent Fourier lift or the claimed logarithmic mixing time.

Converting conditional one-coordinate information into a joint law while preserving the averaging convention.
