# Force a fixed k-plane by a unitary incidence manifold of degree one

[Family 256](../families/openai-math-256.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000363.md) · [Batch B061](../../batches/B061/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let s_1,...,s_n be signs with sum epsilon=+1 or -1, let k>=1 and m=nk. For every fixed P in U(m), some X in U(k) makes diag(X^(s_1),...,X^(s_n))P fix a complex k-plane. No continuous choice of X or of that plane is asserted.

Remove P along a unitary path. A positive and negative pair cancels through diag(X,I) R_u diag(I,X^(-1)) R_u^(-1), where R_u rotates the two k-blocks by pi u/2. Move the remaining identity blocks to -I. The map is therefore homotopic to F0(X)=X^epsilon direct-sum (-I_r), r=m-k.

Let R={(W,E):W in U(m), E a k-plane, W fixes E pointwise}. It is a compact connected bundle over the Grassmannian with fiber U(r), of real dimension 2kr+r^2=m^2-k^2. Hence U(k) times R has the same dimension as U(m). The map G0(X,W,E)=F0(X)W^(-1) has a unique preimage of I: X=I, W0=I_k direct-sum (-I_r), E=E0. In tangent coordinates A in u(k), B in u(r), Z in complex r-by-k matrices, its derivative is

dG0(A,B,Z)=[[epsilon A,2 Z*],[-2 Z,-B]],

an isomorphism onto u(m). Thus the unique fiber is regular and the mod-two degree is one, including r=0. Homotopy invariance gives the same degree for G(X,W,E)=F(X)W^(-1). It cannot miss I, and a preimage supplies the required fixed plane. Keeping E in the domain is what produces a smooth incidence manifold of the correct dimension.

## Role in the source argument

Supplies a mixing unitary with a large fixed space for the source planar construction.

## Antecedents and comparison

[Klyachko and Thom, New topological methods to solve equations over groups](https://arxiv.org/abs/1509.01376), Section 1.1 on pages 2-3, explains the Gerstenhaber-Rothaus strategy of removing coefficients by homotopy and using nonzero degree in compact unitary groups; those pages were read. That discussion does not contain this exact fixed-plane incidence lemma. Its independent priority is unresolved.

## Checks and limitations

Eighteen tangent models verify unique block decomposition, dimension matching and the derivative against central finite differences. Unitary block homotopies verify both endpoints and intermediate unitarity, including k=1 and r=0.

Numerical derivatives do not prove a degree theorem or existence for every P. The compactness, unique fiber and degree argument were inspected analytically; the subsequent group theorem is outside this extraction.

## Possible applications

Forcing spectral multiplicity through an incidence space rather than a singular locus of matrices with a large fixed space.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
