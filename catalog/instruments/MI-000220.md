# Singular concentration and common branch mass produce many nearby opposite prefixes

[Family 153](../families/openai-math-153.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000130.md) · [Batch B014](../../batches/B014/README.md)

## Mathematical content

For the fair-bit Bernoulli law with 1/2<lambda<1, assume singularity and let xi<=mu_0,mu_1 have mass b>0. There exist a in (0,L/2) and c>0 such that for every Q>1 and all sufficiently large n, at least c 2^n words u of length n starting with zero have at least Q(2lambda)^n distinct words v starting with one satisfying

$$|s_u(\lambda)-s_v(\lambda)|\le(L-a)\lambda^n.$$

The constants a,c do not depend on Q; the threshold for n may. Choose a so the tail belongs to [a,L-a] with probability greater than 1-b/8. Singular concentration gives a compact K with Lebesgue measure below epsilon and mass above 1-epsilon, where epsilon<b/8 and 2 epsilon Q/a<b/4. For large n, at most 2epsilon/(a lambda^n) bins of length a lambda^n meet K. The two branch measures, restricted to central tails, have total common bin mass at least 3b/4.

Let U_i(J) contain the length-n words starting with i whose central tail interval s_u+lambda^n[a,L-a] meets bin J. The branch mass in J is at most 2^(-n)|U_i(J)|. Discard bins with |U_1(J)|<Q(2lambda)^n; the lost common mass is less than b/4. Each zero-starting word meets at most C_a=ceil((L-2a)/a)+2 bins, so the surviving union contains at least b 2^n/(2C_a) words. Every such word shares a bin with the required number of one-starting words, and interval geometry gives the distance bound.

The finite mass principle is general: if K bins carry masses alpha_iJ<=2^(-n)|U_i(J)|, their common mass totals beta, and each left word occurs in at most C bins, then discarding right degrees below D leaves at least 2^n(beta-K 2^(-n)D)/C distinct left words, each sharing a bin with at least D right words.

## Role in the source argument

Amplifies positive common branch mass into many close prefix pairs, a combinatorial input to later arithmetic approximation.

## Checks and limitations

Read the restrictions, discarded-bin mass and bounded-incidence calculation. Exact finite interval models check the general mass principle, multiplicities and distance conclusion; they do not test singularity of a parameter.

Distinct words may have equal prefix values. The threshold depends on Q and on compact approximation. This lemma alone does not give irreducible polynomial approximants or the claimed arithmetic classification.
