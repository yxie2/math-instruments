# Combine posterior experiments with their exact Bayes reweighting

[Family 229](../families/openai-math-229.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000301.md) · [Batch B054](../../batches/B054/README.md)

## Mathematical content

For a uniform four-state input I, a posterior law mu has barycenter u=(1/4,...,1/4), and its conditional observation density under I=i is 4p_i. Conversely any such law defines an experiment this way. If two experiments are conditionally independent given I and have posterior marginal laws mu and nu, define U_i=4p_i q_i and z=sum_i U_i. Their joint marginal law is z mu(dp)nu(dq), of total mass one, and the combined posterior is U/z when z>0. Its value on z=0 is immaterial.

This factor z cannot be dropped. Contradictory pure posteriors have zero actual probability despite positive mass under an unweighted product. Iterating the identity gives the usual normalized product update with the corresponding marginal reweighting. Coordinate-permutation symmetry is preserved.

A symmetric channel sends p to C_lambda(p)=u+lambda(p-u). In the four-state Hadamard coordinates Lp, A(p)=|Lp|^2=4sum_i(p_i-1/4)^2, so A(C_lambda p)=lambda^2 A(p) and TV(p,u)<=sqrt(A(p))/2. The source uses ferromagnetic lambda; the algebra holds for every stochastic symmetric channel, lambda in [-1/3,1].

## Role in the source argument

Keeps the source posterior recursion and moment calculations normalized under the actual observation law.

## Antecedents and comparison

[Mezard-Montanari, Reconstruction on trees and spin glass transition](https://arxiv.org/pdf/cond-mat/0512295), equations (8), (11) and (17), give the normalized posterior recursion, Bayes relation and unconditional reweighting. These passages on PDF pages 7-9 were read. The selected product law is an instance of that established recursion.

## Checks and limitations

Exact rational finite channels compare direct conditional-product probabilities with the reweighted posterior law, check edge-channel scaling and include contradictory pure observations as a zero-mass control.

These identities do not verify the source higher-moment inequalities or its sharp reconstruction thresholds.

## Possible applications

Implementing finite posterior recursions without confusing conditional independence with independence of observed posterior vectors.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
