# Recursive spectral cuts separate PSD matrices with a dimension-free overlap charge

[Family 126](../families/openai-math-126.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000115.md) · [Batch B011](../../batches/B011/README.md)

## Mathematical content

For L>=1 real symmetric positive semidefinite matrices A_1,...,A_L on the same finite-dimensional Euclidean space, there are mutually orthogonal projections P_s summing to I such that

$$\sum_z\sum_{s\ne z}\|P_sA_zP_s\|_F^2\le2^{L-1}\sum_{z\ne z'}\operatorname{tr}(A_zA_{z'}).$$

The right side counts ordered pairs; zero projections are allowed. The constant depends on L but not the ambient dimension.

For two matrices A,B, take P as the nonnegative spectral projection of A-B and Q=I-P. In these blocks A_12=B_12, A_11>=B_11 and B_22>=A_22 in PSD order. Therefore tr(AB)>=||PBP||_F^2+||QAQ||_F^2. For many matrices, split A_1 against B=sum_{j>=2}A_j, then recurse on Q A_j Q within the range of Q.

Compression can increase pair overlaps, so it needs a charge: tr(A_j Q A_k Q)<=2tr(A_j A_k)+2tr(A_j P A_k P). Summing the additional terms is bounded by 2||PBP||_F^2<=2tr(A_1B). The first-label errors cost at most tr(A_1B). Thus the recursive constant doubles at each step, yielding 2^(L-1). This argument does not assume simultaneous diagonalization.

## Role in the source argument

Separates Gram-matrix contributions of different Fourier labels in the source smoothing argument without an ambient-dimension factor.

## Antecedents and comparison

The initial spectral projection is the classical [Helstrom binary discriminator](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0019-9958(67)90302-6), as credited by the source; that publisher page was not retrievable in this batch. The selected addition is the recursive Frobenius-error accounting. Targeted searches found the release and later expositions, not a verified earlier statement of the full bound.

## Checks and limitations

Checked the block trace calculation and the compression charge. Numerical constructions recurse on noncommuting PSD families, including singular and disjoint-support examples, and check projection identities and the summed inequality.

Positivity is essential for the trace comparisons. The bound is exponential in the number of labels, and controls diagonal compressions of unwanted matrices, not their entire off-diagonal blocks.

## Possible applications

Orthogonal allocation of nearly disjoint PSD mass, Fourier Gram decompositions and dimension-independent matrix approximation.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
