# Express P1 fiber integration as an involution and a divided-difference rule

[Family 067](../families/openai-math-067.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000206.md) · [Batch B033](../../batches/B033/README.md)

## Mathematical content

For a smooth projective P1-fibration p:B->D of smooth connected projective varieties, use rational cohomology and c=c_1(T_p). Leray-Hirsch gives alpha=a+cb uniquely with a,b pulled back from D. Relative GRR applied to O_B yields p_*c^2=0, so c^2 is pulled back. Hence bar(a+cb)=a-cb is a ring involution.

Writing delta=p* p_*, on even cohomology

$$\delta(a+cb)=2b,\qquad\bar\alpha=\alpha-c\delta\alpha,\qquad\delta(\alpha\gamma)=(\delta\alpha)\gamma+\bar\alpha\,\delta\gamma.$$

If h has fiber degree one, ell=2h-c and alpha-h delta alpha are pulled back. The power identities are

$$\delta(h^a)=\sum_{k=0}^{a-1}h^k(\ell-h)^{a-1-k},\qquad\delta(c^a)=(1-(-1)^a)c^{a-1}.$$

These are polynomial identities, so no division by c or 2h-ell is required. On top cohomology the involution negates the integral. No global rank-two vector-bundle presentation of B is assumed.

Generates the source characteristic-number relations through fiber integration.

## Antecedents and comparison

The construction follows classical Leray-Hirsch and relative GRR; the product rule is the standard algebra of a rank-two extension with a reflection. It is not a new general cohomology theorem.

## Checks and limitations

Exact quotient-ring product rule and thirty power identities, including identities valid when the formal divisor is not invertible.

The symbolic algebra assumes the geometric decomposition and GRR input established in the selected source proof.
