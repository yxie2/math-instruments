# Extend to a kernel normalizer using unique conjugation implementers

[Family 031](../families/openai-math-031.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000162.md) · [Batch B022](../../batches/B022/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let U be an open normal subgroup of a profinite ambient group G, let alpha:U->P be a continuous open homomorphism with open image A, and let N=ker alpha. Put T=N_G(N). Conjugation by t in T induces an automorphism of U/N=A. Suppose every such automorphism has a unique implementer beta(t) in P, meaning beta(t)alpha(u)beta(t)^(-1)=alpha(tut^(-1)).

Uniqueness implies beta(t1t2)=beta(t1)beta(t2), and beta(u)=alpha(u) on U. It also gives uniqueness of any extension inducing these conjugation maps. Since beta is continuous on the open subgroup U, its homomorphism property makes it continuous everywhere. Its image contains the open subgroup A; the compact-to-Hausdorff quotient theorem and openness of its image then make beta an open map.

In the source, P is the Galois group of the maximal solvable extension of Q. Uchida's reconstruction supplies the implementers; its uniqueness gives trivial centralizers of open subgroups. These are substantive inputs, not consequences of abstract centerlessness alone.

There are finitely many conjugates N_i of N because U normalizes N. If a distinct conjugate N_i were contained in N, iterating its conjugating element until a power lies in U would create a finite cycle of inclusions, forcing equality. Thus beta(N_i) is nontrivial for every distinct conjugate; it is normal in A because N_i is normal in U. This is the useful bridge from a kernel stabilizer to a family of nontrivial normal images.

## Antecedents and comparison

The source invokes [Uchida (1979)](https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/tmj1949/31/3/31_3_359/_article/-char/en) for unique field realization. The publisher record was verified; its full reconstruction proof was not independently audited. The abstract normalizer and uniqueness argument is standard group theory.

## Checks and limitations

Read the complete extension and conjugate-kernel argument. An exact order-72 finite wreath-product model has a proper order-36 normalizer, unique implementers, two kernel conjugates and nontrivial other-kernel image. An abelian control exhibits nonunique extensions when the centralizer condition is absent.

A finite group model cannot establish Uchida reconstruction, profinite continuity or the full open-homomorphism theorem. Both existence and uniqueness of implementers must be supplied.

## Possible applications

Extending local symmetry data to its maximal kernel-stabilizing domain when automorphisms have rigid realizations.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
