# Measure subset-sum compression by a bounded-difference deficiency

[Family 138](../families/openai-math-138.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000471.md) · [Batch B075](../../batches/B075/README.md)

## Mathematical content

For fixed integer weights c_i, define Sigma(V)={sum_(i in I)c_i:I subset V} and d(V)=|V|-log2|Sigma(V)|. This is exactly the exponent saved by storing distinct sums. Adding an index replaces the support by a union with one translate, so 0<=d(V+i)-d(V)<=1. For disjoint U,V, the sumset bound |Sigma(U union V)|<=|Sigma(U)||Sigma(V)| gives d(U union V)>=d(U)+d(V). Equal-size sets related by one exchange have deficiencies differing by at most one.

If V_q is a uniform q-subset of [n], expose an ordered sample without replacement. Conditional continuations after two possible next indices are coupled by swapping those indices. Their final sets either agree or differ by one exchange, so the conditional expectation ranges over an interval of length at most one. Orthogonality of Doob martingale differences gives Var d(V_q)<=q. Coupling successive q using one random permutation also makes E d(V_q) nondecreasing. These statements use support cardinality, not Shannon entropy of the random subset sum.

## Role in the source argument

Finds restrictions likely to retain useful compression from repeated sums.

## Antecedents and comparison

The sumset inequality and martingale bounded-difference argument are classical. The source uses them as a quantitative interface for a preliminary distinct-sum search; no new general concentration theorem is claimed.

## Checks and limitations

Enumerates subset supports for integer arrays with zeros, negative weights, repeated weights and distinct binary powers; checks increments, exchanges, superadditivity and every fixed-size variance.

The tests do not certify the preliminary search cost or the later sub-meet-in-the-middle exponent.

## Possible applications

Quantifying the stability of support compression under random restrictions.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
