# Bound empirical error using only the observed subspace

[Family 114](../families/openai-math-114.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000270.md) · [Batch B049](../../batches/B049/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let P be a finite irreducible reversible lazy Markov chain with stationary law pi. Let Q be an orthogonal projection in L2(pi) fixing constants. Assume Var_pi(Qh)<=K_obs <h,(I-P)h>_pi for every h. For G=QG and an initial law nu<=U pi, the mean squared error of the average of G over N successive states is at most 2 U K_obs Var_pi(G)/N.

To prove it, center g=G-E_pi G and set h=(I-P)^(-1)g on the mean-zero subspace. Orthogonality gives <g,h>=<g,Qh>. Cauchy-Schwarz and the restricted inequality imply <g,h>^2<=Var_pi(G) K_obs <g,h>, hence <g,(I-P)^(-1)g><=K_obs Var_pi(G). Laziness makes all nonconstant eigenvalues lie in [0,1), so integrated covariances equal this resolvent expression and are nonnegative. The stationary empirical variance is therefore at most 2 K_obs Var_pi(G)/N.

The path law from nu is dominated by U times the stationary path law, which transfers the squared-error bound even though the nonstationary estimator can be biased. A restricted inequality only for h=Qh is insufficient for the resolvent step: the resolvent need not stay in range Q. A block projection chain need not describe the full observed trajectory.

## Role in the source argument

Estimates class probabilities in the common-matroid-bases algorithm using the observable relaxation constant.

## Antecedents and comparison

[Chen-Vigoda-Yang, Theorem 5(ii) and Section 3.6](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.26599v1) already give the restricted resolvent and stationary empirical-variance proof. That argument was read directly. The source includes warm-start domination in the displayed time-average estimate.

## Checks and limitations

Exact four-state slow/fast chains test the restricted quadratic-form inequality and empirical second moments under stationary and warm starts. The observable bound remains fixed as the unobserved relaxation time diverges.

The source coupled-flow construction establishing its restricted constant is not certified. This is an error bound for selected averages, not a total-variation mixing theorem.

## Possible applications

Monte Carlo estimation when nuisance variables mix slowly but the quantities being estimated satisfy a stronger restricted variance inequality.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
