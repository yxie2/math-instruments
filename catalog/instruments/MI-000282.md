# One finite field supplies unit-separated spectra and all unit determinant roots

[Family 027](../families/openai-math-027.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000160.md) · [Batch B022](../../batches/B022/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Fix a number field K and rank r. Choose distinct primes p,q>r and let F=K(sqrt(2),zeta_pq), with ring of integers R. The values alpha_i=zeta_pq^(i-1), 1<=i<=n<=r, are distinct units with unit pairwise differences. For i!=j the exponent i-j is prime to pq, and the cyclotomic norm of 1-zeta_pq^(i-j) is Phi_pq(1)=1. It is therefore a unit already in the cyclotomic ring and remains one in R.

If an R-linear matrix M is annihilated by the product of (T-alpha_i), the usual Lagrange polynomials

$$e_i=\prod_{j\ne i}\frac{M-\alpha_j I}{\alpha_i-\alpha_j}$$

have coefficients in R and split the module into spectral summands. Those summands are projective; freeness is not automatic over a ring of integers. The source retains free graded modules and uses split exact sequences with free quotients to choose the needed integral frames.

Dirichlet's theorem gives finitely many generators u0,...,us of R^times, including a generator of its finite torsion subgroup. Adjoin zeta_r and one rth root vi of each ui. The resulting extension L/F is finite and contains an rth root of every unit, since a product of generator powers has the corresponding product of vi powers as a root. Every vi and its inverse are integral, hence units of O_L. Thus a GL_r(R) matrix can be scaled to determinant one over this same L, independently of its determinant. This is a matrix-level arithmetic provision; preserving all surface relations and boundary prescriptions still requires the source's geometric construction.

## Role in the source argument

Prevents the arithmetic field from growing with every chosen spectrum, determinant or representation.

## Antecedents and comparison

The finite-generation input is [Milne, Algebraic Number Theory, Theorem 5.1](https://www.jmilne.org/math/CourseNotes/ANTc.pdf). The cyclotomic norm and integral interpolation arguments are classical. The source combines them into a field chosen uniformly before its geometric construction; no independent priority claim is made.

## Checks and limitations

Read all three arguments. Exact rational-polynomial Euclidean arithmetic checks integral inverses of ten differences in three cyclotomic rings, together with Phi_pq(1)=1.

The program does not compute a unit basis for arbitrary number fields, verify a character variety, or turn a projective summand into a free one. Uniformity follows from the finite-generation proof, not the finite examples.

## Possible applications

Integral diagonalization and determinant normalization when a single coefficient field must serve an entire parameter family.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
