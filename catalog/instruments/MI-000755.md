# Select scale-dependent boxes with small normalized connection mass

[Family 264](../families/openai-math-264.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000504.md) · [Batch B081](../../batches/B081/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let f be a Lipschitz function on a domain in R^3, and take centers z in a precompact subdomain. For fixed K define Q_l(z)={|y-z|<Kl, |s-f(z)|<Kl}. Every such box lies in the shrinking layer |s-f(y)|<=K(1+Lip f)l, and each spacetime point belongs to boxes with a set of centers of volume at most C_K l^3.

For a locally integrable connection magnitude |Gamma|, Fubini gives integral_z l^(-3) integral_(Q_l(z)) |Gamma| <= C_K integral_(shrinking layer) |Gamma|, which tends to zero. The graph has four-dimensional Lebesgue measure zero; absolute continuity of the integral completes the argument. Therefore centers z_l can be selected along arbitrarily small scales with l^(-3) integral_(Q_l(z_l)) |Gamma| tending to zero. The source's local L^2 assumption is more than this selection step needs.

The centers need not converge to a prescribed point. For example |x|^(-p) in four dimensions, 1<p<2, is locally L^2, but a box fixed at its singular origin has normalized mass proportional to l^(1-p), which diverges.

## Role in the source argument

Finding favorable neighborhoods from weak integral control without assuming a pointwise trace.

## Antecedents and comparison

This is Fubini averaging and absolute continuity of the Lebesgue integral, adapted to a Lipschitz exit graph. It is not a trace theorem on an arbitrary chosen curve.

## Checks and limitations

Layer-volume and box-center counting are checked for flat and affine graphs. Exact radial homogeneity supplies a locally L^2 counterexample to fixed-center smallness.

The argument selects boxes only. It does not establish the geometric exit construction or inextendibility.

## Possible applications

Finding favorable neighborhoods from weak integral control without assuming a pointwise trace.
