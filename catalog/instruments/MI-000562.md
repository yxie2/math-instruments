# Use the exact affine incidence variance before any random selection

[Family 162](../families/openai-math-162.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000331.md) · [Batch B058](../../batches/B058/README.md)

## Mathematical content

For U contained in F_q^2, let i(l)=|U intersect l| over all q(q+1) affine lines. Counting points and ordered pairs gives

sum_l i(l)=(q+1)|U|, and sum_l i(l)^2=|U|^2+q|U|.

Consequently sum_l (i(l)-|U|/q)^2=q|U|-|U|^2/q<=q^3/4. If V_d=(1/q)sum_(l parallel d)(i(l)-|U|/q)^2, then sum_d V_d=|U|-|U|^2/q^2.

The diagonal ordered pairs lie on q+1 lines, while every distinct ordered pair lies on exactly one. This proves the second moment without independence or regularity assumptions. The directional sum follows because parallel classes partition the line set. The identity therefore applies to a subset already produced by a random procedure, after fixing that subset; additional concentration claims still need their own probability argument.

## Role in the source argument

Provides a deterministic variance budget for incidence profiles of selected or deleted point sets.

## Antecedents and comparison

[Murphy-Petridis](https://arxiv.org/abs/1601.03981), Lemma 1 on PDF page 2, states exactly the second-moment identity. Pages 1-2 were inspected. The centered equality and directional normalization are immediate algebraic consequences, so this is a reusable classical instrument, not a new identity.

## Checks and limitations

120 subsets in prime planes of orders 2,3,5,7,11 verify all moments and directional sums using exact rational arithmetic.

The finite checks use prime fields, while the counting proof covers prime powers. They do not validate the source random selection or counterexamples.

## Possible applications

Bounding incidence fluctuations uniformly over all point sets, including adaptively selected ones.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
