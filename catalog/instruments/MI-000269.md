# Owned envelope intersections compose finite certificates against every reply

[Family 187](../families/openai-math-187.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000154.md) · [Batch B020](../../batches/B020/README.md)

## Mathematical content

A full template (A,T,h), with finite A contained in T, means: whenever it is Maker's turn, A is already owned, and Breaker avoids T, Maker has already won or can force a win within h further actual claims of fresh cells. Additional stones outside these conditions are allowed.

Given a nonempty list of valid children (A_i,T_i,h_i) and a pivot p, set

$$T=\{p\}\cup\bigcup_iT_i,\quad
A=\left(\bigcup_iA_i\cup\bigcap_iT_i\right)\setminus\{p\},\quad
h=1+\max_i h_i.$$

If p is free, claim it. If it is already owned, make a fresh replacement move; it cannot be Breaker's because p belongs to T. Maker then owns every A_i and the entire intersection of the T_i. A legal Breaker reply cannot lie in that intersection, so at least one child envelope avoids the reply. All earlier Breaker stones avoid that child too. Apply its strategy. A reply outside the union of envelopes is covered by every child. This proves composition by induction, with no null Maker move.

For Snaky S={(0,0),(1,0),(2,0),(3,0),(3,1),(4,1)}, the six base templates are (S without one cell,S,1). The source certificate has 728 numbered cards, including these bases, with 898 additional inline composition nodes and 4,089 references. Every reference is to an earlier card; square-grid isometries transport templates. Inline coordinates stay in the current frame.

The complete reconstruction in this batch gives final A empty, |T|=251 and h=21, with T contained in {0,...,16}^2. It also checks the local reply condition at every composition node. Together with the read induction, these data yield the stated 21-move strategy. Fixing the first surviving child and a fixed enumeration for replacement moves gives one policy for every legal continuation. The same induction stays inside the final finite envelope; before any of the first 21 Maker moves at most 40 cells are occupied, so a fresh replacement exists in its 251 cells.

## Role in the source argument

Turns a compact finite certificate into a uniform strategy on an infinite board, including arbitrarily distant replies.

## Antecedents and comparison

[Sieben (2008), Sections 2-3](https://math.colgate.edu/~integers/ig7/ig7.pdf) already uses territory intersections and proof sequences of core/neighborhood situations. The source contributes this concrete 21-move certificate and explicit actual-move bookkeeping. This comparison does not establish an exhaustive priority claim.

## Checks and limitations

The short upstream verifier passed. A separately written stack parser reconstructs every numbered and inline node, checks all local reply classes including the outside class, checks the normalization identity at every node, and obtains the same endpoint. The exact pinned certificate is bundled with its SHA256, provenance and Apache 2.0 license.

This is complete finite certificate reconstruction plus a read mathematical induction, not a Lean proof build, an executable playing-policy test or a proof that 21 is optimal. The older 25- and 35-move certificates are outside this reconstruction.

## Possible applications

Reusable proof certificates for monotone achievement games with unbounded opponent move spaces.
