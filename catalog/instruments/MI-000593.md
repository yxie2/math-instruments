# Compute a weighted cycle phase from its monodromy

[Family 256](../families/openai-math-256.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000362.md) · [Batch B061](../../batches/B061/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let tau be a finite faithful trace with tau(I)=1, and let h(exp(2 pi i t)) be the fractional part of t, so h(1)=0. For unitaries Q_1,...,Q_a, define the block cycle T(e_j tensor v)=e_(j+1) tensor Q_j v, with cyclic indices. Put C=Q_a...Q_1. With the unnormalized block trace tau_a, one has

tau_a(h(T))=(a-1)/2+tau(h(C)).

Conjugating by the scalar diagonal (1,zeta,...,zeta^(a-1)), zeta=exp(2 pi i/a), sends T to zeta T, so all these rotated operators have the same phase trace. The scalar identity sum_(j=0)^(a-1) h(zeta^j lambda)=(a-1)/2+h(lambda^a) holds even at the phase cut. Apply bounded Borel functional calculus and trace. The diagonal blocks of T^a are cyclically reordered products of the Q_j, hence are unitarily conjugate and each has phase trace tau(h(C)). This gives the formula.

If the coefficient trace has mass b instead, the constant becomes b(a-1)/2. Phase is not continuous from both sides at identity: positive small rotations approach zero, negative small rotations approach the full trace mass.

## Role in the source argument

Expresses a planar boundary operator invariant in terms of products around its individual boundary cycles.

## Antecedents and comparison

This is a root-of-unity averaging identity combined with conjugacy of cyclic products. The source states that its spectral-phase framework follows the Kervaire companion. No independent priority or novelty claim is made for the cycle formula.

## Checks and limitations

Twenty-four block cycles with noncommuting unitary weights verify the identity and trace normalization. Half enforce identity monodromy; separate controls check the one-sided phase cut.

Finite matrix checks do not certify the arbitrary-group von Neumann algebra assembly or the planar inequality.

## Possible applications

Reducing the phase trace of a block permutation to the monodromy of each cycle.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
