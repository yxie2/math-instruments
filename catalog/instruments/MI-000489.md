# Use covariance rank to obstruct an interior entropy maximum

[Family 113](../families/openai-math-113.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000268.md) · [Batch B049](../../batches/B049/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let a finite family of Boolean vectors in {0,1}^s all have coordinate sum m. On a face of their convex hull of dimension d, take x in the relative interior with 0<x_e<1 for every retained coordinate. Let H(x) be maximum entropy among laws with mean x, F(x)=-sum x_e log x_e, B(x)=-sum(1-x_e)log(1-x_e), and J_c=F-cB.

The entropy maximizer is positive on the face atoms and has exponential form p_theta(a)=exp(theta.a)/Z(theta). Positivity follows by mixing with a positive feasible face law: opening a zero mass gains order u log(1/u), outweighing the linear loss. The covariance K has image equal to the face tangent space and rank d; K_ee=x_e(1-x_e).

Along an exponential tilt theta+t v, x'=Kv and x'' remains tangent. With g=grad J_c and D=diag(-1/x_e+c/(1-x_e)), the second derivative of J_c-H is (Kv)^T D(Kv)+v^T Kv+(g+theta).x''. At a relative interior local maximum, stationarity kills the last term. Hence KDK+K is negative semidefinite. Sum its quadratic forms over covariance eigenvectors, dividing each by its positive eigenvalue: d+tr(KD)<=0. No commutation between D and K is required.

Using the Boolean diagonal and sum x_e=m gives tr(KD)=-s+(c+1)m. Therefore an interior local maximum requires d-s+(c+1)m<=0. A separate lower bound for the face dimension can rule out such maxima. The matching source supplies such geometry and a boundary induction; neither follows from this local trace calculation.

## Role in the source argument

Converts the companion entropy inequality into a dimension question for minimal faces.

## Antecedents and comparison

[Wainwright-Jordan, Section 3.5.1](https://people.eecs.berkeley.edu/~jordan/sail/readings/wainwright-jordan-fnt.pdf), equations (3.41a-b), identifies the gradient and Hessian of log Z with mean and covariance; the retrieved manuscript labels this Proposition 2, not Proposition 3.1. Those pages were read. The source adds the second-variation trace use. No exhaustive priority search for that combination is claimed.

## Checks and limitations

Exact covariance matrices from enumerated perfect matchings check rank, Boolean diagonals, the trace formula and the pseudoinverse-weighted second variation, including noncommuting K and D. Symmetric examples show a positive trace excluding a local maximum.

Finite examples and the local argument do not prove the global face-dimension estimate or all boundary entropy inequalities.

## Possible applications

Entropy comparisons on constant-weight Boolean polytopes where face dimensions are easier to control than individual covariance eigenvalues.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
