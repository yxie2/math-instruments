# A weighted gap and Pauli occupation bound certify every mixed fermionic state

[Family 278](../families/openai-math-278.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000135.md) · [Batch B016](../../batches/B016/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Use the preceding orbitals, assume Delta n is in L^1, and suppose the weighted gap holds on the admissible weighted Sobolev class:

$$\tfrac12\int g^2|\nabla\xi|^2\ge d\left(\int g^2|\xi|^2-\tfrac12|\int g^2\xi|^2\right).$$

The formal potential has absolute density integrability, with

$$\int n|V|\le\tfrac14\|\Delta n\|_1+\tfrac12\|\nabla\sqrt n\|_2^2+\tfrac12\int n|\nabla\theta|^2+d.$$

Writing ghat=g/sqrt(2), the ground-state transform gives q_V[f]>=d(||f||^2-|<ghat,f>|^2), first for smooth compactly supported complex f. Local L^2 integrability of V and H^1-to-L^4 approximation extend it to compact H^1 functions; radial cutoffs extend it to all f in H^1 with integral |V||f|^2 finite. No ordinary global Hamiltonian or closed form with potential V is assumed.

For any three-fermion ensemble Gamma with density n and finite kinetic expectation, its one-body matrix gamma satisfies 0<=gamma<=I and Tr gamma=3. Absolute integrability allows summing the orbital inequality over all occupied natural spinors. The low-energy projection Pg tensor I_spin has rank two, so

$$T[\Gamma]+\int Vn\ge d(3-\operatorname{Tr}(\gamma(P_g\otimes I_2)))\ge d.$$

The determinant ghat-up wedge ghat-down wedge h-up attains equality. Hence the exact ensemble constrained-search minimum is d-integral Vn. Arbitrary mixtures, complex orbitals and spin coherences are included. The finite-dimensional algebra generalizes immediately: H>=d(I-P), rank P=r, 0<=gamma<=I and Tr gamma=N imply Tr(gamma H)>=d(N-r). This last statement is an elementary consequence, not a new many-body theorem.

## Role in the source argument

Promotes a two-orbital construction to a rigorous lower bound against all ensemble competitors.

## Antecedents and comparison

[Frank-Seiringer, equation (2.12) at p=2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0803.0503v2) records the established ground-state representation. Pauli occupation bounds and finite-rank trace inequalities are classical. The selected feature is the cutoff and absolute-integrability argument avoiding an unjustified global Hamiltonian assumption.

## Checks and limitations

Read every step of the certificate proof, including the complex test functions and cutoffs. Finite arbitrary density matrices on a three-fermion wedge space test the one-body bound, trace certificate and a saturating determinant; a weighted graph model checks the ground-state transform.

The weighted gap is an input, not established by these finite tests. The certificate alone does not prove failure of ensemble potential representability.

## Possible applications

Certifying mixed-state constrained minima from a positive orbital and a spectral-gap estimate.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
