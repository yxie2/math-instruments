# Deferred filters yield a product success law on the same recorded adaptive run

[Family 279](../families/openai-math-279.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000136.md) · [Batch B016](../../batches/B016/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Consider a bounded adaptive controller with L slots, padding early completion by dummy transitions. Its unique verifiable final data D determine, for each recorded transition input, a good-output predicate, one canonical good output and the exact dyadic ordinary good mass. Every all-good evolution completes with D, even when good outputs are supplied directly. Trials use fresh registers and leave previous workspace untouched. Independent retention coins can equalize the good mass to the same z at every good prefix.

Run the controller with ordinary/guess transitions, retain the classical history coherently, and evaluate all filters only after final verification. The total accepted probability is exactly z^L. For the proof, define an analytical experiment using the true data D to evaluate the same filters immediately, on the same transitions and coins. Passing every immediate filter implies an all-good completed run, hence verification and the deferred filters pass. Conversely, a verified deferred success supplies the true D and passes every immediate filter. The events agree pathwise.

Conditioning only on a prefix that has passed the immediate filters, the next pass probability is z. Induction gives z^L. Conditioning instead on eventual completion would change transition distributions and would not justify this calculation. Orthogonal retained classical records make path probabilities add; recording internal labels before their intended quantum interference would change the algorithm.

In the source, n>=128, L=n^10, W=n(n^5+1), s>=1-2^(-2n), delta=2^(-2n), and z=1-2^(-n). Thus (1-delta)s>=z and p=z^L>=1-n^10 2^(-n)>1/2, with polynomial denominator bit length. These conclusions are conditional on the separately constructed process interface.

## Role in the source argument

Makes the success probability known exactly although the information needed to compute each filter appears only at the end.

## Antecedents and comparison

Deferred measurement and orthogonal classical-history bookkeeping are established. [Brassard et al.](https://arxiv.org/pdf/quant-ph/0005055v1) requires an invertible full preparation for amplification. The same-run event equivalence and retrospectively computable transition filters are the selected source-specific interface.

## Checks and limitations

Read the pathwise event equivalence and prefix induction. Exact finite adaptive controllers enumerate every ordinary/guess branch, including dummy padding and aborts, and compare immediate and deferred acceptance with z^L.

Unique valid final data, all-good completion and fresh trial registers are essential. The test controllers do not validate the number-theoretic factoring subroutines.

## Possible applications

Calibrating bounded adaptive algorithms when final certificates determine exact probabilities of earlier useful transitions.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
