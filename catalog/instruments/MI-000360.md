# Prune using the original density before multiplying local fiber bounds

[Family 073](../families/openai-math-073.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000199.md) · [Batch B032](../../batches/B032/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let rho be a source probability, nu a finite pin measure, and a_0<...<a_J dyadic depths. Suppose every retained pair (x,z) in G', with z in a depth-a_j cube Q, has local directional fiber mass at the next scale at most f_j rho(Q), measured in a superset E_j of G'.

For eta=R^{-e}, delete pairs whose source cube at any endpoint depth has rho(Q intersect G'_x)<eta rho(Q). At most (J+1)eta nu(total) pair mass is deleted. Call the remainder G''. No density assertion about G'' is needed.

Fix a final angular cap and call a cube hit if it contains a G'' witness in that cap. Geometric containment puts all pre-deletion G' mass in every hit child inside the parent's local test. Thus

$$\sum_{P\subset Q,\ P\text{ hit}}\rho(P)\le\eta^{-1}\sum_{P\text{ hit}}\rho(P\cap G'_x)\le\eta^{-1}f_j\rho(Q).$$

Iteration gives the final cap mass at most eta^{-J}(product_j f_j)rho(Q_0). Radial containment uses separation from the pin. If the local tests instead use perpendicular distance to the full line through the pin and witness, bounded pin-source distances above suffice; no radial Lipschitz estimate near the pin is required.

## Role in the source argument

Makes scale-by-scale directional savings multiply without assuming a nonexistent density lower bound after the last deletion.

## Antecedents and comparison

The mechanism is standard pruning and tree multiplication. The pre-deletion density and the full-line variant are useful implementation details.

## Checks and limitations

One hundred exact finite-tree models include sparse pruning and nonvacuous single-branch product bounds.

The geometric local-fiber estimates and child containment are hypotheses, not verified by the abstract tree models.

## Possible applications

Multiscale fiber estimates with controlled mass loss and changing retained supports.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
