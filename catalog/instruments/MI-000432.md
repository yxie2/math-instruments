# Transport a cut hereditary algebra while preserving every represented matrix norm

[Family 303](../families/openai-math-303.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000235.md) · [Batch B041](../../batches/B041/README.md)

## Mathematical content

For positive d in M_n(C), v in M_m(C) with d Cuntz-subequivalent to v, let h=(d-epsilon)_+, epsilon>0. There is a rectangular partial isometry w in M_(m,n)(C**) with w*w=e_h, ww*<=e_v, w Her(h) contained in M_(m,n)(C), and w Her(h) w* contained in Her(v).

Choose s in the rectangular algebra with ||d-s*vs||<epsilon/2 and put z=v^(1/2)s h. Then (epsilon/2)h^2<=z*z<=(||d||+epsilon/2)h^2. Its polar part w has the required supports. For cutoff functions g_j of |z| vanishing near zero, w g_j(|z|)=z g_j(|z|)/|z| lies in the algebra. Approximation gives algebra membership for wk, k in Her(h).

Conjugation is an injective star homomorphism on Her(h), commutes with positive cutoffs and maps Her(l) onto Her(wlw*). In every representation, conjugating back by w* gives the reverse norm inequality, so ||pi(wkw*)||=||pi(k)||. The same applies entrywise at every matrix amplification. A continuous nonnegative f vanishing near zero consequently admits an algebra element R=w f(d)^(1/2) with R*R=f(d), RR* in Her(v) and ||R||^2=||f(d)||.

The single rectangular transport matters when several elements and their mixed products must move together.

## Role in the source argument

Moves finite tuples and their cutoff copies into one hereditary corner with common norm control.

## Antecedents and comparison

[Antoine-Perera-Thiel, Paragraph 2.4 in Section 2.1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1905.03208) explicitly recalls Rordam s lemma, including exact implementation of a cut with the other square in a target hereditary algebra. That statement was checked. The source adds rectangular and represented-norm bookkeeping.

## Checks and limitations

One hundred rectangular finite-matrix models check support identities, cutoff covariance, exact square implementation and norm equality; maximum numerical error is below 4e-14.

These tests do not prove general bidual membership or a pure-infiniteness theorem. The selected argument supplies that membership using functional calculus.

## Possible applications

Transporting multiple comparison witnesses without losing matrix-level or quotient norm information.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
