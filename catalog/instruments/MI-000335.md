# Enforce a barycenter while optimizing a variance-penalized envelope

[Family 377](../families/openai-math-377.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000186.md) · [Batch B028](../../batches/B028/README.md)

## Mathematical content

For a continuous subquadratically growing slice f, let C_s f(x) be the supremum of sum_i theta_i f(y_i)-sum_i theta_i|y_i-x|^2/(2s) over finite probability configurations with mean x. Put g(y)=f(y)-|y|^2/(2s). Then

$$C_s f(x)-\frac{|x|^2}{2s}=\min_A\max_y\{g(y)-A\cdot(y-x)\}.$$

Both extrema are attained. The dual objective grows at least |A|-C by testing y=x-A/|A|, so minimizing slopes stay bounded. The negative quadratic confines their maximizing positions. At a minimizing slope A_0, x belongs to the convex hull of the maximizing set: otherwise a separating direction would lower the maximum. A finite convex combination of maximizers then attains the primal value; at most m+1 points suffice in R^m.

The resulting affine majorant of g bounds the integral against every probability measure with mean x and finite second moment, so allowing such measures does not improve the value. It also identifies C_s f-|x|^2/(2s) as the least concave majorant of g. Common subquadratic control yields continuity and stability under local uniform limits.

If every finite configuration satisfies sum theta_i f(y_i)-f(x)<=kappa r, where r^2=sum theta_i|y_i-x|^2, then

$$0\le C_s f(x)-f(x)\le\kappa^2s/2.$$

This follows by completing the square in kappa r-r^2/(2s); equality requires r=kappa s. For f(x)=kappa|x| in one dimension, symmetric points at plus/minus kappa s attain equality at x=0.

## Role in the source argument

Produces finite contact configurations from a global horizontal Jensen defect.

## Antecedents and comparison

This is classical concave-majorant duality and finite-dimensional convex separation expressed through a variance penalty. Its use in a nonlinear PDE contact argument is distinct from the duality statement itself.

## Checks and limitations

Sixty finite-support linear programs agree with their affine-majorant duals and use at most m+1 active points. Forty-five exact absolute-value examples verify the continuum envelope formula.

Finite LPs do not prove universal attainment or the source common-time subsolution rule. The latter is a separate unaudited analytic step.

## Possible applications

Replacing arbitrary probability mixtures by finite optimizing configurations with explicit supporting affine certificates.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
