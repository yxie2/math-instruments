# Reduced envelopes normalize exactly and commute with template composition

[Family 187](../families/openai-math-187.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000154.md) · [Batch B020](../../batches/B020/README.md)

## Mathematical content

A reduced template (P,E,h) requires Maker to own P and Breaker to avoid E. Neither P contained in E nor P disjoint from E is assumed. For disjoint Maker and Breaker positions, it has exactly the same admissible positions as the full template (P,P union E,h). Conversely (A,T,h) can be reduced to (A,T without A,h).

For nonempty children (P_j,E_j,h_j), put U=union P_j, V=union E_j, I=intersection E_j and H=U union I. At pivot x the reduced composition is

$$P=H\setminus\{x\},\qquad E=(V\setminus H)\cup\{x\},\qquad h=1+\max_jh_j.$$

The identity

$$U\cup\bigcap_j(P_j\cup E_j)=U\cup\bigcap_jE_j$$

proves that normalizing every child, then applying full composition, gives required set P and full envelope P union E. To see the identity, consider a cell outside U: it belongs to no P_j, so membership in every P_j union E_j is precisely membership in every E_j. Cells in U already belong to both sides.

Thus reduced certificates use the same soundness induction, including already-owned pivots and legal replacement moves. Removing owned cells from avoidance sets changes the representation, not the set of legal starting positions or the height bound.

## Role in the source argument

Makes the source reduced-envelope construction compatible with the full-envelope strategy proof and exposes a representation invariant.

## Antecedents and comparison

[Sieben (2008), Section 3](https://math.colgate.edu/~integers/ig7/ig7.pdf) uses disjoint core/neighborhood situations. The source allows overlap and supplies the explicit normalization and composition identity. This is an elementary equivalence, not a new game-theoretic principle.

## Checks and limitations

Read the full normalization and reduced-composition proof. Check exact equality with full composition at every node of the 21-move certificate and in random finite models allowing arbitrary overlap of P and E.

This verifies the representation conversion. It does not reconstruct the distinct 25-move table printed in the source appendix, nor does it reduce the number of moves automatically.
