# Match logarithmic-kernel damping to the singular endpoint scales

[Family 005](../families/openai-math-005.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000240.md) · [Batch B042](../../batches/B042/README.md)

## Mathematical content

For -1<=x<1, 0<s<1 and 0<epsilon<1/8, set rho(x)=1-epsilon(1-x), sigma(s)=1-epsilon sqrt(1-s), and tau=1-epsilon. Write z=1-x exp(i arccos s) and z_epsilon=1-rho(x)sigma(s)x exp(i arccos s). The identity |z|^2=(1-x)^2+2x(1-s), together with separate estimates for x<=0, 0<=x<=1/2 and x>=1/2, gives

$$|z_\epsilon-z|\le3\epsilon|z|,\qquad
-\log|z|^2\le-\log|z_\epsilon|^2+6\epsilon.$$

The endpoint scales are therefore matched uniformly even as x,s approach 1. Use rho(x)rho(x prime) in the pure power kernel and sigma(s)sigma(s prime) in the pure Chebyshev kernel. Their moments are the same B_k and C_k that occur in the cross kernel, so the combined interaction is exactly -log 2-(1/2) sum_k (B_k-2C_k)^2/k after inserting diagonals.

For the pure power kernel with xx prime<0, the correct upper comparison costs at most 4epsilon; monotonicity alone applies only to nonnegative products. Diagonal removals must be handled separately.

## Role in the source argument

Makes the source many-variable majorant estimate uniform near a joint singular endpoint while preserving square completion.

## Antecedents and comparison

[Garoufalidis-Popescu, Lemma 3.1](https://people.mpim-bonn.mpg.de/stavros/publications/popescu.pdf) records Haagerup s Chebyshev logarithmic identity; its statement was checked. The matched factors and endpoint estimates were checked directly in the source.

## Checks and limitations

144 sixty-digit cross-kernel tests include distances 1e-20 from endpoints. Sixty separate damped-kernel models check the square identity with 700 terms.

Numerical checks do not establish uniform inequalities or the final irrationality certificate. Those claims are separated in the source argument.

## Possible applications

Regularizing coupled logarithmic energies without losing their common moment structure.
