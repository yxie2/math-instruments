# Concentrate a sum by counting how often each independent input is read

[Family 184](../families/openai-math-184.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000352.md) · [Batch B060](../../batches/B060/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let X_j be bounded functions of independent inputs, with range lengths b_j. If each input enters at most p functions, where p>=1 may be real, each one-sided tail at distance a>0 of sum_j(X_j-E X_j) is at most exp(-2a^2/(p sum_j b_j^2)). When all ranges vanish, the tails are zero.

For nonnegative Z_j with the same input sets, integrate inputs one at a time and apply Holder with exponent p, padding with a constant factor if fewer than p functions use the input. This gives E product Z_j <= product (E Z_j^p)^(1/p). For a variable of range length b, the second derivative of its centered log moment-generating function is a tilted variance at most b^2/4. Integrating twice yields the Hoeffding bound s^2 b^2/8. Apply these two facts to Z_j=exp(s(X_j-E X_j)) and optimize exponential Markov.

The source uses this because correspondence-coloring matchings do not provide globally independent color labels. Its support bound controls input participation instead.

## Role in the source argument

Makes local expectation estimates simultaneous while allowing dependence between list entries.

## Antecedents and comparison

[Gavinsky, Lovett, Saks and Srinivasan](https://arxiv.org/abs/1205.1478), Theorem 1.1 on page 2, gives a sharper KL-form read-k inequality for indicators; that statement and its repeated-input sharpness example were read. The source proves the bounded heterogeneous-range variant using generalized Holder and Hoeffding. No novelty is claimed for the principle.

## Checks and limitations

Thirty dependent finite-product models verify 900 one-sided tails, with unequal ranges and noninteger p. A repeated-input control shows that substituting p=1 can give a false bound.

Finite tails do not verify the source support-count estimates or its complete coloring iteration.

## Possible applications

Concentration for local functions sharing independent flags, with explicit dependence cost.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
