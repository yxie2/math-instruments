# Select a joint eigenvalue wedge and force independent graded operator directions

[Family 063](../families/openai-math-063.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000205.md) · [Batch B033](../../batches/B033/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Over an algebraically closed characteristic-zero field, choose an algebraically independent joint diagonal tuple lambda of a commuting matrix family. For each other tuple mu choose one coordinate j(mu) that separates it from lambda, and set

$$p(T)=\prod_{\mu\ne\lambda}\frac{T_{j(\mu)}-\mu_{j(\mu)}}{\lambda_{j(\mu)}-\mu_{j(\mu)}};\qquad A_0=p(A_1,\ldots,A_r).$$

Only its diagonal values matter: it need not be an idempotent on generalized eigenspaces. In a common triangular basis, the invariant alternating trace is r! m_lambda d lambda_1 wedge ... wedge d lambda_r, which is nonzero.

For A_j=D_j+sum_gamma q^gamma gamma_j S_gamma, with D_j constant, dA_j=sum q^gamma gamma_j S_gamma omega_gamma and omega_gamma=sum_l gamma_l dq_l/q_l. Expanding a nonzero alternating trace forces at least one nonzero ordered product S_gamma1 ... S_gammar whose degree vectors are linearly independent. If S_gamma lowers a grading in [0,n] by d_gamma-1, that product forces sum_j(d_gammaj-1)<=n. In the source application, d_gamma>=iota gives r(iota-1)<=n, conditional on the independent joint tuple.

## Role in the source argument

Converts a spectral independence statement into independent curve degrees and a codimension budget.
