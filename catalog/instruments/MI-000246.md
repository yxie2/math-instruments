# A positive triangular metric gives an explicit rectangular derivation implementer

[Family 288](../families/openai-math-288.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000143.md) · [Batch B018](../../batches/B018/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let sigma:A->B(K) and lambda:A->B(F) be unital star representations and let Delta:A->B(K,F) satisfy Delta(ab)=Delta(a)sigma(b)+lambda(a)Delta(b). If Delta is completely bounded and d=||Delta||>0, form

$$\Phi(a)=\begin{pmatrix}\lambda(a)&\Delta(a)/d\\0&\sigma(a)\end{pmatrix},\qquad G=1+\|\Delta\|_{\rm cb}/d.$$

This is a unital homomorphism with cb norm at most G. Paulsen's theorem supplies S with S Phi S^(-1) a star representation and ||S||||S^(-1)||<=G. Put D=S* S. The metric identity D Phi(a)=Phi(a*)* D gives D11 lambda(a)=lambda(a) D11 and D11 Delta(a)/d+D12 sigma(a)=lambda(a)D12. Since D11>=||D^(-1)||^(-1) I,

$$V=-dD_{11}^{-1}D_{12},\qquad \Delta(a)=V\sigma(a)-\lambda(a)V,\qquad \|V\|\le dG^2.$$

The inverse of D11 commutes with lambda(A). Its norm is bounded by ||D^(-1)||; this is what controls the implementer rather than merely proving its existence. Zero derivations or zero Hilbert spaces use V=0. Different domain and codomain multiplicities cause no change to the block calculation.

## Role in the source argument

Converts the later cb estimates into spatial implementations with an explicit uniform constant.

## Antecedents and comparison

The source expressly uses [Ozawa, Lemma 1.2](https://www.kurims.kyoto-u.ac.jp/~narutaka/notes/similarity.pdf), with different diagonal representations, and [Paulsen, 1984](https://doi.org/10.1090/S0002-9939-1984-0754708-X). The triangular-metric method itself is established.

## Checks and limitations

Read the block proof. Finite rectangular representations check multiplicativity, the metric identity, recovery of V and its condition-number estimate, including unequal multiplicities.

A numerical metric constructed for a known finite implementer does not prove Paulsen similarity or the global bounded-to-cb implication. The latter is not an assumption-free consequence of this card.

## Possible applications

Recovering rectangular intertwiners from a positive metric while retaining norm control.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
