# Remove stable kernels before passing from virtually free to free mapping tori

[Family 258](../families/openai-math-258.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000365.md) · [Batch B061](../../batches/B061/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let V be finitely generated and virtually free, and phi an endomorphism. Its iterated kernels eventually stabilize. Choose a finite quotient q:V->Q with torsion-free kernel, and let F be the intersection of kernels of all homomorphisms V->Q. There are finitely many such maps. Thus F is fully invariant, finite-index, normal and free of finite rank. The ranks of phi^n(F) cannot increase. Once constant, the surjections between successive images are isomorphisms by Hopficity of finite-rank free groups, so iterated kernels stabilize on F. Their increasing quotients modulo this common intersection have size bounded by [V:F], so they stabilize on V as well.

If N is the stable kernel, V/N is isomorphic to phi^n(V), hence finitely generated and virtually free, and the induced map is injective. The ascending torus is unchanged: every element of N already dies under iteration of its stable-letter relation.

For injective phi, begin with such an invariant free core F0 and replace it by F_infinity=union_(j>=0) phi^(-j)(F0). This increasing sequence has bounded finite index, so it stabilizes. Injectivity ensures torsion-freeness. Now phi^(-1)(F_infinity)=F_infinity, and phi induces an automorphism of the finite quotient. A positive power acts trivially there. The subgroup generated by F_infinity and that power of the stable letter has finite index and is an ascending torus of an injection of a finite-rank free group.

## Role in the source argument

Makes the filled end-group base suitable for an injective ascending-torus theorem.

## Antecedents and comparison

Finite-index invariant cores and rank stabilization use classical Hopficity. [Geoghegan, Mihalik, Sapir and Wise](https://math.ivanovo.ac.ru/tg-seminar/archives/Geoghegan-Mihalik-Sapir-Wise/2001a.pdf), Lemmas 3.1-3.2 and Theorem 3.3 on printed pages 296-297, supply related ascending normal-form and Hopficity arguments; those proofs were read. They are antecedents rather than a verified source for this exact virtually-free reduction.

## Checks and limitations

Endomorphisms of the virtually cyclic groups C_d times Z test stable kernel windows and, in injective cases, exact finite-quotient stabilization of the preimage core. The first instrument in this batch separately checks the stable-letter orientation.

Finite windows do not prove kernel stabilization for all virtually free groups or the later virtual-specialness input. Those reductions are based on the inspected analytic group argument.

## Possible applications

Preparing possibly noninjective virtually free endomorphisms for results about ascending HNN extensions of free groups.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
