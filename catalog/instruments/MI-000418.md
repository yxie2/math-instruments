# Prove a cut-polynomial quotient is finite by selecting a nonzero level set

[Family 044](../families/openai-math-044.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000228.md) · [Batch B039](../../batches/B039/README.md)

## Mathematical content

For finitely many slots d, nonnegative integers w_d and symmetric nonnegative integers b_de, set P=C[x_d] and let J contain, for every nonempty subset S, the polynomial

$$q_S=\prod_{d\in S}x_d^{w_d}\prod_{d\in S,\ e\notin S}(x_d-x_e)^{b_{de}}.$$

Every common zero of these polynomials is the origin, if any common zero exists. For if one coordinate equals a nonzero a, take S to be the entire level set with value a. Each displayed factor of q_S is then nonzero, a contradiction. Nullstellensatz implies either J=P or all variables are nilpotent modulo J. Hence P/J is finite dimensional. Invariants under any finite group preserving the grading and ideal remain finite dimensional and have a polynomial Hilbert series with nonnegative integer coefficients.

The quotient may be zero: a component with no framing and no cut interaction contributes a unit cut. For no slots at all, P=C and the quotient is C.

## Role in the source argument

Establishes that the source cut-quotient Hilbert series is an actual finite polynomial before counting or comparing it.

## Antecedents and comparison

The [Hausel-Sturmfels toric hyperkahler paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0203096) supplies the established cocircuit setting cited by the source. The elementary level-set argument is recorded with its exact hypotheses, without an exhaustive novelty claim.

## Checks and limitations

Thirty exact Groebner models with one to three slots include fifteen unit ideals; a finite grid also checks the nonzero-level-set obstruction.

No equality with the target cohomology or cut recurrence is certified by finiteness alone.

## Possible applications

Finite-dimensionality certificates for graph, quiver and multiset polynomial quotients.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
