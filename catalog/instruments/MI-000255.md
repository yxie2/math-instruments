# Vanishing normalized lengths pass through noninjective directed limits

[Family 194](../families/openai-math-194.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000147.md) · [Batch B019](../../batches/B019/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let lambda be a nonnegative additive length on a module category, monotone under submodules and quotients, with lambda(M)=sup_N lambda(N) over finitely generated submodules. Assume all modules under consideration, their direct sums and their colimits remain in this category. In the source this is ensured by one fixed power of the maximal ideal annihilating every module.

Direct sums of zero-length modules have length zero: each finite generating set lies in a finite subsum. Sums and directed colimits of zero-length modules are quotients of such direct sums. For an increasing union M=union_n M_n, finite generation gives lambda(M)=sup_n lambda(M_n).

If M=colim_n M_n and lambda(M_n)->0, then lambda(M)=0 even when transition maps are not injective. Any finite generating set of N contained in M lifts to one stage, and hence lies in the image of every later stage. Therefore 0<=lambda(N)<=lambda(M_n) for all sufficiently large n. Take the limit and then the supremum over N.

For a p-power-root tower A_n over a regular local ring of dimension h, the source uses lambda(M_n tensor Lambda)=p^(-nh) length_(A_n)(M_n) on finite-stage presentations, then infima over presentations and suprema over finite submodules. These normalizations are compatible under flat tower extension. They allow nonzero modules of zero length: over the one-variable perfect root tower, quotients by successively smaller positive powers t^(1/p^n) have lengths p^(-n), and their colimit is the nonzero residue-field quotient of length zero.

## Role in the source argument

Justifies passing to infinite auxiliary modules and discarding length-zero errors without asserting actual acyclicity.

## Antecedents and comparison

This uses the established normalized-length framework, including [Cai-Lee-Ma-Schwede-Tucker, Section 2.2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2209.04046v4). The elementary finite-generator proof makes the noninjective-limit use explicit; it is not a new length theory.

## Checks and limitations

Read the proof and prior length formulation. Exact finite root-tower monomial quotients check normalization, additivity and decreasing lengths. Finite linear diagrams check that representatives of a finite generating set remain controlled by later images.

The common annihilating power is needed to stay inside the specified category. Finite truncations do not certify the infinite normalized-length construction or its additivity. Length zero is weaker than module vanishing.

## Possible applications

Passing almost-vanishing homology through noninjective approximation systems with a controlled additive size.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
