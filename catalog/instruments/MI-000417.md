# Detect hard Lefschetz in nonzero tensor factors

[Family 043](../families/openai-math-043.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000227.md) · [Batch B039](../../batches/B039/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let V,W be nonzero finite graded vector spaces in characteristic zero, with degree-two operators e_V,e_W. If e_V tensor 1+1 tensor e_W satisfies hard Lefschetz on V tensor W with center r, then the two factors satisfy hard Lefschetz with centers r_V,r_W and r_V+r_W=r.

Decompose each factor into homogeneous Jordan strings. Every pair of strings is an invariant graded direct summand of the tensor product. The Lefschetz maps are block diagonal, so their invertibility holds on each pair. Its extreme nonzero degrees force its center to be the sum of the two string centers. Since every pair has center r and neither factor is zero, all strings in each factor have one common center. This is exactly hard Lefschetz for that factor. The converse follows from the usual tensor-product sl2 representation.

## Role in the source argument

Passes a Lefschetz property between a geometric product and its constituent factors.

## Checks and limitations

One hundred exact tensor examples compare block ranks and factor centers: 68 satisfy the criterion and 32 fail it.

The zero-factor exclusion is essential. No statement about infinite-dimensional or positive-characteristic representations is asserted.
