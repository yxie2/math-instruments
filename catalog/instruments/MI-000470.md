# Use nonintegral inertia to force a twisted category to vanish

[Family 069](../families/openai-math-069.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000254.md) · [Batch B046](../../batches/B046/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Suppose a central G_m in the inertia of an algebraic stack acts on a line L with fixed nonzero integer weight m. For the strongly equivariant twisted D-module category D_Z(L^s), if sm is not an integer then the category is zero. The statement is compatible with locally closed restriction and external spectator twists.

On the frame torsor, inertial scaling is 2-isomorphic to projection. Strong Kummer equivariance identifies one pullback with a constant G_m factor and another with Kummer exponent sm. The latter has zero de Rham cohomology: on Laurent polynomials the Euler differential t d/dt-sm has eigenvalues n-sm on t^n, all invertible, with inverse preserving finite Laurent support.

The constant factor has de Rham cohomology containing C as a direct summand, so tensoring with it detects zero even for unbounded complexes. Applying de Rham direct image gives vanishing on a smooth chart; conservative pullbacks give the asserted result. The strong equivariance and stack-descent setup are part of the hypothesis.

## Role in the source argument

Removes strata carrying incompatible central inertia before later global constructions.

## Checks and limitations

204 Laurent-monomial models check invertibility at nonintegral exponents; an integral exponent supplies a kernel control.

These checks do not implement twisted D-modules, strong equivariance or categorical descent.

## Possible applications

Vanishing tests for strata with a nontrivial central weight under an irrational twist.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
