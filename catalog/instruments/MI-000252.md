# Separated spectra encode four self-adjoint generators into two after amplification

[Family 296](../families/openai-math-296.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000145.md) · [Batch B018](../../batches/B018/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Suppose N=W*(a1,a2,a3,a4) with all a_i self-adjoint. Choose C>||a1||+||a2|| and set

$$A=\begin{pmatrix}a_1&0\\0&a_2+CI\end{pmatrix},\qquad B=\begin{pmatrix}a_3&I\\I&a_4\end{pmatrix}.$$

Then W*(A,B)=M_2(N). For ||a1||<lambda<C-||a2||, spectral calculus recovers E11=1_(-infinity,lambda)(A), then E22=I-E11. The off-diagonal corner E11 B E22 is exactly E12; its adjoint is E21. The first corner contains

$$a_1E_{11}=E_{11}AE_{11},\quad a_2E_{11}=E_{12}AE_{21}-CE_{11},\quad a_3E_{11}=E_{11}BE_{11},\quad a_4E_{11}=E_{12}BE_{21}.$$

Thus it contains N E11; the matrix units generate every other corner. One nonself-adjoint generator A+iB has A and B as its real and imaginary parts. The strict spectral separation provides explicit decoding and does not depend on the spectra being simple.

## Role in the source argument

Performs the final finite encoding once a half-trace corner is known to have four self-adjoint generators.

## Antecedents and comparison

This is a classical spectral-calculus and matrix-unit encoding mechanism. The card does not attribute novelty to four-to-two amplification or infer the source generator theorem from this elementary step alone.

## Checks and limitations

Read the complete proof. Random and degenerate Hermitian inputs check the spectral projection, matrix units and exact recovery of all four corner entries.

The four-generator hypothesis is essential as an input. This construction gives generation of M2(N), not automatically generation of N. A spectral cut cannot recover the intended corner if the two spectra are allowed to overlap.

## Possible applications

Encoding finitely many operator parameters into a small generating tuple with an explicit inverse procedure.
