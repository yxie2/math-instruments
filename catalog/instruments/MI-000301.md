# A weighted-divergence kernel gives a stationary flow with an exact mean shift

[Family 139](../families/openai-math-139.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000169.md) · [Batch B024](../../batches/B024/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let xi have smooth density proportional to exp(-H), let M be smooth and globally Lipschitz, and suppose a smooth bounded matrix K satisfies the rowwise weighted-divergence identity

$$\sum_j(-\partial_j K_{ij}+K_{ij}\partial_jH)=M_i-u_i.$$

For s>0, start (Y_0,G_0) with law xi tensor N(0,I), and solve

$$\dot Y=-K(Y)^TG/s,\qquad \dot G=(M(Y)-u)/s.$$

Local smoothness and linear growth give a complete flow in both time directions. Its weighted divergence under xi tensor Gaussian is zero: the Y divergence contributes g dot (M-u)/s and the G divergence contributes its negative. The flow Jacobian therefore preserves this product law.

Integrating the second equation up to time one gives

$$sG_0+\int_0^1M(Y_t)\,dt=u+sG_1\ \stackrel{law}=\ u+sG,$$

where G is standard Gaussian. The final variables Y_1,G_1 are independent; G_1 need not be independent of initial anchors. The transpose on K is necessary when K is nonsymmetric.

The source constructs K using transport derivatives and then approximates this ideal object with a finite compiler. Existence and quantitative bounds for that kernel are additional inputs here. The exact initial sample and unknown u appearing in the velocity prevent this identity alone from being an implementable sampler.

## Role in the source argument

Provides the exact Gaussian mean-shift comparison law behind the source recursive centering argument.

## Antecedents and comparison

[Ma, Chen and Fox, Equation (3) and Theorem 1](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper/2015/file/9a4400501febb2a95e79248486a5f6d3-Paper.pdf) give an established skew-symmetric recipe for invariant dynamics. [Courtade, Fathi and Pananjady, Definition 2.1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1703.07707v2) describes the standard coordinate Stein-kernel identity. Here the observable is M-u, and the extracted consequence is the integrated mean law, conditional on a suitable bounded kernel.

## Checks and limitations

36 nonsymmetric linear Gaussian models verify the invariant covariance, matrix-exponential flow and integrated output law. Omitting the transpose fails all 36 models; final Gaussian coordinates can correlate with initial anchors.

Linear Gaussian checks do not certify nonlinear transport-kernel estimates, exact initialization, the implementable compiler or its complexity.

## Possible applications

Constructing exact comparison laws for mean estimation and analyzing approximate stationary-flow implementations.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
