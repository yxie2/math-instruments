# A nilpotent centralizer realizes every allowed motion of its kernel filtration

[Family 204](../families/openai-math-204.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000150.md) · [Batch B019](../../batches/B019/README.md)

## Mathematical content

For nilpotent t on a finite-dimensional complex V, put F_b=ker(t) intersect im(t^(b-1)). The group C(t)={g invertible:gt=tg} induces the full stabilizer of this filtration on ker(t). Its contragredient action likewise induces the full stabilizer of the corresponding filtration on ker(t*) in V*. The group C(t) is irreducible.

Write V as a direct sum of Jordan modules C[t]/(t^a). A module map from a row of length a to one of length b can send its kernel vector nontrivially to the target kernel vector exactly when a<=b. When a<=b, sending the cyclic generator to c t^(b-a) times the target generator realizes any desired coefficient c. These are precisely the entries permitted by preservation of F_b. Thus every filtration-preserving kernel matrix lifts to a commuting endomorphism.

An invertible kernel action makes the entire lift invertible. Otherwise its nonzero t-stable kernel would contain a nonzero vector annihilated by t, contradicting invertibility on ker(t). Applying the same reasoning to t* and taking inverse transpose proves the dual assertion.

Finally C(t) is the determinant-nonzero open subset of the vector space End_(C[t])(V), so it is irreducible. Consequently finitely many nonempty open positioning requirements within C(t) can be met simultaneously. This does not assert that arbitrary independent, exact prescribed kernel and dual-kernel maps can both be realized by one element.

## Role in the source argument

Makes generic filtered-positioning bounds available using transformations that commute with the nilpotent operator.

## Antecedents and comparison

The source identifies this as dual to the quotient-filtration action in [Fresse, Section 3.6](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0706.3656v2). Its proof is elementary Jordan-module lifting; the centralizer action is established.

## Checks and limitations

Read the full proof and the cited quotient-filtration statement. Exact finite-field Jordan models lift random invertible allowed kernel matrices, verify commutation and full invertibility, and check the dual action.

Irreducibility and the open-set conclusion are algebraic-geometric statements over the complex numbers; a small finite field need not meet every nonempty open set. Separate surjectivity on kernel and dual kernel is weaker than joint independent prescription.
