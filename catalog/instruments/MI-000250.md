# Finite predecessor families force every irreducible class to be periodic

[Family 292](../families/openai-math-292.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000144.md) · [Batch B018](../../batches/B018/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let theta:Gamma->Gamma be a homomorphism and D a finite set of classes of nonzero finite-dimensional irreducible unitary representations. Suppose each pi in D is a subrepresentation of pi' composed with theta for some pi' in D. Then every pi in D satisfies pi equivalent to pi composed with theta^p for some p>=1.

Choose a predecessor chain tau_0=pi, tau_i contained in tau_(i+1) composed with theta. A repetition tau_l equivalent to tau_(l+p) gives tau_l contained in tau_l composed with theta^p. Equal dimensions turn this inclusion into equivalence. Iteration makes every tau_l composed with theta^(kp) irreducible.

Every intermediate pullback tau_l composed with theta^j is also irreducible: any proper invariant subspace would remain invariant under the smaller image theta^(kp)(Gamma) for kp>=j, a contradiction. The initial chain now places pi inside the irreducible tau_l composed with theta^l, so they are equivalent. Composing the cycle equivalence with theta^l transfers periodicity back to pi.

This is stronger than the statement that a finite predecessor graph eventually reaches a cycle. The backward propagation uses finite dimension and persistence of invariant subspaces under further pullback. It needs neither injectivity nor surjectivity of theta.

## Role in the source argument

Turns a finite family of analytic representation matches into a periodicity contradiction.

## Antecedents and comparison

Pigeonhole repetition and invariant-subspace arguments are elementary. Targeted extraction identifies a useful combination, not an established claim of historical novelty. The source supplies the full short proof.

## Checks and limitations

Read the proof. Exhaustive subsets of cyclic-group characters check all endomorphisms in small orders. S3 character decompositions check examples where pullback of a two-dimensional irreducible becomes reducible.

A generic finite directed graph with a predecessor at every vertex does not make every vertex periodic. Finite-dimensional irreducibility and pullback structure are essential; the analytic construction of D remains a separate input.

## Possible applications

Obstructing a finite collection closed under representation predecessors when one distinguished class has no periodic pullback.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
