# A decomposable primitive in a polynomial Hopf algebra has a primitive root

[Family 316](../families/openai-math-316.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000174.md) · [Batch B025](../../batches/B025/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let H be a connected graded commutative Hopf algebra over F_2, polynomial on positive-degree generators. Every decomposable primitive f is a square z^2 with a unique primitive root z. If H has a homology Steenrod action satisfying Cartan, an annihilated square has an annihilated root.

Let pi:H->QH=H^+/(H^+)^2 send 1 to zero. The map partial=(1 tensor pi)Delta is a derivation because pi(ab)=epsilon(a)pi(b)+epsilon(b)pi(a). For a homogeneous polynomial generator y_i,

$$\partial y_i=1\otimes[y_i]+\text{terms of smaller positive degree in the second factor}.$$

If partial f=0, inspect generators of largest degree occurring in f, then descend. The triangular formula forces every ordinary polynomial derivative of f to vanish. In characteristic two all its monomial exponents are therefore even, giving a unique square root. A decomposable primitive satisfies partial f=1 tensor pi(f)=0.

The reduced coproduct of z has square zero because z^2 is primitive. But H tensor H is polynomial, so Frobenius is injective and that reduced coproduct vanishes. Finally Cartan gives Sq_*^(2a)(z^2)=(Sq_*^a z)^2 and odd squares act by zero. Injective Frobenius transfers annihilation to the root.

The generators themselves need not be primitive. Polynomiality, connected grading and the absence of nilpotents supply the needed triangularity and injectivity.

## Role in the source argument

Allows the source to descend from decomposable spherical candidates to primitive square roots while retaining Steenrod annihilation.

## Antecedents and comparison

The argument uses classical polynomial derivations and Frobenius. It is extracted for its explicit treatment of nonprimitive polynomial generators and its separate uses of reducedness; no historical novelty claim is made.

## Checks and limitations

The coproduct Delta(y)=y tensor 1+1 tensor y+x tensor x gives a non-primitively generated polynomial Hopf example. Exact kernels on 166 decomposable monomials through degree 24 give four primitive basis vectors, all with primitive roots. A truncated algebra control shows why a nonprimitive root of the zero primitive can appear when Frobenius is not injective.

The finite coproduct test does not build the source infinite-loop-space homology or verify its spherical classification. The Steenrod conclusion is checked by its formal Cartan argument, not a full Steenrod-module computation here.

## Possible applications

Descent through powers in connected polynomial Hopf algebras without choosing primitive algebra generators.
