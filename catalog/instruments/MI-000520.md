# Preserve smallness of a particular error through a Neumann correction

[Family 352](../families/openai-math-352.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000291.md) · [Batch B053](../../batches/B053/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let P:X->Y and D_app:Y->X be bounded operators between Banach spaces with P D_app=I+R and ||R||<=r<1. Then D=D_app(I+R)^(-1) is a right inverse and ||D||<=||D_app||/(1-r). Neither injectivity of P nor smallness of ||D|| is required.

Suppose X embeds continuously into a weaker space X' and a distinguished error splits as E=f_c+f_e. The exact identity D f_e=D_app f_e-D R f_e gives

||D E||_(X') <= ||D||_(Y->X') ||f_c||_Y + ||D_app f_e||_(X') + ||D||_(Y->X') ||R f_e||_Y.

Thus the corrected output can be small even if the original error is not small in Y and the inverse norm remains bounded away from zero. It is enough to control the approximate output and the defect on this particular exterior source.

The source uses the inner output cutoff to split its compact error. The exterior part misses the inner input support exactly, so only the outer zero-data solution and outer commutator remain. Higher regularity makes its approximate output small in X', and short-time lower-derivative estimates make its defect small. This produces the three small terms beta^(2-2delta), eta^((alpha'-alpha)/4) and eta^((1-alpha)/4), conditional on the earlier geometric and parabolic estimates.

## Role in the source argument

Supplies the small corrected residual needed for the source nonlinear contraction despite the absence of a small full inverse norm.

## Antecedents and comparison

This is the classical Neumann-series correction of a parametrix, with the exact rearrangement D(I+R)=D_app applied to one source. A targeted literature search did not establish priority for the source cutoff split; the algebraic identity itself is not presented as new.

## Checks and limitations

Exact rational rectangular operators verify the right-inverse identity, the particular-error bound and finite Neumann remainder bounds. The models have a nontrivial kernel and an inverse norm that need not tend to zero.

No finite model verifies the source support geometry, fourth-order Schauder estimates or singularity construction.

## Possible applications

Gluing arguments where the residual is large in the forcing norm but its correction is small in the norm used for the nonlinear step.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
