# Extract a nearby unitary intertwiner from the off-diagonal block of an almost-flip

[Family 289](../families/openai-math-289.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000368.md) · [Batch B062](../../batches/B062/README.md)

## Mathematical content

For a unital von Neumann algebra A, the source proves dist(h,A prime)<=||ad_h restricted to A||_cb by trace-class duality. Vectorize a preannihilating functional as <(I tensor h)xi,eta>; projection onto the closure of (I tensor A prime)xi has matrix entries in A, fixes xi and annihilates eta. Commuting h past this projection and compressing to finite matrices gives the estimate. In particular d_cb(A,B)<=gamma gives d(A prime,B prime)<=4gamma after normalizing approximate unit-ball elements.

For normal unital representations rho,theta of P with e=||rho-theta||_cb<1/4, put pi=rho direct-sum theta and let S swap the two summands. The commutator of S with pi(P) has cb norm at most e. If e>0, choose D in pi(P) prime with ||D-S||<2e. Its lower-left block T satisfies T rho(a)=theta(a)T and ||T-I||<2e, so T is invertible. Its polar unitary u also intertwines, because T*T commutes with rho(P), and ||u-I||<=2||T-I||<4e. For e=0 take u=I. The cb hypothesis is essential to this route; ordinary generatorwise closeness is not substituted.

## Role in the source argument

Converts a commutant-distance estimate into a spatial identification with explicit constants.

## Antecedents and comparison

This is classical commutant-distance and polar-decomposition perturbation theory. The selected source proves its stated, nonsharp constants directly. No earliest-priority claim is made for the formulation.

## Checks and limitations

Finite amplified matrix representations with a nontrivial positive commutant factor check the intertwiner, polar unitary and distance estimate.

The script starts from an explicit invertible intertwiner. It does not compute a cb norm or certify the infinite-dimensional commutant-distance theorem or universal stability.

## Possible applications

Turning an invertible near-identity intertwiner into a unitary without losing the intertwining relation.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
