# Test mixed commuting squares before asserting a block projection is a functor

[Family 209](../families/openai-math-209.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000327.md) · [Batch B057](../../batches/B057/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Over a regular local ring B with nonzero regular parameter g, take the two-term complex X=[B^2 --diag(g,1)--> B^2]. An encoded matrix M=(a,b;c,d) represents chain matrices M_1=(a,b;gc,d) and M_0=(a,gb;c,d). Their composition law has upper-left entry a'a+g b'c. Taking only the upper-left block preserves upper-upper and lower-lower compositions, but this does not ensure naturality for mixed squares.

Use the four encoded maps

U=(1,1;0,1), U'=(1-g,1;0,1), L=(1,0;1,1), L'=(1,0;1,1-g).

All are chain isomorphisms, since 1-g is a unit. The horizontal maps U,U' are upper triangular and the vertical maps L,L' are lower triangular. Direct multiplication of the actual chain matrices gives L'U=U'L in both degrees. The rows X --U--> X -->0 and X --U'--> X -->0 are split exact, so this is an allowed diagram in the cited modified S-construction.

After taking upper-left blocks, the vertical arrows are both 1, while the horizontal arrows are 1 and 1-g. The projected composites differ by g, so the image is not a strictly commuting diagram. This disproves the stated functor assignment in the cited version. Even if the two maps are chain homotopic, that does not supply a morphism in a category requiring strict commutation. It does not by itself disprove the intended K-theoretic vanishing statement; a construction with additional coherent compatibility would be a different argument.

## Role in the source argument

Separates a concrete defect in a cited proof construction from the manuscript independent claim of a Gersten counterexample.

## Antecedents and comparison

[Mochizuki, Local Gersten's conjecture for regular system of parameters, version 8](https://arxiv.org/abs/1503.07966v8), Conventions 11 and Definition 12 on page 9 and Lemma 13(III) with its proof on pages 10-11, were checked directly. They contain the block composition law, the upper/lower restrictions and the asserted functors targeted by this example. A targeted search did not establish whether this exact objection had appeared earlier; no exhaustive novelty claim is made.

## Checks and limitations

Exact symbolic multiplication verifies both actual commuting squares, all chain-map conditions and unit determinants, and the nonzero projected difference g. The model B=Q[g] localized at (g) satisfies the required ring hypotheses.

The calculation concerns this specific version and strict assignment. It does not settle the intended vanishing theorem or the source new K-theoretic counterexample.

## Possible applications

Auditing proposed categorical operations that preserve separate classes of compositions but may fail on naturality squares mixing those classes.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
