# Two-point entropy gains survive future averaging when the pair is fixed by the past

[Family 148](../families/openai-math-148.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000129.md) · [Batch B014](../../batches/B014/README.md)

## Mathematical content

For bounded X define Hbar_s(X)=integral_0^1 H(floor(X/s+u))du, in bits. Equivalently Hbar_s(X)=h(X+V_s)-log2 s for independent uniform V_s on [0,s]. Write G_(s,t)=Hbar_s-Hbar_t and Delta_v=1-G_(v,2v). If J is an independent fair bit and 0<s<v<t, then

$$G_{s,t}(X+vJ)-G_{s,t}(X)\ge\Delta_v(X)-s/v-v/t.$$

At scale v the entropy increase is exactly Delta_v, since V_v+vJ is uniform on [0,2v]. Comparing independently shifted fine and v-grids loses at most s/v: a fine cell crosses a random coarse boundary with that probability and then has at most two labels. At scale t, condition additionally on X; the two possible shifts lie in different cells with probability v/t, so the increase is at most v/t.

Here is the conditional interface. Fix the past, hence a pair displacement v and a contraction R. Let tau be the random future law with E[tau|past]=D_R mu. If Delta_(v/|R|)(mu)>=delta, convexity of Delta gives E[Delta_v(tau)|past]>=delta. Individual future laws need not satisfy that lower bound.

For an integer M>4 use s=r/M,t=Mr and v in [r,4r). The endpoint loss is at most 5/M. If a past-measurable prefix law has a fair-pair decomposition of total weight c, with all its pair distances in this interval and each satisfying the averaged deficit bound, concavity and convolution monotonicity of G_(s,t) give expected entropy gain at least c(delta-5/M). It is at least c delta/2 when 5/M<=delta/2. These last properties use the integer ratio t/s=M^2. Conditioning on the future before fixing the deficit would not justify the step.

## Role in the source argument

Converts finite pair mass into an expected window gain while retaining the tail averaging that supplies nonsaturation.

## Antecedents and comparison

[Varju, Lemma 22 and Proposition 20](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1602.00261v4) establish the matching-scale identity and an earlier surrounding-window gain. The source gives the displayed endpoint losses and uses conditional Jensen averaging. These are compared as interfaces, not claimed to originate all two-point entropy methods.

## Checks and limitations

Read the smoothing and information proofs and conditional Jensen step. Piecewise exact integration over rational shift breakpoints checks the identities and gains; logarithms are evaluated numerically. Mixtures of future laws check the averaging direction.

The future average and selected displacement must be compatible with the same past. An unconditional average cannot be substituted after an additional selection depending on the future. The needed deficit of the averaged tail is an input here.

## Possible applications

Entropy growth in random environments when useful structure is chosen before the remaining randomness is revealed.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
