# Use fixed block masses to remove large predictable cost offsets from entropy regret

[Family 121](../families/openai-math-121.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000213.md) · [Batch B035](../../batches/B035/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let Q be a compact convex set of probability vectors in R^d, containing a strictly positive vector and having a fixed total mass in each coordinate block. Let f_0>=...>=f_S be coordinatewise monotone vectors in [0,1]^d. Set Delta_t=f_{t-1}-f_t, ghat_t=-f_{t-1}, and u_t=ghat_t+(1-2kappa)Delta_t, where 0<kappa<1/2. Suppose each within-block range of Delta_t is at most kappa/eta.

Let rho_t maximize H(rho)+eta<sum_{j<t}u_j+ghat_t,rho> over Q. Then for every comparator rho* in Q and every prefix s,

$$\sum_{t\le s}\langle f_t+\kappa\Delta_t,\rho_t\rangle\le\sum_{t\le s}\langle f_t,\rho^*\rangle+\frac{\log d}{\eta}+2\kappa.$$

Subtract the minimum decrement in each block. Its contribution is identical at every feasible vector, so only the remaining small range needs the exponential estimate exp((1-2kappa)x)<=1+(1-kappa)x for 0<=x<=kappa. Entropy optimality supplies the KL penalty on Q itself; summing its potential increments and using sum Delta_t<=1 proves the bound. L1 error at most zeta in each chosen distribution adds at most s zeta.

## Role in the source argument

Controls refinement regret despite large cost changes that are nearly constant within each child block.

## Antecedents and comparison

[Rakhlin and Sridharan 2013, Section 2](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v30/Rakhlin13.pdf) supplies optimistic FTRL and exponential-weight antecedents. The source-specific lemma exploits invariant block masses to discard block offsets; no exhaustive priority claim is made.

## Checks and limitations

1,600 prefix regret checks use exact blockwise softmax maximizers; 910 steps violate the stronger global range bound while satisfying the within-block condition.

Tests use products of block simplexes. Arbitrary constrained action hulls and the numerical source optimizer are not computationally certified.

## Possible applications

Online or iterative approximation where each component has fixed probability mass and costs move mostly by componentwise offsets.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
