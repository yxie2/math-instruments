# Control binary entropy-production curvature through two moments

[Family 119](../families/openai-math-119.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000273.md) · [Batch B049](../../batches/B049/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Put L=log 2, psi(r)=[(1+r)log(1+r)+(1-r)log(1-r)]/2, and F(psi(r))=r atanh(r), for 0<=r<1. If h=L-psi(r) is binary entropy in nats, then 1/2<=h^2 F''(L-h)<=4L^2/3 for 0<h<=L.

For v=atanh r, differentiation gives F''(psi(r))=cosh(v)^2 A(v), where A(v)=2 integral_0^1 (1-s^2)cosh(2vs) ds. This positive representation gives F''(0)=4/3 and increasing curvature. To control its divergence, put t=r^2 and x=atanh(r)/r, continuously x=1 at zero. Define a probability measure on [0,1] with density 1/[x(1-tu^2)], and set b=E[1/(1+u)], n=E[u^2]. Direct integral identities yield h^2 F''(L-h)=b^2(1+n).

Jensen and Cauchy-Schwarz give b>=1/(1+sqrt(n)), hence the lower bound 1/2. For the upper bound, covariance differentiation shows b decreases from L toward 1/2 and n increases. Reweight the measure by 1-u^2. Then beta=(b-1/2)/(1-n) equals half the new expectation of (1+u)^(-2), so beta decreases and is at most beta_0=3(L-1/2)/2. Consequently b^2(1+n)<=b^2[2-(b-1/2)/beta_0]. The latter polynomial is increasing for b in [1/2,L], since 3L>2, and its value at L is 4L^2/3.

The two endpoint constants are attained at h=L and as h tends to zero, respectively. F itself has no finite value at information L. These formulas concern binary entropy, not the companion square-root Hellinger profile.

## Checks and limitations

High-precision evaluations test the derivative, positive-integral and two-moment identities, both bounds and the monotone reweighting ratio, including points very close to both endpoints.

These numerical checks are not an interval certificate and do not prove the global cube inequality. The general scalar justification is the displayed analytic argument.
