# Cancel lift dependence in a Pfaffian deletion cocycle

[Family 207](../families/openai-math-207.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000326.md) · [Batch B057](../../batches/B057/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Fix an infinite-order element g of a group G, J=C_G(g), H=J/<g>, and Y=<g>\G. The H-action on Y is free. Choosing representatives of ordered-pair orbits and antisymmetrizing gives a J-invariant skew function beta on pairs of lifts such that beta(g^r h,g^s h')=beta(h,h')+s-r. It need not be integer-valued: for G=<t> and g=t^2, invariance forces beta(1,t)=1/2.

For a list of 2m lifts let p_m be the Pfaffian of its beta matrix. On 2m+1 projected vertices define psi_m by the alternating sum of p_m over all one-vertex deletions. If u=sum_i epsilon_i and b=sum_(i<j) beta(h_i,h_j) epsilon_i wedge epsilon_j, this sum is the top coefficient of u wedge b^m/m!. Changing lifts replaces b by b+u wedge v. Every correction term has two factors u and vanishes. Thus psi_m descends, is alternating, vanishes on repeated projected vertices and is H-invariant. A second alternating deletion cancels every twice-omitted list, proving the cocycle identity.

For the source finite complex path weights v_k, idempotence gives deletion consistency and trace cyclicity gives invariance under the twisted rotation (h_0,...,h_(2m-1)) to (h_1,...,h_(2m-1),g h_0). Ordinary cyclic rotation negates the Pfaffian; the twist adds one to its last column. Last-column expansion and deletion consistency give q_m=-q_m+q_(m-1), where q_m=v_(2m)(p_m) and q_0 is the class trace lambda. Hence q_m=2^(-m)lambda, and the same is the value of psi_m on the associated even exterior cycle. This is a detector construction; obtaining vanishing of its evaluation requires a separate theorem.

## Role in the source argument

Pairs a class trace with a quotient-space cocycle while avoiding any requirement that the raw skew function descend.

## Antecedents and comparison

The underlying mechanisms are classical exterior-algebra cancellation, Pfaffian expansion and the identity that two alternating deletions compose to zero. The selected source detector and the prerequisite finite path identities were read in full. No earliest-priority claim is made for this trace-detector assembly.

## Checks and limitations

32 exact skew-matrix models, with Pfaffians up to size 8, verify lift-shift invariance, alternating-deletion closure, the twisted-rotation identity and vanishing for repeated vertices.

The program checks the algebraic identities, not the source sparse-chain theorem or its infinite-order trace-vanishing conclusion.

## Possible applications

Building higher cocycles from lift-dependent pair data when the change of lifts is an additive vertex potential.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
