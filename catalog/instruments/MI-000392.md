# Propagate an affine shadow from a clean four-by-four square

[Family 155](../families/openai-math-155.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000215.md) · [Batch B036](../../batches/B036/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let p>200 be prime and N=p^2. Suppose a nonzero F_p-valued array on {0,...,N-1} times Z obeys the source word rule on every nonvertical integer line. Each such word agrees, away from at most p indices, with an affine function modulo p that is not identically zero.

Among (N-3)(K-3) four-by-four squares in an N-by-K rectangle, at most 16p(3K+2N) meet a bad cell on a row or a diagonal of slope 1 or -1. For sufficiently large K a clean square remains. Affineness of its rows and both diagonal directions forces its sixteen values to equal An+Bm+C. One can verify this by second differences or by repeated two-point determination.

Call a cell good when this form vanishes or its value agrees with the array. A line containing four consecutive good cells is entirely good: excluding the at most one zero of each of two affine forms still leaves two distinct common nonzero points. The clean square first fills four rows and then, using diagonals with four good cells, every adjacent row. Thus the affine form describes the array wherever it is nonzero. This is the affine-shadow stage, not the full aperiodicity argument.

## Role in the source argument

Extracts a global algebraic shadow from line constraints with sparse exceptional sets.

## Antecedents and comparison

[Greenfeld and Tao, Theorem 4.7 and Lemma 4.8](https://arxiv.org/html/2309.09504v2) already give the clean-square and extension mechanism. This is an explicit antecedent, not an inferred new theorem.

## Checks and limitations

The row/diagonal constraint matrix has nullity three over five prime fields. Two primes greater than 200 satisfy the explicit clean-square count.

Finite checks do not construct an infinite strip, audit higher p-adic structure or prove absence of all periods.

## Possible applications

Recovering affine structure from locally affine restrictions with controlled exceptions.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
