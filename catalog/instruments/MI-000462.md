# Expose returning sweep activity by shifting the rank

[Family 183](../families/openai-math-183.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000250.md) · [Batch B044](../../batches/B044/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Sweep a generic planar configuration by increasing slope s, marking the k smallest values y_i-s x_i. The activity of a time interval is the number of distinct points whose membership changes. For activity at least b>=4, put c=floor(b/4). Classify the interval by whether at least b/2 active points were initially unmarked.

In that class, compare the endpoint sets at rank k+c. At least ceil(b/2)-c>=c initially unmarked active points lie outside the initial rank-(k+c) set E. Each enters rank k at some time and therefore moves below at least c+1 members of E. Because pairwise projection differences are affine, these reversed comparisons persist to the final time. Either at least c such points enter the final E prime, or one remains outside and forces c+1 original members outside. Thus endpoint distance is at least c.

For the other class apply the argument to complementary marks, giving rank k-c. Feasibility follows from the number of initially available active points. For b<4, positive activity already forces distinct endpoints since the sum of marked first coordinates strictly increases at every switch.

## Role in the source argument

Replaces membership changes that can cancel at endpoints by visible displacement at one of two nearby ranks.
