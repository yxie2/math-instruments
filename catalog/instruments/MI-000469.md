# Force contact transitivity from a symmetric two-point jet

[Family 062](../families/openai-math-062.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000253.md) · [Batch B046](../../batches/B046/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let X be a connected compact complex contact manifold. Suppose a symmetric B in H^0(X times X,L box L) vanishes to second order on the diagonal and has second symbol theta^2. Then H^0(X,L) -> J_x^1 L is surjective for every x, global contact vector fields span every tangent space, and the identity component of the contact automorphism group acts transitively.

In a frame write s=y-x and alpha for theta. Symmetry forces b(x,x+s)=alpha_x(s)^2+alpha_x(s)(partial_s alpha)_x(s)+O(s^4). Differentiating in the first slot with y fixed gives, through degree two,

$$D_v^{(1)}b=-2\alpha(s)\alpha(v)+\alpha(s)d\alpha(v,s)-\alpha(v)(\partial_s\alpha)(s)+O(s^3).$$

Put V=H^0(X,L). Evaluations span V*, so B decomposes in V tensor V. Its nonzero slices B_x define beta:X -> P(V), with beta*O(1)=L. If d beta(v)=0, then D_v^(1)b=k b. The linear term forces alpha(v)=0; the quadratic term gives d alpha(v,.)=k alpha. Contact nondegeneracy then gives v=0. Thus beta is an immersion.

The slice and its tangent derivatives are linearly independent, so dualizing yields all ordinary first jets of actual global sections. Contact Hamiltonians span tangent spaces; their local flows give open orbits. Connectedness gives transitivity. No nondegeneracy of B as an element of V tensor V is required.

## Role in the source argument

Converts a global two-point identity into infinitesimal and then global homogeneity.

## Checks and limitations

Forty affine-chart points of the standard symplectic projective model have full first-jet rank; an exact symbolic calculation checks the fixed-second derivative formula.

The source construction of B is outside this audit. Local model checks do not certify the contact Fano or quaternionic classification.
