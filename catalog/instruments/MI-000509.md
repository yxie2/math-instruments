# Choose scales where a summable cost loses to a divergent test budget

[Family 152](../families/openai-math-152.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000285.md) · [Batch B051](../../batches/B051/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let p>0, let J_i be pairwise disjoint finite nonempty sets of integer scales, and let R_i>0. Suppose sum_k B_k^p<infinity while sum_i |J_i| R_i^p=infinity. Then there are i tending to infinity and k_i in J_i such that B_(k_i)/R_i tends to zero. Otherwise some positive lower bound c holds for all sufficiently late tests, forcing sum_k B_k^p>=c^p sum_i |J_i|R_i^p=infinity.

There is also a finite quantitative form: over any finite collection of windows, min_(i,k in J_i)(B_k/R_i)^p is at most (sum_(k in union J_i) B_k^p)/(sum_i |J_i|R_i^p). Disjointness prevents counting the same cost infinitely often.

The source takes p=16, b_i=log_2 m_i, b_(i+1)=b_i+K_i, and J_i={b_i+j:ceil(K_i/16)<=j<=floor(K_i/8)}. Eventually |J_i|>=K_i/32, and the separately proved word-rate bound gives sum K_i R_i^16=infinity for R_i=ell_i/m_i. Selecting k=log_2(m_i D) gives B_k n=(B_k/R_i)D ell_i=o((D-1)ell_i), since D>=2. Thus partition costs bounded by C B_k 2^k become negligible relative to the available label information. The conclusion is a subsequence, not a uniform bound at all scales.

## Role in the source argument

Matches the source symbolic information scales to a fixed smooth model summable partition-cost sequence.

## Antecedents and comparison

The general statement is an elementary weighted pigeonhole argument. The complete source cheap-scale proof and word-rate divergence proof were read. No new general summability theorem or exhaustive priority claim is made.

## Checks and limitations

Exact finite weighted windows verify the quantitative ratio bound. Explicit disjoint dyadic windows with a summable cost illustrate the decaying selected ratios; a repeated-window example fails selection if disjointness is removed.

The divergent word-rate budget and summable smooth-model budget are separate hypotheses; these tests do not establish either full construction.

## Possible applications

Selecting observation scales after an adversarial but summable error sequence has been fixed.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
