# Retaining one ensemble transfers matrix entropy production to free Fisher information

[Family 298](../families/openai-math-298.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000146.md) · [Batch B018](../../batches/B018/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Fix an operator-norm cutoff R with chi_R(X)>-infinity. Choose increasing dimensions d_k and uniform laws A^(d_k) on positive-volume microstate sets with moment accuracy (k,1/k) and normalized entropy at least chi_R(X)-1/k. This selection precedes noise times and polynomial tests. Put Z^(d)(s)=A^(d)+sqrt(s)G^(d), where the independent GUE coordinates have real Hilbert-Schmidt covariance 1/d.

With H_d=d^(-2)h+(n/2)log d and matrix score F_i=-(1/d)grad_i log f_s,

$$H_d'(s)=\tfrac12 I_d(s),\qquad I_d(s)=d^{-3}\int\|\nabla\log f_s\|^2f_s=\sum_i\mathbb E\operatorname{tr}_d(F_i^2).$$

Positive-time smoothing justifies integration by parts. The identity sum_alpha E_alpha D E_alpha=Tr(D)I for a real orthonormal Hermitian basis yields E tr_d(F_i P)=E(tr_d tensor tr_d)(partial_i P), with the original prefix/suffix order. Completion of squares bounds I_d below by every self-adjoint polynomial variational test.

Assume the required polynomial trace products converge to those of Y_s and that its conjugate variables exist. Taking fixed-test limits, then the supremum, gives liminf_d I_d(s)>=Phi*(Y_s). A countable dense polynomial test family supplies measurability when moments vary continuously. Entropy concavity under translation mixtures gives H_d(Z(epsilon))>=H_d(A); integrate from epsilon to t, then decrease epsilon to zero. Fatou gives

$$\liminf_d H_d(Z^{(d)}(t))\ge\chi_R(X)+\frac12\int_0^t\Phi^*(Y_s)\,ds.$$

Every term uses the same dimension subsequence and terminal law. No continuity of entropy at zero or replacement by a terminal entropy maximizer is needed.

## Role in the source argument

Allows a later upper bound specific to the evolved structured ensemble to be compared with free entropy production.

## Antecedents and comparison

The matrix/free Fisher comparison is established in [Jekel-Pi, Proposition 6.1](https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/48620); their Theorem 6.4 integrates it in an entropy comparison. This card records the source-specific insistence on keeping the original ensemble available for an additional structural estimate.

## Checks and limitations

Read the variational and flow arguments. Finite Hermitian bases check normalization and noncommutative derivative order; exact Gaussian calculations and a numerical bounded-input convolution check de Bruijn with the same law throughout.

Finite checks do not prove asymptotic freeness, moment uniform integrability or existence of conjugate variables. Those are inputs here. A new terminal ensemble could lose the structure used in the next card.

## Possible applications

Combining a known entropy-production inequality with a deficit that only holds for a specially retained random matrix model.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
