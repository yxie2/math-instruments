# Separate positive and null Cartan data by weighted squares

[Family 254](../families/openai-math-254.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000359.md) · [Batch B061](../../batches/B061/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Write C=2I-B, with B symmetric and entrywise nonnegative, diagonal zero. If a principal block C_T is positive definite, Perron-Frobenius gives spectral radius(B_T)<2 and

C_T^(-1)=(1/2)sum_(j>=0)(B_T/2)^j.

It is entrywise nonnegative and strictly positive within each connected block. Prescribed exterior coordinates have a unique harmonic extension p_T=C_T^(-1)B_(T,out)p_out; nonnegative data therefore extend nonnegatively.

More generally, on a connected block G, suppose v is strictly positive and Cv>=0 coordinatewise. For every real x,

x^T C x=sum_(i<j) b_ij v_i v_j(x_i/v_i-x_j/v_j)^2 + sum_i ((Cv)_i/v_i)x_i^2.

If at least one entry of Cv is positive, zero energy forces all ratios x_i/v_i equal along the connected graph and then forces that common ratio to vanish. Thus C is positive definite. Otherwise Cv=0. In the source, positive definite principal blocks correspond to spherical Coxeter subsets; this last identification is a separate classical Coxeter input.

## Role in the source argument

Controls harmonic extension and identifies when a nonnegative Cartan inequality must be an exact null relation.

## Antecedents and comparison

The inverse-positivity argument is classical nonnegative-matrix theory, and the quadratic identity is the weighted ground-state transform. The source gives a self-contained proof; no new general linear-algebra theorem is claimed.

## Checks and limitations

One hundred twenty connected weighted-matrix cases check the exact quadratic identity numerically, positive inverse and harmonic extension, and Perron-vector null controls.

These checks do not verify the categorical construction, height filtration or Salvetti contractibility.

## Possible applications

Maximum principles and positive/null alternatives for symmetric matrices with nonpositive off-diagonal entries.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
