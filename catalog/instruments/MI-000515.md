# Use dual-basis averaging to construct a central radical annihilator

[Family 202](../families/openai-math-202.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000288.md) · [Batch B052](../../batches/B052/README.md)

## Mathematical content

For a finite-dimensional symmetric algebra A over a field, let tau(ab) be its nondegenerate associative symmetric pairing and (v_i),(v_i*) dual bases. The canonical tensor sum v_i tensor v_i* makes H(a)=sum v_i a v_i* independent of the chosen bases and central. The identity tau(H(a)b)=Tr(L_b R_a) implies H(a) rad(A)=0: if b is in the radical, L_b is nilpotent and commutes with R_a, so the trace vanishes; replacing b by bc for arbitrary c and using nondegeneracy gives the assertion.

For A=kX with tau the identity coefficient, H(a)=sum_(x in X) x a x^(-1). The double contraction d=sum_(x,y)[x,y]=sum_i H(v_i)v_i* is also central and annihilates the radical. On a semisimple block M_s(k), where the pairing is t Tr with t nonzero, matrix units yield H(a)=t^(-1)Tr(a)I and d=t^(-2)I. These formulas still hold when the characteristic divides s; division by s is never used.

On a nonsemisimple block, a central element with nonzero residue scalar is a unit, so it cannot annihilate the nonzero radical. Thus d has zero residue scalar there and a nonzero scalar on a split semisimple block. The source further connects these blocks to ordinary defect-zero characters through its characteristic-zero commutator scalar |X|^2/chi(1)^2; that connection also needs its separate cocenter and modular block inputs.

## Antecedents and comparison

[Lorenz-Tokoly, Projective modules over Frobenius algebras and Hopf comodule algebras](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1008.4056), Section 2.4, Lemma 2 and Section 2.4.2, give the Higman trace, its trace identity and radical-annihilating image; these passages and proofs were read. The selected source spells out the group commutator and matrix-block contractions.

## Checks and limitations

Exact group-algebra calculations verify the averaging trace identity, conjugacy-class support and radical annihilation in explicit small examples. Matrix-unit sums check both contractions in characteristics dividing the matrix size.

Finite examples do not certify the full ordinary/modular character comparison or the source final local-global theorem.

## Possible applications

Detecting semisimple block contributions through central elements without dividing by representation dimensions.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
