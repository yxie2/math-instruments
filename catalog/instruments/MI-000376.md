# Avoid small collective images by a Grassmann incidence dimension bound

[Family 294](../families/openai-math-294.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000207.md) · [Batch B034](../../batches/B034/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let E,F be smooth Hermitian complex bundles of ranks d,r over a compact smooth manifold of real dimension 2h. For a finite collection of pairs (s,t), with 1<=s<=d and 0<=t<=r, assume

$$(qs-t)(r-t)-s(d-s)>h.$$

Then q smooth bundle maps T_i:E->F can be chosen so that dim sum_i T_i(y)S>t for every base point y and every s-plane S in E_y, simultaneously for the selected pairs.

Choose finitely many smooth sections whose evaluations span each Hom(E_y,F_y), using local frames and a partition of unity. If their q-tuples form a complex parameter space of dimension M, the incidence of (y,S,U,T) with T_i S contained in a t-plane U has real dimension

$$2M+2h+2s(d-s)+2t(r-t)-2qs(r-t)<2M.$$

Restriction followed by quotient is surjective, so this incidence is a smooth kernel bundle. Its projection to the parameter space is null by the lower-dimensional Sard argument. A finite union of such bad sets cannot exhaust the parameter space.

## Antecedents and comparison

This is classical incidence avoidance and Sard transversality. The explicit inequality is the useful extracted budget.

## Checks and limitations

Six hundred exact dimension and coordinate-constraint models verify the count and equivalent inequality.

Finite models do not verify uniform transversality over all subspaces or over a nontrivial bundle; the general selected proof was read.

## Possible applications

Constructing several bundle maps that avoid simultaneous low-rank images without choosing pointwise generic maps inconsistently.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
