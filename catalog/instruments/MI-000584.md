# Couple all probability rows through one rejection stream

[Family 184](../families/openai-math-184.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000353.md) · [Batch B060](../../batches/B060/README.md)

## Mathematical content

For probability distributions p_i on a common finite nonempty set J, there is a joint law of Y_i with the prescribed marginals and

Pr(Y_i != Y_j)<=2d/(1+d), where d=TV(p_i,p_j).

Use one shared sequence of independent trials (Z,U), with Z uniform on J and U uniform on [0,1]. Row i accepts its first trial with U<=p_i(Z). Its acceptance probability per trial is 1/|J|, and its accepted label has law p_i. Every row stops almost surely. For two rows, the first trial accepted by either is accepted by both with probability sum min(p_i,p_j)/sum max(p_i,p_j)=(1-d)/(1+d). This implies the disagreement bound; later acceptance can also produce equal labels. Although the trial sequence is infinite, the resulting label vector lies in finite J^I and hence has finite support.

The source groups neighbors by these simultaneous labels. It does not assert that every pair attains the optimal disagreement d, which is generally impossible for three or more rows.

## Role in the source argument

Preserves agreement across related local transition rows when splitting graph vertices.

## Antecedents and comparison

[Angel and Spinka](https://arxiv.org/abs/1903.00632), Section 2.1 Proposition 5 and its proof on pages 4-5, give this construction in greater generality, and identify its finite form with Kleinberg-Tardos rounding. Those pages and their three-variable obstruction on page 1 were read. This is an explicitly classical extraction.

## Checks and limitations

Sixteen exact rational joint laws are computed by eliminating the no-acceptance self-loop recursively. All marginals and 48 pairwise bounds pass. The three overlapping two-point laws give disagreement 2/3 while pairwise TV is 1/2.

The program verifies the coupling, not the later triangle-mass or independence-number estimates.

## Possible applications

Coordinating many local samples while controlling disagreement for every pair simultaneously.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
