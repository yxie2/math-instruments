# Dependent matched updates preserve caps and charge variance through finite orbits

[Family 022](../families/openai-math-022.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000139.md) · [Batch B017](../../batches/B017/README.md)

## Mathematical content

In a cell, let plus and minus raw loads P,M each be at most N0. Fractionally match mass min(P,M). A matched plus portion is retained when its indicator S succeeds, and its matched minus portion is retained otherwise. Unmatched plus mass is kept on success; unmatched minus mass is multiplied by 1-1/p. Assume every S has orbit mean 1/p under a finite translation group, with the old weights and matching rule transported equivariantly. Colors may be completely dependent.

Each matched portion contributes its full raw mass between the two sides. Only the larger side has unmatched mass, which cannot increase, so the new load is at most max(P,M)<=N0. In the source, the before-mask probabilities are pi_I(a)=(1-1/p)pi_(I')(a) and pi_I(pa)=(1-1/p)^[nu>=2] pi_(I')(a)/p; the latter residual factor is absorbed in the plus load. These identities give preservation of each transported label's weighted first mass.

For old accounting classes A with fixed widths r_A, let m_A be old mass and Y_A(g) transported post-mass. Equivariance gives E_g Y_A(g)=m_A and makes group translation permute the complete class family. Thus the actual total quadratic increment is

$$J=\sum_A r_A(\mathbb E_gY_A(g)^2-m_A^2)=\sum_A r_A\operatorname{Var}_gY_A(g)\ge0.$$

Coarsening equal-width classes adds nonnegative cross products. With class mass capped by N0, total weighted mass inserted at most W, and deletion retaining inherited equivalences, deleting mass d costs at most 2N0 d in square energy. The final energy is at most N0 W; telescoping bounds all variance and coarsening increments by O(N0 W) (3N0 W under this bookkeeping). Splitting a class after removing a connector would introduce an uncharged loss and is disallowed.

At a new cell, old-child contributions x_D<=N0 satisfy sum x_D^2<=N0 x0. If x0>N0, clipping loss x0-N0 is at most (x0^2-sum x_D^2)/N0. This count uses one mask-probability factor, not its square.

## Role in the source argument

Controls broad-pair energy without pretending the arithmetic success indicators are independent.

## Antecedents and comparison

Fractional coupling, variance identities and telescoping capped energy are classical. The source-specific content is their translation-equivariant combination with inherited classes and mask probabilities. No new general martingale theorem is claimed.

## Checks and limitations

Read the update and energy proof. Exact correlated color models check cap preservation and marginal masses; cyclic orbit models check the actual summed variance identity, clipping and the cost of deletion. A split-class control shows the missing loss if inherited equivalences are broken.

Marginal mean preservation alone does not imply a nonnegative actual summed increment; the complete orbit transport is needed. The existence and geometry of the source partitions are separate inputs.

## Possible applications

Accounting for dependent arithmetic rounding when a conserved first mass and a capped second moment must be maintained together.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
