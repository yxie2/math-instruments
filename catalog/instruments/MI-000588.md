# Encode a group action in a terminating monoid normal form

[Family 250](../families/openai-math-250.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000357.md) · [Batch B061](../../batches/B061/README.md)

## Mathematical content

For a group V acting on Z, use group labels g and symbols s_z,t_z, together with an absorbing zero. Besides group multiplication and zero absorption, impose

t_y s_z=1 if y=z and zero otherwise; g s_z=s_(gz); t_z g=t_(g^(-1)z).

Every nonzero element has a unique normal form s_(z_1)...s_(z_a) g t_(w_1)...t_(w_b), omitting the identity group label. In particular V embeds as units even when its action on Z is not faithful.

Orient each relation to shorten words, multiply adjacent group labels and erase identity labels. Termination follows from length. The overlaps of group products join by associativity and the action law. The remaining overlap t_y g s_z reduces consistently because y=gz if and only if g^(-1)y=z. Zero and identity overlaps join immediately. Local confluence and termination give confluence, and the irreducibles are precisely the displayed forms. This is an algebraic presentation with all group and action labels; it is not by itself a finite presentation or a polynomial-time normalizer for arbitrary input data.

## Role in the source argument

Provides a faithful action encoding before the source finite compiler is applied.

## Antecedents and comparison

This is an elementary application of the classical terminating-rewriting criterion. The source proof was read in full. No external earliest-priority claim is made for its action-monoid presentation.

## Checks and limitations

All short words and 300 longer random words for the S3 action on three points are reduced along every possible rewrite path. Unique terminal forms and six distinct group labels are verified.

A finite action test does not establish the later self-referential compiler, finite ring presentation or higher finiteness.

## Possible applications

Embedding a group and a permutation action in a prefix/deletion algebra with an explicit normal form.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
