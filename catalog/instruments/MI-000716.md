# Keep loop attempts when matching discrete and continuous clocks

[Family 211](../families/openai-math-211.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000483.md) · [Batch B076](../../batches/B076/README.md)

## Mathematical content

For a connected multigraph with n edges, choose a uniformly random incident half-edge at each step, counting both half-edges of loops. With stationary mass mu(x)=deg(x)/(2n), let P be the transition matrix and G=n(I-P). Then <u,Gv>_mu=(1/2) sum_e (u(e+)-u(e-))(v(e+)-v(e-)). Parallel edges each contribute; loops contribute zero energy but affect degrees and attempted transitions. Connectivity makes the kernel of G the constants.

Attach independent exponential waiting times of mean 1/n to every attempted step, including loops. For S_k their partial sums and N(t)=max{k:S_k<=t}, Y_t=X_(N(t)) has generator n(P-I), and Y_(S_k)=X_k. Doob's inequality gives E max_(k<=floor(nT))|S_k-k/n|^2<=4 floor(nT)/n^2.

If the cable edge length is a_n, interpolation satisfies d(Xbar_(nt),Y_t)<=a_n+omega_(T+1)(Y,Delta+1/n) on Delta+1/n<=1. Thus vanishing a_n and a continuous limiting path modulus transfer convergence from the Poissonized walk to cable interpolation. Counting only vertex changes would give the wrong clock when loops are present.

## Role in the source argument

Preserves both the factor of two in the energy convention and the correct attempt rate during de-Poissonization.

## Antecedents and comparison

Reversible-chain Dirichlet forms and Poissonization are classical. The source makes loop attempts, cable motion and the unhalved companion operator 2G explicit; no new martingale inequality is claimed.

## Checks and limitations

Multigraph models check the generator and energy by independent matrix and edge computations, the Poisson mixture against a matrix exponential, and a loop-deletion negative control. Simulated exponential clocks illustrate, but do not prove, the maximal bound.

The common-space path convergence and limiting modulus are assumptions, not consequences of the finite checks.

## Possible applications

Avoiding time-normalization errors when passing from random graphs with loops to continuous diffusions.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
