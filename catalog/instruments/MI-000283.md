# Two flags reconstruct through a once-crossing gallery with compatible graded maps

[Family 027](../families/openai-math-027.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000160.md) · [Batch B022](../../batches/B022/README.md)

## Mathematical content

For two flags F,H in V, let m_ij be the successive double differences of dim(F_i intersect H_j). A common splitting V=direct sum V_ij realizes the two flags as row and column prefixes. Refine in row-first order, sort to column-first order by adjacent interchanges, and merge. Every inverted pair crosses once.

Conversely a gallery of such split/interchange/merge operations with each pair crossing at most once admits one common splitting respecting the specified quotient identifications. At a crossing, express the new second block as a graph into the first and adjust its lift. Before that crossing the first block always precedes the second; the adjustment therefore preserves all earlier prefixes and graded identifications. Induction handles the whole gallery. Over a ring, the same construction requires the indicated graded quotients to be free, so the short exact sequences split.

Suppose maps on the F-graded pieces and H-graded pieces preserve their induced refinements and agree on the double-graded modules. In common splittings, a simultaneous extension has matrix blocks V_ij -> V'_i'j' only when i'<=i and j'<=j. Equal-row blocks are prescribed by the F maps; equal-column blocks by the H maps; the prescriptions agree on their overlap. Blocks strictly lowering both coordinates are arbitrary. Invertible diagonal blocks make the whole extension invertible.

Every sorting order is a linear extension of this coordinatewise order, so the same matrix respects every intermediate flag. The free lower-both blocks parameterize an affine space of extensions. In constant-rank families this description is local, after splitting the subbundles; no global canonical splitting is asserted.

## Antecedents and comparison

Simultaneous two-flag splittings and triangular extension matrices are standard linear algebra. The source explicitly relates its flag operations to Mellit and Su stratifications and isolates reconstruction with free integral graded modules. Those broader geometric antecedents were not independently compared theorem by theorem here.

## Checks and limitations

Read the entire gallery and block-extension proof. Exact integer matrices check 100 split six-block models against every coordinate-order extension; varying the lower-both blocks leaves both families of graded maps unchanged.

The check uses already split rank-one blocks. It does not certify arbitrary family splittings, the seam equations or the full density argument. Agreement on double-graded overlaps and the once-crossing condition are essential hypotheses.
