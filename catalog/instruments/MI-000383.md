# Preserve a lower spectral-rank signal under normalized block averaging

[Family 301](../families/openai-math-301.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000210.md) · [Batch B034](../../batches/B034/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let lambda_alpha be positive rational weights summing to one. Form the diagonal block average H_av of completely positive contractive candidates H_alpha in a separate normalized matrix/UHF direction; retain unnormalized traces on internal matrix directions. Orthogonality gives, for positive s, exact weighted averages of tau(H_alpha(s)), tau(H_alpha(s)^2), and d_tau((H_alpha(s)-eta)_+). An ordinary arithmetic average need not have these square or rank identities.

Suppose y>=0, the v_alpha are contractions, 0<=g(t)<=1_{(eta,infinity)}(t), and

$$\left\|T-y^2\right\|<\epsilon^2/2,\qquad T=\sum_\alpha\lambda_\alpha yv_\alpha^*g(H_\alpha(s))v_\alpha y.$$

Compress by p=1_{(epsilon,infinity)}(y). Then pTp>=(epsilon^2/2)p. Positivity and traciality yield

$$d_\tau((y-\epsilon)_+)\le\frac{2\|y\|^2}{\epsilon^2}\,d_\tau((H_{av}(s)-\eta)_+).$$

The constant and eta are independent of candidate count, rational multiplicities and internal matrix size. For extended weights, an infinite right side is immediate; otherwise finite row comparison puts the relevant cuts in the normal trace's finite part, where compression and monotone convergence apply. Stable folding preserves these trace conventions.

## Role in the source argument

Retains a nonzero lower rank witness while simultaneously averaging approximation errors.

## Antecedents and comparison

Orthogonal block functional calculus and spectral projection compression are classical. The extraction records the normalization convention and candidate-independent constant.

## Checks and limitations

Sixty rank-deficient block models, with 180 noncommuting matrix pairs, verify trace, square, rank and lower-bound formulas. A scalar negative control shows ordinary arithmetic averaging does not preserve the square formula.

Finite tests do not construct the stable folding or cover all extended weights; the selected proofs supply those steps.

## Possible applications

Convex approximation of operator maps with a spectral witness that must survive the averaging step.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
