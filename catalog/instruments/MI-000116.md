# Four-effect coherence control for two-sided PPT images

[Family 272](../families/openai-math-272.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000082.md) · [Batch B002](../../batches/B002/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let $\omega$ be a density matrix on $\mathbb C^p\otimes\mathbb C^q$ of the form

$$\omega=(\mathcal L\otimes\mathcal M)(\chi\chi^*),$$

where $\chi\in\mathbb C^r\otimes\mathbb C^s$, and $\mathcal L$, $\mathcal L\circ T_r$, $\mathcal M$, and $\mathcal M\circ T_s$ are completely positive. Neither $\chi$ nor the maps must be normalized; $T_r,T_s$ denote matrix transposition in fixed bases. For arbitrary positive local matrices $X_0,X_1,Y_0,Y_1$, set $D_{ij}=X_i\otimes Y_j$ and $p_{ij}=\operatorname{Tr}(D_{ij}\omega)$. The extracted inequality is

$$\|\sqrt{D_{00}}\,\omega\sqrt{D_{11}}\|_1
\le\sqrt{p_{00}p_{01}}+\sqrt{p_{10}p_{11}}+2\sqrt{p_{01}p_{10}}.$$

The local matrices need not sum to the identity. To obtain the estimate, pull them back through the adjoint maps, normalize the two Alice-side effects on the support of their sum, and split the input at the spectral comparison of the resulting commuting effects. Complete positivity gives a direct block estimate; complete positivity after transposition of **both** input factors gives a reversed estimate. Applying the two estimates to the four terms of the split vector proves the displayed bound.

## Role in the source argument

At each complete public transcript, the coherence norm becomes the root fidelity between two conditional states held by Eve. The absence of pointwise POVM normalization is essential for integrating transcript densities. Tensor closure permits joint local processing of arbitrarily many input copies.

## Antecedents and comparison

Private-state methods already connect off-diagonal trace norms with conditional-state fidelity: see [Horodecki et al., Secure key from bound entanglement](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0309110v2). The [2018 study of key-undistillable states](https://arxiv.org/abs/1612.08938v4) is a close problem-level antecedent. The specific four-effect estimate is the candidate extracted device; the broader relation between entanglement and secrecy is established background. This is a targeted comparison, not an exhaustive novelty determination.

## Checks and limitations

Checked the support normalization, the need for a global input transpose in the reversed bound, and the four-term triangle inequality against the source proof.

The represented class is a hypothesis and must not be replaced by all PPT states. The operational 1/5 unhalved trace-norm gap additionally uses the source protocol and transcript theorems; it is not a consequence of positivity alone. The corresponding conventional trace distance is 1/10.

## Possible applications

Potential use: an obstruction to coherent equal-label branches in a two-sided channel construction. Any extension to a different protocol model requires a compatible transcript representation.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
