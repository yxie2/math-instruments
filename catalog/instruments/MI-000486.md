# Transport a weighted graph norm through an intertwining bijection

[Family 324](../families/openai-math-324.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000265.md) · [Batch B048](../../batches/B048/README.md)

## Mathematical content

For a bounded linear Q:A->H, define Z_Q as sequences (s_i) with sum_i 2^(-i)||s_i||<infinity and Qs_i->0, with norm max{sum_i 2^(-i)||s_i||, sup_i||Qs_i||}. Let B:A_1->A_2 and h:H_1->H_2 be zero-preserving bi-Lipschitz bijections satisfying Q_2 B=h Q_1. Applying B coordinatewise maps Z_(Q_1) bijectively to Z_(Q_2): its Lipschitz bound controls the weighted sum, and h controls the norm-null output. The inverse identity Q_1 B^(-1)=h^(-1)Q_2 proves the reverse inclusion. The global Lipschitz constants are at most max{Lip B,Lip h} and max{Lip B^(-1),Lip h^(-1)}.

The source realizes this with E=F(U direct-sum_2 V), q the linearization of h-pi_U, Q_1(e,v)=(qe,v), and B(e,v)=e+delta(qe,v). The identity q delta(u,v)=h(u,v)-u gives qB=hQ_1. For z in E, put x=h^(-1)(qz); then B^(-1)(z)=(z-delta(x),pi_V x). This uses only h^(-1), not surjectivity of q.

If a||x-x'||<=||h(x)-h(x')||<=b||x-x'||, L_q=||q|| and K_q=max{L_q,1}, the source obtains Lip B<=1+K_q and Lip B^(-1)<=1+2L_q/a using the sum norm on E direct-sum V. The coordinatewise graph bounds are therefore max{1+K_q,b} and max{1+2L_q/a,1/a}. These are estimates for all pairs, not only distances from zero.

## Role in the source argument

Separates the elementary nonlinear graph-space equivalence from the source difficult construction of h and its linear obstruction.

## Antecedents and comparison

[Godefroy-Kalton, Lipschitz-free Banach spaces](https://kaltonmemorial.missouri.edu/assets/docs/sm2003c.pdf), Theorem 2.12, exhibits a related triangular splitting; the statement and inverse-type formula were read. Their Corollary 3.2 requires an actual Lipschitz right inverse of a quotient map. The displayed nonlinear diagrams do not provide one.

## Checks and limitations

Finite sequences under a nonlinear piecewise-linear intertwining map test both weighted-sum and supremum norms and the global pairwise constants. A non-null output sequence is a control: a finite weighted sum alone does not put it in Z_Q.

The program does not construct the source Hilbert-space h, prove complete continuity of q, or exclude linear copies of c0(V). The general transport formulation isolates the step present in the selected proof.

## Possible applications

Moving nonlinear equivalences to sequence spaces whose norm couples a summable input with a uniformly controlled, vanishing output.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
