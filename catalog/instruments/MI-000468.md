# Recover a contact vector field from a first jet in a frame-independent way

[Family 062](../families/openai-math-062.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000253.md) · [Batch B046](../../batches/B046/README.md)

## Mathematical content

On a complex contact manifold theta:T_X -> L with F=ker theta, every holomorphic section a of L determines a unique contact vector field K_a. In a frame theta=e alpha and a=e f, its equations are alpha(K_a)=f and df(w)+d alpha(K_a,w)=0 for w in F.

Choose any local R with alpha(R)=1 and write K_a=fR+Z with Z in F. Nondegeneracy of d alpha on F determines Z uniquely. Cartan's formula shows that the second equation is exactly preservation of F. Under alpha prime=rho alpha and f prime=rho f, the extra terms cancel because alpha(K_a)=f, so the solutions glue and their values depend only on the ordinary first jet of a.

This is a differential splitting on sections, not an O_X-linear splitting of the contact tangent sequence. The first derivative is essential.

## Role in the source argument

Turns globally generated contact-line jets into spanning contact vector fields.

## Antecedents and comparison

[LeBrun, Proposition 2.1 and Remark 2.1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/dg-ga/9409001), pages 6-7 of the preprint, give the identification and distinguish it from a vector-bundle splitting. The statement and local proof were checked.

## Checks and limitations

Thirty exact Darboux polynomial Hamiltonians satisfy contact value and Lie-derivative preservation identities.

Local tests do not establish global generation of contact-line jets.

## Possible applications

Translating section-jet information into infinitesimal contact automorphisms.
