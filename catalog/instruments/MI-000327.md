# Approximate separated couplings by graph plans with exact cell masses

[Family 373](../families/openai-math-373.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000182.md) · [Batch B027](../../batches/B027/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let mu be an absolutely continuous compactly supported probability measure on finitely many components separated by eta>0. Suppose a three-fold coupling pi is supported on triples from distinct components. Partition each component into cells A_i of diameter at most delta, with masses m_i, and put q_ijk=pi(A_i times A_j times A_k).

Split each source cell A_i into B_ijk of mass q_ijk. From every positive-mass piece, choose two Borel maps sending its normalized density to the normalized restrictions of mu on A_j and A_k. Gluing them gives T_2,T_3 preserving mu, since all three marginals of the array q equal m. The graph plan has exactly the same product-cell masses as pi.

All occupied blocks retain pairwise separation eta. The three-body Coulomb cost varies by at most 6 delta/eta^2 within any block, so

$$\left|\int c\,d(\mathrm{id},T_2,T_3)_\#\mu-\int c\,d\pi\right|\le6\delta/\eta^2.$$

The source constructs the required Borel transports using conditional distribution functions and conditional quantiles. Exceptional zero-density prefixes are assigned fixed distribution functions; generalized inverses are Borel through rational sublevel tests. Flat intervals have zero conditional probability. This supplies actual measurable maps without assuming densities are everywhere positive.

Applying the construction to a separated optimal coupling proves equality of infima and finite epsilon approximants. It gives no deterministic optimizer and imposes no cyclic relation between T_2 and T_3.

## Role in the source argument

Shows that the source nonattainment claim, if its support certificate holds, is compatible with arbitrarily accurate deterministic approximations.

## Antecedents and comparison

[Colombo-Di Marino](https://cvgmt.sns.it/media/doc/paper/2127/MultiColDim.pdf) already prove equality for Coulomb cost, including cyclic maps; their Lemma 2.3 is the cell-cost oscillation estimate. The present extraction records the source finite separated-cell construction and explicit Borel transport details, not a new equality-of-infima theorem.

## Checks and limitations

Forty exact rational cell arrays verify source splitting and both pushed marginals; 2,400 separated Coulomb samples verify the stated oscillation bound.

The numerical models use intervals to check block algebra. They do not construct the source three-dimensional density, certify the Rosenblatt argument mechanically or establish the claimed optimal support.

## Possible applications

Constructing deterministic approximate transports while preserving marginals and controlling a cost singularity through support separation.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
