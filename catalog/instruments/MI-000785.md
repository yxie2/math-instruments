# Square a commuting perturbation to locate all fixed points

[Family 347](../families/openai-math-347.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000519.md) · [Batch B085](../../batches/B085/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let g be a Hamiltonian involution of a closed symplectic manifold and h_j Morse functions on its fixed components F_j. Extend them smoothly and average over g to obtain an invariant f. Its Hamiltonian flow psi commutes with g and restricts on F_j to the Hamiltonian flow of h_j.

A smooth vector field on a compact manifold has a positive lower bound tau for periods of nonconstant orbits. In a common flow chart, if m is the maximum speed on a period-T trajectory and L its Lipschitz constant, averaging the velocity around the closed trajectory gives m<=LTm; sufficiently small T forces m=0.

For 2epsilon<tau, (psi^epsilon g)^2=psi^(2epsilon). Every fixed point is therefore an equilibrium of f and then a fixed point of g, so the entire fixed set is the union of Crit(h_j). At such a point the derivative splits into + and - eigenspaces of dg. On the tangent space, (exp(epsilon A_+)-I)/epsilon tends to the invertible Hamiltonian Hessian A_+. On the normal space, -exp(epsilon A_-)-I tends to -2I. Thus all fixed points are nondegenerate for one sufficiently small epsilon. Smooth time reparameterizations in successive intervals give a periodic Hamiltonian generating the composition.

## Role in the source argument

Replacing a fixed submanifold by prescribed isolated critical points while proving there are no extra fixed points elsewhere.

## Antecedents and comparison

The source explicitly relates its short-period estimate to Yorke; the required bound is only positive and need not have the optimal constant. Commuting finite-order perturbations and the tangent/normal linearization are classical mechanisms.

## Checks and limitations

Block Hamiltonian matrix examples check the square identity, tangent and normal nondegeneracy and a resonant-time negative control. The tests do not enumerate fixed points on the constructed compact manifolds.

Commutation is essential; smallness must exclude periodic orbits and tangent resonances. This does not construct the source involution or establish the headline deficit.
