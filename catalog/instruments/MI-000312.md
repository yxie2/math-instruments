# Bound restriction images uniformly using only a fixed part of the generic fiber

[Family 038](../families/openai-math-038.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000175.md) · [Batch B026](../../batches/B026/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Fix k such that kL embeds X. For an integral d-dimensional subvariety W, let I_W(h) be the dimension of the image of H^0(X,hL) in H^0(W,hL). Its degree in the embedding is D=k^d(L^d.W)>=D_0=k^d.

A general linear projection W->P^d is finite and generically separable. Select only D_0 distinct points of its geometric generic fiber, regardless of how large D is. Products of at most D_0-1 affine linear separators interpolate arbitrary values on those selected points. After homogenization, ambient forms of degree D_0 have rank at least D_0 over the base function field. Choose D_0 independent forms from a fixed complex monomial spanning set.

Multiplying each by all base monomials of degree q-D_0 gives independent restricted sections: a relation would be a function-field relation between the chosen forms. Thus

$$I_W(qk)\ge k^d\binom{q-k^d+d}{d},\qquad (d!I_W(qk))^{1/d}\ge qk-k^{d+1}.$$

For each residue r modulo k, fix h_r congruent to r with h_rL globally generated. Some section is nonzero on any given W; multiplication by it is injective on restriction images because W is integral. For all sufficiently large h,

$$(d!I_W(h))^{1/d}\ge h-C_d,\qquad C_d=\max_r h_r+k^{d+1}.$$

The same constant can serve as a threshold. It depends on X,L,k,d, not on W or its degree. No surjectivity of restriction is needed.

## Role in the source argument

Supplies uniform restricted-section growth while the source minimizing center varies.

## Antecedents and comparison

The proof combines classical generic finite projection, point interpolation and Hilbert-function counting. The extracted feature is choosing a fixed minimum fiber sample to make the constants independent of W; historical novelty of this estimate was not established.

## Checks and limitations

432 exact Hilbert-function comparisons for monic projective hypersurfaces and 240 residue-class bounds pass.

These models test the counting bound, not the generic projection and separability argument for every subvariety or the source minimization theorem.

## Possible applications

Uniform estimates over families of subvarieties without controlling their individual degrees or assuming restriction surjectivity.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
