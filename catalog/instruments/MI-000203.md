# A local ultrafilter rank interface excludes independent covers on a double ray

[Family 185](../families/openai-math-185.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000122.md) · [Batch B012](../../batches/B012/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Assume a matroid Q on L disjoint union R, both labeled by the same set D, an ultrafilter U on D, and an ordinal-valued rank rho on U antitone under inclusion. Require every basis B of Q to satisfy: if B_L belongs to U, then D minus B_R belongs to U and rho(B_L)>rho(D minus B_R). These are substantive input assumptions.

Place bundles e_n, each a copy of D, on edges of the double ray with vertices indexed by integers. At vertex n put a copy Q_n on e_(n-1) union e_n, oriented outward from the central bundle e_(-1): for n<=-1 its L side is e_n, while for n>=0 its L side is e_(n-1). The even vertices' supports partition the ground set, as do the odd vertices' supports. Form their two direct-sum matroids M0,M1.

Suppose independent sets of M0,M1 cover the ground set. Shrink one to make a partition. Exactly one endpoint's allocation of the central bundle lies in U. Follow that side outward. If x_k in U is the allocation received at the next vertex and z_k its outward allocation, then x_(k+1)=D minus z_k. Extend this vertex's independent allocation to a basis B. With u=B_L and y=D minus B_R, one has x_k subset u and y subset x_(k+1). Upward closure of U and the basis interface yield

$$\rho(x_k)\ge\rho(u)>\rho(y)\ge\rho(x_{k+1}).$$

This is an infinite strictly descending sequence of ordinals, a contradiction. Hence the direct sums have no independent cover. If Q is also self-dual, so are the sums, and the preceding reduction rules out a mixed packing/covering partition. The local rank interface suffices for the covering obstruction; self-duality is used for the mixed-partition consequence.

## Role in the source argument

Turns a local basis constraint into a global counterexample after the source constructs the required Q, ultrafilter and rank.

## Antecedents and comparison

Direct sums, duality and well-founded ordinal descent are standard. [Bowler and Geschke](https://www.math.uni-hamburg.de/home/geschke/papers/UniformMatroid7.pdf) provide earlier self-dual infinite-matroid background with additional hypotheses. The source-specific item isolated here is the outward orientation and inclusion-compatible strict rank propagation, conditional on the local interface.

## Checks and limitations

Read the full assembly argument and the endpoint orientation. Finite bookkeeping checks verify bundle incidence and the inclusion/antitonicity chain in finite rank models. They do not construct the required infinite local matroid or ultrafilter.

The preceding construction of Q and its ordinal rank is not verified by this card or its script. Weak rank decrease would not contradict an infinite path. No finite-length experiment proves the infinite obstruction.

## Possible applications

Assembling local feasibility rules into global impossibility when every feasible step must decrease a well-founded resource.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
