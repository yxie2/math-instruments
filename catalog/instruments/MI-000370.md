# Recover the central frame determinant from a Green-weighted Ricci integral

[Family 051](../families/openai-math-051.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000204.md) · [Batch B033](../../batches/B033/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let f be a finite-area holomorphic disc and B a holomorphic frame of f*TX, with N=B* h(f) B uniformly positive, continuous up to the circle, W^{1,p} for p>2, and N=I on the circle. In the source normalization R_h=-i partial barpartial log det h,

$$\partial_z\partial_{\bar z}\log\det N=-R_h(f',f').$$

The holomorphic nonvanishing determinant of B contributes no curvature. Green's identity and the zero boundary values of log det N give

$$\log\det N(0)=\frac2\pi\int_{\mathbb D}\log(1/|z|)R_h(f',f')\,dS=P_{R_h}(f).$$

The limit uses the stated integrability and continuity, not a smooth extension across the boundary. The factor 2/pi corresponds to partial_z partial_barz=Delta/4.

## Role in the source argument

Relates the size of the central variation frame to a curvature functional of the entire disc.

## Antecedents and comparison

This is the standard curvature formula for a determinant line combined with the planar Green identity. It is not a new curvature formula.

## Checks and limitations

Twenty-four exact radial polynomial log-determinants verify the curvature sign, Green weight and area normalization.

The tests assume a frame; they do not establish the source boundary-unitary frame construction or global canonical positivity.

## Possible applications

Converting boundary normalizations of holomorphic frames into central determinant bounds.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
