# Sorting periodic windows preserves a closed-walk edge multiset

[Family 128](../families/openai-math-128.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000113.md) · [Batch B010](../../batches/B010/README.md)

## Mathematical content

A nontrivial closed walk in the substring graph has equally many up and down edges, say P>0. Repeating the walk yields a bi-infinite letter text A of period P: up moves append a letter at the right endpoint of a window, and down moves delete its left letter. Every visited word is its actual window in A. An occurrence is recorded when some window contains it, and occurrence counts are taken modulo the walk's turn P, even if A has a smaller least period.

For a family of walks, the number of recorded s occurrences is sum_{r ending in s} u(r), equal to m(s) for the base multiplicities. Group walks whose periodic texts agree up to translation, and align a representative text of least period p. A walk of turn P_j=d_j p contributes d_j passages at each residue of the window's left endpoint.

At each integer x collect the exit endpoints Z_j(x+rp)-rp, 0<=r<d_j, and sort this multiset to obtain z_1(x)<=...<=z_D(x). Incoming endpoints are the previous exit multiset. Pairing within the original walks has entry<=exit, so sorted incoming endpoints also satisfy z_i(x-1)<=z_i(x). Moreover z_i(x-1)>=x and z_i(x+p)=z_i(x)+p. Layer i consists of the vertical up moves from window [x,z_i(x-1)) to [x,z_i(x)), followed by the down move to [x+1,z_i(x)).

At each x and right endpoint e, the number of up edges crossing e equals the number of entries at most e minus the number of exits at most e; it depends only on the two endpoint multisets, not their pairing. Down edges are determined by exits. Sorting therefore preserves every edge with multiplicity, while each new layer has up cost p per turn and the layers are pointwise ordered. Empty windows after a down move are allowed.

## Role in the source argument

Replaces intertwined periodic walks by ordered layers so later connection operations can be charged to explicit repeated letters.

## Antecedents and comparison

The underlying graph and up/down costs come from the [collapsing framework](https://arxiv.org/abs/1809.08669v3). Monotone order-statistic matching is classical. The extracted source-specific interface preserves actual substring edges and distinguishes a walk turn from the least text period.

## Checks and limitations

Read the cyclic window and edge-multiplicity proof. Finite periodic window systems compare edge multisets before and after sorting, including multiple turns over one primitive period and empty windows.

A layer must represent actual windows of the same aligned periodic text. Sorting arbitrary unrelated strings would not preserve graph edges. This decomposition does not itself supply the subsequent connection bound.

## Possible applications

Normal forms for periodic multigraph walks and bookkeeping for algorithms that rearrange cyclic string assemblies.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
