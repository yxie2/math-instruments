# Regularize a determinant potential while preserving every subset rank

[Family 174](../families/openai-math-174.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000347.md) · [Batch B060](../../batches/B060/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let B be the indexed union of q edge-disjoint spanning trees on n>=2 vertices, and rho>0. There are vectors z_e in R^(n-1) with sum z_e z_e^T=I, squared norms at most (1+rho)/q, and exactly the same rank for every edge subset as its incidence vectors.

Write b_e for incidence vectors in an orthonormal coordinate system on 1-perp. Set N(a)=sum exp(a_e)b_e b_e^T and h(a)=log det N(a)-(1/q)sum a_e. The q disjoint tree terms in Cauchy-Binet, followed by AM-GM, bound h below by a finite L. Minimize h(a)+gamma||a||^2. At its minimizer, gamma||a||^2<=h(0)-L and ||grad h||<=2 sqrt(gamma(h(0)-L)). Choosing gamma sufficiently small makes each coordinate of the gradient at most rho/q. The derivative formula gives exp(a_e)b_e^T N(a)^(-1)b_e<= (1+rho)/q. Thus z_e=exp(a_e/2)N(a)^(-1/2)b_e has the claimed properties. Positive individual scalings and a common invertible map preserve every subset rank. This is an existence argument, not a bit-complexity estimate.

If A is a subset of B and sum_(e in A) z_e z_e^T >= cI, consider a partition into d nonempty parts. The span of within-part incidence vectors has dimension at most n-d. Projecting onto its orthogonal complement and taking traces gives c(d-1)<=((1+rho)/q)|A crossing the partition|. The Nash-Williams-Tutte criterion then supplies floor(cq/(1+rho)) edge-disjoint spanning trees in A.

## Role in the source argument

Turns an isotropic spectral lower bound into an integral tree-packing guarantee without losing the graphic rank structure.

## Antecedents and comparison

[Barthe, On a reverse form of the Brascamp-Lieb inequality](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/9705210), Section 2.2, Proposition 3, uses the same determinant quotient, Cauchy-Binet and weighted AM-GM lower bound. Pages 10-11, including the sufficiency argument, were read. The source adds an explicit regularization and projected-trace interface; no earliest-priority claim is made.

## Checks and limitations

Eight small indexed tree unions verify isotropy, the norm bound, all subset ranks and partition lower bounds for selected subfamilies. Numerical residuals and counts are recorded in results.json.

Numerical minimizers do not certify arbitrary-size existence or polynomial running time. The selected proof establishes existence analytically; the full thin-tree argument is not checked.

## Possible applications

Passing from matrix lower bounds to combinatorial packing when rank preservation matters.
