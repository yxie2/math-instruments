# Amplify a finite tensor saving by weighting sectors by dimension

[Family 130](../families/openai-math-130.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000282.md) · [Batch B050](../../batches/B050/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Fix an invertible nonmonomial q by q matrix A and b>=2. Assume a specified word on exactly Q=q^b coordinates implements A tensor-power b using g<b q^(b-1) calls to A plus finitely many monomial factors. A call acts as A direct-sum I_(Q-q), up to permutation. This finite winning word is a hypothesis.

For j tensor groups, a call slot splits exactly as the direct sum of A tensor-power r with multiplicity binomial(j,r)(Q-q)^(j-r), for r=0,...,j. Weight sectors by their dimensions q^r, not by the number of sectors. If L_A(k) is scalar circuit cost and f(k)=L_A(k)/q^k, a call slot costs E f(K) per coordinate, with K binomial(j,q/Q). Each monomial slot costs at most one scalar multiplication per coordinate and is charged.

Synchronizing the fixed word across j=floor(k/b) groups, then handling fewer than b remaining axes, gives f(k)<=C_0+g E f(K). Put rho=q/(Qb); the finite saving says g rho<1. Choose alpha in (0,1) with g rho^alpha<1. Concavity gives E(K+1)^alpha<=(rho k+1)^alpha. A strong induction, absorbing C_0 and finitely many small k, proves f(k)=O((k+1)^alpha), hence L_A(k)=O(q^k(k+1)^alpha).

The expectation is bookkeeping for an exact finite direct sum, not random approximation. This implication does not construct the winning word, prove that one exists, transfer the result to every Fourier length, or control coefficient magnitudes and numerical conditioning.

## Role in the source argument

Propagates the companion source proposed finite call saving to arbitrarily large Kronecker powers.

## Antecedents and comparison

[Alman-Li, Kronecker Powers, Orthogonal Vectors, and the Asymptotic Spectrum](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.14489), studies rebalancing and duality for depth-two linear circuits; the abstract was read as context only. That is a different size model from sequential invertible calls. The precise conditional binomial recurrence here was read in the source; no exhaustive novelty claim for it.

## Checks and limitations

Exact small tensor matrices verify sector multiplicities and coordinate dimensions. Finite recurrences with explicitly hypothetical saving parameters test the concavity induction; a no-saving parameter choice fails its contraction condition.

No winning word is furnished by these recurrence tests, and no headline sub-n-log-n Fourier theorem is certified.

## Possible applications

Converting a proven fixed-size saving into an asymptotic improvement when exact block decomposition produces a contracting dimension-weighted recurrence.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
