# Detect a primitive coefficient tensor by its positive sewn norm

[Family 065](../families/openai-math-065.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000440.md) · [Batch B071](../../batches/B071/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let B0 be the source detection subspace and J its primitive middle-degree orthogonal complement. For an error tensor E in (J_C*) tensor r with other inputs fixed in B0, parity forces E=0 if nr is odd. Otherwise E is even. Hodge-Riemann gives the positive Hermitian form h(v,w)=(-1)^(n(n-1)/2) Q(Cv,conjugate(w)). Its dual tensor norm is the ordinary sum of squared absolute coefficients in an h-orthonormal basis.

The source realizes its inverse bilinear kernel as K=(-1)^(n(n-1)/2)(C tensor id)Delta_J using the primitive coevaluation convention. Sew E to E# through K in each slot. Moving from block order to paired order contributes (-1)^(r(r-1)/2) for odd J; this fixed sign must be included. The resulting scalar is the positive norm, not a supertrace.

Reality of ordinary Gromov-Witten classes realizes E# by a second ordinary replica with conjugated coefficients and exchanged first and second Hodge degrees. On J, q=n-p, so the required grading factors are admissible. Only the first replica carries the positive constraint; the second is a fixed scalar test. Curve and genus coefficients are extracted independently. If all admissible tests vanish, the norm vanishes and E=0. Multilinearity then detects the full coefficient rule for all numbers of primitive slots.

## Role in the source argument

Converts vanishing after scalar transport into vanishing of every primitive tensor component.

## Antecedents and comparison

Positive Hermitian tensor duality, Hodge-Riemann polarization and reality of cycle operations are established ingredients. The source-specific point is realizing the conjugate tensor with the permitted tests, including parity and coefficient extraction. Its priority is not established.

## Checks and limitations

Even and odd polarized two-dimensional models check the coevaluation convention, inverse kernel, tensor norm and interleaving sign; omission of the odd sign is a negative control.

These checks do not establish the geometric sewing theorem or admissibility on a new target. Positivity is needed on the final target, not on every intermediate target.

## Possible applications

Recovering a tensor identity from a sufficiently rich family of scalar identities.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
