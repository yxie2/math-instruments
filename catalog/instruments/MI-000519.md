# Optimize a boundary penalty in the dual energy norm

[Family 358](../families/openai-math-358.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000290.md) · [Batch B052](../../batches/B052/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Suppose endpoint data X and nonnegative energy E satisfy |q^T X|^2<=S(q,q)E for every covector q, with S positive definite. For mu>0, a distinguished covector e and any real alpha, split p^T X=(p-alpha e)^T X+alpha e^T X. Cauchy-Schwarz gives

|p^T X|^2 <= [S(p-alpha e,p-alpha e)+alpha^2/mu] [E+mu(e^T X)^2].

Minimizing the first factor at alpha=S(p,e)/(S(e,e)+mu^(-1)) gives S(p,p)-S(p,e)^2/[S(e,e)+mu^(-1)]. This is the dual-energy improvement supplied by one measured boundary component. It is also the rank-one inverse identity p^T(S^(-1)+mu ee^T)^(-1)p when S is represented by a matrix.

In the source, E is the sum of a weighted L2 energy for xi and the Dirichlet energy integral[w_dot^2-F''w^2/2] with w vanishing at both ends and F''<=0. The endpoint functional has Gram form S(q,r)=integral q^T P r + <Phi_q,Phi_r>_(D*). Riesz representation supplies the initial dual bound. Its index form is bounded below by E+mu(e^T X)^2-(p^T X)^2/L, so proving that the optimized factor is at most L suffices for nonnegativity. That last uniform estimate is additional geometric work, not part of the algebraic identity.

## Role in the source argument

Allows a rank-one boundary contribution to compensate a potentially negative tangential term in the source returning-geodesic index form.

## Antecedents and comparison

The optimization is a classical completion of squares, equivalently the Sherman-Morrison rank-one inverse identity. The complete source duality proof was read; no novelty claim is made for the identity. Its use with this geometric energy is the source interface.

## Checks and limitations

Exact rational Hilbert-space models verify the optimized inequality, the rank-one inverse formula and equality at a maximizing vector. They also show that no improvement occurs when S(p,e)=0.

The check does not establish the source uniform bound on S, turning-time margins or absence of conjugate points.

## Possible applications

Improving an energy estimate when a boundary condition controls only one linear component of the endpoint data.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
