# Filter Koszul positions by exponent plus exterior support

[Family 200](../families/openai-math-200.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000317.md) · [Batch B056](../../batches/B056/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let A=F[y_1,...,y_r], let V be a finite-dimensional F-vector space in degree zero, and let W be a graded A-submodule of E=V tensor_F A. Order monomial exponents within each total degree by an addition-compatible order, using the least nonzero position. The possible leading coefficients at alpha form a subspace V_alpha of V. Multiplication by y_i gives V_alpha contained in V_(alpha+e_i), so in(W)=direct-sum_alpha V_alpha y^alpha is a graded submodule.

The position filtration of E/W has associated pieces V/V_alpha. Hence E/W and E/in(W) have the same Hilbert function. To retain every graded Betti number, fix internal degree j in the Koszul complex and put a term y^alpha e_sigma at filtration position alpha+1_sigma. Its degree is j=|alpha|+|sigma|. Multiplying by y_i while deleting i from sigma preserves that position. The associated graded complex is therefore the degree-j Koszul complex of E/in(W).

For a map of finite filtered vector spaces, rank cannot increase after passing to the associated graded: the associated graded of the kernel injects into the kernel of the graded map. Apply this to the two adjacent differentials. Chain dimensions stay fixed, while the ranks can only fall, so beta_(p,j)(E/W)<=beta_(p,j)(E/in(W)). No ordering of a basis of V is part of the construction; each leading coefficient space is retained as a space.

## Role in the source argument

Preserves graded Betti control while the source later changes from ordinary scalar variables to a division-coefficient model.

## Antecedents and comparison

[Mermin-Murai, The Lex-Plus-Powers Conjecture holds for pure powers](https://arxiv.org/abs/0801.0391), Section 2, Definition 2.7 and the following discussion on PDF page 4, records preservation of Hilbert function and increase of graded Betti numbers under taking initial ideals. That passage was read. The selected source supplies a direct filtered-Koszul argument with vector-valued monomial coefficients; no new general semicontinuity principle is claimed.

## Checks and limitations

Four explicit rank-two graded modules over F_5[x,y] are computed degree by degree. Quotient multiplication matrices and their Koszul homology give 84 Betti comparisons, including two strict increases, with equal Hilbert functions.

These checks do not construct the source division algebra or prove the final Lex-Plus-Powers comparison for arbitrary regular sequences.

## Possible applications

Degenerating a module whose coefficient spaces must remain intact while tracking every homological and internal degree.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
