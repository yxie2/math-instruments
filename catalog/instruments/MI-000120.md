# Fourth-moment Gibbs regularization supplies seventh-moment control

[Family 273](../families/openai-math-273.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000083.md) · [Batch B002](../../batches/B002/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let $N$ be the total number operator on a finite positive number of bosonic modes and $W=I+N$. For $k>0$ and bounded $B\ge0$, the form

$$K=kW^4+W^{1/2}BW^{1/2},\qquad\mathcal D(K^{1/2})=\mathcal D(W^2),$$

defines a positive self-adjoint operator with compact inverse. Its normalized Gibbs state $\omega=e^{-K}/\operatorname{Tr}e^{-K}$ is faithful, satisfies $\operatorname{Tr}\omega W^7<\infty$, and uniquely minimizes $\operatorname{Tr}\sigma K-S(\sigma)$ over states with finite fourth moment.

The proof writes $K^{-1}=W^{-2}(kI+W^{-3/2}BW^{-3/2})^{-1}W^{-2}$. For an eigenvector $\psi_j$ with eigenvalue $u_j$ of $K$, the weak eigenvalue equation yields

$$kW^{7/2}\psi_j=u_jW^{-1/2}\psi_j-BW^{1/2}\psi_j.$$

The right side is square summable. Polynomial eigenvalue counting for a finite-mode number operator, followed by the factor $e^{-u_j}$, gives the seventh moment.

## Role in the source argument

Justifies logarithmic and unbounded-generator pairings after a fourth-moment penalty is added to the entropy optimization. The source also adds a thermal port, output thermal replacement and relative-entropy penalty; only the fourth-moment coefficient is sent to zero in the final compactness step.

## Antecedents and comparison

Closed positive forms, compact resolvents, Gibbs minimization and thermal entropy methods are established ingredients. [Beigi--Rahimi-Keshari](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.09572v2) gives a prior one-mode variational route to Gaussian optimizers. The extracted feature here is the particular W^4/W^7 regularity package and its role in the multimode argument. No independent novelty claim is made for this auxiliary lemma.

## Checks and limitations

Checked the inverse factorization, the domain used in the eigenvalue equation, and the exponent 7 arising from the W^(7/2) norm.

Bounds may depend on k and B and need not be uniform as the regularization vanishes. A finite-mode number operator and bounded positive B are hypotheses. The lemma alone does not establish attainment or stationarity of the full two-input entropy functional.

Potential use: regularize a bosonic variational problem so that subsequent traces against low-degree creation and annihilation expressions are defined.
