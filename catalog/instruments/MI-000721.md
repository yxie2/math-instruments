# Transfer relay reliability through a joint connection inequality

[Family 213](../families/openai-math-213.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000486.md) · [Batch B077](../../batches/B077/README.md)

## Mathematical content

For independent percolation on a finite undirected hypergraph and a nonempty node set A,
P(o connected to A and o connected to T)>=P(o connected to A) min_(a in A)P(a connected to T).

For probabilities below one, the cluster-column cone gives h_k=e_k+sum_(j>k)Gamma_(jk)h_j with Gamma>=0. Thus H^(-1)=I-Gamma. First-listed-node probabilities give Hd=1, so row n of Gamma sums to 1-d_n. List A first and o last. For F(U)=1_(U meets A and T), the vector y_i=E F(C(x_i)) obeys (I-Gamma)y>=0. The last row has y_n at least the weighted sum of relay probabilities y_i=P(x_i connected to T), with weights totaling P(o connected to A). If o is in A the result is immediate. Finite-event continuity extends the bound to probabilities zero or one.

Consequently, if every relay reaches T with probability at least 1-b, then P(o reaches A but not T)<=b P(o reaches A)<=b for bonds. For site percolation replace each vertex by a starred node and each edge by a connector; the open hyperedge at x joins x* to its incident connectors. Distinct starred vertices reproduce open site paths. Hypergraph self-connection is automatic, but site self-connection requires the site open. On the site failure event the origin is open, so its connections agree and event inclusion gives the bound <=b. Deterministically fixed bits are allowed.

## Role in the source argument

Controls the chance of reaching a relay set yet failing to reach a reliably accessible target.

## Antecedents and comparison

The proof uses the preceding source cone lemma, whose resampling ingredient has a classical antecedent. The stronger joint inequality is not identified here with ordinary Harris association, and its exhaustive novelty is not established. The source cites a separate site-incidence discussion; that discussion was not independently audited.

## Checks and limitations

Exact hypergraph enumeration checks the joint inequality over all targets and relay sets in small models. Site configurations verify the incidence map, the self-connection discrepancy and the failure bound with deterministic bits.

Finite connection comparison alone does not prove absence of an infinite cluster at criticality.

## Possible applications

Transferring local relay estimates through explorations while preserving the distinction between bond and site conventions.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
