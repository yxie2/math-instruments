# Resolve null-conic polarizations with an explicit nonsplit quotient sequence

[Family 372](../families/openai-math-372.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000184.md) · [Batch B028](../../batches/B028/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Parametrize the projective null conic in C3 by theta(z,w)=(z^2-w^2,i(z^2+w^2),2zw). Its bilinear orthogonal plane H_theta={p:theta.p=0} contains the null line N_theta=C theta. The degree-one columns

$$h_1=(z,iz,w),\qquad h_2=(-w,iw,z)$$

satisfy theta.h_j=0, h_1 cross h_2=i theta and theta=z h_1+w h_2. Thus H is O(-1) direct-sum O(-1), N is O(-2), and

$$0\longrightarrow O(-2)\xrightarrow{(z,w)^T}O(-1)^2\xrightarrow{(-w,z)}O\longrightarrow0.$$

The formulas are homogeneous of the required degrees, so they descend to both projective charts, including their endpoints. For p=c h_1+d h_2, the induced bilinear form is p.p=(-wc+zd)^2.

There are no nonzero global holomorphic sections of H: its ambient components would be constant on the compact conic, and a constant vector orthogonal to every null vector is zero. Hence the quotient sequence does not split holomorphically. Nevertheless H itself has nonscalar holomorphic endomorphisms, since H=O(-1)^2. Any argument eliminating those endomorphisms needs additional transport information.

## Antecedents and comparison

The sequence is a classical projective-line exact sequence written in coordinates suited to the complex null cone. No new general bundle theorem is claimed.

## Checks and limitations

Exact polynomial identities verify the null vector, frame, cross product and quotient norm. Forty-eight projective coordinate samples verify the ranks, including both chart endpoints.

These calculations do not prove the source holomorphic transport matching or its restriction on endomorphisms.

## Possible applications

Keeping track of null lines, quotient metrics and genuine bundle obstructions in complex geometric optics.
