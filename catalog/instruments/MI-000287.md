# Distinct Kummer exponents separate overlapping prime lists simultaneously

[Family 031](../families/openai-math-031.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000162.md) · [Batch B022](../../batches/B022/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Fix m,d>=1, distinct rational primes q_j, and finite sets P_j of primes with q_j not in P_j. The P_j may overlap and may contain q_i for i!=j. There are infinitely many odd primes ell=1 mod m, avoiding any prescribed finite set, such that p^f mod ell is not in the cyclic subgroup generated by q_j mod ell for every p in P_j and 1<=f<=d.

Choose pairwise distinct auxiliary primes s_j larger than m,d and all listed primes, and let C=Q(zeta_(m product s_j)). For a listed prime, its ramification index in C divides the degree of Q(zeta_m), so it is prime to every s_j. Valuations at the distinct listed primes therefore prove independence of their classes in C^times/(C^times)^(s_j).

Kummer theory gives an elementary abelian s_j-extension adjoining s_jth roots of q_j and all p in P_j. Choose an automorphism fixing q_j's root but acting nontrivially on every listed p root. The different extensions have relatively prime degrees over C, so are linearly disjoint and these choices combine. The compositum is Galois over Q. Conjugation preserves whether each root-of-unity multiplier is trivial, since it raises the multiplier to a power prime to s_j.

Chebotarev supplies infinitely many primes with this conjugacy class. They split completely in C, so meet the required congruences. Modulo such a prime, q_j is an s_jth power and p is not. The quotient of the multiplicative residue group by s_jth powers has prime order s_j; a nonzero class remains nonzero under exponents 1 through d<s_j. It therefore cannot enter the subgroup generated by q_j. Overlaps among lists cause no conflict because their restrictions use different Kummer exponents.

## Role in the source argument

Chooses one auxiliary prime excluding all possible partners of several target primes in the source matching argument.

## Antecedents and comparison

This uses classical Kummer independence and the conjugacy-class form of Chebotarev; see [Milne, Algebraic Number Theory, Theorem 8.31](https://www.jmilne.org/math/CourseNotes/ANTc.pdf) for the latter. The source explicitly coordinates overlapping lists with different prime exponents. No exhaustive novelty search for this exact lemma was performed.

## Checks and limitations

Read the full proof, including valuations, conjugacy invariance and overlapping lists. Exact modular arithmetic finds five witness primes for two overlapping lists and checks all powers through d=2 directly against the relevant generated subgroups.

Finding five primes does not prove infinitude, Kummer independence or Chebotarev. The chosen auxiliary exponents must exceed d; otherwise a nonzero quotient class can vanish under a tested power.

## Possible applications

Constructing one residue field that simultaneously distinguishes several multiplicative sources from bounded powers of competing primes.
