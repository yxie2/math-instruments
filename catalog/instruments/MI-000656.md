# Stabilize a polynomial presentation by an explicit exponential shift

[Family 049](../families/openai-math-049.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000425.md) · [Batch B069](../../batches/B069/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Over a characteristic-zero field set P=k[p,s,u,F,J], x=s^2-u^2+pF and H=x^2F-(1+2sx)J-pJ^2-u. A rank-one matrix change first identifies A=P/(H) with a four-variable polynomial ring: for M=((F,s-u),(s+u,-p)), e=(x,-J)^t and n=(J,x)^t, put M'=(I-2ne^t)M. Since e^tn=0 and x=-det M is unchanged, the inverse is I+2ne^t. In primed entries H=-J-u', allowing elimination of u'.

For stabilization define y=s+x(x+u^2), z=sx+pJ; then xy-z(z+1)=p(H+u). On P[p^-1]=k[p,p^-1][x,y,z,u], the derivation Delta=-p partial_u preserves P: Delta(u)=-p, Delta(s)=2pxu, Delta(F)=-4sxu-2u, Delta(J)=-2x^2u, and Delta(p)=0. It is locally nilpotent and Delta(H)=p. Thus exp(w Delta) fixes p and sends H to H+pw.

Writing x0=s^2-u^2, set L=x0^2F-(1+2sx0)J and N=(1-2sx0)F+4s^2J; the coefficient matrix has determinant one. One has H=L-u+pQ0, Q0=2x0F^2+pF^3-2sFJ-J^2. Replacing w by w'=w+Q0 makes H+pw=L-u+pw', so elimination gives A[w]=k[p,s,u,N,w'] up to an explicit k[p]-isomorphism. The distinguished p becomes a coordinate after one variable is added.

## Role in the source argument

Provides a directly reproducible stabilization map independently of the claimed obstruction before stabilization.

## Antecedents and comparison

[Dutta and Lahiri, On Residual and Stable Coordinates](https://arxiv.org/abs/1908.03549), Lemma 3.3 and its proof on PDF page 6, constructs an exponential shift after localization; Theorem 3.4 applies it to stabilization. Those passages were read. The selected source makes the shift and elimination explicit for its presentation.

## Checks and limitations

Symbolic polynomial identities check the matrix inverse, preserved determinant, derivation, finite exponential, unimodular change and final elimination.

The tests verify stabilization identities; they do not establish that p fails to be a coordinate without stabilization.

## Possible applications

Converting a localized coordinate into a global stabilized coordinate with explicit formulas.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
