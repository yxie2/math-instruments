# Localize zero entropy under pointwise attraction

[Family 151](../families/openai-math-151.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000284.md) · [Batch B051](../../batches/B051/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let F be continuous on a nonempty compact metric space X, and let nonempty compact Z satisfy F(Z) subset Z, h_top(F|Z)=0, and d(F^n x,Z)->0 for every x. Then h_top(F)=0.

For a block length m and resolution epsilon, take P=s_Z(m,epsilon/4) Bowen balls of radius epsilon/2 whose union V contains Z, and Q such balls covering X. For each tau>0, pointwise attraction gives, at each x, a finite sample length k_x during which fewer than tau k_x iterates of F^m lie outside V. Openness preserves those visits near x. A finite subcover bounds the selected lengths by J. Concatenating these local segments along any orbit leaves a final remainder shorter than J, so at most tau r+J of its first r blocks begin outside V.

Record the exceptional subset and one ball index for each block. At most 2^r P^r Q^(tau r+J) descriptions occur, and equal descriptions keep two points within epsilon for mr iterates. The entropy at that resolution is at most [log 2+log P+tau log Q]/m. First let tau decrease to zero at fixed m, then let m tend to infinity. No uniform time after which every orbit stays in V is asserted.

The qualitative conclusion also follows from the variational principle: for every invariant probability, dominated convergence and invariance force the integral of d(x,Z) to be zero, so the measure is supported on Z.

## Role in the source argument

Transfers zero entropy of the source two attracting invariant sets to its entire compact phase space.

## Antecedents and comparison

[Misiurewicz, A short proof of the variational principle](https://numdam.org/item/AST_1976__40__147_0.pdf), Section 3, supplies the classical variational principle; its statement was inspected. The direct exceptional-block coding proof was read in full in the source. The localization conclusion is not claimed as novel.

## Checks and limitations

Finite maps with attracting invariant cycles test the concatenated-block budget for every initial state and many lengths. Exact combinatorial counts test the description bound. These finite models illustrate the coding mechanism, not the general compactness argument.

This lemma alone does not prove attraction for the constructed C1 map, its regularity or its action on homology.

## Possible applications

Proving zero entropy when all orbits approach a simple invariant set but entry times vary without a uniform bound.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
