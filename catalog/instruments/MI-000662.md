# Count birational contractions by losses of analytic cycle classes

[Family 056](../families/openai-math-056.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000431.md) · [Batch B070](../../batches/B070/README.md)

## Mathematical content

For a compact complex space V, let C_k(V) be the rational span in H_(2k)(V,Q) of compact irreducible analytic k-cycle classes, and let c_k be its dimension. Suppose a proper bimeromorphic diagram identifies U=X minus A with Y minus B, where X,Y are compact Kahler and dim B<k. Restriction and strict transform give a surjection C_k(X)->C_k(Y). If A contains a k-dimensional irreducible compact analytic subspace, the rank drops strictly.

Indeed H_(2k)(B)=H_(2k-1)(B)=0 by dimension. Borel-Moore localization identifies H_(2k)(Y) with H_(2k)^BM(U). The composed restriction from X sends cycles meeting U to their strict transforms and cycles in A to zero. Every target k-cycle meets U and has a source strict transform. A source k-cycle contained in A has nonzero class, since its integral against a Kahler form to the kth power is positive, and belongs to the kernel.

Consequently a sequence extracting no prime divisors can contract a prime divisor only finitely many times: each causes a drop of c_(n-1), a nonnegative integer. Small transformations preserve that rank. This alone does not bound the number of small flips.

## Role in the source argument

Provides a finite integer budget once the target exceptional loci have become too small to carry the cycles being counted.

## Antecedents and comparison

This is a classical localization and positivity argument, adapted from algebraic cycle ranks to analytic cycle spans. The source cites earlier fourfold cycle-rank methods; no new localization principle is claimed.

## Checks and limitations

Exact cellular chain complexes model collapse of an exceptional sphere, including the localization map, surviving class and strict kernel rank. Blowup intersection matrices test independence and positive detection of exceptional classes.

Finite topological models do not establish the required exceptional-locus dimensions in a source flip sequence.

## Possible applications

Turning geometric loss of a positive cycle into a discrete progress measure.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
