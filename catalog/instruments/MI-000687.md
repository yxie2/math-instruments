# Convert finite l1 cuts into exponential collisions and histogram bounds

[Family 099](../families/openai-math-099.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000456.md) · [Batch B073](../../batches/B073/README.md)

## Mathematical content

A finite l1 metric has a finite cut representation rho(x,y)=sum_A a_A |1_A(x)-1_A(y)| with a_A>=0, obtained by thresholding coordinates and merging equal cuts. Even for infinitely many coordinates, sum_A a_A<=sum_(unordered pairs)rho is finite after ignoring constant cuts.

Select each cut A independently with probability 1-exp(-alpha a_A), and partition points by their membership in the selected cuts. Two points share a cell exactly when no separating cut is selected, with probability exp(-alpha rho(x,y)). For a list L of size s, let H_pi(L) count occurrences per cell. The probability-weighted direct sum H_alpha(L) has l1 distance T equal to E||H_pi(L)-H_pi(M)||_1.

If every cross-distance between L and M is at least a, a union bound on their s^2 occurrence pairs gives T>=2s(1-s^2 exp(-alpha a)). For any pairing of all but t entries on each side, the triangle inequality gives T<=2t+2 sum_paired(1-exp(-alpha rho))<=2t+2alpha sum_paired rho. Repetitions are counted with multiplicity, and histogram mass is s. Thus their normalized l1 distance is twice total variation.

## Role in the source argument

Turns metric separation and partial matchings into opposing bounds in one deterministic l1 target.

## Antecedents and comparison

Threshold-cut representations and independent-cut constructions are classical. The source packages the exact collision kernel with partial histogram matching; exact priority of that combination is not established.

## Checks and limitations

Enumerates every subset of a small cut family and all length-two lists, including repetitions; checks the exact collision probabilities, histogram distances and both matching and separation bounds.

The checks do not certify the source recursive embedding or distortion lower bound.

## Possible applications

Comparing multisets using random partitions while retaining deterministic l1 output.
