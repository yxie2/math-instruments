# Joint sensitivity profiles control recursive gates while block witnesses multiply

[Family 132](../families/openai-math-132.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000117.md) · [Batch B011](../../batches/B011/README.md)

## Mathematical content

For a nested family P_1>=...>=P_H of Boolean predicates on the same raw inputs, let S_b maximize the number of bit flips changing one predicate from b to 1-b. Let J_b maximize the number changing two fixed distinct predicates simultaneously from b to 1-b on the same input. Empty maxima are zero. In the tournament construction with row width r, outdegree M^2 and candidate threshold t, one recursive level satisfies

$$S_1\le M^2S'_0+rS'_1,\quad J_1\le M^2J'_0+rS'_1,$$
$$S_0\le tS'_0+S'_1+3J'_1,\quad J_0\le tS'_0+3J'_1.$$

Primes denote child-family profiles. At an accepting input, fix one satisfied clause; destroying acceptance must break it. For joint destruction, any gate change must cross two distinct child thresholds, so it costs J'_0 instead of S'_0. At a rejecting input, target-repair clauses cost at most tS'_0. There are at most three gate-repair clauses. Except possibly one sink in their induced tournament, repairing a gate crosses both that gate threshold and the strongest target threshold, so it costs J'_1. For joint acceptance, the second parent index supplies the second child threshold even at the possible sink. Candidate lists remain fixed before the changing bit is selected.

The source starts with H_0=d+1 predicates testing distance to M disjoint-support centers in LM bits, L=d+2, at integer radii d,...,0. Centers are distance 2L apart. Thus S_{0,0}<=L, S_{0,1}<=LM and J_{0,0}=J_{0,1}=0: a bit changes the distance by at most one and cannot cross two distinct integer radii.

At level ell there are M k^ell disjoint sensitive blocks at zero, each of size L r^ell, that turn every current predicate to one. To lift a witness, copy the same child block into every child of one target row. All other rows remain zero, so every outgoing gate passes. This is a block-sensitivity lower witness; the recurrence separately controls sensitivity at every input.

## Role in the source argument

Keeps simultaneous-threshold costs small enough under recursion while independently multiplying the number of disjoint accepting blocks.

## Antecedents and comparison

This refines the [earlier gated-tournament architecture](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.00851v1) by passing ordinary and joint sensitivity profiles together. The separated-center base uses elementary Hamming geometry. The final superquadratic parameter choice is beyond this selected recurrence check.

## Checks and limitations

Exhaustively checked small separated-center bases and a finite nested-predicate composition, including joint changes caused by the same raw bit. Disjoint row-block witnesses were checked directly in these small constructions.

Joint sensitivities concern two fixed predicates at one input; summing unrelated individual maxima would lose the needed information. The finite models use small tournaments and their actual label threshold, not the enormous final source parameters.

## Possible applications

Recursive Boolean complexity constructions where ordinary sensitivity and simultaneous threshold crossings have different growth rates.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
