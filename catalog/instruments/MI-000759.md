# Turn pair exclusions into a unique polynomial zero-mode line

[Family 269](../families/openai-math-269.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000506.md) · [Batch B082](../../batches/B082/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Realize a one-particle spin space U_Q as homogeneous polynomials of degree Q in (u,v), and put b_ij=u_i v_j-u_j v_i. In the antisymmetric pair space, relative degrees r are odd; the pair-spin Q-r summand is divisible by b_ij^r. For the positive sum of projectors excluding r=1, zero total energy forces every pair projector separately to vanish. Therefore b_ij^3 divides the full polynomial for every pair.

Each bracket is primitive and linear in u_i over the fraction field of the remaining variables, hence irreducible by Gauss's lemma. Distinct unordered pairs give nonassociate brackets. Unique factorization then implies that their cubed product divides the state. At Q=3(N-1) this product exhausts the degree in each particle's variables, so the quotient is constant.

Conversely the product is antisymmetric; after removing b_ij^3 the maximum pair spin is Q-3. Multiplication by the invariant bracket cannot introduce spin Q-1. Thus the product is annihilated by every pair projector, and the kernel is precisely its line. The same degree count rules out a zero mode with N+1 particles at this fixed flux.

## Role in the source argument

Separating exact algebraic identification of a ground space from the harder quantitative spectral-gap question.

## Antecedents and comparison

[Ortiz et al., arXiv:1306.3268](https://arxiv.org/abs/1306.3268), PDF page 5, already states uniqueness of the filling-1/3 zero mode in spherical and related geometries. The source presents a self-contained polynomial divisibility proof; the zero-mode uniqueness is not a new claim.

## Checks and limitations

Small spherical pair matrices check kernel dimensions at Laughlin flux and one particle above it. Symbolic Vandermonde-cube coefficients produce an independent zero vector and verify antisymmetry and degree exhaustion.

Finite-dimensional kernel uniqueness does not supply a lower bound uniform in N. The source uniform gap theorem needs additional estimates.
