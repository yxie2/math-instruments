# Cancel directional derivatives using a positive weight difference

[Family 210](../families/openai-math-210.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000330.md) · [Batch B057](../../batches/B057/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let U and Y be independent vector-variable blocks of the same size over C, and consider polynomials bihomogeneous of degrees p,q in these blocks, with any additional independent variables. Define D=sum_i Y_i partial_(U_i) and E=sum_i U_i partial_(Y_i). In the Fischer inner product, distinct monomials are orthogonal and the squared norm of X^alpha is alpha!. Differentiation is adjoint to multiplication, so D*=E.

The commutator is ED-DE=N_U-N_Y. Therefore every such polynomial h satisfies

||Dh||^2-||Eh||^2=(p-q)||h||^2.

If p>q, this proves injectivity of D on the entire bihomogeneous space; indeed ||Dh||>=sqrt(p-q)||h||. Additional independent variables cause no change, since their monomial norms simply factor through. A sequence of such operators is injective whenever the appropriate degree inequality holds before each step, including after previous derivatives have shifted the degrees.

The strict inequality matters. At p=q=1 with two coordinates, U_1 Y_2-U_2 Y_1 is a nonzero element of the kernel. Characteristic zero also matters: in characteristic five the derivative of U_1^5 vanishes despite positive U-degree and zero Y-degree. The source uses the characteristic-zero statement to cancel intermediate derivatives in a larger vanishing-on-products induction; that induction is not part of this selected check.

## Role in the source argument

Provides the exact degree condition under which the source recovers a polynomial from its successive directional derivatives.

## Antecedents and comparison

[Ikenmeyer, On McKay's propagation theorem for the Foulkes conjecture](https://arxiv.org/abs/1509.04957v1), Claim 5.1 and its full proof on pages 6-7, uses a two-coordinate raising operator and GL2 weight spaces for injectivity. [Aldaz-Render](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.10400), Section 2, equation (8) and Proposition 3 with its proof on pages 5-6, gives the Fischer inner product and adjoint identity. These passages were read. The source proof is a direct classical commutator argument.

## Checks and limitations

Exact weighted Gram identities are checked on 57 bihomogeneous spaces in dimensions 1,2,3. Modular rank certifies full rational column rank on all 24 tested positive-weight spaces. Equal-degree and positive-characteristic examples test the limits.

These checks do not establish the source degree bookkeeping throughout the full induction or surjectivity of the canonical Foulkes-Howe map.

## Possible applications

Justifying cancellation of differential operators uniformly over a whole polynomial space, with a quantitative norm bound and explicit boundary cases.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
