# Two odd-order projective maps with a loxodromic product admit no finite invariant measure

[Family 050](../families/openai-math-050.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000178.md) · [Batch B026](../../batches/B026/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let A and B be projective transformations of P1 of odd finite order, with no common fixed point, and suppose AB has two eigenvalues of distinct moduli. Then they preserve no nonzero finite positive Borel measure.

A common invariant measure is invariant under T=AB. In a projective coordinate, T acts by z->lambda z with R=|lambda|>1. The half-open annuli R^n<=|z|<R^(n+1), n in Z, are pairwise disjoint, cover C*, and are shifted by T. All have equal mass; finiteness and positivity force that mass to be zero. Thus the measure is supported on the two fixed points of T.

Its nonempty support is preserved by A and B. An odd-order permutation of at most two points is the identity, so every support point would be fixed by both maps, contradicting the hypothesis. Half-open annuli retain boundary circles and allow atomic measures and nonreal lambda.

The source uses determinant-one lifts

$$a=e^{i\pi/3},\quad s=\frac12-\frac{i(1+2\sqrt2)}{2\sqrt3},\quad A=\begin{pmatrix}a&0\\0&a^{-1}\end{pmatrix},\quad B=\begin{pmatrix}s&1\\\sqrt2/3&1-s\end{pmatrix}.$$

They satisfy A^3=B^3=-I, trace(A^(-1)B)=-sqrt(2), and trace(AB)=1+sqrt(2)>2. The relative lift has order eight; AB has distinct positive reciprocal eigenvalues. The nonzero off-diagonal entries of B exclude either eigenline of A being common. These finite identities supply the dynamical hypotheses independently of the source vector-bundle limit argument.

## Role in the source argument

Contradicts the invariant positive curvature measure obtained from the source hypothetical squared seminorm.

## Antecedents and comparison

Concentration of projective invariant measures under expanding elements is a classical Furstenberg-type mechanism. The extracted proof is an elementary rank-two annulus argument with explicit matrices, rather than a claimed new general measure theorem.

## Checks and limitations

Exact algebraic-number arithmetic verifies determinants, matrix powers, relative order and product trace. Both possible support sizes are checked for odd-order permutations, with 75 half-open annulus instances including boundary radii.

Finite matrix checks do not replace the written countable-additivity argument. They also do not establish the source bundle construction, ampleness or quadratic-limit compactness.

## Possible applications

Ruling out invariant metrics or norms by converting them to finite positive measures and combining incompatible projective dynamics.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
