# Keep the zero-set atoms when turning a squared seminorm into curvature

[Family 050](../families/openai-math-050.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000178.md) · [Batch B026](../../batches/B026/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let a finite nonnegative plurisubharmonic function on a holomorphic line bundle be a Hermitian quadratic on each fiber and not identically zero. In a holomorphic frame write it as u(z)|w|^2. Then log u, allowing minus infinity, is subharmonic and locally integrable.

Regularize by u_delta=u*rho_delta+delta using a fixed nonnegative radial approximate identity. The Levi matrix of u_delta(z)|w|^2 at w=1 is positive semidefinite, so u_delta(u_delta)_(z zbar)-|(u_delta)_z|^2>=0 and log u_delta is subharmonic. Radial means give decreasing convergence to log u.

To rule out a nonintegrable limit, begin at a point z_0 with u(z_0)>0. A common upper bound C_0 and the submean inequality give

$$\int_{B(z_0,R)}(\log u_\delta)^-\,dA\le\pi R^2(C_0-\log u(z_0)).$$

Monotone convergence gives local integrability and L1 convergence. Overlapping disks propagate this property across the connected base. Frame changes add log|g|^2 with g nowhere zero, so the measures dd^c log u glue. A bundle isometry covering T makes this measure T-invariant.

On O(-b) over P1, compare with the reference weight (1+|z|^2)^b. Their logarithmic difference is a globally integrable function, whose distributional dd^c has total mass zero. With dd^c=i partial partialbar, the resulting positive measure has mass 2 pi b. Zeros and their atoms remain included. For u=|z|^(2r)(1+|z|^2)^(b-r), the diffuse mass is 2 pi(b-r) and the atom at zero has mass 2 pi r.

## Role in the source argument

Converts the source hypothetical quadratic limit into a nonzero finite measure with a prescribed total mass.

## Antecedents and comparison

[Raufi, Definition 2 and Proposition 1.1 in the preprint](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1211.2948) records the established squared-norm/logarithmic-norm relation, decreasing regularization and local integrability of log determinants. The source gives a rank-one proof with explicit negative-part and total-mass accounting.

## Checks and limitations

44 radial singular-metric models verify the polynomial Levi identity and diffuse-plus-atomic mass, including a purely atomic curvature example. Omitting the zero-set atom loses the prescribed mass.

These models do not prove the source nonsmooth compactness theorem or existence of a nonzero quadratic limit on its bundle.

## Possible applications

Producing invariant measures from degenerate metrics without losing curvature concentrated at their zeros.
