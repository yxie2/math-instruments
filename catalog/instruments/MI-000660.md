# Recover a normalized Gepner eigencharge and control the remaining coordinates

[Family 055](../families/openai-math-055.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000429.md) · [Batch B069](../../batches/B069/README.md)

## Mathematical content

For a smooth quintic threefold, normalized Chern coordinates v=(r,c,d,e) identify the real numerical Grothendieck group with R^4. Riemann-Roch gives chi(v,w)=5(re'-cd'+dc'-er'+(5/6)(rc'-cr')); its matrix has determinant 625. The four line-bundle vectors (1,k,k^2/2,k^3/6), k=0,1,2,3, span, and integrality puts the numerical group in Z times Z times (1/10)Z times (1/30)Z.

The numerical action of Phi=T_O composed with tensor O(1) is M with rows (-4,-20/3,-5,-5), (1,1,0,0), (1/2,1,1,0), (1/6,1/2,1,1); its characteristic polynomial is z^4+z^3+z^2+z+1. Let lambda=exp(2 pi i/5), t0=(1/2)cot(pi/5), u=(5-sqrt(5))/10. The unique charge satisfying ZM=lambda Z and Z(O_x)=-1 is

Z(v)/5=((lambda-1)/5)r+(t0^2-5/6+i t0/2)c+(-1/2+i t0)d-e.

In particular Im Z/(5t0)=d+c/2+ur. The (d,e) coefficient matrix of (Re Z,Im Z) has determinant 25t0, so v maps isomorphically to (r,c,Re Z,Im Z). Thus every fixed norm obeys ||v||<=C(|r|+|c|+|Z(v)|). This algebraic estimate alone does not control r,c for semistable objects.

## Role in the source argument

Reduces full numerical support to controlling rank and first Chern character in addition to the charge.

## Antecedents and comparison

[Toda, Gepner point and strong Bogomolov-Gieseker inequality for quintic 3-folds](https://arxiv.org/abs/1305.0345v3), section 3.3, PDF page 9, already gives the normalized charge and unique eigenvector calculation. That passage was read. The source uses equivalent real coefficients and explicit norm bookkeeping; the charge is not new.

## Checks and limitations

Exact rational matrices and cyclotomic reduction check the Euler determinant, line-bundle span, fifth-order action and normalized eigenvector; inverse-matrix bounds check norm control.

Numerical action and charge identities do not prove existence of a compatible slicing or the Gepner stability theorem.

## Possible applications

Separating a computable numerical charge from the harder categorical support and filtration problem.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
