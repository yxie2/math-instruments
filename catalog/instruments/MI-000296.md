# Order positive degree discrepancies first to expose a rank bottleneck

[Family 135](../families/openai-math-135.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000167.md) · [Batch B024](../../batches/B024/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Write h(r,t)=binomial(r+t-1,t). Split variables into disjoint groups V,U of sizes v,u and replace them by differentiation in V and multiplication in U. These operations commute, so T_(prod Q_j)=prod T_(Q_j). For homogeneous factors of degrees e_j summing to n=k+m, expand their bidegrees as (i_j,e_j-i_j), with sum i_j=k. Let lambda=k/n, rho=k/m and delta_j=i_j-lambda e_j.

Apply the factors with delta_j>0 first. If their derivative and multiplication degrees total I,J, the corresponding term factors through H_V(a-I) tensor H_U(b+J), giving the rank bound h(v,a-I)h(u,b+J). This is a genuine intermediate-space bound; one does not assert multiplicativity of the projected map F_g from bidegree (a,b) to (a-k,b+m).

For a>=k, b>0, put D=h(v,a)h(u,b), alpha=a/(a+v), beta=b/(b+u). If beta>=alpha^rho and (1-1/(a+v))^(-n)<=2, the elementary dimension ratios imply

$$\frac{h(v,a-I)h(u,b+J)}D\le2\alpha^{I-\rho J}=2q^{\sum_j|\delta_j|},\qquad q=\alpha^{(1+\rho)/2}.$$

Here sum delta_j=0 gives the equality in the exponent. Summing ranks over bidegree assignments and then dropping the sum constraint yields

$$\operatorname{rank}F_{\prod Q_j}\le2D\prod_j\left(\sum_{i=0}^{e_j}q^{|i-\lambda e_j|}\right).$$

The paper chooses its parameters so these hypotheses hold. The underlying factorization works over any field; a useful lower bound for the specific target polynomial is a separate problem.

## Role in the source argument

Makes products with incompatible factor degrees lose rank under the source measure.

## Antecedents and comparison

[Amireddy, Garg, Kayal, Saha and Thankey, Definition 2.1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2211.07691) already defines degree residue through deviations of integer derivative allocations from proportional factor degrees. The source implements this established imbalance principle through a commuting-operator intermediate space. No exhaustive comparison or novelty certification was performed.

## Checks and limitations

Thirty-six polynomial models over fields of characteristics 2, 3 and 101 check 108 exact bidegree factorizations, including 96 nonempty positive-discrepancy subsets, their ranks and the normalized dimension estimate.

These finite checks do not establish the target IMM rank lower bound, trace-moment estimates or the claimed asymptotic circuit lower bound.

## Possible applications

Rank measures for products of homogeneous objects when commuting operators admit an advantageous application order.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
