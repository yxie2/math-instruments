# Count transported divisorial valuations with an additive field test

[Family 054](../families/openai-math-054.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000251.md) · [Batch B046](../../batches/B046/README.md)

## Mathematical content

For normal projective models X,Y with birational map f, transport their prime divisorial valuations to one function field F using f*. Their symmetric difference is finite: outside isomorphic dense opens, only finitely many divisorial components remain. Given an isomorphism-invariant residue-field weight u, define c_u(f) as the sum over target-acquired valuations minus the sum over source-lost valuations.

On a common field for a composition, 1_(D_Z)-1_(D_X)=(1_(D_Y)-1_(D_X))+(1_(D_Z)-1_(D_Y)). Multiply by u of the residue field and sum; the finite-support identity proves additivity and c_u(f inverse)=-c_u(f). Isomorphisms in codimension one have value zero.

For a smooth blowup pi along connected centers Z_i of codimension at least two, each exceptional divisor has a purely transcendental function field over that of Z_i. If u is unchanged by those extensions, c_u(pi inverse)=sum u(Z_i). Distinct valuations are counted separately even when their residue fields are isomorphic. For a closed generic-fiber point, its exceptional divisor contributes once, not multiplied by its residue-field degree.
