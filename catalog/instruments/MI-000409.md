# Realize a constant-volume finite jet by polynomial shears

[Family 338](../families/openai-math-338.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000223.md) · [Batch B038](../../batches/B038/README.md)

## Mathematical content

For fixed n,d, every invertible d-jet fixing zero whose Jacobian determinant equals its center determinant through degree d-1 is the d-jet of a polynomial automorphism of C^n. The automorphism and inverse degrees have a bound depending only on n,d; coefficients are polynomially bounded in the jet coefficients and inverse linear part.

After normalizing the derivative, the first discrepancy in degree m is a homogeneous vector polynomial X_m with div X_m=0. Such fields are spanned by l(z)^m v with l(v)=0. To see this, a functional annihilating all these fields gives a covector polynomial A(l) proportional to l. The identities l_i A_j=l_j A_i force A(l)=B(l)l, precisely the image of the transpose of divergence. Taking annihilators gives the spanning statement.

Each field has exact time-c map z->z+c l(z)^m v, with inverse obtained by changing the sign and Jacobian determinant one. Fix a finite spanning list and linear decomposition in each degree. Composing these shears corrects degree m without altering lower degrees. Induct through d and restore the linear part. Only a fixed number of polynomial operations occur, giving the degree and coefficient bounds. In dimension one the positive-degree divergence kernel is zero.

## Role in the source argument

Replaces formal coordinate jets by globally invertible polynomial maps while controlling the finite data.

## Antecedents and comparison

[Forstneric and Larusson, proof of Theorem 3.1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1402.4342) explicitly uses Andersen divergence-free shear generation and fixed finite bases. The source records a finite-jet realization and its coefficient bookkeeping.

## Checks and limitations

Seven exact homogeneous-space rank calculations match the divergence-kernel dimension; forty shears have exact polynomial inverses and Jacobian one.

The computation checks the spanning and shear ingredients, not every jet realization or the source global dynamical construction.

## Possible applications

Implementing a finite formal change of coordinates by an actual polynomial automorphism with a prescribed constant Jacobian.
