# Pay for rare radius guesses with disjoint client populations

[Family 125](../families/openai-math-125.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000277.md) · [Batch B050](../../batches/B050/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let P=sum_(p in D) d(p,O)>0 be the cost of a comparison facility set, and let m>=1. For a sampled client w define q(w)=min{q>=0:q |D intersect B(w,q)|>=P/m}. If d_1<=...<=d_n are its distances to clients, including itself, then q(w)=min_j max{d_j,P/(mj)}. Closed balls make this minimum attained.

For r=d(w,O), a correct guessed radius lies in [r,(1+kappa)r+kappa q(w)], with 0<kappa<=1. Attach a persistent leaf at w with that edge length. The source uses three finite menus. The expensive menu applies only when r>10q(w) and the distance to every earlier leaf exceeds 10r; it chooses j=ceil(r/(kappa q(w))) with probability proportional to 2^(-j).

The closed client balls of all expensive additions are disjoint. Otherwise two centers satisfy d(w_a,w_b)<=q_a+q_b<(r_a+r_b)/10. Lipschitzness of distance to O gives 9/11<r_a/r_b<11/9, and the earlier correct leaf would be within 3r_b of the later center, contradicting the menu condition. Every client in one such ball contributes more than 0.9r to P. The population inequality therefore yields sum r/q<=10m/9, and at most m additions give sum j<=(10/(9kappa)+1)m.

If every other menu choice costs at most a fixed factor c_kappa and each expensive entry has probability at least c_kappa 2^(-j), the product of prescribed menu probabilities is at least c_kappa^m 2^(-(10/(9kappa)+1)m). This holds for adaptive leader order. It bounds radius-guess losses only; a separate argument must supply useful leaders and certify a successful terminal prefix. Removing earlier leaves invalidates the disjointness argument.

## Role in the source argument

Removes an aspect-ratio or m log m loss from the source radius-guessing probability.

## Antecedents and comparison

[Cohen-Addad et al., arXiv:2503.10972v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2503.10972v2), Section 4 overview and Section 4.3.1, supply the sampled leaders and dummy-center construction; those passages were read. The source adds the population radius and amortized geometric menu. This targeted comparison does not establish first priority for that refinement.

## Checks and limitations

Exact finite line metrics verify the radius formula, nontrivial expensive-menu sequences, disjointness and the total index budget. A repeated leader after deleting its earlier leaf demonstrates the need for persistence.

The tests do not validate the source guide-progress, submodular completion or approximation factor.

## Possible applications

Sampling a sequence of unknown geometric scales when disjoint populations can charge the total logarithm of the guessing probability.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
