# Four independent digit constraints bound a singular divisor after fixing labels

[Family 191](../families/openai-math-191.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000126.md) · [Batch B013](../../batches/B013/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let B be prime, h=B^k, and 2<=r divide L<B. For any fixed three labels, independently choose every digit d_iju uniformly from the L/r numbers in {1,...,L} with a prescribed residue modulo r. Residues may depend arbitrarily on the label, row and digit position; repeated labels still use fresh independent draws. Put C_ij=sum_(u<k) d_iju B^u modulo h. Every entry is a unit because its lowest digit is positive and below B.

Write the second Smith divisor as D=B^b. Conditional on the labels,

$$\Pr(b\ge j)\le(r/L)^{4j}\quad(1\le j\le k).$$

Further condition on the first column and the first entries of columns two and three. Vanishing of all two-by-two minors modulo B^j requires C_iv=C_i1 C_11^(-1) C_1v modulo B^j for i,v in {2,3}. Each fixed target determines j base-B digits of one of four independent entries. Its probability is zero or (r/L)^j. Averaging preserves the bound uniformly over labels.

With theta=B(r/L)^4<1, the tail-sum identity gives E[D|labels]<=1+sum_(j=1)^k theta^j<=1/(1-theta). In particular, for the source parameters L=r^10, B<=200k^2L^3 and sufficiently large r, this conditional expectation is at most two. If labels are independent uniform on a set of size r^d and E is their collision event, E[D 1_E]<=2Pr(E)<=6r^(-d). Conditioning avoids incorrectly multiplying two dependent unconditional quantities.

## Role in the source argument

Retains a label-collision saving while controlling the singular divisor that appears in orbit and lattice estimates.

## Antecedents and comparison

This is an elementary independent-coordinate small-ball argument and a tail-sum moment calculation. Its source-specific use is uniformity after fixing arbitrary labels, combined with the norm obstruction. No separate historical novelty claim is made for the conditioning principle.

## Checks and limitations

Read the conditioning and tail-sum proof. Exact small digit distributions, including repeated labels, check vanishing-minor probabilities, divisor moments and the conditional bound; arbitrary label signatures are included.

Independence of the four remaining entries after conditioning is essential. Label equality does not permit reusing digits. The bounded moment needs theta<1, even though the tail estimate itself does not.

## Possible applications

Combining rare algebraic-label events with singularity weights without assuming independence between those events and the weights.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
