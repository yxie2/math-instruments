# Remove high-codimension collisions from a distributional first-order equation

[Family 270](../families/openai-math-270.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000507.md) · [Batch B082](../../batches/B082/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let S be a finite union of linear collision subspaces of codimension nine in the center space, including after the trace-zero constraint. Suppose F,G are L^2 and D F=G distributionally away from S, for the source constant-symbol first-order center charge with locally bounded mass multiplier.

Choose rho_epsilon as the product of cutoffs vanishing within epsilon of each subspace and equal to one beyond 2 epsilon. On a compact set, tubular coordinates give ||grad rho_epsilon||_2^2<=C epsilon^(-2)epsilon^9=C epsilon^7. Intersections do not cause a new singularity: the derivative of a finite product is bounded by the sum of the individual derivatives.

Test the equation against rho_epsilon phi. The commutator error is at most ||F||_2 ||phi||_infinity ||grad rho_epsilon||_2/sqrt(2), which tends to zero. The other terms converge by dominated convergence, so the equation holds globally. The minimal/maximal domain argument then places F in the full Euclidean graph domain. Finite-group averaging preserves the assertion in the required permutation-symmetry subspace; no additional boundary condition on collisions appears.

## Role in the source argument

Passing an equation across collision strata after obtaining a limit only on their complement.

## Antecedents and comparison

This is the classical capacity-removal mechanism for a first-order equation with L^2 data. The source application uses codimension nine, comfortably above the threshold of the elementary power cutoff estimate.

## Checks and limitations

Exact tubular cutoff integrals check the epsilon^7 scaling and the finite-product derivative bound. A codimension-one jump function controls the false assertion that every null set is removable.

The elementary power estimate does not settle the critical codimension-two case. The result does not establish profile existence, tightness or uniqueness of the BFSS threshold state.
