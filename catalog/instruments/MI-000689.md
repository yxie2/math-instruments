# Compute simplex shadow area from its longest parallel chord

[Family 100](../families/openai-math-100.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000458.md) · [Batch B073](../../batches/B073/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let S be a full-dimensional simplex in R^d, d>=2, of volume V. For a unit direction u, put b_i=directional derivative of barycentric coordinate lambda_i and c=sum_(b_i>0)b_i=sum_(b_i<0)(-b_i)>0. The overlap S intersect (S-su) is described by lambda_i>=s max(0,-b_i). It is a translate of (1-sc)S for 0<=s<1/c, a point at 1/c and empty above it. Thus the longest u-parallel chord has length L=1/c.

If A is the (d-1)-volume of the orthogonal projection, the set of projected fibers whose chord exceeds s has measure A(1-s/L)^(d-1). Integrating those superlevel measures gives V=AL/d. Therefore A=dV/L, and minimizing over directions yields min A=dV/diam(S). For a regular tetrahedron of edge length ell this is ell^2/(2sqrt(2)). The homothetic overlap also yields the full chord-length distribution, not just its first moment.

## Role in the source argument

Identifies the most economical cylinder direction for a simplex cell.

## Antecedents and comparison

[Heinrich, Lower and Upper Bounds for Chord Power Integrals of Ellipsoids](https://www.m-hikari.com/ams/ams-2014/ams-165-168-2014/heinrichAMS165-168-2014.pdf), printed page 8268, states exactly A L=dV and attributes it to Martini and Weissbach (1992). That page and its reference were read. This is a known geometric identity used in the source application.

## Checks and limitations

Random triangles and tetrahedra compare independently computed projected convex-hull volumes, simplex determinants and longest-chord linear programs.

The tests do not certify radial-sweep localization or the full cylinder bound.

## Possible applications

Selecting projection directions using a diameter or a chord optimization instead of a separate shadow minimization.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
