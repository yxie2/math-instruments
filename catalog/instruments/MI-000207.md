# Reversal-normalized compositions enumerate weighted linear forests without duplicates

[Family 189](../families/openai-math-189.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000124.md) · [Batch B013](../../batches/B013/README.md)

## Mathematical content

A linear forest on t vertices with positive integer edge weights of total at most B is represented uniquely up to weighted graph isomorphism by a sorted multiset of tuples. For each path component list its edge weights, replace that tuple by the lexicographically smaller of it and its reversal, then sort the component tuples with repetitions retained. A singleton is the empty tuple. The constraints are sum_p(len(p)+1)=t and sum_p sum(p)<=B.

Enumerate recursively by selecting a reversal-normalized first tuple no smaller than the preceding one, subtracting its vertices and weight, and continuing. At zero remaining vertices emit the empty list for every nonnegative remaining budget. While generating candidate tuples, extend rejected prefixes too: a longer tuple can meet normalization and lower-bound tests even when its prefix does not.

An independent count uses reversal orbits. For r>=1 edges and weight m>=r, the number of chain types is a(r,m)=(binom(m-1,r-1)+b(r,m))/2. Palindromic compositions number b(2s,m)=binom(m/2-1,s-1) for even m and zero otherwise, while b(2s+1,m)=binom(floor((m-1)/2),s). The forest count is

$$\sum_{L=0}^B[x^t y^L]\frac1{1-x}\prod_{r\ge1}\prod_{m\ge r}(1-x^{r+1}y^m)^{-a(r,m)}.$$

Only finitely many factors contribute. The source's 42 pairs 5<=k<=17 and max(3,floor(k/2))<=t<=min(8,k), with B=k-t, contain 3,099 pattern instances. This is enumeration of weighted forests, not of ambient Ramsey graphs or successful exclusion certificates.

## Role in the source argument

Makes the finite search space complete and independently countable before any deduction rules are applied.

## Antecedents and comparison

This is the standard reversal-orbit count for compositions followed by an ordinary multiset generating function. Its value here is a fully specified enumeration interface and a separate count, not a new counting theorem. Earlier cycle-Ramsey context is [Keevash, Long and Skokan](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1807.06376v1).

## Checks and limitations

Read the uniqueness proof and palindrome formulas. Separate recursive tuple generation and generating-function arithmetic reproduce all 42 counts and their total, with duplicate detection on the generated patterns.

Matching the pattern count does not verify the rules that exclude those patterns. Pruning tuple generation at a failed prefix would be incomplete. Unused weight budget and repeated singleton components must remain allowed.

## Possible applications

Auditable finite reductions involving weighted paths, with a count independent of the exclusion procedure.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
