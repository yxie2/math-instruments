# Certify tensor Gram blocks using integer-scaled orthogonal projections

[Family 367](../families/openai-math-367.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000183.md) · [Batch B028](../../batches/B028/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Work over a five-dimensional Euclidean space. For rank two, let F'=F+F^T and v=tr(F'). Then Pi F=5F'-I v is ten times the orthogonal projection onto symmetric trace-free matrices.

For rank three, put F'=sum_{pi in S3} F^pi and v_i=sum_j F'_{jji}. Define (Jv)_ijk=delta_ij v_k+delta_ik v_j+delta_jk v_i. Since tr_12(Jv)=7v, the operator 7F'-Jv is 42 times the symmetric trace-free orthogonal projection.

For the hook component instead set F'=3(F+F^(12))-sum_pi F^pi and Bv=2delta_ij v_k-delta_ik v_j-delta_jk v_i. Then

$$\Pi F=8F'-B(\operatorname{tr}_{12}F')$$

is 48 times an orthogonal projection. Indeed P_0=S_12-Sym projects onto first-pair symmetric tensors H with H_ijk+H_ikj+H_jki=0. Their other traces equal -tr_12(H)/2. On this space tr_12(Bv)=8v and <H,Bz>=3<tr_12(H),z>, so subtracting B(tr_12 H)/8 is the orthogonal trace-free projection.

The unnormalized alternating sum is six times the alternating projection. All four operators are self-adjoint and positive semidefinite on the full tensor spaces, not only on inputs already satisfying their symmetry constraints. This lets a Gram block inherit nonnegativity from its scalar weight.

## Role in the source argument

Supplies the nonnegative tensor blocks in the source algebraic certificate.

## Antecedents and comparison

These are explicit realizations of classical orthogonal tensor projections. The integer scales and hook trace correction are the useful source normalizations.

## Checks and limitations

Exact integer matrices of sizes 25 and 125 satisfy P^T=P and P^2=cP. Their ranks are 14, 30, 35 and 10 at scales 10, 42, 48 and 6 respectively; the specified trace-free conditions also pass.

This verifies the finite operators completely in dimension five, not the source polynomial coefficient tables or their assembly into a universal geometric inequality.

## Possible applications

Exact positivity certificates for tensor-valued polynomial expressions without numerical eigenvalue tolerances.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
