# Repair exceptional centers by an open neighborhood and a vanishing tail

[Family 084](../families/openai-math-084.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000450.md) · [Batch B072](../../batches/B072/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Suppose a finite random periodic grid set B has expected density at most p. Enlarge each realization to an open B+ at additional density at most p. Let R consist of centers x for which some t in [1,2] misses B+ at every tested index n in a finite set N. The miss conditions form a closed subset of the compact product T x [1,2]; its projection R is closed.

Assume the all-scale failure probability is at most 2p at stable centers and the unstable centers have density at most p. Finite Fubini gives E rho(R)<=3p, while E rho(B+)<=2p. Some realization therefore has rho(B+)+rho(R)<=5p. Choose an open epsilon-neighborhood V of the closed set R with rho(V)<=rho(R)+p. Then H=B+ union V has density at most 6p.

Outside R, the finite tested indices hit H at every normalized scale. For x in R, every sufficiently large n satisfies t2^-n<epsilon uniformly over t in [1,2], so the full dyadic tail hits V. This completes all centers; the new indices need not belong to the original finite test set. Compactness of the scale interval supplies closedness, and uniform decay supplies the tail repair.

## Role in the source argument

Converts a small exceptional set of centers into a deterministic open hitting set.

## Antecedents and comparison

[Kolountzakis, Infinite patterns that can be avoided by measure](https://eigen-space.org/ps/universal.pdf), section 2.2 and the completion discussion in section 3.2, use an open cover of exceptional translation parameters and the accumulation point zero. PDF pages 6 and 10-11 were visually read. This repair mechanism is an established antecedent; the source spells out its periodic and compact-scale implementation.

## Checks and limitations

Finite periodic interval models check compact exceptional sets, neighborhood measure growth, uniform tail entry and the expectation-based choice of a realization.

The tests and repair do not establish the assumed all-scale failure probability or its routing construction.

## Possible applications

Removing a small set of bad base points when the tested patterns converge uniformly to their centers.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
