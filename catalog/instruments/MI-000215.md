# Sublinear word entropy forces cancellation against dissociated characters under arbitrary coupling

[Family 145](../families/openai-math-145.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000128.md) · [Batch B014](../../batches/B014/README.md)

## Mathematical content

On a probability space let chi_l, indexed by a countable abelian group L, have modulus one, chi_0=1, products equal to constant phases times chi_(l+l'), and expectation zero for nonzero l. Let (l_m) be dissociated: every nonempty finite sum of distinct l_m with signs plus or minus one is nonzero. Suppose F_m take values in one fixed finite subset of the closed unit disk and the word entropy H_n=H(F_0,...,F_(n-1)), in nats, is o(n). Then

$$\frac1n\sum_{m<n}|\mathbb E F_m\overline{\chi_{l_m}}|\longrightarrow0.$$

There is no independence assumption on F and chi. For deterministic |w_m|<=1 and 0<=theta<=1, expansion of the product bounds gives E exp(theta Re sum_m w_m chi_(l_m)) <= exp(2 theta^2 n): every nonconstant signed character product integrates to zero. Consequently the upper tail at epsilon n is at most exp(-epsilon^2 n/8).

For a>0, words having probability at least exp(-an) number at most exp(an). The total probability of all other words is at most H_n/(an). Align the phases of the expected correlations, apply the deterministic tail estimate separately to the likely words, and use a union bound. This bounds the average absolute correlation by

$$\varepsilon+H_n/(an)+\exp(an-\varepsilon^2n/8).$$

Take a=sqrt(H_n/n)+n^(-1/2) and then let epsilon decrease after n tends to infinity. The character law is never conditioned on the realized word. Such conditioning would destroy the exponential estimate.

## Role in the source argument

Cancels low-complexity names against structured oscillations even when both are defined on the same probability space.

## Antecedents and comparison

The exponential step is an established dissociation principle; see [Green, Proposition 17](https://www.math.cmu.edu/~ploh/2006-additive-combinatorics/sources/Ben%20Green/Structural%20theory%20of%20set%20addition.pdf). The extracted interface combines it with entropy compression under arbitrary coupling. Targeted comparison does not establish priority for that combination.

## Checks and limitations

Read the product expansion and likely-word union bound. Finite Walsh-cube examples use words chosen from the same signs, checking the exponential estimate and entropy bound; controls remove dissociation or sublinear entropy.

Orthogonality alone is weaker than dissociation. Linear word entropy can permit correlation one, as can repeated copies of one character with only one bit of word entropy.

## Possible applications

Controlling averages of dependent observables when their sequence has few likely names.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
