# Interpolate a fixed number of moving points away from a prescribed subscheme

[Family 046](../families/openai-math-046.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000423.md) · [Batch B069](../../batches/B069/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let W be projective, F a closed subscheme, and L very ample. If I_F tensor L^m0 is globally generated, then for every ell distinct points outside F and every m>=m0+ell-1 the evaluation map H^0(W,I_F tensor L^m)->direct sum_i L^m|p_i is surjective, with one degree bound for all tuples.

For each p_i choose a section a_i of I_F tensor L^m0 nonzero there. Very ampleness supplies, for each j!=i, a section of L zero at p_j and nonzero at p_i. Multiply these ell-1 sections and then multiply by a_i. Their evaluations form an invertible diagonal matrix. Additional powers of L can be supplied by sections nonzero at p_i. No compactness of the moving configuration space is needed.

## Role in the source argument

Supports dimension counts that must hold simultaneously for all fibers away from a fixed embedded subvariety.

## Antecedents and comparison

This is classical very-ample point separation and global generation, with the uniform degree made explicit. No new interpolation theorem is claimed.

## Checks and limitations

Exact multivariate polynomials for many rational point configurations produce diagonal evaluation matrices with the predicted degree.

These interpolation checks do not establish the source complete-intersection construction, largeness or non-Steinness.

## Possible applications

Replacing a pointwise sufficiently-large degree by one degree for all tuples of a fixed size.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
