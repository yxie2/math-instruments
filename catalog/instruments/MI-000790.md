# Keep the arclength commutator when deriving a warping-slope barrier

[Family 351](../families/openai-math-351.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000522.md) · [Batch B086](../../batches/B086/README.md)

## Mathematical content

For a smooth doubly warped metric ds^2+phi^2 g_(S^p)+r^2 g_(S^q) under Ricci flow, time differentiation at a fixed manifold point obeys [partial_t,partial_s]=lambda_0 partial_s, with lambda_0=-p phi_ss/phi-q r_ss/r. Differentiating the warping equation WITH this commutator gives
partial_t r_s=(r_s)_ss+(p phi_s/phi+(q-2)r_s/r)(r_s)_s+[(q-1)(1-r_s^2)/r^2-p phi_s^2/phi^2]r_s.
For p,q>1, the reaction points inward at a positive maximum above one or a negative minimum below minus one. With the stated smooth pole values r_s=+/-1 and phi_s=0, offending extrema are interior, so the slopes remain bounded by their initial suprema and one. The scalar lower bound follows separately from (partial_t-Delta)R=2|Ric|^2.

For a scalar F(r,t), the geometric composition identity is (partial_t-Delta)F=F_t at fixed r-r_s^2 F_rr-(q-1+r_s^2)F_r/r. It remains valid across the equator even though using r as a coordinate requires r_s nonzero. Omitting the commutator or confusing the two time derivatives changes the equation.

## Role in the source argument

Deriving barriers in moving geometric coordinates without importing an incorrect fixed-coordinate evolution equation.

## Antecedents and comparison

These are classical doubly warped Ricci-flow identities and maximum principles. The source uses them as preliminary bounds; no scalar upper bound follows from this step.

## Checks and limitations

Symbolic differentiation verifies the commutator-dependent slope equation and the scalar composition formula. Random positive warping data test the reaction signs outside the barrier; omission of the commutator fails the identity.

The computations do not construct a Ricci flow or control the full curvature. The source pole conditions and smooth compact slabs remain assumptions.

## Possible applications

Deriving barriers in moving geometric coordinates without importing an incorrect fixed-coordinate evolution equation.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
