# Use stabilizer weights to obtain a finite invariant trace

[Family 214](../families/openai-math-214.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000286.md) · [Batch B051](../../batches/B051/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let a unimodular locally compact automorphism group act with finitely many vertex orbits and compact open stabilizers. For representatives o_i put sigma_i proportional to vol(Gamma_(o_i))^(-1), normalized to sum one. For every invariant nonnegative pair mass m, sum_i sigma_i sum_y m(o_i,y)=sum_i sigma_i sum_x m(x,o_i). On each pair orbit, outgoing and incoming counts are stabilizer volumes divided by the common intersection volume, so weighting cancels the numerator. Tonelli handles infinite nonnegative sums; absolute summability handles signed or complex masses.

For bounded invariant operators on counting-measure l2(V), define tr S=sum_i sigma_i S(o_i,o_i). This is a positive normalized trace. Cauchy-Schwarz makes the product diagonal sum absolutely convergent, and mass transport yields tr(AB)=tr(BA). For invariant A>=0 and self-adjoint B, positivity of A^(1/2)BA^(1/2) between -||B||A and ||B||A gives |tr(BA)|<=||B|| tr A. Also A(x,x)<=sigma_i^(-1)tr A on type i.

The weights specify the random root type. They do not replace counting measure in adjoints, positivity or operator norms. Equal weighting of unequal types generally destroys the trace identity.

## Role in the source argument

Provides averaged positive quantities and cyclic operator identities across different vertex types in the source critical-bound argument.

## Antecedents and comparison

[Aldous-Lyons, Processes on Unimodular Random Networks](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0603062), Section 5, pages 27-28 in the retrieved version, gives this trace and the mass-transport proof of cyclicity; those passages were read. The source specializes the construction to finitely many orbit types. This is an identified antecedent, not a new trace theorem.

## Checks and limitations

Exact finite permutation-group models with unequal orbit sizes check the stabilizer weights, mass transport, trace cyclicity and diagonal bounds. An explicit unequal-type example fails cyclicity under equal type weights.

Finite group models do not prove the infinite-graph mass-transport theorem or the source critical operator bound.

## Possible applications

Using finite trace estimates on infinite invariant operator algebras with several geometric vertex types.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
