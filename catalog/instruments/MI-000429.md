# Select two prescribed diagonal-shift exponents with linear permanent overhead

[Family 108](../families/openai-math-108.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000233.md) · [Batch B040](../../batches/B040/README.md)

## Mathematical content

For k>=1, 0<=q<=floor(k/2), r=k-2q and D=diag(theta_i), put G_D(X)=[s^q t^q] per_k(X+sI+tD). Its subset formula sums (product_(i in T) theta_i) per(X_(C,C)) over disjoint q-sets S,T, where C is their complement. No extra factorial appears in this expansion.

A count gate with k original and h=k-q additional vertices on each side forces exactly q distinguished diagonal edges. Each prescribed q-set has (h!)^2 completions. Use two such gates, one for each color, and 2k signed equality gadgets to synchronize their distinguished edges with the main diagonal choices. The total matrix order is k+2(2k-q)+6k=11k-2q, and

$$\operatorname{per}(A(X))=(-2)^{2k}((k-q)!)^4 G_D(X).$$

Identity padding reaches any larger order m. Homogenizing every constant entry gives per B(X,z)=a_0 z^(m-r)G_D(X). Over C, a_0 is nonzero, and any fixed affine substitution Lambda gives bdc(G_D composed with Lambda)<=bdc(per_m): substitute in an approximating determinant sequence and rescale one row. No bound on approximating matrix entries is required.

The integral coefficient formula specializes to any field, but the projection scalar can vanish in positive characteristic, in particular characteristic two.

## Role in the source argument

Transfers an obstruction for a selected coefficient polynomial to a permanent of linearly larger order.

## Antecedents and comparison

Classical permanent-projection methodology, with an explicit source assembly and normalization. The targeted comparison with [Dell et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1206.1775) establishes a gadget antecedent, not novelty of this exact overhead.

## Checks and limitations

Twelve full weighted-matching computations for k=1,2, through order 22, independently compare the constructed matrix permanent with the subset formula, including zero and repeated diagonal parameters.

No smooth-slice, conductor or cubic complexity lower bound follows from these finite selector checks alone.

## Possible applications

Embedding fixed coefficient extractions in a larger permanent without an interpolation sum.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
