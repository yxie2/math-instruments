# Recognize an integrated divided-difference kernel as random unitary conjugation

[Family 262](../families/openai-math-262.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000308.md) · [Batch B055](../../batches/B055/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let B be Hermitian with eigenvalues z_i, 0<sigma<1 and a>0. Define a Schur multiplier in its eigenbasis by

P_ij=integral_0^1 (1+tau(1-tau)(z_i-z_j)^2/a)^(sigma-1)dtau.

Draw tau uniformly in [0,1], V with gamma shape 1-sigma and unit rate, and then T conditionally Gaussian with variance 2V tau(1-tau)/a. The gamma Laplace transform gives E exp(iTx)=integral_0^1(1+tau(1-tau)x^2/a)^(sigma-1)dtau. Hence P(Z)=E[exp(iTB) Z exp(-iTB)]. In particular the coefficient matrix is positive semidefinite with unit diagonal, and P is unital, trace-preserving, positive and self-adjoint for the Hermitian trace pairing. The same law works for every pair of eigenvalues, including repetitions.

In the source this kernel appears by integrating the divided-difference derivative at Y_s=(sI-B)^2+dI, with a=d+delta. Completing the square in tau(s-z_i)^2+(1-tau)(s-z_j)^2 leaves tau(1-tau)(z_i-z_j)^2; integration over s gives the displayed kernel. This representation permits trace inequalities for individual conjugations to be averaged. It does not say that every positive Schur multiplier is a mixture of this one-parameter conjugation group.

## Role in the source argument

Converts the source integrated tangent operator into an even probability average that can be removed in its specific trace pairing.

## Antecedents and comparison

[Hiai, Matrix Analysis: Matrix Monotone Functions, Matrix Means, and Majorization](https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/iis/16/2/16_2_139/_pdf), Theorem 2.3.1 on printed page 159, states the classical divided-difference derivative formula with attribution to Daleckii-Krein; that statement was read. The gamma integral and Gaussian characteristic function give the averaging representation directly. No new general functional-calculus theorem is claimed.

## Checks and limitations

Deterministic quadrature compares the gamma mixture and displayed kernel, tests positive semidefiniteness with repeated eigenvalues, and checks trace preservation and self-adjointness on noncommuting inputs.

The checks do not verify the source rank-one field constraint or the full Lieb-Thirring conjecture.

## Possible applications

Identifying a matrix multiplier as a concrete probability average so positivity and trace-pairing properties become transparent.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
