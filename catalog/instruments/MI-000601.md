# Bound a sum of free generators by splitting cancellation prefixes

[Family 289](../families/openai-math-289.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000370.md) · [Batch B062](../../batches/B062/README.md)

## Mathematical content

For m distinct free generators s_i and the left regular representation lambda, ||sum_i lambda(s_i)||<=2 sqrt(m). Let F_i project onto reduced words beginning with s_i inverse. These projections are mutually orthogonal. Split lambda(s_i)=A_i+B_i, where A_i=lambda(s_i)(I-F_i) and B_i=lambda(s_i)F_i. The ranges of A_i are orthogonal because their words start with the distinct letters s_i. Thus ||sum A_i xi||²=sum ||(I-F_i)xi||²<=m||xi||². By Cauchy-Schwarz, ||sum B_i xi||<=sqrt(m)(sum ||F_i xi||²)^(1/2)<=sqrt(m)||xi||. Adding gives the bound. The estimate is deliberately nonsharp.

## Role in the source argument

Bounds an averaging operator used in a later representation obstruction.

Finite-support checks do not prove the later separable C-star construction or its distance estimates.
