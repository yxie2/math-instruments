# Transfer both coordinates of a support through a quadratic density transform

[Family 228](../families/openai-math-228.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000496.md) · [Batch B079](../../batches/B079/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let Q(beta,h) be finite convex and H(beta,d)=sup_h(hd-Q(beta,h)). Fix a density d for which H(.,d) is finite concave. If Fenchel equality holds at (beta,z,d), then
{e:(e,d) in partial Q(beta,z)}=-partial_beta^+ H(beta,d).
The forward inclusion follows by taking the supremum in h in the joint supporting inequality. The reverse follows from Q(beta',h')>=h'd-H(beta',d) and the supergradient inequality.

For a>0 set F(beta,h)=sup_(d>=0)[hd-H(beta,d)+beta a d^2/2]. Suppose d attains this supremum and the stated conjugacy and convexity hypotheses hold. Set z=h+beta a d. Comparing with any d'>=0 gives
H(beta,d')>=H(beta,d)+z(d'-d)+beta a(d'-d)^2/2.
Thus z supports H in density. For every e in -partial_beta^+ H(beta,d),
(e,d) in partial Q(beta,z), and (e+a d^2/2,d) in partial F(beta,h).
The latter vector supports the fixed-density branch, which touches F at the maximizer. At a differentiability point it is the full gradient. Density convergence alone would not determine its first coordinate.

## Role in the source argument

Tracking energy and density together under mean-field-type pressure transforms.

## Antecedents and comparison

This is a direct application of Fenchel equality and supporting branches. Exact priority of the source formulation is not established; no new general conjugacy theorem is claimed.

## Checks and limitations

Quadratic density costs with affine temperature dependence permit exact optimization. Checks compare full gradients, the shifted field, and the strengthened density inequality over a grid, including a boundary maximizer at zero.

The checks do not prove the physical hypotheses on H, existence of the continuum pressure, or a common transition temperature.

## Possible applications

Tracking energy and density together under mean-field-type pressure transforms.
