# Lexicographic derivative extrema turn crossing sheets into a packing obstruction

[Family 098](../families/openai-math-098.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000088.md) · [Batch B003](../../batches/B003/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Under a proposed bi-Lipschitz embedding of the sheet space into $\mathbb R^k$ with lower constant 1 and upper constant $D$, collect all good-point derivative pairs $(|F_x|^2,|F_y|^2)$ in one compact closure $K\subset[0,D^2]^2$. Let $A$ maximize the first coordinate, and $B$ maximize the second on the first-coordinate face $s=A$. Compactness gives

$$\mathbb E(A-s_n)\to0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad\limsup\mathbb E t_n\le B$$

for arbitrary probability-space-valued pairs $(s_n,t_n)\in K$. No quantitative modulus for the second extremum is needed.

Choose fixed $N>(1+2D)^k$ and recurring aspect ratios $W_j=n$. Near $(A,B)$, the source obtains horizontal and vertical selected-sheet errors

$$n^2\langle|G_H^x-F_x|^2\rangle\to0,\qquad
\langle|G_V^y-F_y|^2\rangle\to0.$$

The horizontal estimate has an explicit $O(n^{-2})$ bound after multiplication by $n^2$; the vertical estimate uses only compactness. On one period block of width $Nnr$ and height $Nr$, line selection and Cauchy–Schwarz make normalized offsets vary by at most $\omega_n=N\sqrt{2N\epsilon_n}\to0$. At cross-color intersections two permitted displacements are separated by $\sqrt2r$. Transport along the selected row and column gives $N$ offsets in a radius-D ball, pairwise separated by at least $\sqrt2-2\omega_n>1$. Volume packing contradicts $N>(1+2D)^k$.

## Role in the source argument

Uses a quantitative first-direction estimate to pay for long horizontal lines and a qualitative second-direction estimate for bounded normalized vertical lengths. The same sheet must be used on both arms of each crossing.

## Antecedents and comparison

Differentiation and separation-versus-stretch arguments precede this source, notably [Laakso](https://doi.org/10.1007/s000390050003). The elementary lexicographic compactness lemma is not presented as a new compactness principle. The candidate instrument is its asymmetric use with recurring crossed sheets and finite-dimensional packing.

## Checks and limitations

Read the two energy estimates and crossing selection in full. Checked the variance expansion, the endpoint errors divided by strip width, the n-scaling and the final packing count.

The compact set K and target parameters must remain fixed as n grows. Independent horizontal and vertical branch maps would not provide the common crossing values. This mechanism is tied to the supplied geometry; it is not a general assertion about all doubling subsets.

## Possible applications

Designing rigidity arguments with several directional energies when only the first direction admits an effective rate, and isolating the geometric compatibility needed for a packing contradiction.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
