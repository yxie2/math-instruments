# Extract a nonnegative variance from a Cartier-divisor lattice filtration

[Family 195](../families/openai-math-195.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000287.md) · [Batch B052](../../batches/B052/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Suppose D=P^2 is an effective Cartier divisor in an integral Noetherian scheme V, with conormal I|D=O_D(1). Let L' be coherent torsion-free of rank N>0 and trivial on V minus D. For F=L'|D define v(F)=2ch2(F)/N-(c1(F)/N)^2. Then v(F)>=0, even though F itself need not be torsion-free.

Clearing denominators and twisting gives I^b O_V^N subset L' subset O_V^N for some b. Twisting preserves v. The quotient Q has finite layers Q_j=I^jQ/I^(j+1)Q, each a quotient of O_D(j)^N. With n_j=rank Q_j, one has 0<=n_j<=N and c1(Q_j)>=j n_j. Derived Cartier restriction gives [F]=[O_D^N]+sum_j([Q_j(1)]-[Q_j]), hence c1(F)=sum n_j and 2ch2(F)>=sum(2j+1)n_j. The key nonnegative identity is

N sum_j(2j+1)n_j-(sum_j n_j)^2 = sum_j n_j(N-n_j)+2 sum_(j>l)n_j(N-n_l).

The source compares this lower bound with finite pushforward f:X->P^2. Writing H=f*O(1) and h=H^2, surface Riemann-Roch, Bogomolov and Hodge index give v(f_*G)<=1/4-[K_X^2-2c2(X)]/(12h) for every torsion-free H-slope-semistable G. A slope-width-one filtration adds at most 1/4 by the variance-mixture identity. If all extension hypotheses hold, the resulting necessary condition is K_X^2-2c2(X)<=6h. The calculation is an obstruction interface; it does not by itself furnish a surface or a counterexample ring.

## Role in the source argument

Turns agreement with a trivial bundle off the exceptional divisor into the source lower bound that conflicts with its proposed surface geometry.

## Antecedents and comparison

The source proves the displayed layer inequality directly using standard Cartier restriction and Chern characters. Its upper comparison explicitly invokes classical surface Riemann-Roch, Bogomolov and Hodge index. The selected proof and algebraic completion of squares were read; a targeted comparison does not establish priority for the combined interface.

## Checks and limitations

Exact bounded integer layer sequences verify the nonnegative identity, including equality cases. Symbolic computation checks the Riemann-Roch coefficient comparison and completion of squares; weighted rational examples check the 1/4 mixture allowance.

These algebraic checks do not establish the geometric prerequisites or the source headline nonexistence theorem. The linked formal target does not state that theorem.

## Possible applications

Deriving numerical obstructions from extensions across a negative divisor when a bundle is trivial on the complement.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
