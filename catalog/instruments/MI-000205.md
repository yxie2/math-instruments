# Fresh occurrence randomness yields product equations with an explicit collision coupling

[Family 188](../families/openai-math-188.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000123.md) · [Batch B013](../../batches/B013/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Unfold the paired-query rule into a deterministic tree of all possible calls and give every candidate occurrence a fresh independent uniform priority. Different root trees are independent. If each list has at most M entries, a specified descending path of length j has probability 1/j!, so the probability that one exists is at most M^j/j!. Finite branching implies an almost surely finite dependency tree, simultaneously for all thresholds by using threshold one and taking a countable intersection over possible nodes. Finite-depth outputs stabilize measurably.

Let q_e(t) be singleton survival in this auxiliary model and q_(e,T)(t) survival when T is omitted only at that root. For T={e,f,g}, put I_(e,T)(t)=integral_0^t q_(f,T)(v)q_(g,T)(v) dv. Candidate sets at f and g become disjoint after T is omitted, and their descendant occurrence variables are independent. Therefore

$$q_e(t)=\prod_{T\ni e}(1-I_{e,T}(t)),\qquad
q_{e,T}(t)=\prod_{S\ni e,\ S\ne T}(1-I_{e,S}(t)).$$

Pair-root survival is q_(f,T)q_(g,T). Bounded measurable probabilities first make I Lipschitz; the finite products then make q continuous and finally C1. No unproved fixed-point selection is needed because the occurrence process constructs the solution.

For the source's one or two independently sampled roots, let C be the event that some triangle type repeats among all exposed candidate lists of visited calls. All candidates are exposed before sorting, including those above threshold or skipped by early stopping. Couple first exposure of each type to its finite-process priority and later exposures to fresh variables. Deferred decisions give the independent law, and the two executions agree until a repeated type is exposed. Thus total variation of their answer vectors is at most Pr_ind(C). The same proof works for any fixed finite root list chosen independently of priorities.

Separates exact auxiliary factorization from the separate task of bounding collisions in the actual finite process.

## Antecedents and comparison

Independent backward branching appears in [Spencer](https://cs.nyu.edu/~spencer/papers/asympacking.pdf), and decreasing-time finiteness in [Penrose and Sudbury, Proposition 1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0503519v1). The selected package specifies which exposures the collision event must include; these classical principles are not new claims.

## Checks and limitations

Read the product and deferred-decision proofs. Exact recursion on a two-triangle graph checks the auxiliary formulas, and seeded coupled executions check pathwise agreement whenever all exposed types are distinct. Larger collision probabilities and terminal asymptotics are not estimated.

The products are not finite-graph survival identities. A collision estimate counting only traversed candidates would omit information used to sort the lists. Almost-sure finite evaluation is not a uniform deterministic depth bound.

## Possible applications

Comparing dependent finite explorations with independent recursive models while explicitly accounting for adaptive exposure.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
