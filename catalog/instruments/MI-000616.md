# Translate conformal derivative integrability into a Sobolev pullback bound

[Family 072](../families/openai-math-072.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000385.md) · [Batch B064](../../batches/B064/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let phi:Omega->D be a conformal bijection and suppose integral_Omega |phi'|^s is finite. For p>2 and s in (4/3,4), set q=ps/(p+s-2), so 1<q<p and s=q(p-2)/(p-q). Then

||grad(u composed with phi)||_(L^q(Omega)) <= ||grad u||_(L^p(D)) (integral_Omega |phi'|^s)^(1/q-1/p).

Indeed, write the q-th power integrand as (|grad u|^p composed with phi times |phi'|²)^(q/p) times |phi'|^(q-2q/p), apply Holder, and change variables in the first integral. Local approximation extends the calculation to the homogeneous Sobolev space. For p=infinity, q=s and the direct chain rule gives the factor (integral |phi'|^s)^(1/s). Constants depend on the map and exponents. The source obtains the integrability input from its much stronger Brennan theorem; this extraction is only the conditional composition implication.

## Role in the source argument

Turns a derivative-integrability statement into a concrete function-space mapping property.

## Antecedents and comparison

[Goldshtein and Ukhlov, Brennan's conjecture for composition operators on Sobolev spaces](https://arxiv.org/abs/1212.3999), Composition and Equivalence Theorems and following Remark 2, gives this exponent relation and the infinity case. Pages 4-6 were read, including the equivalence proof. The catalogue treats this as a known implication, not a new result.

## Checks and limitations

Exact exponent arithmetic and quadrature for disk automorphisms with polynomial test functions check the chain rule, change of variables and Holder bound.

Smooth automorphisms do not prove the derivative-integrability input for arbitrary simply connected domains.

## Possible applications

Converting conformal integrability information into controlled Sobolev composition operators.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
