# Separate task starts, raw matches and reused arrays in prefix recomputation

[Family 138](../families/openai-math-138.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000472.md) · [Batch B075](../../batches/B075/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Raw tuple occurrences on each side have one bucket among q and a binary top mask of length m; a valid occurrence yields a distinct entry keyed by exact weight and mask. Equal entry keys must have the same bucket. Let J_L,J_R count raw occurrences, including repetitions and invalid overlaps, and U_L,U_R bound all distinct valid entries.

A dictionary has capacity C. Left overflow skips its bucket. A nonempty completed left array is held while right tasks are explored depth first: overflow splits on the next mask bit, while overflow at a full mask is dropped. Compatible masks must be selected before raw tuple generation. At a fixed depth, right tasks have disjoint possible entries, and every overflow witnesses more than C entries. Hence the total number of tasks is at most 2q+2(m+1)U_R/C.

If a task with Z potential occurrences costs at most H+KZ, total generation work is at most H[2q+2(m+1)U_R/C]+K[J_L+(m+1)J_R]. Each right occurrence belongs to only one prefix at each depth; multiplying raw processing by the total task count would lose this benefit. Completed right arrays are disjoint and contribute at most U_R to final checks. Root checks reuse left entries at most U_L times in aggregate; nonroot tasks contribute at most 2(m+1)U_R in repeated left-array lengths. Total check-input length is therefore at most U_L+(2m+3)U_R.

## Role in the source argument

Makes a depth-first regeneration schedule compatible with small dictionaries without hiding repeated-work costs.

## Antecedents and comparison

Prefix-tree charging and distinct-versus-occurrence accounting are established techniques. The source gives three separate bounds for its asymmetric left-skip/right-refine schedule; exact priority of this formulation is not established.

## Checks and limitations

Simulated capped dictionaries count tasks, raw visits and every reused check array, including invalid duplicates and full-mask overflow. A late-filter control shows why depth charging needs early filtering.

The tests do not establish the source probabilistic resource bounds or complete Subset Sum algorithm.

## Possible applications

Auditing low-memory algorithms that trade storage for repeated generation.
