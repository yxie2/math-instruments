# Compress a cover after independent Bernoulli smoothing

[Family 175](../families/openai-math-175.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000349.md) · [Batch B060](../../batches/B060/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let R be a nonempty family of subsets of a finite ground set, each of size between 1 and s, and let 0<r<1. There is a nonempty Q contained in R, of size at most ceil(2r^(-s)), such that for every S containing a member of R, independent Bernoulli-r noise Z satisfies

Pr(S union Z contains a member of Q)>=1/2.

Starting with Q empty, add a witness T in R whenever Pr(Z contains a member of Q | Z contains T)<1/2. Adding T increases the unconditional success probability by more than r^|T|/2 >= r^s/2. Thus fewer than 2r^(-s) additions are possible, no witness can repeat, and the process stops. Conditioning Z to contain T has the same law as T union fresh Bernoulli noise. At termination every witness has smoothed success at least 1/2, and monotonicity transfers this to every S containing a witness. This conclusion concerns success after adding noise; it does not say that Q deterministically covers every such S.

## Role in the source argument

Reduces the number of tests needed for the source smoothed transfer argument.

## Antecedents and comparison

The probability-increment proof is an elementary greedy packing argument. The complete source proof was inspected; no priority claim or external novelty certification is made for this formulation.

## Checks and limitations

Thirty-six finite families are checked with exact rational product probabilities, verifying each increment, stopping bound and every relevant superset.

The program does not prove the subsequent decomposition or expectation-threshold transfer.

## Possible applications

Replacing a large witness family by a bounded subfamily when success is measured after independent noise.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
