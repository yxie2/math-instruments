# Compare Radon masses to Haar volumes uniformly over translated set shapes

[Family 255](../families/openai-math-255.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000195.md) · [Batch B031](../../batches/B031/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let L be a connected simply connected nilpotent Lie group with Haar measure mu and a left-invariant Riemannian metric. Suppose a Radon measure rho, compact positive-volume sets E_j and positive a_j satisfy K^{-1}a_j<=rho(zE_j)<=Ka_j for all z,j. Then a_j/mu(E_j) is comparable to a_0/mu(E_0), with constants independent of the shapes and diameters of E_j.

For E contained in B(R), Tonelli and unimodularity give

$$\mu(E)\rho(B(T-R))\le\int_{B(T)}\rho(zE)\,d\mu(z)\le\mu(E)\rho(B(T+R)).$$

First apply this to E_0 to obtain (KD_0)^{-1}q_0 mu(B(T))<=rho(B(T))<=KD_0q_0 mu(B(T)), where q_0=a_0/mu(E_0) and D_0 is a large-scale doubling constant. For each E_j choose T sufficiently large relative to its diameter and use the displayed bounds again. This gives (K^2D_0^2)^{-1}q_0<=a_j/mu(E_j)<=K^2D_0^2q_0. The radius depends on j; the final constants do not. Absolute continuity of rho is unnecessary.

## Role in the source argument

Turns uniform masses of boundary-image translates into bounded differences of logarithmic height characters.

## Antecedents and comparison

The proof is classical invariant integration plus doubling. The reusable point is uniformity across arbitrary compact shapes and allowance of singular Radon measures.

## Checks and limitations

One hundred twenty finite Tonelli identities and 124 singular counting-measure interval examples are checked.

These models do not construct the source boundary homeomorphisms or establish their translate-mass assumptions.

## Possible applications

Comparing geometric volume growth when the transported measure has no known density.
