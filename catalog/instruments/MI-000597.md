# Absorb a projective representation by multiplying the regular cocycle

[Family 286](../families/openai-math-286.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000366.md) · [Batch B062](../../batches/B062/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let alpha and nu be scalar unitary 2-cocycles on a countable group L, and let v_h v_g=nu(h,g)v_(hg) be a projective unitary representation on V. For the twisted left regular action lambda_alpha(h)delta_g=alpha(h,g)delta_(hg), the diagonal unitary T(delta_g tensor xi)=delta_g tensor v_g*xi satisfies

T(lambda_alpha(h) tensor v_h)T*=lambda_(alpha nu)(h) tensor I.

Indeed, its coefficient on delta_g tensor xi is alpha(h,g)v_(hg)*v_h v_g xi=alpha(h,g)nu(h,g)xi. If b commutes with every v_g, T also fixes I tensor b under conjugation. No ICC hypothesis or finite dimension is needed for this identity. The source then uses finite-dimensional V and the finite-factor module embedding criterion, under ICC, to transfer finiteness and normality of modules. That module criterion is a separate classical input.

## Role in the source argument

Changes the twisting cocycle while retaining specified commuting endomorphisms.

## Antecedents and comparison

This is the projective form of classical Fell absorption. The source gives the complete diagonal-unitary calculation. No earliest-priority or arithmetic novelty claim is made.

## Checks and limitations

Weyl projective representations of finite abelian groups check both nontrivial cocycles, the conjugation identity and a commuting ancillary action.

Finite abelian models test the identity only; they are not ICC examples and do not verify the source module classification.

## Possible applications

Removing projective coefficients from a regular representation without losing its commuting coefficient algebra.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
