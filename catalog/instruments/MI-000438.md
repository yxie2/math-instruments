# Choose a homology projection preserving a subspace that is not a subcomplex

[Family 320](../families/openai-math-320.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000238.md) · [Batch B041](../../batches/B041/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let (H,delta) be a finite-dimensional graded complex over a field and K a graded subspace, not necessarily stable under delta. There exists a chain projection P, homotopic to identity, whose image consists of cycle representatives mapping isomorphically onto homology, and with P(K) contained in K intersect ker(delta).

Write Z=ker(delta), B=im(delta). Choose A_0 complementing K intersect B in K intersect Z, then extend to a complement A of B in Z. Choose W_0 complementing K intersect Z in K, then extend it to a complement W of Z in H. All choices are degreewise. In H=B direct-sum A direct-sum W, projection onto A maps K into A_0 and is a chain map.

The inverse of delta:W -> B, extended by zero on A and W, gives s with identity-P=delta s+s delta. Tensoring such projections over a field preserves the homology class of a cycle; telescoping gives the tensor homotopy with the graded signs. This can turn a sum with designated kernel factors into cycle representatives retaining those factors.

## Role in the source argument

Retains kernel factors when passing through the source second homology, although those kernels need not form subcomplexes.

## Antecedents and comparison

Elementary splitting of finite complexes over a field, with complements chosen relative to one subspace. No first-priority claim.

## Checks and limitations

Fifty exact graded complexes verify projection, chain-map, homotopy and preservation identities. Twenty-six chosen subspaces are not subcomplexes.

No canonical or simultaneous projection for an arbitrary collection of subspaces is asserted. The source tensor and manifold conclusions require additional inputs.

## Possible applications

Selecting homology representatives compatible with one algebraic kernel constraint.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
