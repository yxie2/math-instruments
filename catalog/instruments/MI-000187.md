# An exponentially rare residual branch corrects a rational approximate sampler exactly

[Family 131](../families/openai-math-131.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000114.md) · [Batch B010](../../batches/B010/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let p_i=a_i/Q be a rational probability law on M states, with integer a_i>=0 and sum a_i=Q. For delta=2^(-D), suppose TV(p,uniform)<=delta^2 and M delta<=1. Then (1-delta)p_i<=1/M, so

$$r_i=\frac{1/M-(1-\delta)p_i}{\delta}\ge0,\qquad
\operatorname{Unif}=(1-\delta)p+\delta r.$$

The residual law has nonnegative integer weights w_i=2^D Q-M(2^D-1)a_i, whose sum is MQ. With probability 1-delta use the cheap sampler p. Only in the delta branch enumerate the states, compute M and the exact a_i, and sample an integer-weighted residual. Exact uniform integers can be generated by rejection from unbiased bits. The usual branch need not know M, provided an upper bound supplies the accuracy condition in advance.

For the source switch chain, put B=binom(n,2), q0=12 binom(n,4), D=4B+10, gamma0=1/[24n^2 binom(n,4)] and t=ceil(gamma0^(-1)(B+2D+2)). The claimed gap bound gives the needed accuracy for p=P^t from a fixed realization. The matrix q0 P has integer entries; propagating integer counts for t steps supplies Q=q0^t. State enumeration and exact tabulation cost at most 2^(2B) times a polynomial in n, so multiplication by delta controls their expected contribution.

The output is exactly uniform by the mixture identity. Running time is polynomial in expectation under the stated mixing and tabulation bounds, and termination is almost sure. The rare exhaustive branch and rejection loops have no asserted worst-case polynomial bound.

## Role in the source argument

Converts the source approximate switch sampler into exact uniform sampling without paying the full enumeration cost on ordinary runs.

## Antecedents and comparison

This is an explicit rational implementation of the established [Gobel-Liu-Manurangsi-Pappik reduction, Algorithm 1 and Section 3.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.00882v2). The rare correction principle is not new; the selected value is its concrete integer weights and cost accounting for this chain.

## Checks and limitations

Exact rational finite Markov laws check domination, integer residual weights, their sum and the exact mixture identity. A deliberately insufficient accuracy example produces a negative residual, showing why an unquantified approximate sampler does not suffice.

The reduction is conditional on a sufficiently accurate sampler and exact law tabulation. It does not independently prove the source mixing theorem. It is a theoretical expected-time construction and can be impractical.

## Possible applications

Exact simulation from rational rapidly mixing chains when exponentially costly exact law computation can be confined to a sufficiently rare branch.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
