# Concentrate an edge cochain by subtracting three prefix gradients

[Family 243](../families/openai-math-243.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000341.md) · [Batch B059](../../batches/B059/README.md)

## Mathematical content

On the positively oriented L by L by L grid, L>=2, let ell be an F3-valued edge cochain and let t count faces with nonzero oriented curl. A vertex potential phi can be chosen so that r=ell-partial^T phi has at most 2Lt nonzero edges; the displayed proof actually yields Lt. Subtracting a gradient preserves the functional ell(k) on every divergence-free flow k.

First sum direction-1 entries from coordinate zero to i-1 to form phi_1. The residual has direction-1 entries zero, and its direction-2 and direction-3 increments in i are exactly the 12 and 13 curls. Choose a slice i_0 with at most t_23/L nonzero 23 curls. Sum the direction-2 entries on that slice to form a potential phi_2 independent of i; this makes direction-2 entries on the slice zero. Sum the remaining direction-3 entries along j=0 to form phi_3 independent of i,j.

On the chosen slice, each nonzero direction-3 entry must occur after a nonzero 23 curl on its line, costing at most L positions per curl. Copying these values across L slices costs at most L^2(t_23/L)=Lt_23 entries. Moving away from the slice creates at most Lt_12 and Lt_13 additional entries. Direction 1 remains zero, so total support is at most Lt. If t=0, the residual is zero, proving that every curl-free cochain on this box is a gradient.

## Role in the source argument

Converts few nonzero commutator tests into a short set of edge coordinates supporting a central-flow functional.

## Antecedents and comparison

Successive gauge fixing and exactness of curl-free cochains on a contractible box are classical. The selected proof makes the support cost explicit and provides the slightly stronger intermediate Lt bound. This extraction does not assert earliest priority for that quantitative implementation.

## Checks and limitations

80 cochains on boxes of side 2,3,4,6 include 20 gradient controls. Exact F3 arithmetic verifies unchanged face curls, zero first-direction entries and the stronger support bound Lt.

The program does not construct all hereditarily finite objects or establish the small-orbit support and logic-transfer theorems.

## Possible applications

Finding sparse representatives of linear functionals on cycle spaces when only a few local curls are nonzero.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
