# Upgrade a small bad fraction to a uniform subset-sum lower bound

[Family 092](../families/openai-math-092.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000263.md) · [Batch B048](../../batches/B048/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let H be a finite abelian group. Average a nonnegative g over k independent uniform subset-sum shifts: A(x)=2^(-k) sum_epsilon g(x-sum epsilon_i w_i). Averaging also over a uniform x, distinct bit labels give independent uniform arguments, since their difference contains some shift with coefficient +1 or -1. Thus E_(w,x)(A(x)-gbar)^2=2^(-k)(E g^2-gbar^2), for every finite abelian group.

Suppose the first-stage good set G={A>=(1-delta)gbar} has complement fraction beta<=exp(-2 theta). Add j fresh shifts and let N_j(x) count the good bit labels, with multiplicity. Then N_(j+1)(x)=N_j(x)+N_j(x-w). For Phi_j=E_x exp(-theta N_j(x)), conditional on the past,

E_w Phi_(j+1)=Phi_j^2,

by the bijection (x,w)->(x,x-w). Conditional Markov bounds the chance that log Phi_(j+1)>2 log Phi_j+theta delta by exp(-theta delta). Outside j such failures, log Phi_j<=2^j(-theta+1)+(2^j-1)theta delta. Since each x contributes at least |H|^(-1)exp(-theta N_j(x)), this gives simultaneously

N_j(x)/2^j>=1-delta-1/theta-log|H|/(theta 2^j).

Combining this with the first-stage lower bound and nonnegativity gives a uniform weighted average. The source chooses log|H|<=b^4, g<=exp(b^.65), gbar in [1/2,2], b^.69<=s<=b, delta=b^(-.1), k=floor(s/2) and theta=(log 2)s/16. For sufficiently large absolute b it obtains a (1-3delta)gbar lower bound at every x, with failure at most exp(-(log 2)b^.69/8)+b exp(-(log 2)b^.59/16).

Once the all-x event holds, an offset may be selected using other information. The bit labels are not independent samples; the potential identity does not require that false assumption.

## Role in the source argument

Lets later lattice blocks use earlier random blocks at base points that depend on other choices.

## Antecedents and comparison

[Li-Liu, Theorem 4.1](https://github.com/xliu2022/xliu2022.github.io/blob/4b360838cb034784293bb6ba2128483fcec6e3ce/Unpolished_Manuscripts/Two-Stage%20Binary%20Correction%20for%20Nearly%20Linear%20Lattice%20Coverings.pdf) uses exactly the potential-square identity and greedy shifts for a dense set; that full proof was read. The source gives a weighted, high-probability version using random shifts and a preliminary second moment. No exhaustive novelty claim for this refinement.

## Checks and limitations

Exact enumeration on small cyclic groups verifies the second-moment formula and potential square, including even-order groups and colliding subset sums. Exponential values are represented by a rational base to avoid floating-point ambiguity.

Finite checks do not validate the source asymptotic threshold b0 or the lattice-covering construction. Uniformity requires fresh independent shifts and counts labels with multiplicity.

## Possible applications

Replacing average-case coverage by simultaneous coverage of every base point without a direct union bound over the group.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
