# Planted posterior resampling gives recoverable fragments and outside-coordinate control

[Family 176](../families/openai-math-176.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000132.md) · [Batch B015](../../batches/B015/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let a finite indexed family A_b subset X have positive weights nu_b summing to one, |A_b|<=m with m>=1, and Pr(J subset A_b)<=a^|J| for every nonempty J. Empty and repeated labels are allowed. Draw source c from nu and independent W from product Bernoulli(rho), 0<rho<1. Set Y=W union A_c and draw target b with probability

$$\frac{\nu_b\rho^{-|A_b|}\mathbf1_{A_b\subseteq Y}}{Z(Y)},\qquad Z(Y)=\sum_d\nu_d\rho^{-|A_d|}\mathbf1_{A_d\subseteq Y}.$$

Keep T=A_b minus W. Then T subset A_c intersection A_b and A_b subset W union T. The law of Y has density Z relative to Bernoulli(rho), and the unconditional target has law nu. At a specified W only the source is guaranteed to retain that law.

If a/rho<=exp(-50), the event Z(Y)<exp(-10m) or |T|>m/2 has probability at most exp(-9m). For fixed b and nonnegative f depending only on W outside A_b,

$$\mathbb E[\mathbf1_{\{\text{target}=b,\ Z(Y)\ge e^{-10m}\}}f(W)]\le e^{11m}\nu_b\mathbb E f(W).$$

The proof gives a useful general intermediate bound for any gamma>0: failure of Z>=gamma or |T|<=m/2 has probability at most gamma+gamma^(-1) sum_(r>m/2) binom(m,r)(a/rho)^r, and the outside-test factor is gamma^(-1)(1+a/rho)^m. These follow from Pr(|A_c intersection A_b|=r)<=binom(m,r)a^r and independence between outside coordinates and the forced inclusion A_b minus A_c subset W.

## Role in the source argument

Shrinks a target to a fragment that remains spread by comparison with the source, while bounding reweighting of later tests outside the target.

## Antecedents and comparison

The planting and posterior transition are already in [Mossel et al., Sections 2.1-2.3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2209.11347v2), a preprint later published as A Bayesian proof of the spread lemma. The extracted outside-coordinate domination and capacity-dependent cutoff are the release source's stated refinements.

## Checks and limitations

Read the density calculation and intersection sum. Exact finite enumeration checks posterior marginals, both fragment inclusions, cutoff bounds and outside tests, including duplicate and empty labels and a very sparse law meeting the exp(-50) assumption.

The target is not generally prior-distributed conditional on W. The outside-test condition is essential to the displayed factorization. The local lemma alone does not prove an entire dependent tree can be covered.

## Possible applications

Posterior-driven fragment reductions that leave a recoverable target and quantified distortion of disjoint-coordinate events.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
