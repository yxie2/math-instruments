# Average a square-root Hessian cost on a fixed support

[Family 082](../families/openai-math-082.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000447.md) · [Batch B072](../../batches/B072/README.md)

## Mathematical content

For Phi(T)=tr(T^(3/2)) and supported Hermitian K, write b_T(K)=(3/2)<K,(L_sqrt(T)+R_sqrt(T))^-1 K>. The inverse acts on the support. In a positive eigenbasis, b_T(K)=(3/2)sum |K_ij|^2/(sqrt(t_i)+sqrt(t_j)), the Hessian of Phi.

Suppose T_omega have the same support E almost everywhere, K_omega are supported Hermitian directions and their operator norms are integrable over a probability space. Their averages have the same support and b_(average T)(average K)<=average b_T(K), allowing an infinite right side.

Put A=average sqrt(T_omega). Vector Cauchy-Schwarz gives A^2<=average T; square-root order then gives A<=sqrt(average T). For a positive definite operator M, <K,M^-1 K>=sup_X[2<K,X>-<X,MX>]. Apply this on Hermitian matrices to M=L_sqrt(T)+R_sqrt(T), use the preceding order, and move the supremum outside the average in the bounding direction. No uniform lower bound on the positive eigenvalues is required.

## Role in the source argument

Allows probability mixtures of Gaussian-window Grams without a condition-number penalty.

## Antecedents and comparison

[Hansen and Zhang, arXiv:1402.2118v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/1402.2118v3), Theorems 1.2 and 1.3 and the power examples in the introduction, place this positive-definite Hessian convexity in the established matrix entropy class. Those statements were read. The source gives a direct square-root proof and a fixed-support integrable-average formulation.

## Checks and limitations

Random complex Hermitian examples compare the Sylvester and eigenbasis Hessians, finite mixtures, support-preserving zero extensions and large eigenvalue ratios.

Finite matrices do not justify the source analytic heat estimates or certify the triangular transform theorem.

## Possible applications

Preserving curvature control while averaging positive matrices and their directions together.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
