# Certify positivity between rational knots using an upper curvature bound

[Family 354](../families/openai-math-354.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000190.md) · [Batch B029](../../batches/B029/README.md)

## Mathematical content

If f has upper second derivative bound K on [a,b], then f-Kh^2/2 is concave and

$$f(h)\ge\frac{b-h}{b-a}f(a)+\frac{h-a}{b-a}f(b)-\frac K2(h-a)(b-h)\ge\min(f(a),f(b))-\frac{K(b-a)^2}{8}.$$

Thus endpoint lower bounds alone are insufficient; a quantified curvature loss certifies the whole interval. The source has 24 rational knots from 0.64 to 2.25. With b_h,s_h its tabulated integer lower bounds, chi=1 for intervals contained in [1.02,1.62] and zero otherwise, it uses

$$M_{a,b}=\min_{h=a,b}\left\{\frac{b_h+\chi s_h}{10^5}-E(h)\right\}-\frac{K(b-a)^2}{8},$$

where K=2.4 inside and 2/a^2 outside, E(h)=.008 max(D_+/h-b_-,0)^4, D_+=.989743318611, b_-=1-1/pi_-, and pi_-=3.14159265358979. Exact rational arithmetic verifies all 23 displayed interval margins. The smallest is 9/100000, on [1.42,1.46] and [1.46,1.50].

The arithmetic prescription uses fixed denominator 10^40, outward-rounded products and reciprocals, and integer square-root comparisons. An alternating arctangent series with Machin's formula proves the input pi enclosure. These operations remain valid for dependent inputs, although enclosures may widen.

This extraction checks the interpolation table conditional on the source knot lower bounds and upper second-derivative estimates. It does not independently reproduce the transcendental knot evaluations or prove those analytic curvature estimates.

## Role in the source argument

Extends a finite scalar certificate to the entire parameter range required by the source geometric exclusion.

## Antecedents and comparison

The chord-error estimate and outward arithmetic are classical rigorous-numerics tools. The reusable contribution here is a transparent, exactly reproducible conditional certificate using the source data.

## Checks and limitations

All 23 rational interval claims pass, with exact minimum margin 9/100000. The Machin pi enclosure and 101 outward square-root models are checked with integer and rational arithmetic.

The endpoint and curvature bounds are inputs, not outputs of this script. Consequently the verified table is not a certification of the complete isoperimetric theorem.

## Possible applications

Replacing sampled numerical positivity by a proof covering every parameter value, once endpoint and derivative bounds have been established.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
