# Turn finite-valued gain and bounded bias into pathwise average bounds

[Family 104](../families/openai-math-104.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000459.md) · [Batch B073](../../batches/B073/README.md)

## Mathematical content

A submartingale taking values in a fixed finite set is eventually constant almost surely. After shifting it to be nonnegative, E[(Y_(t+1)-Y_t)^2]<=E[Y_(t+1)^2-Y_t^2]. Telescoping bounds the expected sum of squared jumps. A positive minimum gap between distinct values then bounds the expected number of changes, so that number is finite almost surely. Its limit has expectation at least the starting value.

Bounded martingale differences have vanishing time average almost surely: orthogonality gives variance O(T), Chebyshev at T=j^2 is summable, and bounded increments control the gaps between squares.

For a fixed Max strategy, assume finite-state gains G and biases psi satisfy selected-edge equalities, chance conditional equalities, and at Min vertices G_next>=G_current with w+psi_next-psi_current>=G_current whenever gains agree. Then G(v_t) is a finite-valued submartingale. After stabilization, nonchance residuals R_t=w_t+psi_next-psi_current-G_current are nonnegative, and chance residuals form bounded martingale differences. The identity average(w)=average(G)+average(R)+(psi_start-psi_end)/T gives liminf average(w)>=G_infinity almost surely, hence the expected lower bound G_start. Reversing inequalities gives the Min upper bound. No stationarity of the opponent is needed.

## Role in the source argument

Separates the probabilistic payoff conclusion from the algebraic construction of gains and biases.

## Antecedents and comparison

Finite-valued submartingale convergence, martingale strong laws and potential telescoping are classical. The extracted interface assembles them for the source gain-bias system; it does not establish a new convergence theorem.

## Checks and limitations

Exact finite Markov paths check squared-jump budgets and gain-bias telescoping. Exhaustive coin paths check martingale orthogonality and the variance estimate; these are finite diagnostics, not an almost-sure proof.

Existence and efficient computation of the required gain-bias data remain dependencies.

## Possible applications

Proving robust long-run guarantees from local certificates against history-dependent choices.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
