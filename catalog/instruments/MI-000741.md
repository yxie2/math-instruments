# Integrate nearest-neighbor collision products using directed graph elimination

[Family 230](../families/openai-math-230.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000497.md) · [Batch B079](../../batches/B079/README.md)

## Mathematical content

For 0<alpha<1, b>0 and bounded planar K, set b_i=min(b,min_(j!=i)|x_i-x_j|) and F_b=product_i b_i^(-alpha). Then F_b is integrable on K^m. Its integral with one point fixed is uniformly bounded as that point ranges over any bounded set.

Bound each factor by b^(-alpha)+sum_(j!=i)|x_i-x_j|^(-alpha) and expand. Every term is a directed graph with outdegree at most one and no self-edge. Remove vertices with no incoming edge: a single alpha singularity has a uniformly bounded planar integral. Remaining components are cycles. A cycle vertex has two incident factors; Cauchy-Schwarz bounds its integral using exponent 2alpha<2. Removing it leaves a chain. A two-cycle has the same 2alpha singularity directly.

For the fixed center x_0, translate all variable points into a common ball around zero. Average a simultaneous translation by v in the unit disk. Translation invariance of the deterministic distance kernel bounds the fixed-center integral by an all-variable integral over a slightly larger ball, divided by the unit-disk area. This does not assume translation invariance of any SLE law. At alpha=1 the two-point diagonal already has logarithmically divergent area integral.

## Role in the source argument

Separating collision integration from stochastic hitting estimates and handling a fixed marked point without an invariance assumption on the process.

## Antecedents and comparison

The proof uses classical planar power-kernel integrability, functional directed graphs and translation averaging. No new general integrability theorem is claimed.

## Checks and limitations

Enumerated outdegree-one graphs verify the leaf/cycle decomposition. Radial integrals check the alpha<1 threshold and its divergent endpoint. Finite configurations verify the product expansion and its nearest-neighbor majorization.

These deterministic estimates do not prove the multipoint SLE hitting bound needed to turn them into random mass moments.

## Possible applications

Separating collision integration from stochastic hitting estimates and handling a fixed marked point without an invariance assumption on the process.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
