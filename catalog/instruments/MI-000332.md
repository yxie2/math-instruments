# Use an inverse profile to turn stability into a weighted defect inequality

[Family 375](../families/openai-math-375.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000185.md) · [Batch B028](../../batches/B028/README.md)

## Mathematical content

For an entire solution Delta v=v^3-v with |v|<1, put g(s)=tanh(s/sqrt(2)), U=g^{-1}(v), q=1-v^2, p=grad U, T=Hess U and D=1-|p|^2. Modica's bound gives 0<=D<=1. Direct differentiation yields

$$\Delta U=-\sqrt2 vD,\quad(-\Delta+2\sqrt2 vp\cdot\nabla+2q|p|^2)D=2|T|^2,$$
$$(-\Delta+3v^2-1)q=-q^2D.$$

The defect also has the exact energy interpretation W(v)-|grad v|^2/2=q^2D/4. If D vanishes at one point, the strong minimum principle gives D identically zero, then T=0, so v is a planar transition.

For a stable solution, use q eta as the scalar stability test. Integration by parts gives

$$0\le Q_v(q\eta)=\int q^2|\nabla\eta|^2-q^3D\eta^2.$$

The gradient cross terms are absorbed exactly through the equation for q. This requires no smoothness of the zero set and converts nonplanarity into a nonnegative weighted defect mass.

## Role in the source argument

Provides a scalar test detecting nonplanar transition behavior before geometric surface estimates are available.

## Antecedents and comparison

These equations, the pointwise zero-defect rigidity and the weighted stability inequality occur in [Liu-Luo-Wang-Wei-Wei-Wu, Lemma 2.1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.21680). The source itself cites that antecedent. The calculation is dimension independent; no novelty is assigned to it here.

## Checks and limitations

The defect, energy discrepancy and ground-state-transform identities are verified symbolically.

The identities do not establish global stable classification in dimension seven. Modica, the minimum principle and integration by parts remain analytic inputs.

## Possible applications

Measuring departure from a one-dimensional profile and constructing stability tests before a regular interface is known.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
