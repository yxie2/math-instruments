# Preserve all-prefix guarantees when restarting parity sub-strategies

[Family 104](../families/openai-math-104.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000460.md) · [Batch B073](../../batches/B073/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Assume a finite arena H with |w|<=W has least even priority d, a positional nonnegative-mean-payoff strategy on H, and a winning parity/mean-payoff strategy on R=H minus the Max attractor of the d-priority vertices. Require the R strategy to satisfy, for every epsilon>0, every prefix bound weight>=-epsilon t-C_epsilon; the strategy itself is chosen before epsilon.

In round j, play the positional strategy for j steps, then the R strategy with its initial memory until leaving R, then the attractor strategy to a vertex of priority d. The positional segment has every prefix at least -B, B=(|H|-1)W, by deleting nonnegative cycles. For any delta>0 a round prefix loses at most delta times its length plus K_delta=B+C_delta+(1+|H|)W, including the exit edge.

At time T, at most 1+sqrt(2T) rounds have started. Thus every play prefix has weight at least -delta T-K_delta(1+sqrt(2T)). Put delta=epsilon/2 and bound the square-root term by epsilon T/2+K_delta^2/epsilon. The same strategy satisfies the uniform prefix property with C_epsilon=K_delta+K_delta^2/epsilon. Infinitely many completed rounds visit priority d infinitely often; an infinite unfinished R segment is won by its component strategy.

## Role in the source argument

Strengthens a qualitative strategy combination with a bound stable under repeated invocations.

## Antecedents and comparison

[Bouyer, Markey, Olschewski and Ummels, arXiv:1102.3615v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/1102.3615v2), Lemma 7 and its proof on pages 13-14, already use growing mean-payoff phases with an attractor and a subgame strategy. That full proof was read. The selected source adds an explicit all-prefix budget against arbitrary opponent histories.

## Checks and limitations

Finite paths verify the cycle-deletion budget. Increasing-round examples check every interrupted prefix and the square-root/epsilon estimates; a constant-round negative control retains a negative average.

The checks do not prove the recursive algorithm or its running time.

## Possible applications

Composing long-run strategies when components may restart before their asymptotic guarantee becomes effective.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
