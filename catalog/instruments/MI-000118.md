# A two-block switch with an absorbing flag preserves an entangled Choi corner

[Family 272](../families/openai-math-272.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000082.md) · [Batch B002](../../batches/B002/README.md)

## Mathematical content

For any two PPT maps $\Psi_1,\Psi_2:M_m\to M_m$, define $A_i=\Psi_i^*(I)$, $c_i=(1+\operatorname{Tr}A_i)^{-1}$ and $F_i=I-c_iA_i$. On $\mathbb C^m_0\oplus\mathbb C^m_1\oplus\mathbb C e_\star$, let $W_0,W_1$ be the real coordinate inclusions and $P_\star=e_\star e_\star^*$. For diagonal input blocks $X_{00},X_{11},x_\star$, define

$$\Theta(X)=c_2W_0\Psi_2(X_{11})W_0^*
+c_1W_1\Psi_1(X_{00})W_1^*
+[\operatorname{Tr}(F_1X_{00})+\operatorname{Tr}(F_2X_{11})+x_\star]P_\star.$$

Off-diagonal input blocks are discarded. Since $F_i\ge c_iI>0$, this is a trace-preserving PPT channel on dimension $2m+1$. Its flag is absorbing, and

$$(W_0^*\otimes W_0^*)J(\Theta^2)(W_0\otimes W_0)
=c_1c_2J(\Psi_2\circ\Psi_1).$$

Thus a nonseparable composite Choi matrix gives a channel whose square is not entanglement breaking. The original maps need not preserve trace.

## Role in the source argument

Transfers the source dimension-ten PPT-pair construction to a genuine trace-preserving channel in dimension 21.

## Antecedents and comparison

The source explicitly credits the two-block switch to [Christandl, Müller-Hermes and Wolf, Section IV.A](https://arxiv.org/abs/1807.01266v2), and the trace-preserving flag extension to [Filippov, Proposition 3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2101.05686v3). This card records a reusable combination with an exact corner identity; it does not claim invention of either component.

## Checks and limitations

Checked positivity of the missing-trace effects, trace preservation, the absorbing flag, and the two-step compression identity.

The full Choi matrix has a product summand from the flag. The conclusion is nonseparability of the full matrix, witnessed by a local corner, not a product-free range for the full matrix. The displayed Choi compression uses real coordinate inclusions.

## Possible applications

Potential use: turn non-trace-preserving map-composition examples into physical channels while retaining a specified entangled submatrix.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
