# Extract a cyclic norm solution from a supplied rank-one idempotent

[Family 142](../families/openai-math-142.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000234.md) · [Batch B040](../../batches/B040/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let L/K be a cyclic field extension of degree q with generator rho, and let E=sum_(b=0)^(q-1) L V^b be the cyclic algebra with Va=rho(a)V and V^q=G in K*. Suppose E is split and a rank-one idempotent e is supplied.

The left ideal Ee has K-dimension q. The map L -> Ee, a -> ae, is injective because nonzero a is invertible, and is therefore an isomorphism by dimension. There is a unique h in L with Ve=he, found by solving a linear system in the explicit algebra basis. Iterating with the commutation relation yields

$$G e=V^q e=\left(\prod_{j=0}^{q-1}\rho^j(h)\right)e.$$

Since e is nonzero, the scalar coefficients agree: N_(L/K)(h)=G. This turns an explicit minimal left ideal into a norm witness without using a previously known splitting isomorphism.

## Role in the source argument

Explains exactly why the source construction of a rank-one idempotent would solve its promised norm equation.

## Checks and limitations

The tests deliberately supply split representations and idempotents. Their construction over the source rational-function field and its complexity remain unaudited.
