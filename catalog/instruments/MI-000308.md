# Diagonal transfer forces inverse-automorphism integration weights

[Family 314](../families/openai-math-314.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000173.md) · [Batch B025](../../batches/B025/README.md)

## Mathematical content

For a finite groupoid X, let a_alpha be the order of the automorphism group of an isomorphism-class representative. Its homotopy integral is sum_alpha x_alpha/a_alpha. A functor f:X->Y pushes coordinate functions forward by

$$ (f_!x)_\beta=\sum_{[f\alpha]=[\beta]}\frac{a_\beta}{a_\alpha}x_\alpha.$$

The relative weight includes nonfaithful functors. The homotopy fiber over beta has objects (alpha,eta:f(alpha) isomorphic to beta). The contribution from alpha is the action groupoid Iso(f(alpha),beta)//Aut(alpha), of cardinality a_beta/a_alpha. Kernel automorphisms contribute stabilizers and must not be discarded.

The source obtains the same formula in rational character coordinates, conditional on its evaluation splitting and transfer axioms. Diagonal transfer has coordinates sum_alpha a_alpha e_alpha tensor e_alpha, because its fiber counts isomorphisms. Composing with integration on one factor gives the identity, forcing epsilon(e_alpha)=1/a_alpha. The projection formula restricts f_!(e_alpha) to the coordinate f(alpha); compatibility with absolute integration then forces the relative ratio.

For composable functors the intermediate automorphism orders cancel. This gives the expected composition and integration identities without assuming injective maps on automorphism groups.

## Role in the source argument

Turns the source rational character splitting into weighted homomorphism and homotopy-fiber counts.

## Antecedents and comparison

[Strickland, Section 10 and Theorem 10.2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0011109) already gives inverse-automorphism Frobenius forms and compatible character operations. The source derives the analogous weights for its proposed profinite source group. Establishing that evaluation splitting is a separate task.

## Checks and limitations

288 cyclic-group functors, including 210 nonfaithful ones, verify homotopy-fiber masses by explicit orbits and stabilizers. Another 100 coordinate models verify pushforward composition and integration.

These finite checks do not establish the spectral evaluation splitting, ambidexterity inputs or integral scalar congruences.

## Possible applications

Counting fibers with symmetry and transferring functions while retaining stabilizer multiplicities.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
