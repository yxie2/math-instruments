# Absorb delayed Gaussian errors on every finite energy prefix

[Family 355](../families/openai-math-355.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000292.md) · [Batch B053](../../batches/B053/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let E_j be nonnegative and nonincreasing, with a_j=sqrt(E_j-E_(j+1)) and E_0<1. Choose Q>1, set r_j=sqrt(8 log(1/a_j)) with r_j=infinity at zero, and define R_j=min{Q^j R_in, min_(i<=j)Q^(j-i)r_i}. Then R_(j+1)=min{Q R_j,r_(j+1)}. This remembers every previous restriction while respecting a one-step radius-growth constraint.

Assume E_(j+1)^(1-theta)<=C[a_j+exp(-(1-eta)R_j^2/8)], with 0<eta<c<theta and p=(theta-c)/(1-c) in (0,1/2). Set kappa=(1-eta)/(1-c)>1, q=Q^2 and h=kappa/8. Call j good when its exponential term is at most a_j^(1-c). On good indices the gradient bound and concavity give a_j<=C'(E_j^p-E_(j+1)^p), treating E_(j+1)>=E_j/2 and its complement separately.

At a bad index, a_j<exp(-hR_j^2)=max{exp(-h q^j R_in^2), max_(i<=j)a_i^(kappa q^(j-i))}. Its own positive term a_j^kappa is smaller than a_j, so

a_j<=exp(-h q^j R_in^2)+sum_(i<j)a_i^(kappa q^(j-i)).

If delta=sqrt(E_0), the convolution costs at most rho(delta) sum a_j on each finite prefix, where rho(delta)<=delta^(kappa q-1)/(1-delta^(kappa(q-1))) tends to zero. The initial-radius tail is at most B(R)=exp(-hR^2)/(1-exp(-h(q-1)R^2)). Choosing rho<=1/2 gives sum_(j=0)^n a_j<=2[C'E_0^p+B(R_in)], uniformly in n. This finite-prefix argument avoids assuming summability before closing a bootstrap.

## Role in the source argument

Controls cumulative motion and cutoff errors while the source graphical radius is constrained by propagation from earlier slices.

## Antecedents and comparison

[Colding-Minicozzi, Uniqueness of blowups and Lojasiewicz inequalities](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1312.4046), Lemma 6.22 and its complete proof on pages 42-43, derive summability of square-root energy drops from a discrete gradient inequality. Those passages were read. The selected source adds its delayed-radius convolution; no earliest-priority claim for that addition is made.

## Checks and limitations

Finite nonincreasing energy sequences test the radius recursion, the good/bad split, exclusion of the current index, the convolution bound and the final prefix estimate. A control records why kappa>1 is necessary for exclusion.

The finite sequence checks do not prove that the source mean curvature flow satisfies the required localized inequality or propagation estimates.

## Possible applications

Bootstrap arguments in which a local gradient inequality carries a rapidly decaying error and admissible localization scales depend on their past.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
