# Bound dependent conditional tests through inductive means in a CAT(0) cone

[Family 257](../families/openai-math-257.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000196.md) · [Batch B031](../../batches/B031/README.md)

## Mathematical content

In a complete CAT(0) metric cone, the scalar product <h,u>=(||h||^2+||u||^2-d(h,u)^2)/2 is concave along geodesics. The cone radial identity is exact, so subtracting CAT(0) strong convexity of d(h,u)^2 proves this assertion. For the inductive mean m_1=V_1, m_j=[m_{j-1},V_j]_{1/j}, it gives average_j<h,V_j><=<h,m_t>.

If the V_j are iid, have barycenter zero and second moment one, the variance inequality yields E||m_j||^2<=(1-1/j)^2 E||m_{j-1}||^2+1/j^2, hence E||m_t||^2<=1/t.

Suppose t coupled branches share a vector h with ||h||<=B, have vector norms at most M, each branch marginal is within eta in total variation of the target law, and the leaf tuple is within eta of the iid law. No independence of h and the leaves is needed: pointwise average<h,V_j><=B||m_t||. Consequently

$$E_{target}\langle h,V\rangle\le B\sqrt{1/t+2M^2\eta}+2BM\eta.$$

For each fixed t let eta tend to zero, then let t tend to infinity. The bound is uniform over all such dependent bounded tests.

## Role in the source argument

Controls geometric tests chosen after the random tables and group actions are known.

## Antecedents and comparison

The iid mean estimate is [Sturm 2003, Theorem 4.7, proof (a)](https://publish.illinois.edu/ymb/files/2020/04/Sturm-2003-Probability-measures-on-metric-spaces-of-nonpositi.pdf). The source combines it with cone concavity and a conditional coupling.

## Checks and limitations

All 3,279 tip lists of lengths one through seven in a tripod cone verify concavity and the second-moment bound exactly; 100 bounded-observable total-variation checks also pass.

The source coupling and displacement bounds are assumed. A tripod is a non-Hilbert example, not verification over all CAT(0) cones.

## Possible applications

Uniform tests with data-dependent geometric observables when only the leaf marginals become independent.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
