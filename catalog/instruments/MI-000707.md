# Control local occupation after stopping above a distant level

[Family 147](../families/openai-math-147.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000476.md) · [Batch B075](../../batches/B075/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let X_m, 0<=m<=M, be an integrable real submartingale and V=sum_m E(Delta X_m)^2<infinity. For 0<h<R define C_h=sum_m E[1_(|X_m|<=h) min((Delta X_m)^2,h^2)]. Then C_h<=C h(h+R+sqrt(V))+C M h^2 min(1,V/(R-h)^2), with an absolute constant and no bound on X_0.

Use Phi(x)=sqrt(h^2+x^2)+x, which is nonnegative, increasing and convex, with derivative in [0,2]. Its curvature on [-2h,2h] gives a Taylor remainder at least c h^-1 1_(|x|<=h) min((y-x)^2,h^2). Stop at the first X_m>R, capped at M and equal to zero if the chain starts above R. The nonnegative drift has the correct sign, and telescoping bounds the pre-stop cost by C h(h+R+sqrt(V)); the crossing overshoot is bounded by the largest increment.

After the stop, subtract the predictable nonnegative drift. The resulting martingale starts at zero and has terminal second moment at most V. A return from above R to [-h,h] forces a fluctuation of at least R-h. Its unconditional probability is at most a constant times min(1,V/(R-h)^2). The remaining occupation costs at most M h^2 on this event. No conditioning on a rare crossing event is used.

## Role in the source argument

Charges visits near zero even when the initial value may be arbitrarily far away.

## Antecedents and comparison

Convex occupation estimates and martingale maximal inequalities are classical. The source combines stopping, an overshoot budget and an unconditional return estimate; exact priority of this formulation is not established.

## Checks and limitations

Checks the convex remainder numerically and computes finite random-walk occupation costs for large positive and negative starts, varying drift and jump sizes.

These finite models do not verify the source analytic row estimates or billiard sign-test argument.

## Possible applications

Bounding local quadratic activity without assuming a controlled starting position.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
