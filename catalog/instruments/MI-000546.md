# Check a sparse Hochschild class by a full polynomial certificate

[Family 199](../families/openai-math-199.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000315.md) · [Batch B056](../../batches/B056/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Work in characteristic two with q transcendental. The source gives a ten-dimensional algebra C with basis e,x,y,z,u,v,t,j,f,n, corners eCe={e,x,y,z}, eCf={u,v}, fCe={t,j}, fCf={f,n}, and explicit polynomial structure constants. Its trivial extension T=C semidirect DC has twenty basis letters, with uppercase letters denoting duals. The dual products follow by transposing multiplication: mu_(a,b*)^(c*)=mu_(c,a)^b and mu_(b*,a)^(c*)=mu_(a,c)^b; two dual factors multiply to zero. The trace lambda(a,phi)=phi(1) pairs each letter with its starred letter.

The source specifies p on radical triples by 179 distinct entries with coefficients in {1,q,q^2,q^3}; all omitted entries vanish. Thus the Hochschild identity

a p(b,c,d)+p(ab,c,d)+p(a,bc,d)+p(a,b,cd)+p(a,b,c)d=0

is a finite polynomial certificate. There are 18^4=104976 radical four-words, of which 15250 are composable. The bundled program checks all of them over F_2[q], as well as all 8000 basis associativity triples. It also checks both coefficient identities for the source twisted boundary relation on all 324 radical input pairs, retaining H as an indeterminate through its constant and linear coefficients.

For the f-simple, the bar cycle zeta=q^2[t|x|J]+[t|y|J] has zero differential: tx=j, ty=q^2j, xJ=T and yJ=q^2T cancel in pairs. Since p(t,x,J)=0 and p(t,y,J)=q^3 f, evaluation is q^3, which is nonzero. This detects a nontrivial class through cycle pairing. It does not establish the entire self-extension algebra or the later counterexample construction.

## Role in the source argument

Supplies the source degree-three seed and the explicit boundary relation used in later cone constructions.

## Antecedents and comparison

[Cibils-Marcos-Redondo-Solotar, Cohomology of split algebras and of trivial extensions](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0102194), introduction on PDF pages 1-2, treats Hochschild cohomology of split and trivial extensions; those background passages were read. The cohomological method is classical. The specific multiplication and cochain data are recorded with exact source locators, without asserting earliest priority.

## Checks and limitations

Complete exact polynomial checks of associativity, trace duality, grading, all 179 entries, all 104976 cocycle inputs, both boundary coefficients on 324 pairs, and the cycle differential and nonzero value.

No proof of polynomial generation of Ext, later cone exactness, total acyclicity, or the Auslander-Reiten counterexample is supplied by these finite checks.

## Possible applications

Turning a sparse algebraic construction into a reproducible certificate that tests omitted zero entries as well as the displayed nonzero ones.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
