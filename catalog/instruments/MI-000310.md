# Cartier halving carries an explicit Dickson exponent floor

[Family 316](../families/openai-math-316.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000174.md) · [Batch B025](../../batches/B025/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Over F_2 define C_x on a polynomial monomial to be zero unless every exponent is odd; otherwise halve each exponent after subtracting one. Binomial parity and the Cartan formula give

$$C_x\mathrm{Sq}^{2i}=\mathrm{Sq}^iC_x,\qquad C_x\mathrm{Sq}^{2i+1}=0,\qquad C_x(a^2f)=aC_x(f).$$

The map commutes with GL_k(F_2): in the square-free decomposition, the coefficient of x_1...x_k transforms by the determinant, which is one. It therefore preserves the Dickson invariant algebra D_k=F_2[d_0,...,d_(k-1)]. Its coordinate Jacobian is d_0^(k-1), and Cartier naturality yields

$$C_x\left(d_0^c\prod_{j=1}^{k-1}d_j^{b_j}\right)=d_0^{(c+k-2)/2}\prod_{j=1}^{k-1}d_j^{(b_j-1)/2}$$

when c is congruent to k modulo two and all b_j are odd, and zero otherwise. Small c are handled by multiplying by a large even power of d_0, applying the formula and cancelling in the polynomial domain.

For the Milnor-coinvariant quotient of d_0^pD_k, the above-floor basis has c>p with those parity constraints. If c_0 is the least integer greater than p and congruent to k, its image is precisely the ideal d_0^((c_0+k-2)/2)D_k. The polynomial degree changes from d to (d-k)/2. The strict floor and parity must be carried together through repeated halving; ignoring either changes the target ideal.

## Role in the source argument

Tracks how much divisibility by the top Dickson invariant survives repeated degree halving in the source obstruction argument.

## Antecedents and comparison

The source explicitly identifies the polynomial extractor with the classical Cartier/Kameko map and cites Hung and Giambalvo-Peterson for related transformations. [Hung author report](https://digitalcommons.wayne.edu/math_reports/8/) verifies the earlier squaring-operation context; its linked full text was inaccessible in this audit, so its exact dual formula was not independently compared. The selected source proof was read and its polynomial identities checked.

## Checks and limitations

600 Steenrod identities, 75 linear-change naturality checks, 65 explicitly expanded Dickson monomials and 90 exponent-floor checks pass over F_2 for up to three variables.

Finite polynomial checks do not certify all Steenrod-module assertions, the Tor injection or the source spherical-class obstruction.

Iterative degree reduction in invariant ideals where the least allowed generator exponent is decisive.
