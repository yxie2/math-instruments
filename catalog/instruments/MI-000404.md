# Kill lim-one by uniformly small corrections in a dense Banach tower

[Family 313](../families/openai-math-313.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000221.md) · [Batch B037](../../batches/B037/README.md)

## Mathematical content

For a countable inverse system of complete nonarchimedean normed vector spaces E_j with continuous linear maps f_j:E_{j+1}->E_j of dense image, R^1 lim E_j=0. The same holds after replacing E_j by C(D,E_j) with the supremum norm for a compact profinite parameter space D.

The derived-limit differential is (v_j)->(v_j-f_j(v_{j+1})) on the product. To solve for arbitrary right-hand sides a_j, construct finite solution strings. At stage h, density chooses a new terminal value such that e_h=a_h+f_h(v_{h+1})-v_h is small. Redefining earlier entries backward changes entry j by f_j...f_{h-1}(e_h). Continuity of finitely many maps lets all these changes be at most 2^{-h}. Each fixed entry converges by completeness, giving a full solution.

For compact parameters, finite clopen step functions uniformly approximate continuous functions; approximate their finitely many values using dense images. Thus the induced maps remain dense. Applied degreewise to complexes, surjectivity of the product differential identifies the derived limit with the termwise kernel complex. Closed images and surjective transition maps are unnecessary.

## Role in the source argument

Controls inverse limits while retaining compact parameter families and complex degrees.

## Antecedents and comparison

[Le Bras, Proposition 3.5](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1801.00422v1) explicitly includes complete metric groups with eventual dense transition images. This source lemma is a concrete special case with a parameter-uniform proof.

## Checks and limitations

Four hundred eighty exact rational extension steps verify the backward equations and p-adic error bounds even when earlier maps amplify errors.

Finite scalar models have surjective maps and test the correction protocol only; they do not simulate a complete infinite-dimensional dense nonsurjective tower.

## Possible applications

Passing to countable analytic limits when approximation is available but exact lifting at one stage is not.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
