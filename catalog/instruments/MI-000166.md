# Ramsey averaging and transposed couplings build reversible chart transport

[Family 327](../families/openai-math-327.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000105.md) · [Batch B008](../../batches/B008/README.md)

## Mathematical content

For a finite ordered set Lambda and epsilon>0, the source constructs a finite B and a law nu on strictly increasing charts C:Lambda->[B] such that the laws of C(A) and C(A') have total-variation distance less than epsilon for every pair of increasing subtuples of equal length.

For each chart collect all signed differences of those tuple point masses in a finite direct sum of ell1 spaces. A separating dual functional is a finite family of bounded functions on tuples. Bin their values into a fixed number of colors. A sufficiently large finite Ramsey set makes every same-length comparison small for one chart, contradicting separation from zero. A convex combination of chart vectors therefore gives the desired law. This is an existence proof with no practical bound on B.

Now let partial increasing bijections T_s:D_s->R_s be indexed by a finite set of letters with a fixed-point-free inverse involution, T_(s^-1)=T_s^-1. Couple two nu-charts by matching C(T_s(D_s)) with C'(D_s) maximally, then sample the full charts conditionally. Call the coupling lambda_s; both marginals are nu, and the full matching condition $C'(a)=C(T_s(a))$ for all a in D_s fails with probability at most epsilon. Define the inverse coupling by transposition.

At each step choose a fresh independent uniform letter s, then choose C' according to lambda_s(C,C')/nu(C), discarding zero-mass charts. The stationary edge mass is lambda_s(C,C') divided by the number of letters, invariant under (C,s,C')->(C',s^-1,C). Thus the marked chain is reversible, letters remain independent and uniform, and the rank match holds at every stationary step with probability at least 1-epsilon. The bound is averaged over stationary charts, not a uniform conditional success bound for each chart.

## Role in the source argument

Encodes finite pieces of ordered motion inside a stationary reversible walk while preserving the coordinate ranks needed by the source’s displacement argument.

## Antecedents and comparison

Finite Ramsey theory, separation and maximal coupling are classical. The extracted package couples partial ordered maps with a common chart law and uses transposition to impose detailed balance. The surrounding [Markov-type framework](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0410422) is established; the package’s priority is not certified.

## Checks and limitations

Read the separation argument, conditional full-chart reconstruction and edge-mass identity. Explicit small translated charts verify total-variation matching, marginal preservation, inverse coupling and stationary balance. These computations do not prove the general Ramsey existence assertion.

The finite chart set may be enormous. Per-step error is a stationary probability; a length-n path may cost n epsilon by a union bound. The construction requires consistent inverse maps and does not turn an arbitrary directed motion into exact reversible transport.

## Possible applications

Finite reversible approximations of partial ordered dynamics when exact charts would not have an invariant probability law.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
