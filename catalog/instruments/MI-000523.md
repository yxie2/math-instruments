# Convert exponentially close stopped approximants into all-orders Gevrey bounds

[Family 355](../families/openai-math-355.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000292.md) · [Batch B053](../../batches/B053/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let k,R_0,d,c>0, R_j=R_0 Lambda^j with Lambda>1, r_j=d R_j^(-2/k), and let F_j be holomorphic on the complete disk |s|<=r_j. Assume ||F_j-F_(j-1)||<=C exp(-cR_j^2) on that disk for j>=1, with a fixed analytic bound for F_0. Put D_0=F_0 and D_j=F_j-F_(j-1). Cauchy's estimate and

sum_j exp(-cR_j^2) R_j^(2n/k)<=C_1 A^n Gamma(1+n/k)

show that f_n=sum_j [s^n]D_j converges and has Gevrey order 1/k. The moment bound follows by reserving half the exponential for a summable geometric-scale series and maximizing t^(n/k) exp(-ct/2).

For 0<vartheta<1, stop at the largest J with |s|<=vartheta r_J. The exact remainder identity is

F_J-sum_(n<N)f_n s^n = sum_(j<=J)(D_j-T_(N-1)D_j)-sum_(j>J)T_(N-1)D_j.

The first sum uses Cauchy remainders inside the disks. For the second, x_j=|s|/r_j>vartheta gives sum_(n<N)x_j^n<=N vartheta^(-N)x_j^N. The same moment bound yields C_2 A_2^N Gamma(1+N/k)|s|^N for every N, including orders beyond optimal truncation. Any additional difference bounded by C exp(-b/|s|^k) obeys the same all-orders estimate. Identifying these coefficients with a particular formal stationary action requires a separate comparison; formal notation alone does not supply it.

## Role in the source argument

Provides the source quantitative asymptotic bounds for its stopped actions and, conditionally on exponential comparison, its lateral sector arrays.

## Antecedents and comparison

[Lastra-Malek, Gevrey versus q-Gevrey asymptotic expansions for some linear q-difference-differential Cauchy problem](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2312.11064), Annex II, Theorem 7, recalls the classical Ramis-Sibuya theorem deriving common Gevrey asymptotics from exponentially flat sector differences; the full statement was read. The selected argument instead uses complete shrinking disks and Cauchy telescoping. No novelty is claimed for the general exponential-to-Gevrey principle.

## Checks and limitations

High-precision analytic models test the exponential moment bound, coefficient growth and the exact stopped-remainder identity for orders before and after the optimal scale. These are finite approximant models with explicit poles outside their allowed disks.

The checks do not construct the source contour arrays, justify its endpoint estimates or prove its formal critical-arc exclusion.

## Possible applications

Obtaining quantitative asymptotic expansions from a sequence of finite-domain analytic problems whose overlap errors decay exponentially.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
