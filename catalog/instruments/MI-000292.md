# Proportional cancellation gives exact reconstruction on a refinement graph

[Family 085](../families/openai-math-085.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000165.md) · [Batch B023](../../batches/B023/README.md)

## Mathematical content

For finite nonnegative measures U,V of masses P,N, put d=min(P,N), remove mu_+=(d/P)U and mu_-=(d/N)V, with a zero measure when its denominator is zero. The residual rho=(U-mu_+)-(V-mu_-) has constant sign, total mass P-N and total variation |P-N|. The removed difference beta=mu_+-mu_- has mass zero and variation at most 2d. The supports of U,V need not be disjoint.

Apply this at the finest level of a positive refinement phi_s=sum_t lambda_st phi_t, using the actual positive and negative parts of a signed measure nu. At each earlier vertex combine the positive and negative parts of its children's constant-sign residuals with coefficients lambda_st, then remove equal mass again. For c_s=integral phi_s dnu, the defect is

$$d_s=\tfrac12\left(\sum_t\lambda_{st}|c_t|-|c_s|\right),$$

with the terminal sum replaced by integral phi_s d|nu|. The resulting residual has mass c_s and constant sign. Composition of refinement coefficients yields the exact measure identity

$$\phi_q\nu=\rho_q+\sum_{l\ge k(q)}\sum_{s\text{ at level }l}\lambda_{qs}\beta_s.$$

Every beta_s is constructed once. It may occur in several ancestor identities with different coefficients; this is not a disjoint allocation. With column sums one and a terminal partition of unity, the cancellation budget telescopes:

$$\sum_s d_s=\tfrac12\left(|\nu|(\text{whole space})-\sum_{q\text{ at first level}}|c_q|\right).$$

The terminal decomposition uses the actual signed density, so c_s=0 does not imply d_s=0. Proportional removal preserves absolute continuity and the nested support constraints in the source.

## Role in the source argument

Builds cancellation pieces with one global budget that can be priced at many observation scales.

## Antecedents and comparison

The removal step is elementary signed-measure algebra, and the total budget is a triangle-defect telescope. The source makes the shared-descendant reconstruction and zero-mass cases explicit. No independent novelty claim is made for these ingredients.

## Checks and limitations

Read the full construction and induction. Forty-five exact rational atomic models with overlapping supports and shared descendants check 540 measure identities and the total defect budget, including zero residual mass with positive cancellation.

Atomic models test measure algebra, not the disk kernel estimates or smooth densities. Column normalization, terminal partition of unity and support nesting must be supplied by the refinement system.

## Possible applications

Separating a signed input into coherent residuals and locally cancelling pieces without double-counting its total cancellation budget.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
