# Recover a ranked mass law from interior factorial statistics

[Family 011](../families/openai-math-011.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000402.md) · [Batch B066](../../batches/B066/README.md)

## Mathematical content

For random labeled mass partitions summing to one, assume their ordered factorial measures converge locally on D_d={t_i>0, sum t_i<1} to product_i dt_i/t_i for every d. Successive sampling without replacement proportional to remaining mass weights a tuple by product_i t_i/(1-sum_(j<i)t_j). The resulting interior density is h_d(t)=product_i(1-sum_(j<i)t_j)^(-1).

The triangular substitution t_i=A_i product_(j<i)(1-A_j) sends independent uniforms on (0,1)^d to this density; its Jacobian cancels h_d. Thus the interior limit already has probability mass one. Compact cutoffs then exclude escape to every boundary face, including exhausted samples padded with zeros, and give full weak convergence of the first d draws.

The expected unseen mass tends to 2^(-d). If V is the full decreasing rearrangement and S_d sorts the selected d members and pads zeros, then 0<=V_i-S_(d,i)<=r, where r is the unseen mass. Indeed, any mass larger than r must have been selected. A bounded continuous test has error at most omega_F(eta)+2||F||_infinity E(r)/eta. Taking the source limit, then d to infinity, then eta to zero yields convergence of every finite collection of ranked masses to PD(1). The prime-predecessor application additionally needs the source interior arithmetic statistics.

## Role in the source argument

Handles small factors and simplex boundaries without a separate total factorial-mass estimate near zero.

## Antecedents and comparison

[Arratia, Kochman and Miller, Extensions of Billingsley's Theorem via Multi-Intensities](https://arxiv.org/abs/1401.1555), Lemma 2 and section 3.3, supply the earlier size-biased multi-intensity criterion and characterization. Pages 5-8, including the lemma proof, were read. The source gives an explicit boundary and sorting implementation of this established mechanism.

## Checks and limitations

Exact finite sampling probabilities, symbolic stick-breaking Jacobians, and exhaustive subcollection sorting bounds check the probability conversion and remainder control.

These checks do not establish factorial convergence for primes or a new Poisson-Dirichlet characterization.

## Possible applications

Passing from local point-process statistics to a full ranked distribution with controlled missing mass.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
