# Assemble a block diagonal rank certificate from low-degree punctured-line weights

[Family 252](../families/openai-math-252.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000193.md) · [Batch B030](../../batches/B030/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Choose distinct marks a_i in S^h over F_q and product Lagrange polynomials f_i of total degree at most h(|S|-1), with f_i(a_j)=delta_ij. Put H(z)=f(z)f(z)^T, so rank H<=1 and H(a_i)=E_ii. Assume 2h(|S|-1)<q-1.

On each affine line every entry of H has degree less than q-1 and sums to zero over the field. If line i is punctured only at a_i, then sum_{z on punctured line i}H(z)=-E_ii. For arbitrary equally sized matrices Y_i and Z(z)=sum_{i incident at z}Y_i, exchanging finite sums gives

$$\sum_z H(z)\otimes Z(z)=-\bigoplus_iY_i.$$

Therefore sum_i rank(Y_i)<=sum_z rank(Z(z)). If there are N blocks with rank at least rD/4, at most m points, and local ranks at most 2D except on E where the ambient bound rD is used, then NrD/4<=2mD+r|E|D. Dividing by D leaves a certificate independent of the quotient size.

Other marked points on a line must remain in its incidence sum. Removing all marks changes the line sum and generally destroys the block diagonal identity.

## Role in the source argument

Turns local factorizations into a global contradiction when the independent combinatorial parameters beat the rank budget.

## Antecedents and comparison

This is a concrete assembly of Lagrange interpolation, finite-field power sums and subadditivity of rank. The extraction makes the puncture convention and exceptional-point terms explicit.

## Checks and limitations

Sixty exact finite-field tensor models verify the identity and rank inequality. Deleting every marked point instead of only the own mark fails a control case.

The small local ranks and source incidence budget are conditional inputs; the source triangle-complex construction is not certified.

## Possible applications

Local-to-global rank obstructions and finite-field incidence certificates.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
