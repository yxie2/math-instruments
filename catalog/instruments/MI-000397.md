# Separate isolated singular points using a local Artin determinant

[Family 166](../families/openai-math-166.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000217.md) · [Batch B036](../../batches/B036/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Over an algebraically closed field, let S be selected isolated common zeros of equations of degree at most L>=1 in c variables. At z in S choose c constant linear combinations f_i forming a local parameter sequence. Write f(Y)-f(Z)=Q(Y,Z)(Y-Z), with Y-degree of every matrix entry at most L-1.

In the local Artin quotient A_z, det Q(z,Z) is nonzero: the comparison from the Koszul resolution on f_i to that on the coordinates identifies this determinant as a nonzero socle class. For every other common zero y, one coordinate of y-Z is a unit in A_z; the adjugate identity then gives det Q(y,Z)=0. Applying a linear functional on A_z that is nonzero on det Q(z,Z), and normalizing, produces s_z of degree at most c(L-1), equal to one at z and zero at every other common zero, even on positive-dimensional components.

This uses a divided-difference determinant, not a potentially vanishing Jacobian. From s_z, the polynomials 3s_z^2-2s_z^3 and (Y_j-z_j)s_z^2 interpolate values and first coordinate jets on S. Their degrees are at most max(3c(L-1),2c(L-1)+1). The equations 1-sum s_z and (Y_j-z_j)s_z isolate exactly S.

## Role in the source argument

Preserves polynomial interpolation when isolated solutions are singular or nonreduced.

## Antecedents and comparison

The underlying regular-local and Koszul facts are classical; see [Stacks, regular local rings](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/00NQ) and [regular sequences imply Koszul regularity](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/062F). These references support ingredients, not the source global distance claim.

## Checks and limitations

Exact divided-determinant coefficient functionals separate the two points of x^2(x-1)=y^2=0, including the nonreduced origin; first jets are checked. A separate model has an isolated point beside a line of zeros.

Examples do not certify general effective bounds or the full distinct-distance proof.

## Possible applications

Degree-controlled isolation and jet interpolation without reducedness or a nonzero-Jacobian assumption.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
