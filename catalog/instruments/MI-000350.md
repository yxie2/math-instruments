# Bound affine product-density perturbations independently of dimension

[Family 253](../families/openai-math-253.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000194.md) · [Batch B030](../../batches/B030/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let independent coordinates xi_i have density f^2, where f is nonnegative, even, smooth, supported on [-1,1], positive inside and normalized in L2. For differentiable positive-determinant invertible T_t and means mu_t, let p_t be the density of mu_t+T_t xi. Set L=T_t^{-1}dot(T_t), v=T_t^{-1}dot(mu_t). Pullback gives the derivative norm of (Lx+v) dot grad F+(tr L)F/2, where F=product_i f(x_i).

Write A=int(f')^2, C=int x^2f^2 and B=int(xf'+f/2)^2. Integration by parts and parity give the exact squared norm

$$A\|v\|^2+B\sum_iL_{ii}^2+AC\sum_{i\ne j}L_{ij}^2+\tfrac12\sum_{i<j}L_{ij}L_{ji}.$$

Indeed centered single-coordinate terms are orthogonal, and off-diagonal terms interact only for the same unordered coordinate pair. Thus it is bounded by K(||L||_HS^2+||v||^2), where K=max(A,B,AC+1/4), independent of dimension.

For T_t interpolating between identity-plus-positive-semidefinite matrices, let Delta=T_1-T_0 and h=mu_1-mu_0. The inverse norm is at most one and T_t^{-1}h=(I-tT_t^{-1}Delta)T_0^{-1}h. Integration gives square-root density distance at most sqrt(K)(||Delta||_HS+(1+||Delta||_op)||T_0^{-1}h||). Total variation is at most this distance. If all changed coordinates lie in one finite block and the outside independent factor is exactly common, this is a bound on the whole field. Thresholding contracts total variation.

## Role in the source argument

Allows the number of sites to grow without a dimension factor in the law comparison used for random partitions.

## Antecedents and comparison

This is a product-score/Hellinger calculation. The exact expanded moment formula is an extraction of the source orthogonality argument, not a claim of a new general statistical metric.

## Checks and limitations

Forty-eight product Gauss-quadrature models in dimensions one through four verify the expanded moment identity and bound, with maximum error below 2.8e-13.

The check density uses a C2 compact polynomial square root, sufficient for its finite integrals but weaker than the source smooth hypothesis. The averaging matrices and partition coupling remain inputs.

## Possible applications

Dimension-independent perturbation bounds for affine product noise and common-background random fields.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
