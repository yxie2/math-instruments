# Push discrepancy data by an attained threshold minimum

[Family 066](../families/openai-math-066.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000441.md) · [Batch B071](../../batches/B071/README.md)

## Mathematical content

For a projective contraction pi:Y->V with the source generalized discrepancy data a and generic sub-lc assumptions, define (pi# a)(P)=min a(F)/m over divisorial F restricting to mP, m>0. Resolve over the generic point of P so that the relevant boundary, nef part and pullback of P have simple normal crossings. The sub-lc threshold after subtracting t pi*P is the minimum of a_i/m_i over components with m_i>0. Components with m_i=0 already satisfy the needed horizontal nonnegativity. Higher monomial valuations cannot lower the minimum. Thus the value is finite and attained, and it may be negative at a base divisor.

Nontrivial restriction of a divisorial valuation to the intermediate function field is a positive multiple of a divisorial valuation in this setting. Attainment and multiplication of the ramification factors give (rho pi)# a = rho#(pi# a). Adding a pulled-back divisor changes every ratio by the same base order, so divisor translation commutes with pushforward. The source applies this to rational discrepancy presentations, retaining their canonical-form data.

## Role in the source argument

Makes repeated contraction compatible with threshold and divisor arithmetic.

## Antecedents and comparison

[Filipazzi, arXiv:1807.04847v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/1807.04847v3), pages 5 and 8, defines the usual and generalized discriminant via these generic thresholds; those definitions were read. The extracted minimum and composition are the valuative implementation of that established construction, not a new discriminant theory.

## Checks and limitations

Exact SNC monomial valuations test attainment, horizontal zero multiplicities, negative thresholds, divisor translations and two-stage multiplicity composition.

Finite valuation models do not construct log resolutions, establish the needed canonical bundle formula or prove bounded complements.

## Possible applications

Keeping threshold normalization consistent across several fibrations.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
