# Determine an entire cofiber homology module from its cyclic generator

[Family 308](../families/openai-math-308.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000513.md) · [Batch B083](../../batches/B083/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let E be a ring spectrum with graded-commutative even coefficient ring R, and let a unit map u:S->Y identify E_*Y with R/J. Suppose b:S^d->Y and mu:Sigma^dY->Y, d even, satisfy mu composed with Sigma^d u homotopic to b. If the E-Hurewicz image of b is a x, with a a degree-zero unit and x in R_d acting injectively on R/J, then the cofiber has E-homology R/(J,x), via the composite unit map.

Every induced E-homology map is R-linear. Its shifted source is cyclic, and the specified homotopy fixes the generator image, so the map is multiplication by a x in EVERY degree. Injectivity makes the long exact sequence a sequence of cokernel short exact sequences. The unit a does not alter the ideal.

The source descends only finitely many objects, maps, homotopies and cofiber equivalences (with inverses and inverse homotopies) to one common ultrafilter-large set of primes. Coefficient linearity then computes the entire module there. It does not require intersecting one prime set for each of infinitely many homological degrees or descending full commutative algebra structures.

## Role in the source argument

Avoiding an unjustified infinite intersection of prime sets when a finite cyclic-module calculation controls every degree.

## Antecedents and comparison

This is the classical regular-element cofiber calculation. Its source use is to reduce all-degree identification to finite generator and coherence data. The needed attaching-map construction remains a separate input.

## Checks and limitations

Finite-degree polynomial bases check injective multiplication by a regular element, the quotient dimensions and independence of a unit scalar. A zero-divisor example displays the additional kernel term in a mapping cone.

A cokernel-only calculation is false if multiplication is not injective. These algebraic checks do not construct spectrum maps or verify the source Hurewicz computation.
