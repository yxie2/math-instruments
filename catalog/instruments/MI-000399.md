# Reduce a restriction kernel modulo a regular transverse label

[Family 168](../families/openai-math-168.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000218.md) · [Batch B036](../../batches/B036/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let A be a polynomial ring, alpha and beta_i pairwise transverse in the sense that alpha is not proportional to any nonzero linear form beta_i. Let E be free and L the kernel of maps E -> direct-sum_i T_i, where T_i is free over A/(beta_i). Multiplication by alpha is injective on every T_i. Therefore alpha v in L implies v in L, and

$$L\cap\alpha E=\alpha L,\qquad J:=\operatorname{image}(L\to E/\alpha E)\simeq L/\alpha L.$$

If L is free over A, J is free over C=A/(alpha). Moreover p=product_i beta_i annihilates all restrictions, so pE is contained in L. After reduction, nonzero bar p gives bar p(E/alpha E) contained in J, proving that J has full generic rank. Empty products equal one.

This is the opening algebraic step of the cited edge-image proposition. It does not prove the subsequent containment by a homogeneous factor of a prescribed degree. If alpha=beta, saturation can fail: L=alpha A has zero image in A/alpha A although L/alpha L is nonzero.

## Role in the source argument

Supplies a free full-rank target lattice over an edge quotient, before any local edge-image comparison.

## Checks and limitations

One hundred twenty exact polynomial kernel models check saturation; product-annihilator and generic-rank identities are exact. A proportional-label example fails saturation.

No general partial-kernel freeness, edge-image degree bound or combinatorial-invariance theorem is certified.

## Possible applications

Preserving a kernel under hypersurface reduction when all output modules see the cutting parameter as a nonzerodivisor.
