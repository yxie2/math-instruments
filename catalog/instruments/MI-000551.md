# Separate principal units before restricting cohomology to a Sylow subgroup

[Family 203](../families/openai-math-203.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000320.md) · [Batch B056](../../batches/B056/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let O=W(k), with k algebraically closed of characteristic p, and let R be an indecomposable O-order. Its commutative center Z is complete local: a nontrivial decomposition of its residue algebra would lift to a central idempotent. Its residue field is k. The unit group splits as Z^times=T times U, where T is the Teichmuller copy of k^times and U=1+rad(Z). Every O-algebra automorphism preserves this splitting and acts trivially on T.

For an integer n prime to p, u to u^n is an automorphism of U. Hensel lifting gives the unique root congruent to 1 modulo rad(Z), because the derivative n is a unit. If a finite group Q acts on Z and S is a Sylow p-subgroup, restriction followed by corestriction multiplies positive-degree cohomology with U coefficients by [Q:S]. That multiplication is invertible on the coefficient module and hence on its cohomology. Restriction H^2(Q,U) to H^2(S,U) is therefore injective.

The T contribution is finite. For d=|Q|, the d-th power map on T is surjective with finite kernel, since k is algebraically closed. Positive group cohomology is killed by d, so the coefficient exact sequence makes H^2(Q,T) a quotient of a finite group-cohomology group with finite coefficients. It follows that restriction H^2(Q,Z^times) to H^2(S,Z^times) has finite kernel. The result controls the remaining central twisting ambiguity for a fixed Q; it is not a uniform estimate over all finite groups.

## Role in the source argument

Provides the finite cohomological ambiguity needed in the source integral kernel-removal argument.

## Antecedents and comparison

[Eisele, The Picard group of an order and Kuelshammer reduction](https://arxiv.org/abs/1807.05110), Lemma 4.4 on PDF page 8, proves prime-to-p power bijectivity on principal units by Hensel lifting and uses it to obtain finite unit-group cohomology for a prime-to-p group. That full lemma and proof were read. The source combines this mechanism with Sylow restriction and the center splitting; no new general transfer principle is claimed.

## Checks and limitations

Fifty-seven power maps on finite principal-unit quotients are checked, with failure controls for p-divisible exponents. Normalized bar-cochain matrices compute H^2 and its Sylow restriction for S3 in characteristics 2 and 3 with trivial and sign actions.

Finite quotients illustrate the divisibility mechanism; they do not replace Hensel completeness or prove the manuscript subsequent block-order finiteness.

## Possible applications

Isolating a finite twisting ambiguity by separating divisible principal units from the residue-field units before using group cohomology.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
