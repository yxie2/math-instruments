# Weighted polynomial grades recover cube edges through an anticommutator

[Family 127](../families/openai-math-127.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000116.md) · [Batch B011](../../batches/B011/README.md)

## Mathematical content

On the sign cube Omega of size N=2^n use the counting inner product. Let V_k be the degree-at-most-k Fourier polynomials, D multiplication by sqrt(w) for w>0, and E_k=DV_k intersect (DV_{k-1})^perp with projections Pi_k. These grades have dimensions binom(n,k). Put L=sum k Pi_k and M=L-(n/2)I, so ||M||_HS^2=nN/4.

Coordinate multipliers Z_i commute with D and raise degree by at most one; self-adjointness therefore gives Pi_s Z_i Pi_r=0 if |s-r|>1. The commutator [M,Z_i] has operator norm at most one: remove the equal-grade part by averaging against sum(-1)^k Pi_k, then conjugate adjacent blocks by sum i^k Pi_k to insert the commutator signs. Consequently |M_xy|<=1/2 off the diagonal.

Block Parseval also gives sum_{x,y}dist(x,y)|M_xy|^2<=nN/4. Hence energy at distance at least two is at most diagonal energy A0, and total neighbor deficit sum_{dist=1}(1/4-|M_xy|^2)<=2A0. For any sign multiplier H, the anticommutator detects diagonal and equal-sign entries. If E_h is the set of ordered cube edges with h(x)=h(y),

$$|E_h|\le2\|MH+HM\|_{HS}^2.$$

For a degree-d threshold polynomial p, first shift slightly positively to remove zero values while preserving its signs. Set w=|p| and h=full_parity*sign(p). Then w h has Fourier support in degrees at least n-d, so Pi_s H Pi_r=0 when r+s<n-d. The probabilities ||Pi_s H Pi_r||_HS^2/N define a coupling (R,S) with both marginals Bin(n,1/2), and ||MH+HM||_HS^2/N=E(R+S-n)^2. Reflection U=S,V=n-R gives equal marginals and V-U<=d. The preceding one-sided estimate bounds this energy by 4d sqrt(n); parity turns equal-sign h edges into sensitive p edges, yielding the source 8d sqrt(n) estimate.

## Role in the source argument

Connects algebraic degree to actual unweighted edge counts while permitting a weight chosen from the threshold polynomial.

## Antecedents and comparison

The weighted degree filtration and three-term recurrence are classical; compare [Xu, Section 3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0401418v2). The selected package combines commutator norm control, a diagonal deficit budget and an equal-marginal grade coupling. No priority certification follows from this comparison.

## Checks and limitations

Constructed weighted grades numerically on small cubes and checked rank multiplicities, tridiagonal blocks, commutator norms, total energy and edge recovery. Polynomial-derived weights also check the vanishing grade region, binomial marginals and energy identity.

Counting pairs are ordered, and the Hilbert-Schmidt norm uses counting measure. Arbitrary zero weights destroy the invertible filtration argument. Small-dimensional tests do not certify the general threshold bound.

## Possible applications

Converting weighted polynomial filtrations into combinatorial boundary estimates and coupling constraints on spectral indices.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
