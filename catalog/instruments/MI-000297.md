# Uniform weak compositions are dominated in the nonnegative factorial basis

[Family 135](../families/openai-math-135.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000167.md) · [Batch B024](../../batches/B024/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let M be uniform among weak compositions of t into r parts, and let d_i>=0 have total H. With falling and rising factorials,

$$\mathbb E\prod_i(M_i)_{d_i}=\frac{(t)_H}{r^{\overline H}}\prod_i d_i!.$$

The value is zero when H>t. Indeed, summing (j)_d y^j gives d! y^d/(1-y)^(d+1); multiply these generating functions and divide the resulting coefficient by the number binomial(t+r-1,t) of compositions.

For independent geometric variables Z_i on the nonnegative integers with mean G=t/r, the corresponding moment is G^H prod d_i!. For t>0 the ratio is

$$\prod_{j=0}^{H-1}\frac{1-j/t}{1+j/r}\le1.$$

Consequently E P(M)<=E P(Z) for every polynomial with nonnegative falling-factorial coefficients. If its total factorial orders are at most H_0<=t/2, then

$$e^{-H_0^2/t-H_0^2/(2r)}\mathbb E P(Z)\le\mathbb E P(M)\le\mathbb E P(Z).$$

Use log(1-x)>=-2x for x<=1/2 and log(1+x)<=x termwise. At t=0 both vectors are zero. The coordinates of M are dependent; equivalently, conditioning independent equal-parameter geometric variables on their sum makes the composition uniform. The comparison is a basis-positivity statement, not an ordering for arbitrary pointwise nonnegative polynomials.

## Role in the source argument

Replaces dependent occupation counts by geometric moments while keeping an explicit multiplicative error.

## Antecedents and comparison

Stars-and-bars coefficient extraction and geometric factorial moments are classical. The reusable feature here is the explicit error over all terms of bounded factorial order. Historical novelty for this formulation was not established.

## Checks and limitations

675 exact rational moments and 252 lower comparisons pass. A polynomial with a negative factorial coefficient reverses the upper comparison.

The source trace expansion must separately have the required nonnegative factorial coefficients and controlled total order. Those global trace estimates were not audited.

## Possible applications

Moment calculations for uniform allocations, conditioned occupancy models and nonnegative combinatorial expansions.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
