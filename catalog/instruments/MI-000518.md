# Obstruct nonpositive curvature by incompatible torus orthogonality

[Family 358](../families/openai-math-358.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000290.md) · [Batch B052](../../batches/B052/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Glue two copies of a once-punctured torus times a circle along their boundary tori by h_2=h_1+f_1 and f_2=h_1. The matrix [[1,1],[1,0]] has determinant -1. Each boundary subgroup embeds as Z^2, with h_i a commutator in the surface group and f_i central in the corresponding vertex group. Thus h_i belongs to the commutator subgroup of the centralizer of f_i.

Suppose the amalgamated group acts properly and cocompactly on a nonempty proper CAT(0) space. Compactness modulo the action and properness show that every translation-length infimum is attained. Infinite-order elements have positive length. The flat torus theorem gives one common invariant Euclidean plane on which the embedded boundary Z^2 acts as a translation lattice.

The cyclic minset of f_i splits as Y_i times R. Every centralizing element acts on the R factor by a constant translation, defining a homomorphism from its centralizer to R. This character vanishes on h_i. On the common plane, the resulting Pythagorean identity forces v(h_i) perpendicular to v(f_i). Writing u=v(h_1), v=v(f_1), the two conditions become <u,v>=0 and <u+v,u>=0. They force ||u||^2=0, contradicting a nonzero lattice vector.

Thus this group has no such CAT(0) action. Standard metric Cartan-Hadamard then excludes a locally CAT(0) metric on the closed manifold, and in particular a smooth metric of nonpositive sectional curvature. Constructing a metric with no conjugate points is a separate task.

## Role in the source argument

Fixes the source topological obstruction before its no-conjugate-points metric is constructed.

## Antecedents and comparison

[Leeb, 3-manifolds with(out) metrics of nonpositive curvature](https://arxiv.org/pdf/dg-ga/9410002), Example 4.1, gives the two-piece orthogonal-basis compatibility obstruction; the complete example on pages 14-15 was read. The source cites Kapovich-Leeb for the broader group-action framework and supplies the minset argument explicitly. This is not a new topology obstruction.

## Checks and limitations

Symbolic Gram-matrix calculations verify the incompatible shear conditions. Exact enumeration of small integral gluing matrices checks when two bases can both be orthogonal for a positive diagonal Gram matrix.

The finite linear algebra does not prove the flat torus theorem or construct the source smooth metric without conjugate points.

## Possible applications

Detecting global curvature obstructions from incompatible geometric structures on a gluing torus.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
