# Bound the degree needed to force minimal Lagrangian intersections

[Family 001](../families/openai-math-001.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000239.md) · [Batch B042](../../batches/B042/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let M be symplectic of dimension 2m over a characteristic-zero field and W a subspace of dimension a. A nonempty open set of Lagrangians L satisfies dim(L intersect W)=max(0,a-m). On any fixed symmetric-matrix big cell, this open contains the nonvanishing locus of one nonzero polynomial of degree at most m, uniformly in W.

For 1<=a<=m, the incidence of lines in W contained in a Lagrangian has dimension a-1+m(m-1)/2<m(m+1)/2, so its image is proper. For a>m use dim(L intersect W)=a-m+dim(L intersect W-perp). The remaining cases are immediate.

On the big cell write L as the columns of [I;T], T symmetric. Compose with M -> M/W. Minimal intersection means rank min(m,2m-a). Since the open meets this cell, some minor of this size is a nonzero polynomial, of degree at most m. If the size is zero use 1. Combining with bounded-degree avoidance allows simultaneous transversality for every W defined over one fixed coefficient field.

## Role in the source argument

Supplies the uniform minor-degree bound needed by the source simultaneous filtration choice.

These finite examples do not prove the incidence statement or construct the source admissible filtration.
