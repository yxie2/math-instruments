# Upgrade strict singularity to a uniformly small restriction

[Family 323](../families/openai-math-323.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000245.md) · [Batch B043](../../batches/B043/README.md)

## Mathematical content

If T:Y -> Z is strictly singular, then for every closed infinite-dimensional M subset Y and epsilon>0 there is a closed infinite-dimensional N subset M such that ||T|N||<=epsilon. A finite collection of strictly singular operators can be made small simultaneously.

Choose unit y_n successively in the annihilators of finite half-norming sets for earlier spans, with ||T y_n||<epsilon 2^(-n)/4 for n>=1. Strict singularity allows this choice inside every finite-codimensional remaining subspace. The norming conditions give prefix norm at most 2 and coefficient bound |a_n|<=4||sum a_i y_i||. Thus ||T sum a_i y_i||<=epsilon||sum a_i y_i||, and closure gives N. Iterate for finitely many operators, since restriction preserves strict singularity.

Merely choosing unit vectors with small images would not control arbitrary linear combinations; the basic-sequence coefficient bound is the necessary additional step.

## Role in the source argument

Finds common infinite-dimensional subspaces on which unwanted operators are perturbatively small.
