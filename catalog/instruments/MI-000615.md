# Convert arcwise measure control into a null trace image

[Family 071](../families/openai-math-071.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000384.md) · [Batch B064](../../batches/B064/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let L be a locally connected compact metric continuum, V relatively open, nu a finite Borel measure, and F contained in V with nu outer measure zero. A function v:F->R need not be measurable. Suppose |v(x)-v(y)|<=A nu(alpha) for every simple arc alpha in V joining x,y in F. Then v(F) has Lebesgue outer measure zero.

Given delta>0, choose relatively open O with F contained in O contained in V and nu(O)<delta. Components O_j are relatively open and at most countable by local connectedness and second countability. Each is arc connected, so diam v(F intersect O_j)<=A nu(O_j). Cover these images by intervals with arbitrarily small summable excess lengths. Their total length is at most A nu(O), which tends to zero. The same reasoning applies on countably many local real branches of a circle-valued trace, yielding Haar-null image. The proof uses outer measure throughout and does not infer measurability of v.

## Role in the source argument

Converts line or arc estimates into compatibility of boundary trace values.

## Antecedents and comparison

The argument is the standard measure-covering mechanism for absolute continuity, phrased for locally connected continua and outer measure. No external earliest-priority claim is made for the source interface.

## Checks and limitations

Finite metric-tree models with weighted edges and arc-controlled values check component diameter and image-cover length inequalities. Nested interval covers illustrate the null-limit step.

Finite trees do not certify the local connectedness of the source generic level continua or its global trace-selection argument.

## Possible applications

Proving a trace image is null even when no measurable representative has been selected.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
