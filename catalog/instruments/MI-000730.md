# Replace Gibbs denominators by additional replicas to pass array limits

[Family 222](../families/openai-math-222.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000492.md) · [Batch B078](../../batches/B078/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Suppose finite arrays of bounded base observables and fresh Gaussian covariance entries converge in distribution. Conditional on the base data, the fresh centered fields have the specified covariance and component independence. Finite Gaussian vectors depend continuously on their covariance even at singular matrices, for example through C^(1/2)g.

For a continuous reweighting c<=A<=C with fixed c>0, set Z=P(A). A bounded continuous r-replica test under the tilted law has expectation E[Z^(-r) P^r(F product_(a<=r) A_a)]. Uniformly approximate z^(-r) on [c,C] by a polynomial. Each monomial Z^j is represented by j additional base replicas evaluated in the SAME fresh fields. The resulting finite-array expectations pass to the limit. Uniform approximation removes the polynomial error. Approximating log z on the same interval handles E log Z.

An independent fresh field for each additional replica would change these moments. In an ultrametric quantization, true diagonal variances and independent residual noise on every visit, even repeated visits to the same leaf, must also be retained. The present extraction does not assume that unrelated observables automatically survive that quantization.

## Role in the source argument

Turns convergence of finitely many replica arrays into convergence after a bounded random Gibbs tilt.

## Antecedents and comparison

Polynomial approximation and replica moment representations are established methods. Exact priority of the source formulation is not established.

## Checks and limitations

Finite random fields compare explicit extra-replica sums with normalization moments, demonstrate failure after refreshing fields independently, check uniform polynomial denominator approximation, and pass a covariance family to a singular limit.

Unbounded reweighting is outside this bounded continuity statement unless uniform integrability is supplied. No perceptron limiting overlap array is proved by these checks.

## Possible applications

Making the denominator and fresh-field dependencies explicit when transferring random measures through a limit.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
