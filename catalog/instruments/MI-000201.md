# Random pair folding makes a simple quotient with a linear collision budget

[Family 181](../families/openai-math-181.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000121.md) · [Batch B012](../../batches/B012/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let J be a finite simple graph and X_i disjoint vertex sets of sizes m_i>=max(8,a^2), with a>=1. Assume degree_J(x)<=a for every x in their union, and |N_J(v) intersect X_i|<=a for every vertex v and every i, including vertices outside the union. There are pairings within each X_i leaving exactly m_i mod 2 vertices unpaired and at most 2 sum_i m_i edges to delete so that identifying paired vertices gives a simple graph. Every quotient edge has one retained original representative, and its order is |V(J)|-sum_i floor(m_i/2), including isolated vertices.

Choose uniform independent pairings, adding a dummy vertex to an odd set. The probability of one prescribed pair is 1/(m-1) for even m and 1/m for odd m; two disjoint prescribed pairs have probabilities 1/((m-1)(m-3)) and 1/(m(m-2)). Let Z count original edges becoming loops plus unordered edge pairs becoming the same nonloop edge.

Loops and common-endpoint collisions contribute at most (4/7) sum_i a^2. Four-distinct-endpoint collisions inside a single X_i contribute at most (16/35) sum_i a^2. Collisions across two folding sets contribute at most (16/49) sum_i a^2, using e(X_i,X_j)<=a*m_j and sum_j e(X_i,X_j)<=a*m_i. Thus

$$\mathbb E Z\le\frac{332}{245}\sum_i a^2\le\frac{332}{245}\sum_i m_i<2\sum_i m_i$$

for a nonempty family. Delete each loop and all but one edge of each parallel class; s-1<=binom(s,2) shows the deletion count is at most Z. For an empty family use the identity quotient. A degree bound only on paired vertices is insufficient: pairing the m-side of K_{m,a} forces am/2 deletions.

## Role in the source argument

Reduces graph order before induction while retaining unique original representatives for later cycle lifting.

## Antecedents and comparison

Random pairing and first-moment cleanup are standard. The selected source package makes both degree and external-neighbor hypotheses explicit and accounts for all collision types. Targeted searches found release-related explanations, but no verified earlier identical lemma.

## Checks and limitations

Read all collision classes, both pairing parities and the cross-set sum. Exact small pairing enumerations check probabilities and expected errors; sampled valid graphs check cleanup and quotient order. Complete bipartite examples check the necessity of external-neighbor control.

The result alone does not lift quotient cycles to simple original cycles: disjoint reserved connecting paths are a separate requirement. The constant two is a convenient bound, not a sharpness claim.

## Possible applications

Controlled graph compression where a simple quotient and a unique representative for each edge are needed.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
