# Persistent correlations at proper subspaces force a dense G_delta generation locus

[Family 296](../families/openai-math-296.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000145.md) · [Batch B018](../../batches/B018/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let U be a complete metric space and H a separable Hilbert space. Suppose closed subspaces H_u are the closures of families of continuous vector-valued functions b(u). Then d_xi(u)=dist(xi,H_u)=inf_b ||xi-b(u)|| is upper semicontinuous, and f_xi(u)=||P_(H_u)xi|| is bounded lower semicontinuous.

Each bounded lower semicontinuous function has a dense G_delta set of continuity points. Intersect these sets for a countable dense set of xi. The uniform bound |f_xi(u)-f_eta(u)|<=||xi-eta|| extends continuity there to every xi.

Assume that whenever H_u is proper, some nonzero xi orthogonal to H_u admits u_l->u and unit W_l in H_(u_l) with |<W_l,xi>|>=c>0. Then u cannot be a simultaneous continuity point: f_xi(u)=0 but f_xi(u_l)>=c. Therefore full-subspace parameters contain a dense G_delta. Their entire locus is G_delta, since it equals the intersection over dense eta_j and n>=1 of the open sets {d_eta_j<1/n}.

In the source H_u=L2(W*(P,u)). For xi=z-E_(N_u)z, s=||xi||_2>0, fix 0<alpha<min(1/4,s/32). The separate perturbation input gives

$$\|u_\ell-u\|_2\le\alpha/\sqrt{2\ell}+1/\ell,\qquad |\tau(\xi^*W_\ell)-\alpha s^2/2|\le4s\alpha^2/(1-2\alpha)+1/\ell.$$

The error term independent of ell is smaller than alpha s^2/4, so the correlation is at least alpha s^2/8 for large ell. Keeping alpha fixed is what makes the contradiction work.

## Role in the source argument

Separates the topological final step from the much harder construction of perturbations with a nonvanishing correlation.

## Antecedents and comparison

The continuity-point and Baire arguments are classical. The source-specific ingredient is the quantitative perturbation input, which this card states conditionally; no proof of that input is claimed here.

## Checks and limitations

Read the full conditional argument. Finite continuous matrix-column families illustrate the lower semicontinuity direction and rank-drop discontinuity, and finite scalar checks verify the stated fixed-amplitude error absorption.

Finite rank examples do not establish a Baire theorem or a factor perturbation theorem. If the guaranteed correlation tends to zero with the perturbation, continuity gives no contradiction.

## Possible applications

Proving generic generation or density of full-span parameters once a persistent-correlation perturbation has been established.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
