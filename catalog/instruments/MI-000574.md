# Use faithful disjoint strips to justify stable group identities

[Family 247](../families/openai-math-247.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000343.md) · [Batch B059](../../batches/B059/README.md)

## Mathematical content

For a unital associative F2-algebra B, let St(B) have the stable Steinberg generators x_ij(a), mapping to elementary matrices. If S,T are disjoint finite coordinate sets, the subgroup generated by x_ij(a), i in S,j in T, is isomorphic to the additive group of S by T entry matrices. The roots commute, root addition collects one coefficient per entry, and the matrix image reads each coefficient directly because all products of these entry matrices are zero.

A word supported on S normalizes the outgoing strip S by T and acts by left multiplication with its S matrix. On the incoming strip T by S it acts by right multiplication with the inverse S matrix. These assertions follow generator by generator from the Steinberg relations, preserving multiplication order over noncommutative B.

If z lies in the kernel of the stable matrix map, take finite S containing a representative of z and the root whose commutation is to be tested, and choose h outside S. Faithful strip actions show that z commutes with x_ih(a) and x_hj(1). Since x_ij(a)=[x_ih(a),x_hj(1)], it commutes with every root, so the stable kernel is central. The availability of a fresh coordinate is essential to this argument; it does not by itself prove injectivity or centrality at a fixed finite rank.

## Role in the source argument

Specifies precisely where equality of matrix entries can be promoted to an abstract Steinberg-group identity.

## Antecedents and comparison

[Weibel, The K-book, Chapter III](https://sites.math.rutgers.edu/~weibel/Kbook/Kbook.III.pdf), Theorem 5.2.1 on chapter pages 35-36, proves stable centrality with the same faithful one-column/one-row and outside-coordinate mechanism. Those pages, including definitions and the full centrality proof, were read. This is a classical instrument.

## Checks and limitations

60 models over the noncommutative coefficient ring M2(F2) verify strip collection and both outgoing and incoming conjugation formulas.

The program checks matrix actions. Faithfulness in the presented group is supplied by the inspected algebraic proof, and no fixed-rank stability or infinite residual-finiteness theorem is certified.

## Possible applications

Safely moving between elementary matrix calculations and abstract root-group relations.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
