# Cap a free word before transferring it to a counting current

[Family 258](../families/openai-math-258.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000364.md) · [Batch B061](../../batches/B061/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Fix an injection theta:Q->F between nontrivial finite-rank free groups with chosen bases. Every nonempty reduced word W in Q has a suffix b of length at most one making Wb cyclically reduced. If Q is cyclic use the empty suffix. Otherwise choose a letter different from the inverses of W's first and last letters. The same choice works for any finite collection of fixed injections out of Q.

The image of the axis of Wb is a uniform quasigeodesic through identity in the target Cayley tree. Its constants depend only on theta, so its distance from the target axis is uniformly bounded. Write red(theta(Wb))=v z v^(-1), with z cyclically reduced and |v| bounded independently of W. Multiplication by theta(b) changes only a bounded suffix of red(theta(W)); cyclic reduction removes a bounded prefix and suffix. Therefore ||eta_(theta(Wb))|| differs from the based length |theta(W)| by a constant depending only on theta.

For each fixed nonempty target word u, its symmetric linear occurrence count N_u+N_(u inverse) differs from the corresponding counting-current cylinder value by O_(theta,u)(1). Endpoint changes affect only boundedly many occurrences, and cyclic wraparound adds at most |u|-1 per orientation, even if |z|<|u|. The cap must precede the injection: arbitrary uncapped words can have unbounded conjugating tails.

## Role in the source argument

Reconciles based annular lengths with cyclic current norms and local word frequencies.

## Antecedents and comparison

The ingredients are standard quasigeodesic stability for finite-rank free-group injections and endpoint occurrence counting. The selected source proof was read fully. No independent novelty claim is made for this bounded-error interface.

## Checks and limitations

Random reduced words are capped once and transferred under identity, a Nielsen automorphism and a conjugated-basis injection. Cyclic lengths and several symmetric pattern counts obey fixed finite error bounds in these explicit models.

The program handles three fixed injections, not a universal Morse constant, and does not certify the source annular-flaring or hyperbolicity theorem.

## Possible applications

Passing from long based words to counting currents while retaining lengths and finite-pattern frequencies.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
