# Normalize expanding bundle maps through a unique determinant minimizer

[Family 294](../families/openai-math-294.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000207.md) · [Batch B034](../../batches/B034/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Suppose continuous bundle maps T_i:E->F over a compact base satisfy dim sum_i T_i(y)S>=2 dim S for every source subspace. Put D_y(P)=sum_i T_i P T_i* and minimize

$$g_y(P)=\log\det(I+D_y(P))-\tfrac32\log\det P,\qquad P>0.$$

Compactness of Grassmann bundles gives epsilon>0 with the 2s-th eigenvalue of D_y(p_s) at least epsilon for every rank-s projection. Thus log det(I+D_y(P))>=2 sum_s log(1+epsilon lambda_s(P)). The scalar function 2 log(1+epsilon t)-(3/2)log t diverges at both endpoints, giving common compact sublevels.

Along P(u)=P_0^{1/2} exp(uH) P_0^{1/2}, the first determinant is a positive exponential sum. Its logarithmic second derivative is the variance of the exponents; the empty and singleton terms make it strictly positive for H nonzero. The second log-determinant term is affine. Hence the minimizer is unique, continuous in y, and invariant under changes of unitary frame, so it glues globally.

With Q=I+D(P), stationarity gives sum_i T_i*Q^{-1}T_i=(3/2)P^{-1}. Therefore X_i=sqrt(2/3) Q^{-1/2}T_i P^{1/2} satisfies sum X_i*X_i=I and sum X_iX_i*=(2/3)(I-Q^{-1})<=(2/3)I.

## Role in the source argument

Provides compatible continuous normalization of the source expanding bundle maps.

## Antecedents and comparison

[Gurvits 2003](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0303055) is the operator-scaling antecedent cited by the source. [Sra and Hosseini, Corollary 2.11](https://optml.mit.edu/papers/sra_hosseini_gopt.pdf) establishes related geodesic convexity of affine log-determinants. The source adds the explicit strictness, compact-sublevel and bundle-gluing argument needed here.

## Checks and limitations

Twenty-four matrix models with guaranteed twofold expansion solve the stationary equations; maximum normalization error below 3.7e-13, and every target bound passes.

Numerical stationarity does not prove global minimality or continuity over a bundle; those assertions rely on the selected proof.

## Possible applications

Continuous balancing of matrix families when a strict subspace-expansion margin is available.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
