# Kill the first prolongation with a passive tensor factor

[Family 041](../families/openai-math-041.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000421.md) · [Batch B068](../../batches/B068/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let T=U tensor W with dim W>1, and h a linear subspace of End(U) tensor id_W. Its first prolongation consists of symmetric bilinear maps B:T times T->T for which B_x belongs to h for every x. Then h^(1)=0.

Fix x=u tensor w_1 and choose w_2 independent of w_1. For every v in U, symmetry gives B_x(v) tensor w_2=B_(v tensor w_2)(u) tensor w_1, where each displayed operator acts on U. The two tensor subspaces U tensor span(w_1) and U tensor span(w_2) intersect in zero. Therefore B_x(v)=0 for all v, and B_x=0. Pure tensors span T, hence B=0. The argument does not require a preferred basis of W invariant under any larger algebra.

## Role in the source argument

Eliminates one possible local connection tensor once a passive auxiliary factor has been identified.

## Antecedents and comparison

This is an elementary first-prolongation calculation within classical prolongation theory. The source uses it inside a larger local tensor obstruction; no new general classification is asserted.

## Checks and limitations

Exact linear systems compute prolongation dimensions for several U,W and contrast W=1, where the full endomorphism algebra has a nonzero symmetric prolongation.

The linear algebra does not verify the source local freeness or global Lagrangian-fibration arguments.
