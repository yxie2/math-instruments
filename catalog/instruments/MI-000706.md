# Close endpoint momentum using an invariant graph before differentiating action

[Family 147](../families/openai-math-147.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000475.md) · [Batch B075](../../batches/B075/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Assume a twist-map collar foliated by individually invariant periodic graphs p=g(theta), with the stated uniqueness of its rational-rotation graphs. A chain with sufficiently small positive angular steps, theta_q=theta_0+2pi l, and interior equations S_2(theta_(j-1),theta_j)+S_1(theta_j,theta_(j+1))=0 determines momenta p_j=-S_1(theta_j,theta_(j+1)), including p_q=S_2(theta_(q-1),theta_q).

The generating relation makes every consecutive pair a map transition, including the last one. The entire orbit segment lies on the invariant graph through its first point. Periodicity of that graph and the endpoint angle advance force p_q=p_0, although endpoint stationarity was not assumed. The segment repeats as a periodic orbit of rotation l/q; rational-graph uniqueness identifies the graph for all such chains.

For a C1 family with fixed q,l, differentiate sum_j S(theta_j,theta_(j+1)). Interior variations cancel. The boundary term is -p_0 theta_0'+p_q theta_q', which vanishes because momenta agree and the endpoint advance is fixed. Thus the action is constant along the family. The invariant-graph input is the step that closes the otherwise unforced boundary term.

## Role in the source argument

Supplies exact periodic action identities before assuming analytic regularity.

## Antecedents and comparison

The first-variation boundary formula and invariant-graph argument are classical. The source uses them after a separate continuous-foliation reduction; that reduction is not independently certified here.

## Checks and limitations

Gauge-transformed integrable generating functions check interior stationarity, momentum closure and phase-independent action for rational rotations.

The tests do not establish the existence or uniqueness of invariant graphs for a general billiard.

## Possible applications

Avoiding an implicit endpoint-stationarity assumption in periodic variational arguments.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
