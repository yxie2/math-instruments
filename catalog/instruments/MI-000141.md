# Causal predictor columns cancel rank-one contractions at a telescoping energy cost

[Family 097](../families/openai-math-097.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000092.md) · [Batch B004](../../batches/B004/README.md)

## Mathematical content

For Euclidean unit-ball vectors $v_i$, choose $0<\sigma<1$, put $b_i=\sigma v_i$, $\eta_i=(1+\sqrt{1-|b_i|^2})^{-1}$ and $C_i=I-\eta_ib_ib_i^T=(I-b_ib_i^T)^{1/2}$. Define coefficient-to-state maps by $R_0x=x_{1:d}$ and $R_ix=C_iR_{i-1}x+b_ix_{d+i}$. Let $x^0$ zero the first d coordinates, and let the predictor rows be $m_ix=\eta_ib_i^TR_{i-1}x^0$.

These rows involve only earlier noise coordinates. If M is their matrix, every column satisfies $|Me_j|^2\le\sigma^2$. For a column injected as b_j, follow $r_i=C_ir_{i-1}$ after its injection; the exact loss $|r_{i-1}|^2-|r_i|^2=(b_i^Tr_{i-1})^2$ telescopes. Since $\eta_i\le1$, it bounds the sum of squared predictor coefficients. Hence

$$\sum_i m_iQm_i^T\le\sigma^2\operatorname{tr}Q$$

for every positive diagonal Q, independently of the sequence length.

If a final coefficient vector has zero initial block and $x_{d+i}=m_ix+\epsilon_i$, then $R_ix=R_{i-1}x+\epsilon_ib_i$ exactly, so all filtered states are the signed prefixes. The source chooses clipped adaptive shifts and imposes final slabs |m_ix|<1/4; causality then proves that clipping was inactive at every assignment.

## Role in the source argument

Connects the adaptive spectral-signing criterion to the separately controlled filtered path body. The small column cost makes all predictor slabs affordable, and cancellation removes the artificial contraction from the final signed walk.

## Antecedents and comparison

Rank-one square-root filters, feedback cancellation and telescoping energy identities are elementary established techniques. The source’s candidate assembly combines them with adaptive spectral signing to obtain simultaneous prefixes. The geometric signing antecedents are [Guo–Fang–Lu](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11189v1) and [Akbas–Sra](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.27172v2).

## Checks and limitations

Finite examples with noncommuting consecutive contractions check the square-root identity, column-energy budgets, causal support and simultaneous cancellation. Zero vectors are included.

The algebra does not by itself produce a bounded signed walk: the length-independent energy estimate for the filtered path body and the adaptive-sign existence theorem remain essential inputs. Final slab membership must be established before removing the clipping operation.

## Possible applications

Building rounding schemes in which a stable auxiliary dynamics is canceled by small causal corrections whose total coordinate cost can be bounded by an energy loss.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
