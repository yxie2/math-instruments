# Prove an expectation inequality with an exact positive certificate modulo a derivative

[Family 281](../families/openai-math-281.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000314.md) · [Batch B055](../../batches/B055/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Consider variables R,K,v,b,j,z,L with B=b-2v>=0, S=z+bv-2Rb>=0 and R,K,v,j,L>=0. Define a derivation D by D(R,K,v,b,j)=(2Rv,2vK+Rj,-Rb,-z-bv,-jv+Kb-L), and W(F)=D(F)+(v+R-K)F.

Set A=1/13, C=53/24, k=19/16, T0=73/48, g=2331/10000, h=87/2500, x=4663/5000, y=6853/10000, m=-1051/400, n=-1073/500. Let Q=Avb+Cv^2+kKv-2kRb+T0 Rv, T=gRv+hjv, and

F2=(Av-2kR)b^2+b[2Cv^2+2kKv+(g+2T0)Rv+hjv]+xv^2j+yv^3+mKv^2+nRv^2.

The identity d_b F2=2Q+T cancels every z-linear term after subtracting (z+Q)^2+ST+W(F2) from z^2-12Rvb^2+6v^4. The remaining polynomial, after retaining L(xv^2+hbv), is the sum of five nonnegative weighted squares and a polynomial with exactly 22 positive rational coefficients. The source lists every square and coefficient; the bundled check reproduces them independently by exact expansion. The v^4 coefficient is c=1661507/1521000000>1/1000.

For the analytic application take R=r^2, K=rs, v=r_x, w=r_xx, z=r_xxx, b=-w/r, j=s_x-(s/r)v, L=-r s_xx, with smooth quotient extensions. If f_x/f=r-s, then f W(F)=partial_x(rfF). Assuming every term integrable, rfF2 vanishes at both infinities, and all displayed signs hold, integration gives E_f[z^2-12vw^2+6v^4]>=c E_f[v^4]. The uncorrected integrand can be negative pointwise; the derivative correction is essential. The algebraic identity alone does not establish the sign or boundary hypotheses for a PDE solution.

## Role in the source argument

Supplies the source conditional strict-curvature estimate on a constant order-parameter interval.

## Antecedents and comparison

[Fantuzzi-Goluskin-Huang-Chernyshenko, Bounds for deterministic and stochastic dynamical systems using sum-of-squares optimization](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1512.05599), Section 4, equations (4.4)-(4.10) and Proposition 3, add zero-mean differential expressions before imposing polynomial positivity. Those passages on PDF pages 8-9 were read. The present certificate uses a spatial divergence at fixed time and specific shape inequalities, rather than their stationary generator; the shared method is classical. No exhaustive novelty claim for the rational coefficients is made.

## Checks and limitations

Exact symbolic expansion verifies cancellation, all five weighted squares, all 22 listed coefficients and the strict rational lower bound. A concrete sign-compatible assignment makes the uncorrected integrand negative, demonstrating that this is an integrated conclusion.

The checks do not prove the source backward/forward shape inequalities, passage to integrable order parameters, full support or QAOA optimality.

## Possible applications

Finding coercive expectation inequalities when pointwise positivity fails but a derivative with zero integral can redistribute the negative terms.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
