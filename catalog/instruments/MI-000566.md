# Cancel singleton tails with an exact distinct-pair coupling

[Family 167](../families/openai-math-167.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000335.md) · [Batch B058](../../batches/B058/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let P have n points, alpha its uniform law, and B a subset of size 2<=k<=n with uniform law nu. Let P_B and pi be the uniform laws on distinct ordered pairs in B and P. Construct R_B by choosing X uniformly in B, then choosing Y uniformly in B excluding X with probability k/n, and otherwise uniformly outside B. When k=n take R_B=P_B=pi. The marginals of R_B are exactly nu and alpha.

For a cell C, put m=|C|, b=|B intersect C|, a=m/n and p=b/k. Direct counting gives P_B(C^2)=p^2-p(1-p)/(k-1), R_B(C^2)=ap-(k/n)p(1-p)/(k-1), and pi(C^2)=a^2-a(1-a)/(n-1). Hence

H_B(C):=P_B(C^2)-2R_B(C^2)+pi(C^2)
=(p-a)^2+(2k/n-1)p(1-p)/(k-1)-a(1-a)/(n-1).

The signed measure has zero total mass and its two marginals sum to zero. It annihilates every symmetric additive kernel h(x)+h(y). In the source, a separate integrated-overlap identity supplies just such a cancellation. Empty and singleton cells contribute exactly zero because every law excludes the diagonal; one must keep this cancellation together rather than integrate separate correction terms over an infinite singleton tail.

On cells with b>=2, P_B(C^2)>=p^2/2. On cells with m>=2, a^2/2<=pi(C^2)<=a^2. These elementary inequalities permit the source to handle large-cell discrepancy and small-cell mass separately, subject to its integrability and averaged-marginal hypotheses.

## Role in the source argument

Prevents infinitely many singleton levels from accumulating a spurious diagonal error in a cell-variance argument.

## Antecedents and comparison

The factors b(b-1)/(k(k-1)) and m(m-1)/(n(n-1)) are classical sampling-without-replacement probabilities. The source explicitly arranges the middle coupling and keeps the signed cancellation intact. No earliest-priority claim is made for that arrangement.

## Checks and limitations

36 distinct-pair models and 7,172 cell identities are checked exactly, including all singleton cells, full-support B=P, marginal identities and cancellation against random additive kernels.

The test does not construct the source infinite hierarchy or prove its integrated additive-overlap identity; the latter is a declared dependency.

## Possible applications

Replacing approximate diagonal corrections with exact cancellation in multiscale decompositions.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
