# Round posterior laws by a barycentric refinement with an explicit reverse garbling

[Family 229](../families/openai-math-229.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000303.md) · [Batch B054](../../batches/B054/README.md)

## Mathematical content

For a uniform three-state input, write the old posterior p(y), marginal rho(dy), and conditional law 3p_i(y)rho(dy). Choose grid weights alpha_y(z)>=0 with sum_z alpha_y(z)=1 and sum_z alpha_y(z)z=p(y). The refined marginal is nu(z)=integral alpha_y(z)rho(dy).

Under input i and old observation y, the correct refinement kernel is alpha_y(z)z_i/p_i(y), with arbitrary choices on null cases p_i(y)=0. Thus the joint law of (I,y,z) is rho(dy)alpha_y(z)z_i. The reverse kernel G(z,dy)=rho(dy)alpha_y(z)/nu(z) is independent of i and recovers the old experiment. Hence observing the labeled grid posterior is more informative in the Blackwell order. Using alpha alone as the input-conditional kernel would be wrong.

For a permutation-symmetric posterior law, one may retain any chosen fraction L_o/W_o in each orbit o and reveal the true input otherwise. Averaging the omitted symmetric mass replaces it by equal masses on the three pure posteriors. This gives an upper completion while retaining the old law as a garbling. Orbit masses can be stored compactly, but the observation remains a labeled posterior: observing only the orbit index of a pure posterior would erase all input information.

These comparisons survive channel composition and conditionally independent experiment products. Their use in a finite certificate still requires checking the actual grid, weights and inequalities.

## Role in the source argument

Provides a rigorously directed rounding and completion operation for the source three-state posterior grid certificates.

## Antecedents and comparison

[Bhatnagar-Maneva, A computational approach to the reconstruction problem](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0903.4812), the survey definitions and Theorems 5, 7 and 8, establish barycentric spreading, composition and convex domination; the relevant definitions and complete proofs on PDF pages 9-12 were read. The selected source makes the reverse garbling and pure-completion bookkeeping explicit. This is not claimed as a new general survey principle.

## Checks and limitations

Exact rational three-state experiments verify the input-conditional refinement kernel, reverse garbling, barycenters and quadratic convex-information increase. Symmetric pure completion and the loss from forgetting labels are checked separately.

The checks do not execute or certify the source full grid computation or establish its threshold theorem.

## Possible applications

Constructing conservative finite posterior approximations while retaining the direction of information comparison.
