# Split a positive dilation balance without subtracting infinite masses

[Family 146](../families/openai-math-146.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000283.md) · [Batch B051](../../batches/B051/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let S be a homeomorphism of a locally compact, sigma-compact space and let mu,nu be locally finite nonnegative Borel measures with S_*mu=mu+nu. Iteration gives mu=(S^(-b))_*mu+sum_(j=1)^b(S^(-j))_*nu. The positive partial sums are dominated by mu, so lambda=sum_(j>=1)(S^(-j))_*nu exists as a locally finite measure.

Define mu_infinity=mu-lambda on relatively compact Borel sets and extend consistently by exhaustion. This is nonnegative and avoids the undefined subtraction infinity-infinity on the whole space. Since S_*lambda=nu+lambda, local subtraction shows S_*mu_infinity=mu_infinity. Equivalently the decreasing remainders (S^(-b))_*mu tend locally to mu_infinity.

If translations tau_r preserve mu and nu and satisfy tau_r S^(-j)=S^(-j) tau_(r/2^j), then each summand, lambda and mu_infinity preserve those translations. In the source, S d(s,t)=d(2s,2t)/2 on the supported distance arrays. To obtain the balance from vague limits, composition with S must preserve compactly supported tests: the source checks properness away from affine arrays and proves that the initial-scale mass vanishes there. Invertibility is only needed on the supported limiting space, where it is established.

## Role in the source argument

Separates terminal-scale contributions from a dilation-invariant remainder before the source final integral balance.

## Antecedents and comparison

This is an elementary positive potential/invariant decomposition, not a new abstract measure theorem. A targeted search found general Riesz-decomposition antecedents but did not yield a checked exact priority reference. The complete selected source proof, including the local subtraction and properness argument, was read.

## Checks and limitations

Exact discrete shift models with nonzero source and nonzero invariant remainder verify the balance, partial sums and limiting decomposition on finite windows. A finite cyclic-space control shows why a finite total-mass model forces nu=0.

The test does not construct or identify the limiting measures from standard-map trajectories, or validate the final entropy contradiction.

## Possible applications

Separating injected mass from recurrent mass in scale-renormalization arguments with infinite total measure.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
