# Retain every threefold numerical class in a product-projective embedding

[Family 055](../families/openai-math-055.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000430.md) · [Batch B069](../../batches/B069/README.md)

## Mathematical content

For a smooth projective complex threefold, rational Chern character identifies Knum_Q with Q direct sum N^1_Q direct sum N_1_Q direct sum Q. Dualization and multiplication by the Todd class are invertible, so the Euler radical agrees with numerical intersection equivalence. For rational ample A, the nondegenerate form (D,D')->A.D.D' makes multiplication by A an isomorphism N^1_Q->N_1_Q. Divisors and their products therefore span the full numerical space.

Given any real ample H*, choose a rational simplex in a transverse ample-cone slice whose interior contains its ray. Scaling its rho(X) independent rays gives very ample L_h with H*=sum_h s_h L_h, all s_h>0. The associated embedding i:X->product_h P(H^0(L_h)^*) has injective pushforward on Knum_R. Indeed ambient line bundles restrict to a spanning family of numerical classes. If i_*v=0, projection formula gives chi(i*W,v)=chi(W,i_*v)=0 for every ambient W, and nondegeneracy forces v=0. The dimension-three divisor-generation step is essential to this argument.

## Role in the source argument

Prevents primitive numerical directions from being discarded when transferring a charge through an ambient product.

## Antecedents and comparison

[Giovenzana, Robotis, Rota and Zuliani, Stability conditions supported on Lefschetz classes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.16013v1), Lemma 4.3, Corollary 4.6 and Example 4.7(2), PDF pages 9-10, give the closely related projection-formula injectivity and threefold case. Those arguments were read. The selected source adds an ample-cone basis adapted to a prescribed real class; no new general injectivity principle is claimed.

## Checks and limitations

Exact Chow rings of P^3 and P^1 times P^2 check divisor-product and line-bundle-character spanning, Euler pairing nondegeneracy and the adjunction rank argument.

Examples check numerical algebra, not the existence of the source stability conditions or its ambient deformation estimates.

## Possible applications

Choosing an ambient embedding that preserves the full numerical lattice rather than a coarse projection.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
