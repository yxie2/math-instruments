# Build a relative monotone zigzag through a greatest new element

[Family 317](../families/openai-math-317.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000222.md) · [Batch B037](../../batches/B037/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let A be a sieve in a poset E and suppose inclusion has a right adjoint retraction r:E->A. If E minus A has a greatest element x, put t=r(x). Define k to be the identity on A and constant t outside A; define g to be the identity on A and constant x outside A.

These maps are monotone. The only mixed case is b in A below a new y: then b<=y<=x implies b<=r(x)=t. The reverse mixed case is excluded by the sieve property. Pointwise, id_E<=g and k<=g, and both comparisons are stationary on A. Also k factors through a retraction E->A. On ordinary nerves this gives a relative homotopy zigzag between identity and the retracted map.

Direct comparability id_E versus k can fail. In the diamond with old chain a<b, new y<x, a<y<x and b<x, the values y and k(y)=b are incomparable; the common upper value x resolves this.

The source uses an additional relative Gray-prism/gluing lemma to transport this zigzag through arbitrary higher-category pushouts. That transport is an external input to this extraction, not established by the elementary poset calculation.

## Role in the source argument

Provides the explicit relative combinatorial homotopy used in the source local couniversality step.

## Antecedents and comparison

Classical monotone-map homotopy on nerves. The reusable feature is the common-upper zigzag and the exact stationarity required for gluing.

## Checks and limitations

Exhaustive naturally labelled posets through five elements produce 603 valid sieve/right-adjoint/greatest-new configurations; every monotonicity and pointwise comparison holds.

The test covers posets and does not build Gray cylinders or arbitrary strict higher-category pushouts.

## Possible applications

Constructing a relative deformation when the obvious retraction is not directly comparable to the identity.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
