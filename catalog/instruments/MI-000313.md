# Rationalize a supporting profile without changing which polytopes contain it

[Family 038](../families/openai-math-038.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000175.md) · [Batch B026](../../batches/B026/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let C be a finite indexed family of rational polytopes, possibly with repetitions. Suppose q lies in their union and a strictly positive vector b satisfies b.q=1 and b.z<=1 on every polytope containing q. Then q and b can be approximated by rational q^0,b^0, with b^0 still strictly positive, so that

$$q^0\in C\Longleftrightarrow q\in C,\qquad b^0\cdot q^0=1,\qquad b^0\cdot z\le1\quad(z\in C\ni q^0).$$

For each polytope containing q, take its smallest face F_C containing q. The functional b is identically one on F_C. Their intersection F is rational and contains q in its relative interior. Choose q^0 rational in F sufficiently close to q to avoid every excluded polytope; finiteness and closedness provide the necessary positive separation.

With q^0 fixed, admissible functionals form the rational polyhedron cut out by c.q^0=1 and inequalities at all vertices of the included polytopes. It contains b. Approximate b by rational points in its smallest face, preserving positivity and any specified open neighborhood.

An additional strict linear budget, such as m sum b_i<n+1, survives by choosing that neighborhood small enough. The two stages matter: unconstrained coordinate rounding does not preserve a supporting equality, even when its numerical error is small.

## Role in the source argument

Replaces real minimizing valuation data by rational data without introducing a forbidden positive-dimensional center or exhausting the strict divisor budget.

## Antecedents and comparison

Rational points are dense in faces of rational polyhedra. The source packages that fact in two stages to preserve the exact indexed incidence pattern as well as the supporting functional.

## Checks and limitations

100 exact rational approximants to an irrational shared-edge profile preserve the included faces, excluded face, equality and an open coefficient budget. Independent rounding fails the equality control.

The examples are not a general polyhedral algorithm. The source existence and geometric properties of the minimizing profile are separate unaudited inputs.

## Possible applications

Converting real optimal data into rational certificates while retaining active constraints and strict slack.
