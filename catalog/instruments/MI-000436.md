# Prescribe one integral moment using balanced valuation nodes

[Family 319](../families/openai-math-319.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000237.md) · [Batch B041](../../batches/B041/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let R=k[[x]] and let distinct nodes u_0,u_1,... lie in xR. Suppose their congruence distances come from nested finite quotients of sizes N_l, enumerated so indices i,j have the same residue at level l exactly when i=j modulo N_l. Put r_m=sum_(l>=1) floor(m/N_l), assumed finite. Then every denominator product_(j!=i,0<=j<=m)(u_i-u_j) has valuation at most r_m: among the other m indices at most floor(m/N_l) share each level-l class.

The weighted evaluation measure

$$D_m=\sum_{i=0}^m\frac{x^{r_m}}{\prod_{j\ne i}(u_i-u_j)}\delta_i$$

therefore has integral coefficients. Its nth moment is zero for n<m, x^(r_m) for n=m, and lies in x^(r_m+n-m)R for n>m. Lagrange interpolation gives the first two identities. The later divided difference is a homogeneous polynomial of degree n-m in the nodes, all in xR. If sum_l 1/N_l<=kappa, then r_m<=kappa m, controlling the integrality cost.

## Role in the source argument

Supplies the source operation measures with exact low-moment cancellation and a linear valuation cost.
