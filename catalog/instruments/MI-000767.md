# Restore whole cube-boundary slots after avoiding a sparse arrangement

[Family 302](../families/openai-math-302.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000510.md) · [Batch B083](../../batches/B083/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let K be compact, f:K->([-1,1]^t)^n and g:K->(-2,2)^(tn) be continuous, with ||g-f||_infinity<1/2. Suppose g has at least s+1 nonzero whole slots at every point, 0<=s<n. Choose a continuous chi on [0,1] that is zero up to 3/4 and equals one at one, and set
g'_j=(1-chi(||f_j||_infinity))g_j+chi(||f_j||_infinity)f_j.
Where chi is nonzero, a coordinate of f_j has magnitude greater than 3/4; the same coordinate of g_j has the same sign and magnitude greater than 1/4. The interpolation cannot make that slot zero. Thus g' retains at least s+1 nonzero slots and agrees with f at every original boundary slot.

The (s+1)st largest slot norm of g' is continuous and positive. Choose 0<delta<1 below its minimum over K. Then G_j=g'_j/max(delta,||g'_j||_infinity) lies in the cube and sends at least s+1 slots to its boundary. It is continuous at zero and fixes every original boundary slot as an entire vector, including its coordinates in the interior of [-1,1].

## Role in the source argument

Making a newly obtained compression compatible with boundary coordinates fixed in earlier steps.

## Antecedents and comparison

The proof uses elementary cutoff interpolation, compactness and radial normalization. Its source role is to preserve prior boundary constraints through successive avoidance steps; no new general normalization principle is claimed.

## Checks and limitations

Finite compact sets with multiple slot dimensions check sign preservation, boundary counts and exact preservation of whole boundary vectors. Small continuous examples check the normalization near zero.

The existence of a sufficiently close sparse-avoiding g is a separate and substantive hypothesis. Pointwise positivity on a noncompact input does not supply the chosen uniform delta.
