# A two-sample density and spectral multiplicity control a matching-cell averaging channel

[Family 126](../families/openai-math-126.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000115.md) · [Batch B011](../../batches/B011/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Fix an even terminal count d>=2 and sufficiently large even k. For parity b choose w in {k/2,k/2+1} with w=b mod 2, and let X be the w-subsets of [k]. Local data m are d ordered distinct terminals and a perfect matching of the remaining vertices, sampled uniformly. For a fixed y in {0,1}^d of parity b, let pi_y^m be uniform on cuts in X with terminal bits y and equal bits on every internal matched pair.

The averaged cut law is uniform on X by permutation equivariance and transitivity. For T_y B(m)=E_{pi_y^m} B, the variance contracts by theta_d(k)=O_d(k^(-1/4)sqrt(log(k+2))). The same coefficient applies to squared Frobenius norms of arbitrary rectangular matrix-valued B, by summing the scalar estimate entrywise.

Here is the reusable spectral certificate. Write s=|y|, h=(k-d)/2, g=(w-s)/2 and g*=min(g,h-g). If K=T_y^*T_y in uniform probability inner products, its two-sample kernel has density P_j/Q_j on intersection class |a intersect a'|=w-2j, where

$$P_j=\frac{\binom gj\binom{h-g}j}{\binom hg},\quad
Q_j=\frac{\binom w{2j}\binom{k-w}{2j}}{\binom kw},\quad
\operatorname{tr}(K^2)=\sum_{j=0}^{g_*}P_j^2/Q_j.$$

Central-binomial estimates bound this trace by O_d(sqrt(k)log(k+2)). Every nonzero permutation-invariant subspace of mean-zero functions on the slice has dimension at least k/2 for k>=6. One proof simultaneously diagonalizes k/2 disjoint transpositions: a nonconstant sign pattern has at least k/2 distinct permutations; if only constant patterns occur, the alternating group acts trivially and transitivity forces constants. Hence a largest mean-zero eigenvalue mu contributes at least (k/2)mu^2 to tr(K^2)-1. This gives the stated contraction.

The matrix-valued function may depend on auxiliary data, provided conditioning fixes the whole function and leaves m uniformly distributed. Conditioning that biases the channel cannot be ignored.

## Role in the source argument

Makes local restriction averaging weak enough to smooth arbitrary matrix factors, uniformly in their matrix dimensions.

## Antecedents and comparison

The eigenvalue-multiplicity mechanism is established; compare [Gowers, Quasirandom groups, Lemma 3.2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0710.3877v1). The source supplies this particular matching-cell channel and its explicit overlap trace. The matrix extension itself is the elementary Hilbert-space sum of scalar inequalities.

## Checks and limitations

Read the exact kernel, binomial and invariant-subspace arguments. Enumerated small cells check the uniform marginal, trace formula, mean-zero spectrum and matrix variance bound; exact rational binomial sums check larger-cell trace values.

The asymptotic holds for fixed d as even k tends to infinity. Small cells need not have a useful contraction constant. This local estimate does not establish the global PSD-rank lower bound.

## Possible applications

Dimension-independent averaging of vector or matrix data on symmetric finite spaces when trace control and representation multiplicity are available.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
