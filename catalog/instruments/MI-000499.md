# Compress a finite distribution while preserving chosen rational features

[Family 125](../families/openai-math-125.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000278.md) · [Batch B050](../../batches/B050/README.md)

## Mathematical content

For finite states s_i with rational probability weights lambda_i and rational feature vectors v_i in Q^d, replace the distribution by one supported on at most d+1 of the same states, preserving total mass and E v exactly.

While more than d+1 positive weights remain, find an integral affine dependence z with sum z_i=0 and sum z_i v_i=0. Such a nonzero dependence has both signs. Set t=min_(z_i>0) lambda_i/z_i and lambda'_i=lambda_i-t z_i. The new weights are nonnegative, preserve the features, and eliminate a positive-support state.

A dependence can be chosen on at most d+2 columns of the augmented feature matrix. Clearing denominators and using minors bounds its bit length polynomially in the original feature encoding. If lambda_i=b_i/D and j attains the minimum, the updated common denominator can be D z_j, with numerators b_i z_j-b_j z_i. Each step adds only a polynomial number of denominator bits, and there are at most M steps. This gives exact polynomial bit complexity for a given finite input list.

Every property true of every retained state survives. Only the chosen feature expectations are preserved: products, correlations and future adaptive transitions need not be. A sequential derandomization must include the statistics needed at each stage rather than assuming the whole law survives compression.

## Role in the source argument

Keeps the companion derandomization distribution lists polynomially supported while retaining the moments used by its estimates.

## Antecedents and comparison

[Bayer-Teichmann, The proof of Tchakaloff's Theorem](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0502473), Corollary 2 and Remark 2, give finite moment matching with mass included as an extra feature; these passages were read. The elimination is the classical constructive Caratheodory proof. The source records an explicit rational denominator bound.

## Checks and limitations

Exact rational models verify support reduction, all chosen expectations and denominator updates. A distribution with preserved first moment but changed second moment is a control against preserving unlisted statistics.

This does not validate whether the companion full algorithm retains every feature needed for its adaptive analysis.

## Possible applications

Finite derandomization, sparse quadrature and scenario reduction with an explicit finite set of required statistics.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
