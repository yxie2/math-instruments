# Exclude equality in a weighted tensor frame inequality by moving projection slots

[Family 219](../families/openai-math-219.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000300.md) · [Batch B054](../../batches/B054/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let d>=3, |p|<2sqrt(d-1)/d, and write the d-dimensional spatial space as Rw plus the zero-sum subspace of R^d, with |w|=1. Put a=d(1-p^2)/(d-1), v_i=pw+sqrt(a)P e_i, where P projects onto that zero-sum subspace. Allow a fixed finite color space. Let P_i project onto span(w,v_i) tensored with colors, and let sigma_i interchange w and v_i orthogonally on this plane, acting identically on colors.

For a symmetric tensor H of order j>=3 satisfying sigma_i^(tensor j) P_i^(tensor j)H=P_i^(tensor j)H for every i, there is c_j>0, depending on the fixed parameters, such that

||H||^2-(1/2)sum_i ||P_i^(tensor j)H||^2 >= c_j ||H||^2.

To see nonnegativity, set e_i^+=(w+v_i)/sqrt(2(1+p)), e_i^-=(w-v_i)/sqrt(2(1-p)), s_i=(e_i^++e_i^-)/sqrt(2), t_i=(e_i^+-e_i^-)/sqrt(2). With q=sqrt(1-p^2)>(d-2)/d, the positive weights b_s=[q-(d-2)/d]/(2q), b_t=1-b_s give sum_i(b_s s_i s_i^T+b_t t_i t_i^T)=I. Apply this frame in one slot and project the other slots into P_i. Swap invariance balances the two contributions.

Equality forces every discarded square to vanish, giving P_i in any one slot of H equal to P_i in all slots. Three distinct slots allow these projections to be moved so that [P_i,P_k] in the first slot annihilates H. Their common spatial kernel is Rw: the transverse projection lines have mutual inner product -1/(d-1). Tensor symmetry then puts every spatial slot in Rw, while swap invariance puts it in Rv_i, forcing H=0. Compactness on the constrained unit sphere gives strictness. Order two fails: the identity tensor has zero loss and obeys the swap conditions.

## Role in the source argument

Eliminates higher Hermite-tensor equality modes in the source projected entropy argument.

## Antecedents and comparison

[Backhausz-Szegedy, On the almost eigenvectors of random regular graphs](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1607.04785), Lemma 11.1 in the retrieved preprint, supplies the weighted frame identity; its proof on PDF pages 30-31 was read. The source cites a published numbering, Lemma 11.3, which was not independently checked. The strict tensor argument was read in the source; no exhaustive priority certification is claimed.

## Checks and limitations

Finite symmetric tensor bases impose all swap constraints by a numerical nullspace calculation and test the restricted quadratic-form eigenvalues. The tight frame is checked directly, and the order-two identity tensor supplies a zero-gap control.

Numerical gaps for fixed parameters do not prove a uniform gap at the spectral boundary, the full entropy inequality or GOE universality.

## Possible applications

Proving strictness of a projection inequality by characterizing equality through tensor slots and noncommuting local projections.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
