# Four-value oracle recovery through a bounded binary feedback recurrence

[Family 241](../families/openai-math-241.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000155.md) · [Batch B021](../../batches/B021/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Suppose F:R->2^N is Borel, has countable fibers, and F(x+y)<=_T F(x) join F(y) for all x,y. For every irrational t in (0,1), a comeager set of pairs (u,v) admits a positive rational delta<1 such that the rational cut of t is computable from

$$F(u)\oplus F(u-v)\oplus F(v-u+\delta t)\oplus F(v-u+\delta(t-1)).$$

Choose the least successful addition-program index at each pair. This is a Borel integer-valued function. Restrict it and F to comeager sets where they are relatively continuous, then require all translations by the countable group H=Q+Qt to remain in those sets. Affine homeomorphisms and coordinate projections preserve the required comeagerness. Near (u,v-u) and (v,u-v), two fixed programs p,q perform the two additions (u+s)+(v-u+a)=v+s+a and (v+s+a)+(u-v)=u+s+a.

Countable fibers let one choose b_-<0<b_+ in a small interval with F(u+b_-) different from F(u+b_+). One bit j differs; relative continuity makes it constant and opposite near the two endpoints. Choose rational delta small enough for these endpoint neighborhoods and the program neighborhoods. Starting at s0=0, set r_n=1 when bit j of F(u+s_n) has the right-endpoint value, and otherwise set r_n=0. Update s_(n+1)=s_n+delta(t-r_n). Near the left endpoint the step is positive; near the right it is negative. This keeps every state between b_- and b_+, regardless of the bit's behavior in the interior.

The four fixed oracles compute each r_n and the next oracle F(u+s_(n+1)) by composing p and q. They need not compute the numerical state s_n. Each query has finite recursive nesting. Summing the recurrence gives s_N=delta(Nt-sum_(n<N)r_n), so, after arranging |s_N|<1, the computable frequency A_N satisfies |t-A_N|<1/(N delta). For any rational query a, search until a lies strictly outside this error interval. Irrationality makes the search terminate.

The finite constants p,q,j and the endpoint bit, and the rational delta, may depend on t,u,v. This is pointwise existence of a reduction, not a uniformly effective choice of those constants.

## Role in the source argument

Recovers the missing real information from a small tuple of affine samples before category avoidance removes auxiliary parameters.

## Antecedents and comparison

[Lutz-Siskind, Theorem 4.3](https://arxiv.org/html/2305.19646v3) recovers a real from four elements of a perfect set together with an oracle computing a countable dense subset. The source instead assumes one Borel map with an addition reduction and prescribes affine sample points. The bounded-frequency identity is elementary; a full historical priority search was not performed.

## Checks and limitations

Read the full proof, including fixed-program neighborhoods and nonuniform constants. Exact rational models test 58 inward-pointing recurrences for 1,000 steps each with unrestricted random interior choices and check the confinement and discrepancy identity.

Finite rational models test the numerical recurrence only. They do not test Borel uniformization, oracle totality, irrational-cut termination or the representation of an arbitrary degree automorphism.

## Possible applications

Recovering a hidden parameter from computable branch decisions when the evolving numerical state is inaccessible.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
