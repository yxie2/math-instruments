# Turn many smooth prime predecessors into large totient fibers

[Family 011](../families/openai-math-011.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000401.md) · [Batch B066](../../batches/B066/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Assume that for a fixed small delta>0 and all sufficiently large X, more than X^(1-delta) primes p<=X have X^delta-smooth p-1. Put k=floor(X^(2delta)), with 3delta<1. Distinct k-subsets give distinct squarefree products m, at least (M/k)^k>X^((1-3delta)k) of them. Their totients are products of p-1, hence X^delta-smooth and at most X^k. There are at most (1+k log X/log 2)^(X^delta)=X^o(k) possible exponent vectors and thus possible totients. Some value n<=X^k has at least X^((1-3delta-o(1))k) preimages, exceeding n^(1-epsilon) when 4delta<epsilon<1 and X is large.

Each fixed fiber is finite: p^a dividing m with phi(m)=n implies p-1 divides n and p^(a-1) divides n, bounding both the primes and their exponents. Therefore the growing lower bounds force infinitely many distinct n. The substantial analytic assertion is the initial supply of smooth shifted primes; the extraction is the conditional combinatorial passage.

## Role in the source argument

Converts a smoothness counting input into a multiplicity theorem for an arithmetic function.

## Antecedents and comparison

The source explicitly attributes this product-and-pigeonhole passage to Pomerance, Theorem B and its proof. That external proof was not independently retrieved in this batch, so the attribution is reported as the source attribution rather than a completed primary comparison.

## Checks and limitations

Exact enumeration of prime subsets checks distinct products, their totients, the exponent-vector count and the finite pigeonhole lower bound.

Small smooth-prime sets do not verify the asymptotic smooth-prime supply or yield a new totient-fiber theorem.

## Possible applications

Producing large fibers whenever multiplicative inputs are numerous but their images use a restricted prime support.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
