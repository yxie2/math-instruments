# Random forbidden classes have a second moment stable under measure changes

[Family 021](../families/openai-math-021.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000102.md) · [Batch B007](../../batches/B007/README.md)

## Mathematical content

At each prime p<=w independently choose one uniform forbidden residue. Let I_j indicate that an integer j avoids all these classes, and let S count survivors in an interval of J integers. Put $V_0=\prod_{p\le w}(1-1/p)$. Then E S=J V0, and for each fixed delta>0,

$$\mathbb E(S-JV_0)^2=o((JV_0)^2)\quad\text{uniformly for }J\ge w^\delta.$$

For nonzero spacing h the pair probability divided by V0 squared is exactly

$$2\mathbf1_{2\mid h}\prod_{2<p\le w}\left(1-\frac1{(p-1)^2}\right)\prod_{\substack{2<p\le w\\p\mid h}}\left(1+\frac1{p-2}\right).$$

Expanding the last product over odd squarefree l gives $\lambda_l=\prod_{p\mid l}(p-2)^{-1}$. The triangular average of $2\mathbf1_{2l\mid h}$ tends to 1/l and is uniformly O(1/l). The series $\sum_l\lambda_l/l$ converges, and its Euler product cancels the preceding product. This proves mean one for the off-diagonal pair factor. The diagonal contributes 1/(J V0)=o(1).

The stable version needs no normalization of the perturbed measure. Let mu be a finite positive measure carrying the same indexed indicators, with mass 1+O(zeta), single-point integrals V0(1+O(zeta)), and for i!=j,

$$\int I_iI_j\,d\mu\le(1+O(\zeta))\mathbb P_{\rm ind}(I_i=I_j=1),\qquad0\le\zeta\le1/2,$$

with uniform constants. Then $\int(S-JV_0)^2d\mu\ll(\zeta+o(1))(JV_0)^2$. Expand the square, using the upper second-moment bound, lower first-moment bound and upper total mass. A zero independent pair probability requires an exactly zero perturbed pair probability; an additive error there is not covered.

## Role in the source argument

Transfers small-variance survival counts to the weighted measures arising from structured prime boxes after the inverse step.

## Antecedents and comparison

The source identifies this as a truncated two-point version of [Gallagher singular-series averaging](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/mathematika/article/on-the-distribution-of-primes-in-short-intervals/DCE557AC8750333E68426FCEEC11858C). [Banks, Ford and Tao](https://arxiv.org/abs/1908.08613v3) provide related random-sieve context. The stable finite-measure formulation is the selected reusable variant.

## Checks and limitations

Read the divisor expansion, Euler-factor cancellation and finite-measure square expansion. Exhaustive small forbidden-class systems verify exact means, pair factors and variances. Controlled density perturbations verify the finite-measure comparison and preserve parity zeros.

The o(1) conclusion is asymptotic with fixed positive delta. Marginal survival estimates alone do not control variance; the pair upper bound and total mass are needed. The verification computes finite identities, not the claimed uniform asymptotic or the full Jacobsthal theorem.

## Possible applications

Second-moment arguments for perturbed sieve distributions when only one-point accuracy and pairwise upper control survive a change of measure.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
