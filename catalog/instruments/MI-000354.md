# Turn summed extension-count errors into a normalized coupling law

[Family 257](../families/openai-math-257.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000196.md) · [Batch B031](../../batches/B031/README.md)

## Mathematical content

For L present branches, let A_i be their nonnegative extension counts, a>0 a common predicted count, Z=sum A_i and D=sum |A_i-a|. Then |Z-La|<=D. When Z>0,

$$d_{TV}\big((A_i/Z)_i,\mathrm{Unif}_L\big)\le D/Z.$$

Thus D=o(La) ensures nonzero normalization and vanishing marginal error. In a random model it suffices that E D=o(lambda a) and L/lambda tends to one in probability: Markov converts expected summed deviations into the required estimate. This controls the average over all present assignments without demanding a simultaneous pointwise estimate for each count.

Apply the same calculation to counts of ordered distinct leaf lists. Uniform sampling of t distinct labels differs from iid uniform sampling on N labels by the collision probability, at most binom(t,2)/N. Exact agreement of branches on a prescribed coordinate set survives normalization because it is a support property of every star.

## Role in the source argument

Builds the probability coupling used for common-coordinate geometric tests from conditional combinatorial counts.
