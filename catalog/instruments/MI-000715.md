# Count square constraints before lifting to a two-list operator bound

[Family 182](../families/openai-math-182.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000482.md) · [Batch B076](../../batches/B076/README.md)

## Mathematical content

For an odd prime p let A(t) indicate the nonzero squares and delta_p=(sqrt(p)+1)/(2p). The normalized kernel A(y-x)-1/2 has constant eigenvalue -1/(2p); its nonconstant Fourier eigenvalues are (G_p(k)-1)/(2p). The elementary change (u,v)->(u-v,u+v) gives |G_p(k)|=sqrt(p), hence operator norm at most delta_p.

For a directed graph with m edges, no loops and at most one edge per unordered pair, independent uniform vertex labels satisfy |E product_(u,v) A(z_v-z_u)-2^(-m)|<=m delta_p. Replace edge factors successively by 1/2. After fixing the other labels, all remaining factors separate between the two endpoints, permitting the single-kernel norm bound. Opposite edges would destroy that separation.

For a kernel K formed only from m constraints between two independent lists, put B=K-2^(-m). Its operator norm to the fourth power is at most the four-cycle moment E B(x0,y0)B(x0,y1)B(x1,y0)B(x1,y1). Expand the sixteen terms. Every copied graph still has distinct unordered edges, the constant terms cancel, and the error is at most 4m(1+2^(-m))^3 delta_p<=32m p^(-1/2). Therefore ||B||_op<=(32m)^(1/4)p^(-1/8). Within-list constraints require separate treatment.

## Role in the source argument

Passes from single finite-field constraints to an operator estimate between lists.

## Antecedents and comparison

Quadratic Gauss sums, edge telescoping and four-cycle Schatten estimates are classical. The card records their explicit combination and graph restrictions, without claiming a new general quasirandomness theorem.

## Checks and limitations

Enumerates small directed graphs, compares Fourier eigenvalues with direct singular values, and checks two-list kernels and fourth moments. A repeated-edge example at a larger prime violates the putative unrestricted counting bound.

The finite checks do not verify reflection-positive tuple laws, lifting moments or the claimed square-difference power saving.

## Possible applications

Testing the exact graph restrictions needed to amplify a single-constraint mixing estimate.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
