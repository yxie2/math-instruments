# A row support function converts column compression into a polar cover

[Family 329](../families/openai-math-329.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000108.md) · [Batch B009](../../batches/B009/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let a real matrix have nonempty finite row set X and column set Y, row vectors R_x, and column combinations f_lambda. Assume distinct rows have sup distance at least one, and the set of f_lambda with ||lambda||_1<=1 has an epsilon-approximation list of size M in the sup norm on X. List centers may be arbitrary functions.

For t>0 put $K=3\operatorname{absconv}\{R_x:x\in X\}+tB_\infty^Y$ and $L=B_\infty^Y$. These are full-dimensional symmetric convex bodies and

$$N(K,L)\ge|X|,\qquad N(L^\circ,(6\varepsilon+2t)K^\circ)\le M.$$

The primal lower bound follows because 3R_x are separated by at least three while any translated unit cube has diameter two. For the dual estimate, the exact support function is $h_K(\delta)=3\|f_\delta\|_\infty+t\|\delta\|_1$, and L-polar is the coefficient ell1 ball. Assign each coefficient vector to its first epsilon-approximant, and choose one actual coefficient representative from each nonempty cluster. Two vectors in a cluster have column-function distance at most 2epsilon and coefficient distance at most two. Their difference therefore has h_K at most 6epsilon+2t, giving the cover.

The small cube ensures interior in the full column space even when the row span is deficient. Choosing representatives inside the coefficient ball controls the thickening term; arbitrary list functions cannot simply be treated as coefficient vectors.

## Role in the source argument

Separates the finite combinatorial construction from the geometric entropy comparison it is intended to violate.

## Antecedents and comparison

This is an elementary support-function and packing argument within the classical [metric-entropy duality formulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0407236). The source records constants and the extra step needed when approximating centers lie outside the column hull; no new polarity principle is claimed.

## Checks and limitations

Read the exact support function and coefficient-cluster construction. Integer matrices and sampled coefficient vectors check the support identity, separation, cluster radii and the necessity of retaining the coefficient term after thickening.

A useful entropy separation still requires a matrix with simultaneously many separated rows and a small column approximation list. The lemma alone does not produce the global counterexample. The cube thickening parameter must be positive.

## Possible applications

Turning asymmetric row and column complexity estimates into covering inequalities for a body and its polar.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
