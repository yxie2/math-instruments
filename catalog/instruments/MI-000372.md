# Remove derivatives of a moving triangular basis by alternating the trace

[Family 063](../families/openai-math-063.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000205.md) · [Batch B033](../../batches/B033/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let A_1,...,A_r be commuting matrices over a field extension L/C, and let A_0 commute with each of them. Use matrix multiplication together with exterior multiplication of Kahler differentials. Then

$$\Theta=\sum_{\pi\in S_r}\operatorname{sgn}(\pi)\operatorname{Tr}(A_0\,dA_{\pi(1)}\cdots dA_{\pi(r)})$$

is unchanged when every A_j, including A_0, is replaced by GA_jG^{-1}, even when G depends on the field variables.

Set B_j(t)=dA_j+t[K,A_j]. Differentiating commutativity gives [A_j,B_l]=[A_l,B_j]. In the derivative of Theta at each factor position, move the degree-zero A_j through the trace. The terms containing [A_j,B_l] cancel in pairs under exchange of the two labels. Thus Theta is independent of t. Taking K=G^{-1}dG proves the formula. The argument never differentiates A_0 and does not suppose [A_0,dA_j]=0.

## Role in the source argument

Permits a variable-dependent common triangular basis without discarding its derivative terms.

## Antecedents and comparison

[Khalkhali, Very Basic Noncommutative Geometry](https://www.math.uwo.ca/faculty/khalkhali/files/BasicNCG.pdf), Section 4.1 and Lemma 4.1, explains generalized trace and the trivial action of inner automorphisms on Hochschild homology. This is antecedent context; the exact displayed form identity is proved in the selected source passage.

## Checks and limitations

Three exact polynomial moving-basis models use nonsemisimple commuting matrices and a nonidempotent selector; the selector does not commute with all differentiated matrices.

Two-form symbolic models supplement the general telescoping proof. They do not establish source quantum eigenvalue independence.

## Possible applications

Differential invariants of commuting matrix families when a simultaneous triangular basis varies with parameters.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
