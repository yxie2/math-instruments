# Bound an adaptively selected witness set by posterior likelihood ratios

[Family 105](../families/openai-math-105.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000231.md) · [Batch B040](../../batches/B040/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Condition on an exterior record under which child blocks are independent. Child C is marked with probability beta; its raw block has base law P when unmarked and a law of density q_C<=L relative to P when marked. Expose raw blocks and independent extra randomness, but neither marks nor metadata revealing them. Bayes gives

$$\Pr(C\text{ marked}\mid X)=\frac{\beta q_C(X_C)}{1-\beta+\beta q_C(X_C)}\le\frac{\beta L}{1-\beta}=:q.$$

A measurable choice of at most m children therefore meets a marked child with probability at most mq. Condition on the exposed data first, sum the conditional probabilities of selected children, then average. The total number of available children does not enter. Coarsening information preserves the bound by the tower property.

If the ambient space is spanned by child subspaces and two data-dependent bilinear forms differ, choose the first pair of child-supported vectors witnessing their difference. At most two children are involved. If unmarked child spaces survive restriction, disappearance of that difference costs at most 2q.

These are unconditional bad-event bounds under the stated exterior conditioning. They may be subtracted from a success probability; they need not remain true after conditioning on success.

## Role in the source argument

Controls adaptive decoding witnesses without paying for every available child.

## Antecedents and comparison

Elementary Bayes and conditional union bounds. The source contribution being extracted is an explicit information boundary, not a new probability inequality.

## Checks and limitations

Sixty-two exact binary data tuples across one to five children check adaptive selection. Revealing a marked event gives a negative control for impermissible conditioning.

The source sampler must separately establish the conditional independence and density hypotheses.

## Possible applications

Adaptive witnesses selected from observations that only weakly reveal hidden modifications.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
