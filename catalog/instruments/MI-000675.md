# Cancel an endpoint lattice contribution by retracting onto deleted faces

[Family 068](../families/openai-math-068.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000444.md) · [Batch B071](../../batches/B071/README.md)

## Mathematical content

For a nonempty closed rational polytope D and polynomial weight W(x,m), the sum over mD intersect Z^s is polynomial at all sufficiently divisible nonnegative exponents, with value W(0,0) at zero. Clear denominators, triangulate, and use the half-open parallelepiped of a lattice simplex. A lattice point of height h satisfies 0<=h<=d. Expanding in products of binomial(l_i,u_i) gives binomial(m-h+d,d+sum u_i), also zero for 0<=m<h. At zero only the origin and zero binomial indices survive. Inclusion-exclusion multiplies W(0,0) by chi(D)=1.

For vectors alpha_i positive under one functional, let u_i=-1 on a nonempty set I and +1 otherwise, and D={x>=0:sum alpha_i x_i=sum alpha_i u_i}. If D is nonempty, set t(x)=max_(i in I) 1/(1+x_i) and r(x)=u+t(x)(x-u). This lies on the union A of faces x_i=0 for i in I, fixes A and preserves the affine equations. The straight homotopy is a deformation retraction, so chi(A)=1. Subtracting those faces therefore cancels the closed count's constant. This explains an endpoint cancellation only under the specified affine sign pattern.

## Role in the source argument

Prevents an eventual counting formula from silently dropping the exponent-zero contribution.

The checks do not verify the fixed-point localization formula or the anticanonical character input in the geometric application.
