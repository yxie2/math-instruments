# Certify a lifted polynomial consequence with modular rank and rational witnesses

[Family 067](../families/openai-math-067.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000206.md) · [Batch B033](../../batches/B033/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Suppose a rational bilinear constraint N(xi_i eta_j)=0 is known. Multiply its equations by extra eta coordinates and identify symmetric products using sorted multisets. Every product vector v_{i,z}=xi_i product_{j in z}eta_j then belongs to a larger linear kernel K; arbitrary vectors of K need not have product form.

Let M be the single rational matrix defining the final lifted kernel, with n columns and denominators prime to p. A modular rank at least n-d gives dim_Q ker M<=d. To turn this bound into a rational certificate, exhibit d rational vectors z_i satisfying Mz_i=0 exactly, and a nonzero d-by-d minor proving their independence. They then form the whole rational kernel. Any rational functional vanishing exactly on those vectors vanishes on every original product solution.

The source applies this architecture to a cubic lift and the functional 32 v_{0,(0,0,0)}-20 v_{0,(0,0,1)}+3 v_{0,(0,1,1)}. With xi_0=eta_0=1 it yields (eta_1-4)(3 eta_1-8)=0, conditional on the reported large-matrix certificate. A rank computed only modulo p cannot by itself prove a rational identity; nor may one assume intermediate rational kernel bases specialize to full modular bases.

## Role in the source argument

Separates a manageable finite-field dimension calculation from the exact rational identities needed for the source numerical constraint.

## Antecedents and comparison

This is standard exact/modular linear algebra and polynomial lifting. Its value is a transparent verification contract, not a new rank principle.

## Checks and limitations

Twenty-seven exact finite kernel certificates and a negative control where modular annihilation fails over Q.

The source 8280 by 6820 matrix, its claimed modular rank 6814, and its six rational witnesses have not been recomputed. This card certifies the general logical architecture and bounded models only.

Auditable computational proofs of polynomial consequences when a direct rational elimination is expensive.
