# Weighted relative moments can be separated into simultaneous embedding coordinates

[Family 094](../families/openai-math-094.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000087.md) · [Batch B003](../../batches/B003/README.md)

## Mathematical content

For distinct $x_1,\ldots,x_n$ in a real $L_p$ space, $1<p<\infty$, write $E$ for the pairs, $\delta_{ij}=\|x_i-x_j\|_p$ and

$$V=\{((z_i-z_j)/\delta_{ij})_{ij\in E}:z\in\mathbb R^n\}.$$

Fix $0<\eta<1/3$ and $b,K\ge1$. Suppose that for **every** strictly positive probability vector $\lambda$ on $E$, a random $F\in V$ exists with $\|F\|_\infty\le K$ almost surely and

$$\sum_e\lambda_e\left|\mathbb E|F_e|^p-b\right|\le\eta b.$$

The distribution may depend on $\lambda$, but $b$ and $K$ must not. Then one obtains an embedding into $\ell_p^d$ with distortion at most $((1+3\eta)/(1-3\eta))^{1/p}$ and

$$d\le\left\lceil C\max\{1,K^{2p}/(\eta^2b^2)\}\log(2n)\right\rceil,$$

where $C$ is absolute. Separate the compact convex set of possible moment vectors from the open coordinate box of radius $2\eta b$. A separating functional $a$, normalized in ell_1, is tested with $\lambda_e=3|a_e|/4+1/(4|E|)$, giving the incompatible bounds $2\eta b$ and $4\eta b/3$. One common distribution therefore exists; bounded independent sampling and a union bound add at most another $\eta b$ per coordinate.

## Role in the source argument

Separates the construction of scalar random increments from the simultaneous embedding requirement. Allowing a common moment b larger than one makes an additive moment error small relative to the metric scale.

## Antecedents and comparison

[Eskenazis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2109.06602) uses Maurey’s empirical method, distance matrices and a change of measure to obtain additive-error dimension reduction for incompressible sets. The source seeks uniform relative control for arbitrary configurations through a weighted test criterion. Convex separation and the concentration step themselves are standard.

## Checks and limitations

Checked the separating-functional argument, positivity of its test weights, the sampling tail exponent and the conversion from pth moments to a common distance scale.

An existential distribution for each pair separately is insufficient. Bounds that depend on the testing probability do not satisfy the hypothesis. This criterion alone provides neither the required distributions nor an efficient algorithm.

## Possible applications

A modular way to assemble metric embeddings from weighted scalar tests, or to identify exactly where a randomized-coordinate construction lacks uniform relative error.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
