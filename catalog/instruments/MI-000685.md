# Linearize a quadratic matrix on angular planes by an explicit Gram factor

[Family 095](../families/openai-math-095.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000454.md) · [Batch B072](../../batches/B072/README.md)

## Mathematical content

For the Choi-Lam matrix Q(y) with diagonal (y1^2+y2^2,y2^2+y3^2,y3^2+y1^2) and off-diagonal -yi yj, write y=(eta cos(theta),eta sin(theta),xi). Let c=cos(theta), s=sin(theta), w=exp(i theta), and
U=2^-1/2[[-w^-1,-w],[s w^-2,s w^2],[c w^-2,-c w^2]],
V=2^-1/2[[0,0],[i,i],[1,1]].
The realification of F=eta U+xi V has four real columns and satisfies F_real F_real^T=Q(y). Check the eta^2, xi^2 and eta xi coefficients separately; the last (2,3) entry uses s cos(2theta)-c sin(2theta)=-s.

Using the four columns as skew-block vectors gives a real symmetric size-20 pencil L_theta in X,Z,eta,xi, linear for fixed theta and with trigonometric coefficients of degree at most three. Its determinant equals p(X,Z,y) first for invertible X and then on the singular boundary by polynomial continuation. Since L_theta(I,I,0,0)=I20, cone membership on the slice is equivalent to L_theta>=0, including zero eigenvalues. Every y lies on such a plane. Producing a single finite global lift from these pencils is a further step, not a consequence asserted by this extraction.

## Role in the source argument

Provides exact local semidefinite descriptions of a matrix polynomial whose angular dependence is nonlinear globally.

## Antecedents and comparison

Gram realification and determinant continuation are classical. The explicit angular factors are the source-specific object. The underlying Choi-Lam positivity is established prior work; no new positivity theorem or global lift is claimed here.

## Checks and limitations

Symbolic trigonometric identities verify every Gram coefficient. Rational points on the unit circle produce exact size-20 slice matrices, with determinant and singular-boundary checks.

The tests do not verify the subsequent degree-two matching identity or moment-based global lift.

## Possible applications

Separating tractable planar slices from the additional compatibility required for a global semidefinite lift.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
