# Use harmonic translation and amplitude variation to force an interaction to vanish

[Family 366](../families/openai-math-366.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000179.md) · [Batch B027](../../batches/B027/README.md)

## Mathematical content

In the source's whole-plane absolute-minimizer setting, write the closed crack as A union B with compact A separated from B. Decompose v=f+F using the preceding compact field p=grad(F), and let G=grad(f). Retain the growth bound integral over B_R of |grad(v)|^2 <= C(1+R). For another allowed compact jump variation let q=grad(P). Write p_t(x)=p(x-t) and I_p(t)=integral G.p_t, with the analogous definition for q.

A compact cutoff of f+F_t+sigma P_t gives, for all sufficiently small translations and every real sigma,

$$0\le2(I_p(t)-I_p(0))+2\sigma(I_q(t)+\langle p,q\rangle)+\sigma^2\|q\|_2^2.$$

The annular gradient error has L2 norm O((1+|sigma|)/R); its cross term with the background is O(R^{-1/2}). Thus no subtraction of two infinite total energies is used.

Because f is harmonic off B and curl(p_t) is supported on A+t, separation makes I_p harmonic in t. The inequality at sigma=0 gives a local minimum at zero, so I_p is constant on the connected translation neighborhood. The remaining quadratic is nonnegative for every sigma and has zero constant term. Its linear coefficient must vanish:

$$I_q(t)=-\langle p,q\rangle.$$

Harmonicity alone is insufficient; minimality is the step that removes the translation dependence.

## Role in the source argument

Turns geometric translations and arbitrary jump amplitudes into exact interaction identities.

## Antecedents and comparison

The strong minimum principle and a quadratic-polynomial minimum are classical. The source contribution under review is the admissible cutoff comparison and its decay estimates in the crack setting.

## Checks and limitations

An exact interaction of two separated unit-disk projected fields is harmonic but nonconstant, showing why minimality is needed. Four hundred twenty amplitude-polynomial cases pass.

No absolute minimizer is constructed or classified by the script. Later deductions removing compact components are outside this extraction.

## Possible applications

Rigidity arguments in which a variationally minimized interaction depends harmonically on translation parameters.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
