# Lift squares and two-square representations in a finite odd-adic algebra

[Family 059](../families/openai-math-059.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000437.md) · [Batch B070](../../batches/B070/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let ell be odd and A a commutative algebra finite as a Z_ell-module, with Jacobson radical J. Then A/J is a product of finite fields, and the J-adic and ell-adic topologies agree: ell A is in J by the geometric-series inverse for 1-ell c, while nilpotence of the radical of A/ell A gives J^N contained in ell A.

A unit u is a square in A if it is a square in every residue field. Lift a residue square root v; both v and 2v are units. Replacing v by v-(v^2-u)/(2v) changes an error in J^k to (v^2-u)^2/(4v^2) in J^(2k), so completeness gives a root.

Every unit is also a sum of two squares in A. In an odd finite field, the sets of squares and u minus squares each have (q+1)/2 elements, so they intersect. Choose such a representation in every residue factor and lift a0,b0. Then c=a0^2+b0^2 is a unit, u/c lies in 1+J and has a square root v. The elements va0,vb0 give u=(va0)^2+(vb0)^2. The algebra need not be etale or reduced.

## Role in the source argument

Constructs scalar operations inside the same commutative operator algebra used for an equivariant isometry.

## Antecedents and comparison

[Stacks Project, Henselian local rings](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/04GE), Definition 10.153.1 and Lemma 10.153.9, gives simple-root lifting and its Newton proof for complete local rings; those passages were read. The selected source packages this with semilocal finite-algebra structure and the elementary two-square count.

## Checks and limitations

Finite quotients of Z_ell[epsilon]/epsilon^2 check Newton lifting and two-square representations, including nonreduced examples; the dyadic unit 3 modulo 8 is a negative control.

Finite precision checks do not prove the source tensor-square isometry or global integral congruence.
