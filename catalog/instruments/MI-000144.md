# Weak convergence plus support-edge convergence controls the capped R-transform uniformly

[Family 234](../families/openai-math-234.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000094.md) · [Batch B005](../../batches/B005/README.md)

## Mathematical content

For a probability tau supported in $[-K,K]$, let $e_\tau=\max\operatorname{supp}\tau$ and $H_\tau(z)=\int(z-\lambda)^{-1}d\tau(\lambda)$ for $z>e_\tau$. For x>0, define $b_\tau(x)$ as the unique solution of $H_\tau(b)=x$ above the edge when it exists, and otherwise set $b_\tau(x)=e_\tau$. Put $R_\tau(x)=b_\tau(x)-1/x$ and $R_\tau(0)=\int\lambda\,d\tau$.

If $\nu_j\Rightarrow\nu$, all supports lie in a common bounded interval, and $e_{\nu_j}\to e_\nu$, then

$$\sup_{0\le x\le1}|R_{\nu_j}(x)-R_\nu(x)|\longrightarrow0.$$

Away from zero, subsequences of inverse values are compact. Limits above the edge obey the inverse equation; a hypothetical wrong edge limit is excluded by evaluating H at a fixed intermediate point. Near zero the uniform estimate

$$R_\tau(x)=\int\lambda\,d\tau+O(K^2x)$$

completes the argument. It follows by expanding $1=\int[1+x(R_\tau(x)-\lambda)]^{-1}d\tau$, using $|R_\tau|\le K$ and $x<(4K)^{-1}$.

For the source functional $\mathcal F(\nu)=\inf_p[S(p)+\tfrac12\int_0^1R_\nu(D_p(r))dr]$, where the common admissible class, S and $0\le D_p\le1$ do not depend on nu, this gives

$$|\mathcal F(\nu_j)-\mathcal F(\nu)|\le\tfrac12\|R_{\nu_j}-R_\nu\|_\infty.$$

No density regularity near the edge is assumed.

## Role in the source argument

Allows approximation by finite spectral alphabets while retaining the edge-capped branch of the variational term.

## Antecedents and comparison

The transform and its edge-sensitive role in spherical integrals belong to the framework of [Guionnet and Maida](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0406121v3). [Fan and Wu](https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.02797v3) study the corresponding invariant Ising setting in the replica-symmetric regime. This card isolates the source’s compactness-and-origin argument, without claiming a new R-transform theory or established priority for the lemma.

## Checks and limitations

Checked both inverse branches and the uniform origin remainder. Computations test atomic approximations and the scalar transform equation. A vanishing-mass distant atom is a negative control showing why support-edge convergence cannot simply be omitted.

The shared support bound and convergence of the upper edges are explicit hypotheses. The conclusion does not by itself prove that the variational functional equals thermodynamic pressure.

## Possible applications

Passing from finite alphabets to rough compact spectral laws in variational problems with edge-capped Stieltjes inverses.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
