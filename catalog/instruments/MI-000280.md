# Weighted adjacent-block success becomes ordinary gap density

[Family 026](../families/openai-math-026.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000159.md) · [Batch B022](../../batches/B022/README.md)

## Mathematical content

For nonnegative random variables W,X,Y, assume Y is either zero or at least one. Write a=E(WX), b=E(WX^2)>0, e=E(WY), and M=E(W^2)<infinity. If Delta=a^2/b-e>0, then

$$\mathbb P(X>0,\ Y=0)\ge \Delta^2/M.$$

Indeed E(W 1_{X>0})>=a^2/b by weighted Cauchy-Schwarz, while E(W 1_{Y>0})<=e. Subtract to obtain weighted good mass at least Delta, then use ordinary Cauchy-Schwarz to remove W. A bound on E(W) alone cannot replace the second weight moment: weights can concentrate all their mass on an arbitrarily small set.

In the source, average over integers m in (X0,2X0], let h=floor(lambda log X0), and let the two variables count log-weighted primes in (m,m+h] and (m+h,m+2h], divided by log X0. A positive variable is at least one. The first weighted moment tends to lambda, the second weighted prime moment is bounded by K_lambda independently of epsilon, and the right-block weighted moment has limsup at most epsilon. Choose epsilon=lambda^2/(2K_lambda) first. A finite bound M_lambda,epsilon for E(W^2) then gives a fixed positive ordinary fraction of successful starts for all large X0.

Assign a successful start to its last left-block prime p. The next prime exceeds m+2h, so the gap after p exceeds h. A fixed p can receive at most h starts, since p-h<=m<p. Thus delta X0 starts yield at least delta X0/h distinct long gaps. Taking lambda>C and X0=floor(p_N/3), the prime number theorem puts all counted left endpoints among the first N primes and converts this into a positive multiple of N. The source's moment estimates, not the elementary transfer alone, supply the needed success events.

## Antecedents and comparison

Weighted positivity and second-moment arguments are standard in sieve methods; [Maynard, Small gaps between primes](https://annals.math.princeton.edu/wp-content/uploads/annals-v181-n1-p07-p.pdf) is a primary antecedent for the source weight framework. The adjacent-block charging and parameter order are the selected reusable formulation, not a certified novelty claim.

## Checks and limitations

Read the complete reduction. Checks cover 500 random finite weighted models and 3,072 exact point-set/block configurations, plus weights concentrated on one point as a control.

The finite models do not establish any prime-distribution estimate. In particular the uniform K_lambda bound and existence of the source weights remain unverified inputs.

## Possible applications

Removing a nonuniform sampling weight while retaining a positive ordinary density and controlling overcounting.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
