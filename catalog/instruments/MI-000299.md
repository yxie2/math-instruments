# Extract the homogeneous term after translation without dividing by factorials

[Family 137](../families/openai-math-137.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000168.md) · [Batch B024](../../batches/B024/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let F be a finite field, 0<=L<|F|, and choose L+1 distinct elements S. Define

$$\omega_s=\left(\prod_{u\in S\setminus\{s\}}(s-u)\right)^{-1}.$$

Lagrange interpolation gives [X^L]p(X)=sum_s omega_s p(s) whenever deg p<=L. Thus, for any homogeneous polynomial Q of degree L and arbitrary vectors a,v,

$$\sum_{s\in S}\omega_s Q(a+sv)=Q(v).$$

The highest coefficient of Q(a+Xv) is Q(v), independently of a. The identity includes L=0, zero v and zero augmentation coordinates. It uses only inverses of distinct-node differences. It remains valid when L is at least the characteristic, where formal differentiation divided by L! is unavailable.

Weights can be recomputed by enumerating nodes, multiplying differences and taking a field inverse. In the source regime L and field cardinality are polynomial in T, node counters and individual field elements need O(log T) bits. Recomputing weights trades time for memory; it does not supply the recursive evaluator's remaining space analysis.

## Role in the source argument

Cancels translations in the source homogeneous lookup evaluation while working in characteristic two.

## Antecedents and comparison

The identity is the leading coefficient of the classical Lagrange interpolation formula. Its use here avoids an invalid factorial-division argument in small characteristic.

## Checks and limitations

225 univariate and 120 translated homogeneous examples pass over F_16 through degree 14. The X^4 characteristic-two control has zero formal derivative but nonzero extracted leading coefficient.

Distinct interpolation nodes and the degree bound are essential. Finite checks do not certify recursive evaluator or machine-simulation space bounds.

## Possible applications

Translation-invariant recovery of homogeneous data over finite fields, including degrees beyond the characteristic.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
