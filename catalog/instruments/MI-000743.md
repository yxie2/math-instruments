# Construct scalar transfer measures while preserving zero and unit eigenvalues

[Family 232](../families/openai-math-232.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000498.md) · [Batch B080](../../batches/B080/README.md)

## Mathematical content

For commuting unitaries V_1,...,V_d, positive Fejer densities N^(-d)||sum_(0<=n_i<N) exp(-i n dot theta)V^n a||^2 have mass ||a||^2 and Fourier moments product_i(1-|m_i|/N)_+ <a,V^m a>. Compactness and uniqueness of trigonometric moments give a positive scalar measure; polarization gives cross measures of total variation at most ||a||||b||. Averaging the unitaries identifies the origin atom with the common invariant projection.

For a commuting unitary U and positive self-adjoint contraction S, the positive operators B_Nk=binom(N,k) S^k(I-S)^(N-k) sum to I. Apply the circle construction to B_Nk^(1/2)a and place its mass at lambda=k/N. Bernstein moment identities converge to <a,U^m S^j a>, yielding a finite measure on circle times [0,1]. Both endpoint atoms are retained.

Its lambda marginal defines F_ab(w)=integral lambda^w d gamma_ab for Re w>0, with 0^w=0. This is holomorphic, bounded by ||a||||b||, and agrees with <a,S^j b> at positive integers. Dominated derivatives use boundedness of lambda^eta |log lambda|^r. No agreement or continuity at exponent zero is asserted: the zero-eigenvalue atom can produce a jump there.

## Role in the source argument

Using analytic matrix elements without silently discarding endpoint spectral mass.

## Antecedents and comparison

These are constructive scalar versions of classical spectral-measure and functional-calculus results, using Fejer and Bernstein approximation. No new spectral theorem is claimed.

## Checks and limitations

Finite commuting matrices verify Bernstein moments, Fejer Fourier coefficients, cross-measure variation, positive complex powers and the exponent-zero discontinuity caused by a kernel projection.

Finite matrices do not establish the source transfer construction or covariance normalization. Positivity of S, not just its norm bound, is essential to this representation.

## Possible applications

Using analytic matrix elements without silently discarding endpoint spectral mass.
