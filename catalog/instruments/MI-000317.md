# A weighted divergence formula separates correctable jet orders from resonance

[Family 048](../families/openai-math-048.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000177.md) · [Batch B026](../../batches/B026/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Use E_t=t partial_t and the meromorphic two-form sigma=t^(-5)dt wedge dz. For D=t^k(bE_t+h partial_z), k>0, and V=t^(ell+1)(AE_t+B partial_z), direct differentiation gives

$$[D,V]=t^{k+\ell+1}\left(\big((\ell+1-k)bA+hA'-Bb'\big)E_t+\big((\ell+1)bB+hB'-khA-Bh'\big)\partial_z\right).$$

The weighted divergence is

$$\mathcal L_D\sigma=t^k((k-4)b+h')\sigma.$$

For k!=4, choosing b=h'/(4-k) makes the flow preserve sigma exactly. At k=4, b disappears from the equation: a nonconstant h cannot be compensated by a vertical coefficient. This is a genuine resonant order, not a removable denominator in the formula.

For a leading field t r E_t, the first order-k correction is Delta A_k=(1-k)br+hr' and Delta B_k=-khr. Higher commutators occur at higher order because k>0. The source uses the time-one flow of -D with the stated pushforward convention, producing exp(ad D) on vector fields.

Split coordinate changes ztilde=psi(z), ttilde=g(z)t with g^4=psi' preserve sigma. Thus the local calculation is compatible with the source fourth-root canonical-bundle convention. Local finite jets and local flows still require separate global compatibility and realization arguments.

## Role in the source argument

Selects transition corrections that change prescribed horizontal jets while controlling the determinant volume.

## Antecedents and comparison

The Lie bracket and density-weighted divergence are classical. The useful source information is the exact coefficient k-4, its resonance, and the leading-action signs.

## Checks and limitations

48 polynomial vector-field brackets and 40 nonresonant volume corrections pass by exact symbolic differentiation. At k=4, h=z supplies an explicit obstruction to cancelling divergence with b.

The checks do not solve the source cohomological obstructions, prove convergence or establish a Hausdorff neighborhood and contraction.
