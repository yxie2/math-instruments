# An entropy optimizer preserves the logarithmic Sobolev deficit under change of reference

[Family 093](../families/openai-math-093.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000090.md) · [Batch B004](../../batches/B004/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Use the convention $\operatorname{Ent}_\nu(g^2)\le2R\int|Dg|^2d\nu$, and put $\lambda=1/R$. The source takes $\nu=\mu*\gamma_s$ with compactly supported $\mu$, $s>0$, and $R>\max\{R^P(\nu),s\}$. It constructs a smooth positive normalized optimizer f satisfying $A_\nu f=\lambda f\log f$, where $A_\nu=-\Delta-D\log\nu\cdot D$. Assume additionally that $\nu=e^{-V}$ is log-concave and $K=D^2V$ is bounded. Put $\eta=f^2\nu$, $h=\log f^2$ and $w=Dh$.

For every probability $p=g^2\eta$ with compact smooth g, the relative Fisher information $\mathcal I$ and relative entropy H satisfy the exact identity

$$\mathcal I(p\mid\nu)-2\lambda H(p\mid\nu)
=\mathcal I(p\mid\eta)-2\lambda H(p\mid\eta).$$

Indeed $H(p\mid\nu)=H(p\mid\eta)+\int h\,dp$, while the Euler equation and integration by parts give $\mathcal I(p\mid\nu)=\mathcal I(p\mid\eta)+2\lambda\int h\,dp$. Hence $R(\eta)\le R$ even though log-concavity of eta is not assumed. The relative score also obeys $A_\eta w=(\lambda I-K)w$ and

$$\mathbb E_\eta\langle w,Kw\rangle\le\lambda|\mathbb E_\eta w|^2.$$

The latter follows by combining the score energy identity with Poincaré under eta.

## Role in the source argument

Turns an entropy extremizer into a new reference measure with the same available entropy coefficient, making its relative score accessible to spectral and prediction estimates.

## Antecedents and comparison

This resembles the established ground-state change of measure for differential operators; the source-specific feature is exact cancellation of the logarithmic Sobolev deficit at the nonlinear Euler equation. The surrounding conjecture and earlier dimension-dependent bounds are discussed by [Bizeul](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.12997). Priority for this exact optimizer formulation has not been established by the targeted comparison.

## Checks and limitations

Checked the two change-of-reference expansions and the sign of the weighted divergence. The bundled Gaussian-tilt calculation verifies the deficit identity in a simple equality model; that model is not a test of the strict optimizer-existence hypotheses.

The optimizer and its operator-domain properties are substantial inputs. An arbitrary positive tilt does not preserve the deficit. The extraction does not assume that the tilted measure is log-concave or reconstruct the source’s existence proof.

## Possible applications

Studying equality and near-equality in entropy inequalities through the tilted law, or testing whether an extremal score is close to the first spectral subspace.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
