# Normalize Gaussian quadratics without a lower covariance eigenvalue bound

[Family 291](../families/openai-math-291.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000509.md) · [Batch B082](../../batches/B082/README.md)

## Mathematical content

For positive matrices S,T, ||sqrt(S)-sqrt(T)||_HS^2<=||S-T||_1. A finite proof sets d=sqrt(S)-sqrt(T), pairs S-T with sign(d), and uses positivity of the diagonal entries of sqrt(S)+sqrt(T) in a d-eigenbasis. For a standard real Gaussian W and symmetric B, q_B=W^T B W-Tr B has variance 2||B||_HS^2 and characteristic modulus product_j(1+4s^2 lambda_j^2)^(-1/4), at most (1+4s^2||B||_HS^2)^(-1/4).

For n unit vectors indexed by E, P_E=n^(-1)sum_(g in E)v_g v_g^T has trace one and rank at most n. Thus q_(sqrt(P_E)) has variance exactly two, even if P_E is singular or its rank grows. In contrast q_(P_E) has variance 2Tr(P_E^2), which can be 2/n. If E,F have n elements, overlapping summands cancel and ||sqrt(P_E)-sqrt(P_F)||_HS^2<=|E symmetric-difference F|/n.

Choose one polynomial p with p(0)=0 and sup_[0,1]|p(s)-sqrt(s)|<eta/sqrt(n). Its Hilbert-Schmidt error on every P_E is at most eta, independent of the ambient dimension. Therefore ||p(P_E)||_HS is between 1-eta and 1+eta, and the same-W quadratic difference is at most sqrt(2)(sqrt(delta)+2eta) in L^2 when |E symmetric-difference F|/n<delta.

Writing R_gh=<v_g,v_h> and xi_g=<v_g,W>, the polynomial quadratic has an explicit sum over words g_1,...,g_r, with coefficient n^(-r)c_r R_(g1,g2)...R_(g(r-1),gr) multiplying xi_(g1)xi_(gr)-R_(gr,g1). This expression can be transplanted as an ordered polynomial even when an empirical covariance array has no operator positivity.

## Role in the source argument

Maintaining randomness uniformly across degenerate covariances while using an exactly equivariant polynomial prescription.

## Antecedents and comparison

The square-root estimate is the classical Powers-Stormer inequality; its original paper metadata was checked at [Powers and Stormer, Communications in Mathematical Physics 16 (1970), 1-33](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01645492), but its full text was not available in this audit. The source finite proof and the Gaussian formulas were read in full. Priority of the combined covariance-normalization interface is not established.

## Checks and limitations

Noncommuting positive matrices check the trace inequality; overlapping singular frames check perturbation and variance bounds. Gaussian quadrature checks the characteristic formula, and direct word sums check the covariance polynomial identity.

These checks do not produce the source commuting rows or prove their joint central limit theorem. The square-root calculus is applied to genuine scalar covariance operators, not to a possibly nonpositive empirical matrix.

## Possible applications

Maintaining randomness uniformly across degenerate covariances while using an exactly equivariant polynomial prescription.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
