# Kill an abelian deck character by integrating the logarithmic energy

[Family 058](../families/openai-math-058.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000435.md) · [Batch B070](../../batches/B070/README.md)

## Mathematical content

A connected regular virtually abelian cover W of a compact connected Kahler manifold has only constant bounded holomorphic functions. Pass to a finite compact base cover so the deck group A is abelian, and pull back a Kahler metric.

Positive harmonic functions normalized by h(o)=1 form a compact convex set in local smooth topology by Harnack and elliptic estimates. An extreme point has a minimal positive ray. For each deck element alpha, cocompact Harnack chains and commutativity give h(alpha x)<=C_alpha h(x) uniformly in x and h. Minimality implies h composed with alpha=c_alpha h. Hence eta=d log h descends to the compact base, although log h itself need not descend.

The identity div(eta sharp)=Delta log h=-|eta|^2 integrates to zero, forcing eta=0 and h constant. Krein-Milman then makes every normalized positive harmonic function constant. Real and imaginary parts of a bounded holomorphic function are harmonic for a Kahler metric; adding constants makes them positive and proves the conclusion.

## Role in the source argument

Rules out a nontrivial bounded holomorphic transverse map on a cover with virtually abelian deck group.

## Antecedents and comparison

[Lyons and Sullivan, Function theory, random paths and covering spaces](https://www.math.stonybrook.edu/~dennis/publications/PDF/DS-pub-0066.pdf), Theorem 1 on printed page 300, proves the stronger nilpotent-cover positive-harmonic statement. That statement and the minimal-ray/character reduction in section 3 were read. The source supplies a direct compact-base abelian argument; the Liouville conclusion is established prior work.

## Checks and limitations

Exact reversible periodic-graph models check the positive-character obstruction through the nonnegative sum r+1/r-2, the discrete counterpart of the integrated logarithmic energy.

Discrete models do not verify elliptic compactness or classify semialgebraic covers.

## Possible applications

Using a descended differential, rather than a descended potential, to rule out positive characters.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
