# Identify Hopf-weight multiplicities with a beta-binomial distribution

[Family 361](../families/openai-math-361.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000295.md) · [Batch B053](../../batches/B053/README.md)

## Mathematical content

For the real degree-l spherical harmonics on S^(2s-1), s>=2, complexify and decompose by bidegrees (b,l-b) in z and conjugate z. The Hopf derivative has eigenvalue i(2b-l). Contraction sum_j d_(z_j)d_(conjugate z_j) maps onto bidegree (b-1,l-b-1): for the monomial norm alpha! beta!, its adjoint is multiplication by sum z_j conjugate z_j, which is injective.

Thus the contribution of b to the multiplicity of -(Hopf derivative)^2 is

h_(l,b)=binom(s+b-1,s-1)binom(s+l-b-1,s-1)-binom(s+b-2,s-1)binom(s+l-b-2,s-1)

=((l+s-1)/(s-1)) binom(b+s-2,s-2)binom(l-b+s-2,s-2).

Missing bidegrees contribute zero. The total N_l=((l+s-1)/(s-1))binom(l+2s-3,2s-3) equals the usual spherical-harmonic dimension. Charges b and l-b give the same squared eigenvalue and are added; complexification does not introduce another factor of two.

The probabilities h_(l,b)/N_l are exactly beta-binomial: take X~Beta(s-1,s-1), then K_l|X~Binomial(l,X). Since E[(K_l/l-X)^2]=E[X(1-X)]/l<=1/(4l), K_l/l converges to X in probability in this coupling. Therefore (2K_l-l)^2/l^2 converges in distribution to Beta(1/2,s-1). The finite multiplicities and limiting law are separate from the later spectral-surplus and geometric realization claims.

## Antecedents and comparison

[Tanno, The first eigenvalue of the Laplacian on spheres](https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/tmj1949/31/2/31_2_179/_pdf/-char/en), Section 3 and Lemma 3.1 on printed pages 181-182, give the classical Hopf-charge decomposition; the complete lemma and proof were read. The exact bidegree counting and beta-binomial argument were checked in the selected source. No priority claim for the distribution formula is made.

## Checks and limitations

Exact polynomial contraction matrices verify small bidegree ranks. Exact combinatorial and beta-integral formulas verify multiplicities, total dimensions, signed-charge aggregation and the mean-square coupling error.

These tests do not establish a spectral surplus at the claimed large cutoff or construct any complete nonnegative-Ricci manifold.

## Possible applications

Replacing high-degree angular multiplicity counts by an exact probability law with a controlled large-degree limit.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
