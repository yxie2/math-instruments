# Recover nonnegative cluster-column coordinates by conditional resampling

[Family 213](../families/openai-math-213.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000486.md) · [Batch B077](../../batches/B077/README.md)

## Mathematical content

In a finite independent hypergraph with every p_e<1, order distinct nodes x_1,...,x_n, not necessarily all nodes. Let E_k say that C(x_k) avoids earlier listed nodes, d_k=P(E_k)>0, and mu_k be its conditional cluster law. Set h_k(i)=mu_k(x_i in U), H=(h_1,...,h_n), and v_k^F(i)=E_mu_k[1_(x_i in U) F(U)]. For nonnegative inclusion-increasing F,
v_k^F-mu_k(F)h_k belongs to cone{h_j:j>k}; in particular H^(-1)v_k^F>=0.

H is unit lower triangular. For S={x_i:i<k}, s=x_k, let W_U=C^(G minus U)(S), c(w)=E F(C^(G minus w)(s)), and PF(U)=E c(W_U). The first-listed-node partition gives v_k^F=v_k^(PF)+sum_(j>k)(d_j/d_k)v_j^(R_F), where R_F(U)=E[1_(s notin W_U)(c(W_U)-c(W_U union U))].

Couple deletion graphs using the same open bits. On s notin W_U, the identity C^(G minus (W_U union U))(s)=C^(G minus U)(s) makes the second cluster shrink as U grows, while the first cluster C^(G minus W_U)(s) grows. Thus R_F is nonnegative increasing. P alternately resamples the separated clusters, so mu_k is stationary. Every row has mass at least a=product_(e containing s)(1-p_e)>0 at {s}, giving oscillation(Pg)<=(1-a)oscillation(g), even at states of zero mu_k mass. Hence P^tF converges uniformly to mu_k(F). Descending induction, iteration of the column identity and closure of the finitely generated cone prove the assertion.

Makes conditional correlation information usable as a nonnegative linear algebra representation.

## Antecedents and comparison

[van den Berg, Haggstrom and Kahn, arXiv:math/0408176](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0408176), Section 2.1, pages 9-11 of the retrieved PDF, was read through the alternating cluster-chain construction and stationarity argument. This establishes the resampling antecedent, not the source column-cone theorem. Exact priority of that stronger finite formulation is not established.

## Checks and limitations

Exact enumeration with rational edge probabilities checks triangular columns, nonnegative inverse coordinates for increasing cluster functions, the first-node mass identity and a separately assembled conditional-resampling matrix.

The checks cover small independent hypergraphs and do not certify any critical-percolation result or extension to dependent edge laws.

## Possible applications

Building nonnegative relay coefficients from conditional cluster laws.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
