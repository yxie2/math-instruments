# Convert a Laguerre-tail recurrence into a strict disk constraint

[Family 371](../families/openai-math-371.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000246.md) · [Batch B043](../../batches/B043/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let M>0, s be nonzero and purely imaginary, t_n=q+n, and rapidly decaying sequences satisfy g_n=B_n-B_(n+1), C_(n+1)=C_n-B_(n+1), and (t_n-M)B_(n+1)=t_n B_n+s C_n. Define

$$p_n=(M/2)|B_n|^2+\operatorname{Re}(s\overline{B_n}C_n).$$

Direct algebra, without dividing by t_n-M, gives p_(n+1)-p_n=(Re t_n-M/2)|g_n|^2. If Re t_n-M/2>0 for n>=K and the tail is not identically zero, decay at infinity gives p_K=-sum_(n>=K)(Re t_n-M/2)|g_n|^2<0. Hence C_K is nonzero and

$$\left|B_K/C_K+s/M\right|<|s|/M.$$

The source has M=ell+5, q=lambda+ell/2-h i b, s=h i Z, so the flux weight is Re lambda+n-5/2. For Re lambda>=-1/32, K>=3 makes it positive. The separate source argument establishing rapid decay and nontrivial normalized tails is required before applying the disk conclusion.

## Role in the source argument

Bounds an infinite recurrence tail by a finite disk for subsequent spectral exclusion.
