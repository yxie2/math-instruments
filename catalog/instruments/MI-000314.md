# A polynomial certificate removes the denominator from a cylinder coordinate

[Family 047](../families/openai-math-047.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000176.md) · [Batch B026](../../batches/B026/README.md)

## Mathematical content

The source starts with P=k[p,s,u,F,J], x=s^2+u^3+p^2F and H=x^2F-(1+2sx)J-p^2J^2-pu. In characteristic zero a locally nilpotent derivation, obtained as -p^2 partial_u in localized coordinates, preserves P and satisfies Delta H=p^3. Thus exp(w Delta) replaces H by H+p^3w through a polynomial automorphism.

Put x_0=s^2+u^3. The determinant-one change

$$L=x_0^2F-(1+2sx_0)J,\qquad M=(1-2sx_0)F+4s^2J$$

has inverse F=4s^2L+(1+2sx_0)M and J=-(1-2sx_0)L+x_0^2M. Over B=k[p,s,u,M], write Q(L)=2x_0F(L)^2-2sF(L)J(L)-J(L)^2. Then

$$H(L)=L-pu+p^2Q(L)+p^4F(L)^3.$$

Set C=Q(0), Q_1=(Q-C)/L and L_*=pu-p^2C; direct substitution gives H(L_*) divisible by p^3. Define h=u-pQ(L)-p^3F(L)^3-p^2w and e_0=-w-pF(L)^3-Q_1(L)h. The decisive certificate is

$$p^3e_0-(L-L_*)=(p^2Q_1(L)-1)(H(L)+p^3w).$$

Hence e is already in T=B[L,w]/(H+p^3w), although localization suggests only (L-L_*)/p^3. One has L=L_*+p^3e and W(e)=-H(L_*+p^3e)/p^3 is polynomial. Since T is a domain with p nonzero, its defining relation gives w=W(e). Localization shows e is algebraically independent over B, so T=B[e]. Transporting these coordinates back by the inverse exponential gives the polynomial cylinder.

The identity at the special fiber, not just a localized inverse, is the reusable certificate.

## Role in the source argument

Provides explicit coordinates for the source cylinder, the positive half of its cancellation claim.

## Antecedents and comparison

[Dutta-Lahiri, Lemma 3.3 and Theorem 3.4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1908.03549) gives earlier exponential and residual-coordinate stabilization methods. The source supplies explicit equation-changing and denominator-removal formulas for its example. This does not establish novelty or correctness of the separate non-polynomiality assertion.

## Checks and limitations

Exact symbolic arithmetic verifies the source relation, derivation, generator nilpotence orders 1,4,2,7,4, linear inverse, root divisibility, ideal certificate and polynomial inverse. The inverse W has 381 terms.

The rigidity proof that A is not polynomial was not audited. The certificate therefore verifies the extracted stabilization mechanism, not a failure of cancellation.

## Possible applications

Turning a coordinate visible after localization into a globally polynomial coordinate by exhibiting an explicit ideal-membership identity.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
