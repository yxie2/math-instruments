# Pay only a root-of-count cost when selecting on one data domain

[Family 140](../families/openai-math-140.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000473.md) · [Batch B075](../../batches/B075/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let finite nonnegative measures eta_j, j=1,...,N, be supported on one measurable domain D of finite lambda-measure. Assume p>1 and integral g d eta_j<=U(integral_D g d lambda)^(1-1/p) for every measurable 0<=g<=1. For routes g_j>=0 with sum_j g_j<=1, put b_j=integral_D g_j d lambda. Holder on the finite index set gives sum_j integral g_j d eta_j<=U sum_j b_j^(1-1/p)<=N^(1/p) U lambda(D)^(1-1/p).

A sufficient condition is eta_j=f_j lambda with ||f_j||_p<=U. Holder and g^(p/(p-1))<=g prove the test estimate. The test formulation itself need not be replaced by a strong Lp-density claim.

If U<=HL r^(beta-m(1-1/p)) and lambda(D)<=V r^m, the radius powers cancel exactly and the selection bound becomes N^(1/p) H V^(1-1/p) L r^beta. The common domain is essential to using sum b_j<=lambda(D); independently chosen reference domains do not automatically give the same normalization.

## Role in the source argument

Controls a finite-memory routing choice among analytic continuation measures.

## Antecedents and comparison

This is a direct classical Holder estimate. Its value here is the shared-domain formulation and explicit cancellation of projection-volume powers, not a new selection inequality.

## Checks and limitations

Finite weighted spaces test fractional routes and density bounds. Disjoint equal cells attain the N^(1/p) factor; scale exponents are checked independently.

The required exact-projection estimates are dependencies, not established by this card.

## Possible applications

Keeping the number of available states from becoming a linear loss in a measure-selection bound.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
