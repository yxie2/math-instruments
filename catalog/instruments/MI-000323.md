# Preserve reciprocal inertia under current-column updates

[Family 369](../families/openai-math-369.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000180.md) · [Batch B027](../../batches/B027/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let M be symmetric with strictly positive entries and suppose its entrywise reciprocal has at most one positive eigenvalue. Fix an index z and c>=0. Then

$$\widetilde M_{ij}=M_{ij}+cM_{iz}M_{zj}$$

has the same reciprocal inertia property. Normalize H_ij=M_iz M_zj/M_ij and a=M_zz. Its z-row is constant a. Schur-complement congruence gives U=a 11^T-H positive semidefinite, with U_ii<a and |U_ij|<a. The normalized updated reciprocal is H/(1+cH), entrywise, and

$$\frac{a}{1+ca}-\frac{H}{1+cH}=\sum_{j\ge0}\frac{c^j U^{\circ(j+1)}}{(1+ca)^{j+2}}\succeq0.$$

This is rank one minus a positive-semidefinite matrix. Negative off-diagonal entries of U cause no difficulty because Schur powers preserve positive semidefiniteness. The direction must be a current column; the statement does not authorize an arbitrary rank-one update.

In a nonnegative chain expansion, admitting a node of weight w sums repeated visits and gives c=w/(1-wM_zz), provided wM_zz<1. Thus the formula propagates reciprocal inertia through finite resolvent elimination.

A related linear-algebra step is useful after a boundary limit: if [[B,ell],[ell^T,0]] has at most one positive eigenvalue, B is nonpositive on ell-perp. For ell_i=1/K_i and B_ij=D_ij/(K_i K_j), D is conditionally negative on sum-zero vectors. When D has zero diagonal, the anchored Gram matrix (D_i0+D_j0-D_ij)/2 is positive semidefinite and represents D as squared Hilbert distances.

## Role in the source argument

Carries a finite reciprocal signature through resolvent updates and identifies the eventual distance geometry.

## Antecedents and comparison

The normalizing Schur complement is the same classical inertia mechanism used in [Agler-McCarthy, Corollary 1.12](https://www.math.wustl.edu/~mccarthy/public_papers/completenevpick.pdf). The explicit update series and chain interpretation are the source-specific packaging reviewed here.

## Checks and limitations

150 sequential column updates and 50 bordered squared-distance models pass. The 45-term Schur series has maximum entry error below 9e-15 in these models.

The finite tests do not prove the boundary kernel has the required bordered inertia. That analytic input remains conditional in the distance-geometric consequence.

## Possible applications

Structure-preserving elimination and conversion of reciprocal kernel inequalities to distance geometry.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
