# Bound message information by the correlation lost under projection

[Family 140](../families/openai-math-140.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000474.md) · [Batch B075](../../batches/B075/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let Q be a joint law of S1,...,St with common marginal p and finite total correlation K=KL(Q||p^t). Let W be finite-valued, with every pair (Si,W) having the same law. Let B be independent of the tuple and W, and Ui=phi_B(Si). Write K' for the expected total correlation of the projected tuple conditional on B. Then t I(S1;W|B,U1)<=H(W)+K-K', with 0<=K'<=K.

Let K_W and K'_W be the corresponding correlations conditional on W and on (B,W). Relative-entropy chain rules before and after projection give the exact identity
t I(S1;W|B,U1)=I((Si);W|B,(Ui))+(K-K')-(K_W-K'_W).
Coordinatewise data processing makes the last parenthesis nonnegative, and the first term is at most H(W). Finiteness of K and W justifies the subtractions. The same argument applies inside conditional experiments when the pair-law and independence hypotheses hold there.

If W is produced from data, sample Si independently from the posterior given those data, independently of W conditional on the data. Each pair (Si,W) then has the original signal-message law. This posterior coupling supplies the required hypothesis; merely matching the Si marginals does not.

## Role in the source argument

Separates message capacity from the geometric task of estimating correlation retained by an independent projection.

## Antecedents and comparison

[Austin, Multi-variate correlation and mixtures of product measures](https://www.kybernetika.cz/content/2020/3/459/paper.pdf), Lemma 4.3 on printed page 474, records established total-correlation identities; that statement was read. [Lelarge and Miolane, arXiv:1611.03888v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/1611.03888v3), Proposition 16 on page 14, states and proves the posterior-resampling identity; both were read. The source combines chain rules into the displayed conditional-information interface; exhaustive priority is not established.

## Checks and limitations

Finite posterior models compute every entropy and correlation term and verify the exact identity for several projections. An equal-marginal but mismatched-pair example violates the proposed bound, testing the essential hypothesis.

These tests do not verify equal-label Gaussian measures, correlation-loss estimates or memory-sample lower bounds.

## Possible applications

Using posterior replicas to divide a message-entropy budget across several statistically compatible signals.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
