# Retain trace-free structure to obtain sharp five-dimensional contraction constants

[Family 367](../families/openai-math-367.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000183.md) · [Batch B028](../../batches/B028/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let A be a symmetric trace-free 5 by 5 matrix with |A|_F=1 and X a fully symmetric trace-free rank-three tensor. With theta=4/5,

$$\|A\|_{op}^2\le\theta,\quad |A^k|_F^2\le\theta^{k-1},\quad |\operatorname{tr}A^k|^2\le\theta^{k-2}\ (k\ge2),$$
$$|\operatorname{tr}A^3|^2\le9/20,\qquad |X:A|^2\le(5/7)|X|^2.$$

For the cubic trace, stationary eigenvalues under sum alpha_i=0 and sum alpha_i^2=1 satisfy one quadratic equation, hence take two values. Multiplicities j and 5-j yield squared cubic trace (5-2j)^2/(5j(5-j)); its largest value is 9/20.

For the contraction, symmetrize zeta tensor A to B_ijk=(zeta_i A_jk+zeta_j A_ik+zeta_k A_ij)/3. Its trace is v=(2/3)A zeta. Its trace-free projection F=B-Jv/7 satisfies

$$|F|^2=\tfrac13|\zeta|^2+\tfrac{10}{21}|A\zeta|^2\le\tfrac57|\zeta|^2.$$

Since <X:A,zeta>=<X,F>, duality proves the bound. Both displayed special constants are sharp for A=diag(4,-1,-1,-1,-1)/sqrt(20), taking zeta in the first coordinate and X proportional to the corresponding F.

When g^2+|p|^2<=1, retain their dependence: for h,b>0, |g|^{2h}|p|^{2b} <= (h/(h+b))^h (b/(h+b))^b. Bounding the two factors separately by one loses this improvement.

Bounds the tensor and scalar factors used in the source graph-remainder estimate.

## Antecedents and comparison

The arguments are constrained eigenvalue optimization, orthogonal projection and the elementary weighted product maximum. They are useful reusable inequalities without an established novelty claim.

## Checks and limitations

Two hundred normalized tensor models pass; the cubic and contraction equality constants are checked exactly.

Random models do not replace the general proof. The full graph bounds and source certificate margins were not audited.

## Possible applications

Sharper error budgets when tensors have trace constraints or scalar factors share a norm constraint.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
