# Common Kraus columns retain conditional partner entropy

[Family 276](../families/openai-math-276.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000084.md) · [Batch B002](../../batches/B002/README.md)

## Mathematical content

For $\gamma,\nu\in[0,1]$, define generalized amplitude damping by

$$\mathcal A_{\gamma,\nu}\begin{pmatrix}1-p&z\\\bar z&p\end{pmatrix}=\begin{pmatrix}1-t&\sqrt{1-\gamma}z\\\sqrt{1-\gamma}\bar z&t\end{pmatrix},\qquad t=(1-\gamma)p+\gamma\nu.$$

Set $v(p)=\gamma\nu(1-\nu)+\gamma(1-\gamma)(p-\nu)^2$ and $e(p)=h((1+\sqrt{1-4v(p)})/2)$ in nats. For every finite-dimensional CPTP partner $\mathcal N$ and pure input

$$\psi=\sqrt{1-p}|0\rangle\psi_0+\sqrt p|1\rangle\psi_1,$$

with unit branch vectors that need not be orthogonal, the source's branch bound is

$$S((\mathcal A_{\gamma,\nu}\otimes\mathcal N)(|\psi\rangle\langle\psi|))\ge e(p)+(1-p)S(\mathcal N(|\psi_0\rangle\langle\psi_0|))+pS(\mathcal N(|\psi_1\rangle\langle\psi_1|)).$$

If $V_\ell$ are Kraus operators of $\mathcal N$, the rectangular matrices with columns $V_\ell\psi_0$ and $V_\ell\psi_1$ retain all three operators $XX^*$, $YY^*$ and $XY^*$. This is the substitution into the thermal block inequality. The function $e$ is convex; recursion and entropy concavity give $S(\mathcal A_{\gamma,\nu}^{\otimes n}(\rho))\ge\sum_j e(p_j)$ for arbitrary mixed input $\rho$ with local populations $p_j$.

## Role in the source argument

Keeps the conditional partner output terms needed to bound an entire Holevo ensemble. Their weighted mean equals the partner marginal of the mean output; an unconstrained minimum-output bound alone would lose this information.

## Antecedents and comparison

[Tang et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28592v1) is a close antecedent for ordinary damping and pure-output channels. The source extends the branch argument through its thermal block inequality. Existing single-use optimizer formulas do not by themselves rule out entangled input advantages. This card records the thermal branch mechanism, with novelty provisional and relative to the sources compared.

## Checks and limitations

Checked the common-column identity for the cross operator, the determinant relation after channel substitution, and the population averaging used in recursion. No orthogonality of the branch vectors is used.

The channel and partner are finite dimensional. The stronger arbitrary-partner conclusion is stated in the manuscript but not in its selected Comparator challenge. Collective decoding is allowed; the product input ensemble does not imply optimal separate measurements.

## Possible applications

Potential use: retain an auxiliary channel or system in an entropy estimate instead of discarding its branch information before optimizing an ensemble.
