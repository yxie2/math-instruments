# Absorb Gaussian center noise while preserving physical oracle queries

[Family 139](../families/openai-math-139.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000169.md) · [Batch B024](../../batches/B024/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Suppose a seeded routine satisfies the deterministic identity Q(x+Delta;z+v tensor Delta/r)=Q(x;z), preserving the same physical oracle queries for every seed and translation. Here z has m vector-valued Gaussian slots, r>0, and the slot vector v has norm at most D_s>0. Put

$$n=\frac r{2D_s},\qquad P=\left(I-\frac{vv^T}{4D_s^2}\right)^{1/2}.$$

The eigenvalues of P lie between sqrt(3)/2 and one. For independent standard Gaussian vectors Z and G of the appropriate dimensions,

$$Q(x+nZ;PG)\ \stackrel{law}=\ Q(x;G).$$

Translate the left side back to x. Its seeds become PG-v tensor Z/(2D_s), a centered Gaussian whose slot covariance is PP^T+vv^T/(4D_s^2)=I. The transformed seed slots therefore have the correct independent standard Gaussian law. They need not be independent of Z, which is no longer an input after translating back to the fixed center.

Leaving the original seed covariance unchanged would inflate it in direction v. In adaptive use, condition on parent information so x and the translation parameters are fixed, and use fresh independent Gaussian variables at this step. Distributional covariance matching alone does not prove that a proposed routine satisfies the required physical-query identity.

## Role in the source argument

Makes the source smoothed-center construction have exactly the original seeded output law without increasing its oracle-query pattern.

## Antecedents and comparison

The covariance identity is standard Gaussian linear algebra. The substantive additional hypothesis is the source deterministic translation identity for the entire physical-query routine; historical novelty of that compilation mechanism was not established.

## Checks and limitations

105 linear query models check the deterministic translation and exact covariance formulas. The largest query discrepancy is below 3.6e-15; using unmodified seeds produces a nonzero covariance error.

These checks do not establish translation invariance for every adaptive subroutine, compiler accuracy or the overall sampling query bound.

## Possible applications

Randomizing a coordinate center without changing the law of a translation-covariant oracle computation.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
