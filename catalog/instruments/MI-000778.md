# Preserve weighted volume while tracking a graph scalar-curvature defect

[Family 335](../families/openai-math-335.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000516.md) · [Batch B084](../../batches/B084/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Write W(h,phi)=R_h-2 Delta_h phi-|d phi|_h^2. For the graph of Nf, put h'=h+N^2 df^2, L=sqrt(1+N^2|df|_h^2), U=N grad_h f/L and phi'=phi-log L. Use the normal nu=(-U,1/L), second fundamental form II=<nabla nu,.> and D=H+partial_nu phi=-div_phi U. Then
W(h',phi')-W(h,phi)=D^2-2U(D)+|II|^2+|d log L|_(h')^2.
Also exp(phi') dV_(h')=exp(phi) dV_h and N^2|df|_(h')^2=1-L^(-2).

The proof combines the weighted Gauss equation with the Jacobi equation for the vertical angle v=1/L. Changing the weight by log v cancels the ambient weighted Ricci contribution. The derivative term -2U(D) remains and cannot be discarded as nonnegative. The identity alone supplies no favorable curvature sign.

## Role in the source argument

Separating the exact geometric bookkeeping from the later choice of a favorable graph and weight.

## Antecedents and comparison

[Perelman, arXiv:math/0211159](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0211159), Section 1.3 on PDF page 6, was read for R+2 Delta f-|grad f|^2 with weight exp(-f); take f=-phi. That antecedent is the weighted scalar expression, not the source graph deformation.

## Checks and limitations

A symbolic one-dimensional graph calculation with nonconstant weight checks every term and the weighted volume equality; removing the derivative term gives a failing control.

The local identity does not prove the existence of a global deformation with controlled error or any simplicial-volume bound.
