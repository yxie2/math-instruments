# A decoding-capacity contradiction forces measure overlap between the two first-bit branches

[Family 153](../families/openai-math-153.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000130.md) · [Batch B014](../../batches/B014/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let X=sum_(i>=0) u_i lambda^i for independent fair bits and 1/2<lambda<1. Define subprobabilities mu_i(A)=Pr(X in A,u_0=i). There is a nonzero finite measure xi dominated by both mu_0 and mu_1. This is overlap of measures, stronger than overlap of their supports, and requires no singularity assumption.

Otherwise mu_0 and mu_1 would be mutually singular. For arbitrarily small eta>0 choose disjoint compact sets K0,K1 with Pr(X in K_(u0))>1-eta. Their separation is delta>0. Choose h with lambda^h<delta/3. The grid variable Q_n=floor(X/lambda^n) has at most L lambda^(-n)+2 values, where L=(1-lambda)^(-1).

Given Q_n and the first i bits, approximate the tail X^(i)=(X-s_(u|i))/lambda^i to error at most lambda^(n-i). For i<=n-h, nearest-compact-set decoding estimates u_i with error at most eta. The binary Fano inequality and entropy chain rule, in nats, yield

$$n\log2\le H(Q_n)+H(u_0,\ldots,u_{n-1}\mid Q_n)\le n\log(1/\lambda)+n h_2(\eta)+O_{\lambda,\eta}(1).$$

Choose eta with h2(eta)<log(2lambda), then let n tend to infinity, a contradiction. The finitely many final bits contribute only to the constant. Mutual singularity is the only negated premise: the conclusion is existence of a common positive measure, not a uniform explicit lower bound for its mass.

## Role in the source argument

Obtains a positive common branch mass before applying the source's singular-concentration argument.

## Antecedents and comparison

[Garsia (1963)](https://msp.org/pjm/1963/13-4/pjm-v13-n4-p09-p.pdf) is an early primary source for entropy and singularity of infinite convolutions. The compact decoding proof here is recorded from the release manuscript; no priority claim for this exact overlap lemma is established.

## Checks and limitations

Read the compact approximation, decoder error and entropy chain. Finite prefix models check the entropy identities and grid cardinality bound; a rational-parameter control shows that atomic truncations can have zero exact first-bit overlap even when the limiting lemma applies.

Finite-grid overlap is not a proof of common mass for the limiting measures. At lambda=1/2 the two first-bit measures are mutually singular up to their boundary, so the strict inequality is essential.

## Possible applications

Ruling out exact symbolic decoding when the bit production rate exceeds the geometric observation capacity.
