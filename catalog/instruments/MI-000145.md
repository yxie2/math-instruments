# Eigenspace symmetry bounds pressure changes caused by spectral multiplicity errors

[Family 234](../families/openai-math-234.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000094.md) · [Batch B005](../../batches/B005/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Fix distinct real eigenvalues $\lambda_1,\ldots,\lambda_m$ with diameter $L=\max\lambda_a-\min\lambda_a$. Let diagonal spectra Lambda and tilde-Lambda have positive integer multiplicities $k_a,\widetilde k_a$, both summing to N. For Haar orthogonal U and any fixed probability prior rho on $\{-1,1\}^N$, set

$$P_N(\Lambda,U)=N^{-1}\log\sum_\sigma\rho(\sigma)\exp\{\tfrac12(U\sigma)^T\Lambda(U\sigma)\}.$$

Then

$$|\mathbb EP_N(\Lambda,U)-\mathbb EP_N(\widetilde\Lambda,U)|
\le\frac L2\max_a\frac{|k_a-\widetilde k_a|}{\min(k_a,\widetilde k_a)}.$$

Arrange the spectra to retain $\min(k_a,\widetilde k_a)$ axes at each eigenvalue, and interpolate by Delta=tilde-Lambda minus Lambda. Convexity bounds the pressure difference between the two endpoint derivatives. At either endpoint, Haar row rotations make expected Gibbs squared projections equal within each eigenspace. A group with q axes, c of which change, therefore contributes the fraction c/q of its total expected squared norm. The total is N, and each changed eigenvalue moves by at most L, proving the bound. Deterministic external fields can be absorbed into the fixed prior.

## Role in the source argument

Controls rounding errors in positive spectral proportions even when the aligned matrices are not close in operator norm.

## Antecedents and comparison

Convex interpolation and rotational invariance are established tools in invariant spin glasses; see [Bhattacharya and Sen](https://arxiv.org/abs/1507.05067v2) and [Fan and Wu](https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.02797v3). The extracted estimate packages endpoint eigenspace symmetry into a relative multiplicity bound. Targeted searches did not establish its historical priority.

## Checks and limitations

Read the alignment and endpoint derivative proof. Finite permutation averaging inside equal-eigenvalue blocks verifies the changed-axis fraction and derivative bound for arbitrary fixed Gibbs weights, including an external-field prior.

This is an expectation under Haar rotation, not a pointwise bound for each U. Every listed multiplicity is positive; a small number of new outlier eigenvalues is not covered by a vanishing relative-error conclusion.

## Possible applications

Removing rational-proportion restrictions in finite-alphabet spectral models and handling deterministic rounding in invariant random-matrix variational arguments.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
