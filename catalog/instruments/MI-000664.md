# Extend a finite cover of a normal subgroup across a lattice quotient

[Family 057](../families/openai-math-057.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000433.md) · [Batch B070](../../batches/B070/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let N be a finitely generated normal subgroup of G with G/N=Z^r. For every finite-index N0<=N there is a finite-index G0<=G with G0 intersect N contained in N0. Take the core of N0 in N, then intersect its finitely many automorphic images to obtain a characteristic finite-index K<=N0. It is normal in G and G/K is finite-by-Z^r.

For an extension 1->F->E->Z^r->1 with F finite, the centralizer E0 of F has finite index and its commutator subgroup lies in the finite central group F intersect E0. If e annihilates F and x_i generate E0, then [x_i^e,x_j^e]=[x_i,x_j]^(e^2)=1. The generated abelian subgroup has finite index; its torsion-free finite-index core is normal free abelian. Multiples of that core separate its nonzero elements, while the finite quotient separates elements outside it. Thus E is residually finite and has a finite-index subgroup disjoint from F. Apply this to E=G/K and pull back. Residual finiteness of the original N or G is not assumed.

## Role in the source argument

Realizes finite covers of a fiber group inside finite covers of the total group when the base group is a lattice.

## Antecedents and comparison

This is a classical characteristic-core and virtually abelian group argument. No general extension theorem for arbitrary quotient groups is claimed.

## Checks and limitations

Exact central extensions of Z^2 by a finite cyclic group check power commutators, finite-index abelian subgroups and simultaneous separation of the finite kernel.

Model groups do not verify finite generation or exactness of the source geometric fiber sequence.

## Possible applications

Transferring statements about all finite covers of a fiber to covers of the total space.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
