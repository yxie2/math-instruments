# Shuffle augmented flags into a product without losing the join sign

[Family 310](../families/openai-math-310.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000172.md) · [Batch B025](../../batches/B025/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Suppose H and K commute and intersect trivially. Adjoin the identity subgroup to X=A_p(H) and Y=A_p(K). Their subgroup products UV, excluding (1,1), form a poset identified with (X^+ times Y^+) minus its least element: intersection with H and K recovers the two coordinates.

For flags u=[U_1<...<U_r] and v=[V_1<...<V_t], sum over words with r letters H and t letters K. Each word traces a monotone grid path; after each step record U_iV_j, with U_0=V_0=1. Give the chain the sign (-1)^nu, where nu counts K-before-H inversions. This defines u boxtimes v in degree r+t-1 and

$$\partial(u\boxtimes v)=(\partial u)\boxtimes v+(-1)^r u\boxtimes\partial v.$$

Faces between unlike steps cancel with the word obtained by interchanging those steps. Remaining faces delete an input vertex, with precisely the displayed signs. Empty flags act as identities, including the empty-empty case in degree -1.

Geometrically, staircase triangulation identifies the product of the two cones. Removing the least vertex gives the radial boundary max(s,t)=1, which rescales to s+t=1, the join. Thus cycles in the two augmented complexes give a cycle in the product-poset model without an exceptional empty-factor convention.

## Role in the source argument

Builds product cycles in commuting elementary-subgroup configurations while retaining coefficients needed by residue detection.

## Antecedents and comparison

This is the classical signed staircase construction for joins, specialized to subgroup products. The commuting and trivial-intersection assumptions justify the coordinate model; they are not consequences of the shuffle algebra.

## Checks and limitations

All 256 pairs of subflags of two four-element flags satisfy the exact signed boundary identity, including empty inputs.

The program checks the chain formula, not nonvanishing of the resulting homology class or the source component-extension theorem.

## Possible applications

Combining augmented cycles while keeping signs and degree shifts explicit.
