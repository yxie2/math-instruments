# Minimize normalized covolume to obtain a stable orthogonal lattice quotient

[Family 239](../families/openai-math-239.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000304.md) · [Batch B054](../../batches/B054/README.md)

## Mathematical content

For a Euclidean lattice L and T>0, minimize det(M)/T^rank(M) over all sublattices, including the rank-zero lattice with determinant one. The minimum is attained: for each rank, a determinant bound bounds a nonzero vector in the discrete exterior-power lattice. Saturating a minimizer in L cannot increase its determinant, so choose a primitive minimizer L_0.

Then det(L_0)<=T^rank(L_0). For every sublattice M of the orthogonal quotient L/L_0=projection_(span(L_0)^perp)L, its full preimage has determinant det(L_0)det(M) and rank rank(L_0)+rank(M). Minimality implies det(M)>=T^rank(M). Thus the quotient, rescaled by 1/T, has no sublattice of determinant below one.

If in addition det(M)>=exp(-Delta)T_0^rank(M) for every M contained in L, where Delta>=0 and T_0>T, comparison at L_0 gives rank(L_0)<=Delta/log(T_0/T). Rank-zero and full-rank minimizers are allowed. This existence and rank bound do not furnish an efficient algorithm for computing the minimizing sublattice.

Removes a controlled-dimensional lattice part before the source projection and Gaussian arguments.

## Antecedents and comparison

[Regev-Stephens-Davidowitz, A Reverse Minkowski Theorem](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1611.05979), Section 2.4 and Proposition 2.5, describe the canonical filtration into scaled stable quotients. The definitions and the proof of its first three properties on PDF pages 12-14 were read. Minimizing log det minus rank times log T is the corresponding supporting-line principle; no new stable-filtration theorem is claimed.

## Checks and limitations

Exhaustive primitive-vector enumeration in two-dimensional integer lattices checks the minimizing ranks, quotient determinant bounds and the logarithmic rank estimate, including rank-zero and full-rank cases.

Low-dimensional enumeration does not verify the source random-matrix hypotheses or supply a high-dimensional polynomial-time minimization algorithm.

## Possible applications

Separating an exceptional low-dimensional lattice component from a quotient with uniform determinant lower bounds.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
