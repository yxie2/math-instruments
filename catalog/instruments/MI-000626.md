# Place cyclic monodromy on a single cut of a skeleton cycle

[Family 019](../families/openai-math-019.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000395.md) · [Batch B065](../../batches/B065/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let a connected graph contain an embedded circle and choose a point eta in the interior of one edge on that circle. Cut there, take p labeled copies, and glue the negative side in copy a to the positive side in copy a+1 modulo p. The resulting degree-p graph covering has monodromy +1 around the chosen circle, hence is connected. Away from eta it is the disjoint union of p copies of the cut graph.

If an analytic curve retracts onto the graph by tau, the same construction pulled back along tau is trivial over any U whose image avoids eta. In the source, eta is chosen at an irrational valuation on an annular skeleton while the ground value group is Q. Its retraction fiber is the singleton eta; placing eta in the prescribed interior region therefore keeps tau(U) away from the cut. The extracted instrument is this topological construction conditional on the skeleton and avoidance inputs. The source subsequently invokes analytic etale-cover constructions, proper GAGA and descent; those steps are not certified here.

## Role in the source argument

Localizes nontrivial monodromy while retaining a chosen exterior sheet.

## Antecedents and comparison

[Baker, Payne and Rabinoff, On the structure of non-Archimedean analytic curves](https://arxiv.org/abs/1404.0279), section 2.3, states the singleton-fiber property at a radius outside the value group. The cyclic graph cover itself is classical. The primary passage was read; no claim about the later algebraization is made.

## Checks and limitations

Finite graph voltage covers check local degree, connectedness, cycle monodromy and splitting after removal of the cut edge. Cutting a bridge gives a disconnected negative control.

Combinatorial edge removal models avoidance of the cut interior. It does not prove existence of the source skeleton, analytic cover or descent.

## Possible applications

Constructing a cover with concentrated monodromy and a trivialization over an exterior region.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
