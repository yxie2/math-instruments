# Project a determinant cone to an exact pseudoinverse epigraph

[Family 095](../families/openai-math-095.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000453.md) · [Batch B072](../../batches/B072/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let Q(y) be an r by r positive semidefinite homogeneous quadratic matrix, n=r+1, and Phi_y([[a,vT],[v,T]])=[[tr(QT),-vTQ],[-Qv,aQ]]. Set p_Q(X,Z,y)=det((det X)Z-Phi_y(adj X)). For fixed y choose n Gram vectors b_j for Q and skew matrices B_j=[[0,b_jT],[-b_j,0]]. Then Phi_y(W)=sum B_jT W B_j and p_Q(X,Z,eta y)=det([[I_n tensor X,eta B],[eta BT,Z]]), with B the stacked B_j.

The identity holds first for invertible X by Schur complement and then everywhere polynomially. Along te-(X,Z,y), e=(I,I,0), its roots are eigenvalues of this symmetric block matrix. Thus p_Q is hyperbolic and cone membership is its pointwise positive semidefiniteness. The chosen factorization need not vary linearly with y.

On X=diag(1,A), projection to (A,Z00,y) is exactly E_Q={A>=0, range Q(y) subset range A, t>=tr(A-dagger Q(y))}. For a singular Schur complement, positivity requires the off-diagonal range inclusion; after it holds, triangular congruence leaves the pseudoinverse residual. The residual here is diag(tr(A-dagger Q),Q). Conversely choose Z=diag(t,Q) to lift every point of E_Q, without taking a closure. A semidefinite lift of the full cone would therefore induce one for this affine image.

## Role in the source argument

Moves a potential lift obstruction from a high-degree determinant to a smaller exact affine epigraph.

## Antecedents and comparison

The block determinant identity, singular Schur criterion and preservation of lifts under affine sections/projections are classical. The source-specific construction packages a quadratic matrix into this cone and epigraph; the extracted step does not establish a no-lift result.

## Checks and limitations

Exact small matrices test the determinant identity at invertible and singular X. Rank-deficient epigraph examples verify the range condition and show a negative eigenvalue if that condition is omitted despite a nonnegative pseudoinverse residual.

The checks do not certify the Hankel seed, finite inverse limits or the source local lift obstruction.

## Possible applications

Retaining singular boundary information when reducing semidefinite representability questions.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
