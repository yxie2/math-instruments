# Read joint tensor annihilation as a bound on the number of active factors

[Family 305](../families/openai-math-305.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000511.md) · [Batch B083](../../batches/B083/README.md)

## Mathematical content

For finite-dimensional V_i and endomorphisms S_i, let N_i=ker S_i. On the tensor product, S_i acts in its own slot and as the identity elsewhere, including passive factors. The intersection of the kernels of every triple product S_i S_j S_k is the sum of tensor subspaces with at least d-2 slots constrained to N_i.

Choose complements V_i=N_i plus M_i. Decompose the domain as a direct sum indexed by the active set A of slots using M_i. For a fixed triple I, the operator kills summands with I not contained in A. On their complementary sum it is the tensor product of the injections S_i|M_i in slots I and identities elsewhere, hence injective. Intersecting these coordinate kernel sums over triples retains precisely |A|<=2.

The images S_i(M_i) may intersect N_i; no direct-sum decomposition of the images is used. Nilpotent S_i are therefore allowed. The same proof works for all k-fold products with at most k-1 active slots, and passive tensor factors do not change it.

## Role in the source argument

Converting a multilinear annihilation condition into a supply of factors that can be replaced by kernel-compatible representatives.

## Antecedents and comparison

This is elementary tensor linear algebra over a field, using injectivity on a complementary DOMAIN. No diagonalization or simultaneous semisimplicity hypothesis is needed. The k-fold extension is an immediate form of the same argument.

## Checks and limitations

Exact matrix ranks compare the stacked triple-product kernel with the predicted tensor sum, including nilpotent, zero and invertible factors and an interspersed passive factor.

Over general rings, tensor products of injections need not remain injective without flatness assumptions. This finite identity does not certify the source disk-embedding obstruction.
