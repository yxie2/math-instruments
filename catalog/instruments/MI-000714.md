# Normalize local root coefficients while retaining progression inheritance

[Family 182](../families/openai-math-182.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000481.md) · [Batch B076](../../batches/B076/README.md)

## Mathematical content

For an intersective h in Z[x] of degree k and positive leading coefficient, fix a p-adic root z_p of multiplicity m_p. Write h(z_p+X)=sum_(j>=m_p)b_(j,p)X^j. The first coefficient has positive valuation u'_p at only finitely many primes: factor h over Z and exclude primes dividing the relevant cleared Bezout identities, derivatives or content.

For those primes set u_p=u'_p+1, D0=product p^u_p and M=product p^(u_p m_p+u'_p). Choose -D0<r<=0 congruent to z_p modulo p^u_p. Then g(x)=h(r+D0 x)/M has integer coefficients and roots w_p=(z_p-r)/D0. Its first nonzero centered coefficient is a unit at every prime. At a bad prime, higher coefficients have valuation at least u_p(j-m_p)-u'_p>=1. Integrality of all centered expansions and translation by integral w_p prove global integer coefficients. Positive arguments remain positive: r+D0 n>=1.

For subsequent scales let lambda(p)=p^m_p extend completely multiplicatively, choose compatible -l<r_l<=0, and define h_l(x)=h(r_l+l x)/lambda(l). These polynomials are integral and intersective; their first centered coefficient keeps its original p-adic valuation. Compatibility gives r_(lD)=r_l-il with 0<=i<D, hence h_l(Dx-i)=lambda(D)h_(lD)(x). This exact identity transfers avoidance on progressions. The ratio l^m_p/lambda(l) is a p-adic unit at p; it need not be an integer.

## Role in the source argument

Makes local arithmetic parameters stable under repeated progression changes.

## Antecedents and comparison

[Lucier, Intersective sets given by a polynomial](https://www.impan.pl/shop/en/publication/transaction/download/product/83175), Section 8, printed pages 81-83, was read, including the proofs of Lemmas 21 and 22. It already gives the auxiliary family and exact inheritance identity. The source adds the explicit preliminary unit normalization; its exhaustive priority is not established.

## Checks and limitations

Exact polynomial arithmetic tests unit normalization with repeated integer roots and multiple bad primes, then inheritance with prime-dependent choices of roots and multiplicities.

Integer-root examples do not cover every intersective polynomial. The tests do not establish exponential-sum bounds or any power-saving exponent.

## Possible applications

Preventing local degeneracy and bookkeeping drift during arithmetic descent.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
