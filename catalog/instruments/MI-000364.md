# Convert small information rate into domination after a likelihood truncation

[Family 078](../families/openai-math-078.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000201.md) · [Batch B032](../../batches/B032/README.md)

## Mathematical content

For probabilities pi absolutely continuous with respect to Lambda, write L=d pi/d Lambda and D=KL(pi||Lambda). The negative information part satisfies E_pi(log L)_-<=1/e because x log(1/x)<=1/e. Hence

$$\pi\{\log L>v\}\le(D+1/e)/v\qquad(v>0).$$

If D=o(ell), choosing v=sqrt((D+1)ell)=o(ell) removes o(1) mass and leaves a subprobability bounded by exp(v)Lambda. This applies with Lambda=pi_C pi_{A|C} pi_{B|C}, whose divergence is I(A;B|C). The conclusion is domination on the exponential scale, not a claim that the original laws are close in total variation.

Conversely, if rho<=K product_i mu_i, then KL(rho||product_i rho_i)<=log K. Expand KL(rho||product_i mu_i) as that total correlation plus sum_i KL(rho_i||mu_i), and use nonnegativity of the marginal divergences.

## Role in the source argument

Moves between entropy-profile independence and pointwise product-law bounds used in geometric incidence comparisons.
