# Pointed wedges preserve AP while accumulating unbounded approximation constants

[Family 330](../families/openai-math-330.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000109.md) · [Batch B009](../../batches/B009/README.md)

## Mathematical content

For countable pointed metric spaces (M_p,o), identify only the basepoints and set the distance between different blocks to d_p(x,o)+d_q(y,o). Each block inclusion and the map collapsing all other blocks to o are pointed 1-Lipschitz maps. Their free-space linearizations I_p,P_p have norm at most one and P_p I_p=Id.

Assume each free space F(M_p) has AP. The maps $R_N=\sum_{p\le N}I_pP_p$ are contractive projections fixing every finitely supported evaluation combination eventually. Density gives strong convergence to the identity, uniformly on compact sets. For a compact C and tolerance epsilon, choose N so that R_N has error below epsilon/2 on C, then choose finite-rank T_p on each retained block with error below epsilon/(2N) on P_p(C). The finite-rank sum $\sum_{p\le N}I_pT_pP_p$ approximates C. Its norm is finite but may grow with C and epsilon, which is allowed for AP.

If for every integer p>=1 a fixed finite subset of F(M_p) obstructs every rank-finite operator of norm at most p at some positive accuracy, the wedge cannot have any finite BAP constant lambda: compress a purported approximant to a block with p>=lambda. The contractive compression preserves its norm and approximation accuracy.

For the source’s 1-separated bounded blocks of diameter B_p, the coefficient map $Jc=\sum_{x\ne o}c_x\delta_x$ obeys $\frac12\|c\|_1\le\|Jc\|\le B_p\|c\|_1$. Testing with half the coefficient signs proves the lower bound; coordinate truncations yield 2B_p-BAP. Thus the wedge construction separates ordinary approximation from a uniform bound on approximants once the quantitative block obstructions have been supplied. A uniformly bounded diameter across all blocks would prevent those obstructions from diverging.

## Role in the source argument

Assembles individual finite-anchor obstructions into one uniformly discrete space while giving a direct proof of ordinary AP.

## Antecedents and comparison

[Kalton](https://kaltonmemorial.missouri.edu/assets/docs/collect2004.pdf) already proves AP for uniformly discrete free spaces. The bounded-block coefficient identification is part of the established [Lipschitz-free-space theory](https://arxiv.org/abs/1811.01265v2). This card isolates the retraction and order-of-approximation argument; the new obstruction would have to come from the blocks.

## Checks and limitations

Read the wedge triangle inequality, linearization and compact-net argument. Finite metric wedges and numerical scalar-test linear programs check the coefficient bounds, block retractions and operator compressions. Finite examples do not establish failure of BAP in an infinite space.

BAP of each block alone does not imply BAP of the wedge with a uniform constant. AP approximants are chosen only after truncating the compact set to finitely many blocks. The source’s final metric is unbounded and nonproper; no proper-space counterexample is inferred.

## Possible applications

Building a single space with local approximation but no globally bounded approximation constant from a sequence of complemented obstructions.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
