# Control functional calculus with a class of state-continuous right multipliers

[Family 300](../families/openai-math-300.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000374.md) · [Batch B062](../../batches/B062/README.md)

## Mathematical content

For a faithful normal state phi, let R_phi consist of uniformly operator-bounded sequences R_n such that ||D_n R_n||_phi tends to zero whenever D_n are uniformly bounded and ||D_n||_phi tends to zero. This is an algebra, closed in the uniform operator norm. It contains constant sequences, bounded centralizer sequences, and bounded sequences converging in state norm. Adding a bounded state-null sequence preserves membership, since ||D_n E_n||_phi<=||D_n|| ||E_n||_phi.

For a fixed right multiplier z, choose c prime in the commutant with c prime xi close to z xi. Then ||Dz xi||<=||D|| ||z xi-c prime xi||+||c prime|| ||D xi||. This proves uniform continuity for bounded D, and a finite net extends it to state-precompact right multipliers. For b in the centralizer, ||Db||_phi<=||b|| ||D||_phi.

If self-adjoint U_n,V_n belong to R_phi, have a common operator bound, and ||U_n-V_n||_phi tends to zero, then every continuous f on a common spectral interval gives f(U_n),f(V_n) in R_phi and ||f(U_n)-f(V_n)||_phi tending to zero. Telescope powers, use the right-multiplier property for each term, and approximate f uniformly by polynomials. Arbitrary bounded moving right multipliers do not suffice: with diagonal state weights w_j tending to zero, D_n=e_nn has state norm sqrt(w_n), but D_n e_n0=e_n0 has the fixed norm sqrt(w_0).

## Role in the source argument

Justifies cutoff and functional-calculus transfers in a nontracial state norm.

## Antecedents and comparison

The proof is classical bounded-set strong continuity made explicit for sequences. The source packages the required right-multiplier hypothesis for its paving construction. No uniform state-norm right-multiplication bound is asserted outside that class.

## Checks and limitations

Growing matrix corners of a fixed diagonal trace-class state exhibit the moving-multiplier obstruction. Centralizer controls and continuous spectral cutoffs check the permitted estimates in explicit models.

Finite corner sequences illustrate the topology but do not establish a dimension-uniform paving bound or all infinite-dimensional functional-calculus limits.

## Possible applications

Preventing an invalid tracial estimate when passing small state errors through products and cutoffs.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
