# Correct mixed central inputs by an explicit bounded Hochschild homotopy

[Family 295](../families/openai-math-295.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000208.md) · [Batch B034](../../batches/B034/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let z be a central projection in a unital algebra M, put q=1-z, and let psi be an n-cochain with values in the bimodule zM. Define cut_z psi(a_1,...,a_n)=psi(za_1,...,za_n). For n>=2 set

$$(J_z\psi)(a_1,\ldots,a_{n-1})=\sum_{i=1}^{n-1}(-1)^i\psi(za_1,\ldots,za_{i-1},q,qa_i,a_{i+1},\ldots,a_{n-1}),$$

and put J_z=0 in degrees zero and one. Then dJ_z+J_zd=id-cut_z, in every degree. For bounded cochains on a C*-algebra, ||J_z psi||<=(n-1)||psi||.

Expand inputs into zM and qM. For a tuple with first qM input at position r, exactly the term inserting q before that input survives in J. In the differential, merging that q with the input gives psi; all other terms cancel or vanish because zMqM=0 and q annihilates the output module. All-zM tuples give zero on both sides. Thus for a cocycle d(J_z psi)=psi-cut_z psi, correcting the part a primitive restricted to zM misses.

## Role in the source argument

Allows separately constructed primitives on central summands to be assembled without losing control of mixed inputs.

## Antecedents and comparison

This is a bar-complex contraction specialized to a central decomposition. The explicit norm bound is independent of the algebra and the projection.

## Checks and limitations

Two hundred forty exact tests in the noncommutative algebra M2 plus C, degrees one through four, use arbitrary multilinear cochains with values in M2.

The tests do not construct primitives on the individual summands or prove the source cohomology-vanishing theorem.

## Possible applications

Patching cohomological solutions across central decompositions while controlling arbitrary mixed arguments.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
