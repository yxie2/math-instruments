# Use transitive Gaussian symmetry to make exponential augmentation log-concave

[Family 217](../families/openai-math-217.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000298.md) · [Batch B054](../../batches/B054/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let g be a standard Gaussian vector and F(g)=log(M^(-1)sum_sigma exp(a_sigma.g)). Assume a finite family of orthogonal symmetries preserves F and acts transitively on the labels of the linear forms. If E is an independent mean-one exponential variable, G=F+E has density

q(z)=exp(-z) E[exp(F(g)) 1_(F(g)<=z)].

Expanding exp(F) and applying the transitive symmetries makes every tilted integral equal. Hence q is a constant times the g-marginal of exp(-|g|^2/2+a_*.g-z)1_(F(g)<=z). The epigraph is convex and this joint density is log-concave. It is integrable because exp(a_*.g-F(g))<=M. Prekopa's theorem makes q log-concave.

Moreover Var(G)=Var(F)+1, and the centered logarithmic moment-generating functions satisfy K_G(t)=K_F(t)-t-log(1-t) for 0<=t<1. The symmetry step is essential: a sum of differently tilted log-concave marginals is not automatically log-concave. The statement does not apply to every convex function of a Gaussian vector.

## Role in the source argument

Allows the source to apply one-dimensional log-concave distribution estimates after adding one controlled independent variable.

## Antecedents and comparison

[Prekopa, On logarithmic concave measures and functions](https://rutcor.rutgers.edu/Prekopa/pdf/SCIENT2.pdf), Theorems 6 and 8, give the classical marginalization principle; the relevant theorem statements and the marginal proof were read. The transitive reduction of this augmented partition function was checked in the source. No new marginal theorem is claimed.

## Checks and limitations

For F=log cosh(a g), deterministic quadrature is compared with the explicit tilted-normal density, and midpoint log-concavity and the centered generating-function identity are checked numerically.

The examples do not prove the source spin-glass fluctuation scale or a log-concavity statement without the specified symmetry.

## Possible applications

Transferring log-concave one-dimensional estimates to symmetric finite Gaussian log-partition functions.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
