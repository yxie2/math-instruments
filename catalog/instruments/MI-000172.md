# Partition labels compress logarithmic distance profiles independently of center count

[Family 329](../families/openai-math-329.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000108.md) · [Batch B009](../../batches/B009/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let a nonempty finite X carry u partitions, each with at most q realized labels. A fixed nonempty family Tcal of index sets T omits at most theta*u indices per set, where 0<theta<1. In the graph for T, distinct points are adjacent if they share a label in any partition in T. Use distance infinity across components. Choose an integer h>=1 with log(3)/log(h+1)<=theta, and put s=ceil(1/theta). For y=(v,T), use the column $g_y(x)=(1-\log(1+d_T(x,v))/\log(1+h))_+$, zero at infinity.

Every nonnegative column combination f of total mass at most one has a list approximant A satisfying f-3theta<=A<=f+theta, where a single fixed list works for all coefficients and

$$\log Q\le hs\log q+\log u+hs2^u\log(1+s2^u/\theta).$$

Average the omitted coefficient mass to find a common index i0 whose omission costs at most theta. At each radius j<h, greedily pick at most s pivot balls until every other ball has residual coefficient mass at most theta. Assign each retained column to its first pivot. Replace that pivot by the fixed representative of its i0-label. For an assigned center v, the pivot replacement is within j+1 of v, so a radius-j membership implies radius-(2j+1) membership about the replacement; the latter implies radius-(3j+2) membership about v.

The profile is the sum of radius-j indicators with weights $\gamma_j=\log((j+2)/(j+1))/\log(h+1)$. They sum to one, and dilating j to 3j+2 increases the profile by at most log(3)/log(h+1). Group coefficients by radius, pivot slot and T, then round down at mesh theta/(s*2^u). The total additional error is at most theta. Only i0, hs labels and h*s*2^u rounded weights need encoding; the graphs and representatives are shared data.

Splitting signed coefficients of ell1 norm at most one into positive and negative parts gives a list of size at most Q^2 and uniform error 6theta. A cluster assigned to one such approximant has column-function diameter at most 12theta.

## Role in the source argument

Makes the column-hull covering count depend on the partition label count, without an additional encoding factor for the much larger set of centers.

## Antecedents and comparison

Convex-hull approximation is established. This package specifically uses a common partition and radius-tolerant profiles to replace centers by labels. It is used in a problem whose established [ellipsoid case](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0407236) and [convexified-packing version](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0407238) have different scopes. The search does not certify priority of the package.

## Checks and limitations

Read all rounding and counting steps. Finite partition systems test shortest-path profiles, greedy residual mass, label replacement, grouped rounding and signed approximation. Separate distance tests cover radii beyond h and disconnected pairs.

The graphs and partitions must be fixed before the coefficient vector. The chosen common index may depend on that vector and is counted. The bound is exponential in u and h, and h may be very large; no efficient compression algorithm is claimed.

## Possible applications

Uniform approximation of structured convex hulls when many centers have a short description through shared equivalence classes.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
