# Derive an inverse-Hessian cap identity from affine stationarity

[Family 353](../families/openai-math-353.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000378.md) · [Batch B063](../../batches/B063/README.md)

## Mathematical content

For a stationary locally strictly convex graph or its invertible affine image, let dA be affine area, nu an inward conormal, H_ij=nu X_ij its positive second form, o an interior point and ell an ambient affine function. If psi(ell),psi'(ell),psi''(ell) vanish outside a compact subset of the hypersurface, then

integral [nu(o-X)|d ell|_(H inverse)² psi''(ell)+n(ell(o)-ell(X))psi'(ell)-n(n+1)psi(ell)] dA=0.

For X=(x,u), put q=ell(X), Z=nu(o-X)=o_(n+1)-u+(x-o prime).Du, H=D²u and delta=1/(n+2). Then DZ=H(x-o prime), tr(H^(-1)D²Z)=n+(x-o prime).D log det H and D²q=ell_z H. Test affine stationarity with Z psi(q). Integrating the log-determinant derivative against dA=(det H)^delta dx replaces it by -delta^(-1)[n psi+(x-o prime).Dq psi']. The remaining coefficients are -n(n+1) and n(ell(o)-q), giving the identity.

Stationarity follows from divergence-free Hessian cofactors and the affine maximal equation. Affine area scales by |det L|^(n/(n+2)); hence the identity transfers to affine images. If an interior ball of radius r is centered at o, the supporting-plane inequality also gives nu(o-X)>=r|nu|.

## Role in the source argument

Controls the inverse-Hessian directional weight on a compact cap.

## Checks and limitations

Exact radial integrals on paraboloids check all three coefficients, including -n(n+1), for compact cap tests in multiple dimensions.

Paraboloid tests validate the identity in an explicit stationary family. They do not verify the source thin-cap estimates or global rigidity.

## Possible applications

Extracting weighted inverse-Hessian information from geometric stationarity with compact support.
