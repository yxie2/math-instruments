# Binary blocks control row-selected products of noncommuting nested projections

[Family 086](../families/openai-math-086.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000166.md) · [Batch B023](../../batches/B023/README.md)

## Mathematical content

For i=1,...,m let P_0^i<=...<=P_T^i be increasing orthogonal projections on a Hilbert space. Put Z_n=P_n^m...P_n^1 and L_T=1+ceil(log2(T+1)). Let finitely many rows B_nu satisfy sum_nu ||B_nu h||^2<=||h||^2. For arbitrary independently chosen prefix indices n(nu),

$$\left(\sum_\nu\|B_\nu Z_{n(\nu)}g\|^2\right)^{1/2}\le L_T^m\|g\|.$$

Also, for m>=1,

$$\sum_{n=1}^T\|(Z_n-Z_{n-1})g\|^2\le m^2L_T^{2(m-1)}\|g\|^2.$$

For m=0 the first estimate has constant one. Set Delta_0^i=P_0^i and Delta_n^i=P_n^i-P_(n-1)^i. Within a layer these increments are orthogonal. Decompose each prefix into at most one dyadic block of each length. Fix one tuple of block lengths, group rows by their actual block tuple, apply the row contraction, and then apply Bessel successively through the layers. The resulting squared sum is at most ||g||^2. There are at most L_T^m tuples of lengths; the triangle inequality in the direct sum of row spaces proves the first bound.

For the variation bound, telescope Z_n-Z_(n-1) by replacing one factor at a time. Discard left contraction factors, use Cauchy-Schwarz over the m positions, and apply the first estimate with rows Delta_n^i and the preceding i-1 layers. This gives m sum_i L_T^(2(i-1))||g||^2, bounded by the displayed constant. Only within-layer orthogonality is used; cross-layer commutation is unnecessary.

## Role in the source argument

Provides logarithmic dependence on the number of stopping generations in the later projection-compression argument.

## Antecedents and comparison

The source explicitly builds on [Mikhailets-Murach, Section 3, Lemma 1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1110.4253), a vector-valued presentation of the classical Rademacher-Menshov binary proof. The extracted formulation allows ordered noncommuting layers and Bessel rows. Historical priority for that exact generalization was not established.

## Checks and limitations

Read the complete proof and the cited binary-prefix antecedent. Operator-norm calculations check 108 random matrix models for all input vectors, including substantially noncommuting layers. Alternating nonnested projections violate the m=1 variation bound.

Finite numerical models do not prove the infinite-dimensional statement. The symbolic proof supplies that extension. This lemma alone does not establish the more elaborate analytic compression theorem.

## Possible applications

Bounding adaptively selected prefixes of several nested approximation procedures without assuming they commute.
