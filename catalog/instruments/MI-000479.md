# Force a strict geometric gap by an odd constraint map and compactness

[Family 349](../families/openai-math-349.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000258.md) · [Batch B047](../../batches/B047/README.md)

## Mathematical content

On S^(m+1), let A_m consist of finite-perimeter sets with half the sphere volume and zero vector centroid. This class is nonempty, for example by two antipodal caps of suitable radius. Let gamma_m be the infimum of perimeter on A_m. BV compactness, continuity of volume and centroid under L1 convergence, and perimeter lower semicontinuity produce a minimizer.

Spherical isoperimetry bounds perimeter below by sigma_m, the equatorial m-sphere area; equality at half volume requires a hemisphere. A hemisphere with pole a has centroid sigma_m a/(m+1), which is nonzero. Thus the constrained minimizer cannot be an equality case, and gamma_m>sigma_m. This argument gives a strict gap, without an explicit numerical gap or a characterization of the constrained minimizer.

For a cycle family Phi on a finite CW complex X, pull back the filling double cover. On that cover the map (x,E) -> (Vol(E)-sigma_(m+1)/2, integral_E y) takes values in R^(m+3) and changes sign under complementation. If it had no zero, normalization would give an equivariant map to S^(p-1), p=m+3, and a quotient map X -> RP^(p-1). The filling-cover class would be pulled back from RP^(p-1), forcing its pth cup power to vanish. Therefore any detecting family with (Phi*lambda)^p!=0 has a zero of this constraint map, and its maximal mass is at least gamma_m.

The general method separates two tasks: topology forces the constraints, and compactness plus an incompatible equality classification turns those constraints into a strict energy gap.

## Role in the source argument

Raises the source detecting-family mass threshold strictly above the equator level before min-max arguments.

## Antecedents and comparison

The cup-power step uses [Hatcher Theorem 3.19](https://pi.math.cornell.edu/~hatcher/AT/ATch3.pdf). The spherical isoperimetric inequality and its equality case were checked in [Boglein-Duzaar-Fusco](https://cvgmt.sns.it/media/doc/paper/2146/BDF-sphere-2013-05-09.pdf), Section 2.3, equation (2.2), p. 5. The gap argument is the standard direct method with exclusion of all equality cases; no new isoperimetric theorem is claimed.

## Checks and limitations

The complete selected proof was read. Symbolic spherical-coordinate integrals check the hemisphere centroid in dimensions m=1,...,7. On S2, two antipodal caps of angular radius pi/3 have half volume, zero centroid and perimeter 2 pi sqrt(3), giving a feasible upper bound on gamma_1, not its optimum.

Finite examples do not prove BV compactness or cup-product detection. No explicit optimal gamma_m, classification of constrained minimizers or source least-volume theorem is certified.

## Possible applications

Variational problems where a cohomological obstruction forces an odd collection of constraints incompatible with every unconstrained minimizer.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
