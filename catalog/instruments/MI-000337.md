# Prescribe a transverse derivative on curves while keeping the function uniformly small

[Family 333](../families/openai-math-333.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000187.md) · [Batch B029](../../batches/B029/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let K be a compact subset of finitely many smooth embedded curves in a coordinate disk. Assume finitely many intersections and tangencies to partial_y near K, with no shared arcs. Given B>0 and epsilon>0, one can choose h smooth and compactly supported in any prescribed precompact neighborhood of K so that |h|<epsilon and h_y=B on K.

First cover the exceptional intersections and tangencies by disjoint small disks and put h_pre=sum_p chi_p B(y-y(p)), with each chi_p one on a smaller disk. Its size is small when the disks are small; its y-derivative is B on the smaller cores.

Away from those cores, use single-curve patches with defining functions f_j satisfying (f_j)_y nonzero. A partition of unity chi_j covers the remaining derivative defect. Add

$$h_j=\chi_j f_j\frac{B-(h_{pre})_y}{(f_j)_y}\,\eta_j(f_j/\delta_j),$$

where eta_j is a compact cutoff equal to one near zero. On f_j=0, every differentiated coefficient or cutoff carries a factor f_j, so (h_j)_y=chi_j(B-(h_pre)_y) exactly. Shrinking delta_j reduces the values without changing these derivatives. Higher derivatives need not remain small.

The tangency qualification matters: on a whole vertical segment, h_y=B would force a fixed change B times its length, preventing an arbitrarily small sup norm.

## Role in the source argument

Separates the small angle displacement from the large transverse turn derivative required by the source velocity construction.

## Checks and limitations

The transverse derivative formula is symbolic; 96 rational support-width choices meet the amplitude budget. A vertical-segment control demonstrates why unrestricted tangencies cannot be allowed.

The checks cover the local formula, not the full curve atlas or the later metric-correction scheme.

Prescribing first derivatives on thin sets while reserving a small C0 perturbation budget.
