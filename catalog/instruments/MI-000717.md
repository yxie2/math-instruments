# Fix a deterministic scale with a bounded monotone center

[Family 211](../families/openai-math-211.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000483.md) · [Batch B076](../../batches/B076/README.md)

## Mathematical content

For any finite real random variable Y, define m(Y) as the unique zero of F_Y(r)=E arctan(Y-r). Bounded convergence makes F_Y continuous with limits pi/2 and -pi/2; strict decrease gives uniqueness. Translation gives m(Y+c)=m(Y)+c. If Y_j converges in distribution to Y, bounded continuous testing gives pointwise convergence of F_(Y_j). Opposite signs at m(Y) plus or minus epsilon trap the zeros, so m(Y_j)->m(Y), without moment assumptions.

Suppose a_n Delta_n converges in distribution to a finite positive Delta, where Delta_n=max(1,diameter of the graph). Define ahat_n=exp(m(log Delta)-m(log Delta_n)) using unconditional laws. The constants ahat_n are deterministic. The center identity gives log(a_n/ahat_n)=m(log(a_n Delta_n))-m(log Delta)->0, proving ahat_n/a_n->1.

Positivity and finiteness of the limiting diameter are essential to taking logarithms and applying weak convergence. An ordinary expectation of log diameter could fail without integrability; bounded arctangent avoids that requirement.

## Role in the source argument

Aligns a geometrically supplied scale with the deterministic convention used by a spectral companion.

## Antecedents and comparison

This is a bounded monotone location-functional argument and a continuous-mapping application. The source supplies a convenient explicit choice; exhaustive priority of that choice is not established.

## Checks and limitations

Finite atomic laws test translation covariance, root bracketing, scale recovery and vanishing contamination at diverging outliers, where ordinary means fail to converge.

Numerical root checks do not establish metric-measure convergence or identify any Liouville geometry.

## Possible applications

Choosing canonical deterministic scales under weak convergence without moment bounds.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
