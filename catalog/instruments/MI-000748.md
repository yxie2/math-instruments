# Retain exposed trajectories to obtain an exact one-sweep energy memory

[Family 238](../families/openai-math-238.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000501.md) · [Batch B080](../../batches/B080/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Expose the trajectories of ell labeled cards in a coordinate-sweep shuffle on {0,1}^d. A feasible complete path specification fixes exactly the visited time-edge coins; all other coins remain independent fair. For a tagged free card, a matching edge with two free endpoints averages their conditional masses, while an edge with one free endpoint transports its mass along the route fixed by the exposed card. The uniform mass on the m remaining positions follows the same updates.

Write w_s for the conditional tagged mass minus that uniform mass. Future exposed-path information fixes only later time coins and does not change this earlier conditional law. With A_i the deterministic pair-averaging operator, w_(s+1)=A_(i_s)w_s+xi_s and E[xi_s|exposed past]=0. A one-free-endpoint edge at x contributes noise plus or minus w_s(x)(delta_x-delta_(x+e_i))/2, independently across edges.

Let E_s=E||w_s||_2^2 and b_s=E sum_x w_s(x)^2 1_(x+e_(i_s) not free). Expanding the last d layers annihilates the old mean-zero flow because the complete coordinate average is projection onto constants. Different-time and different-edge noises have zero expected cross products. A noise from j layers ago spreads over two disjoint subcubes of size 2^(j-1), yielding the exact identity E_t=sum_(j=1)^d 2^(-j)b_(t-j), t>=d.

## Role in the source argument

Keeping a conditional flow adapted while retaining a finite, explicitly weighted memory of recent noise.

## Antecedents and comparison

Averaging and transport in conditional shuffle representations have classical antecedents. Exact priority of this one-sweep identity is not established. The independent coin-cylinder argument is supplied in full by the source.

## Checks and limitations

All coin arrays for small cubes are enumerated to reconstruct conditional path laws independently of the flow formula. Checks compare prefix and full-path conditioning and verify the energy identity for several numbers of exposed cards.

The finite identity does not prove its source contraction estimate or full-deck mixing rate. Endpoint-only conditioning would not provide the same cylinder description.
