# Track repeated cycles in a character-square recurrence

[Family 205](../families/openai-math-205.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000321.md) · [Batch B056](../../batches/B056/README.md)

## Mathematical content

For partitions lambda,nu of n, the tensor-square multiplicity is g(lambda,lambda,nu)=sum_(rho partition n) chi_lambda(rho)^2 chi_nu(rho)/z_rho, a nonnegative integer. Let R_k be the signed matrix for adding a border strip of size k. For a vector v of character coefficients in degree s, Murnaghan-Nakayama gives chi_(R_k^T v)(rho)=chi_v((k) union rho).

The source state F_(s,k,e)(v) sums chi_v(rho)^2 chi_nu(rho) over partitions rho of s with largest part at most k, using weight

e!/[k^(m_k)(e+m_k)!] times product_(j<k) 1/[j^(m_j)m_j!].

Here e records already removed k-cycles. Splitting off one further k-cycle yields

F_(s,k,e)(v)=F_(s,k-1,0)(v)+R_k F_(s-k,k,e+1)(R_k^T v)/[k(e+1)].

Reset k to s and e to zero if 0<s<k. The empty-partition value is v_empty^2. When k=1, the calls have e=0 and the answer is d_s(d_s^T v)^2/s!, where d_s is the irreducible dimension vector. Thus F_(n,n,0)(e_lambda) gives every square multiplicity. The dependence on v is quadratic, so v is part of the memoization state.

All denominators are invertible modulo a prime greater than the starting degree. A nonzero output residue certifies positive multiplicity; a zero output, even for several primes, does not certify absence. A universal candidate must pass for all nu using the same lambda. The bundled check compares the recurrence, implemented by bead moves, against an independent skew-diagram character calculation through n=8. It does not rerun the source claimed range through n=64.

## Role in the source argument

Organizes the source finite base-range search without storing the whole high-degree character table.

## Antecedents and comparison

[Stacey Law, Representation Theory of Symmetric Groups, notes by Leonard Tomczak](https://math.berkeley.edu/~ltomczak/notes/Mich2022/RepSn_Notes.pdf), Theorem 3.25 on page 50, states the classical Murnaghan-Nakayama rule; that statement was read. The source recurrence repackages this character identity with a repeated-cycle factorial weight. The underlying representation-theoretic rule is not new.

## Checks and limitations

Independent cell-connectivity border-strip characters and bead-move recursion agree for 66 tensor squares and 918 multiplicities through degree 8, plus 24 signed character combinations. Character orthogonality is checked exactly. Results list the actual small-degree universal candidates.

The upstream range through 64 was neither rerun nor inferred from the small tests. Zero residues are inconclusive. No large-degree universal-square or Saxl theorem is certified.

## Possible applications

Designing exact modular positivity searches with an explicit recurrence invariant and a clear distinction between positive certificates and inconclusive zero outputs.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
