# An exponential cone density transfers entropy gaps to isotropic-constant gaps

[Family 101](../families/openai-math-101.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000089.md) · [Batch B003](../../batches/B003/README.md)

## Mathematical content

For a full-dimensional convex body $K\subset\mathbb R^n$ with centroid zero, let $\Sigma_K$ be its uniform covariance and define the cone $\mathcal C_K=\{(tx,t):x\in K,t\ge0\}$. The log-concave density

$$f_K(y,t)=\frac{e^{-t}}{n!|K|}\mathbf1_{\mathcal C_K}(y,t)$$

is the law of $(TX,T)$, where X is uniform on K and independently $T\sim\operatorname{Gamma}(n+1,1)$. Consequently

$$h(f_K)=n+1+\log(n!|K|),\qquad
\det\operatorname{Cov}(f_K)=(n+1)^{n+1}(n+2)^n\det\Sigma_K.$$

Thus the entropy-gap inequality $h(f)\ge m+\frac12\log\det\operatorname{Cov}(f)$ in dimension m=n+1 implies, exactly,

$$L_K\le\frac{(n!)^{1/n}}{(n+1)^{(n+1)/(2n)}\sqrt{n+2}},\qquad
L_K^{2n}=\det\Sigma_K/|K|^2.$$

If entropy equality characterizes affine images of independent exponentials, the cone support must be an affine image of the positive orthant. Its unique extreme point forces the translation to vanish; its height-one section is then a simplex. Conversely, the cone over a simplex is a linear image of the orthant and gives equality.

## Role in the source argument

Carries a sharp functional inequality and its equality classification to a sharp geometric bound with the exact dimension shift and factorial constant.

## Antecedents and comparison

This is an established reduction. The source explicitly credits the cone construction to Fradelizi–Meyer and its entropy reduction to [Fradelizi–Marín Sola](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.07406), with moment formulas also attributed to Klartag. Its presence in the new source is useful as a reusable bridge, not as evidence of new priority.

## Checks and limitations

Recomputed the normalization, gamma moments, block covariance and determinant. The finite check verifies the simplex covariance determinant and equality constants in several dimensions, including the interval value L=1/sqrt(12).

The transfer is conditional on the entropy theorem and, for the equality conclusion, on its extremizer classification. Neither follows from these gamma-moment calculations. Covariance must be positive definite, so the body is full-dimensional.

## Possible applications

Converting entropy deficits into geometric extremal bounds and tracking equality through homogeneous cone constructions without losing dimension-dependent constants.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
