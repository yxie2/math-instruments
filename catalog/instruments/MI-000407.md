# Turn a full sieve cover into an exact Street-nerve union

[Family 317](../families/openai-math-317.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000222.md) · [Batch B037](../../batches/B037/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let C_1 and C_2 be full sieves of a strict higher category C covering its objects, or let both be full cosieves. Then their Street nerves cover the nerve of C, and their intersection is the nerve of C_1 intersect C_2. Thus the resulting square is an ordinary pushout of simplicial sets along monomorphisms, hence a homotopy pushout.

For a simplex z:O_m->C in the sieve case, choose a piece containing its final vertex z(m). The oriental has an arrow j->m for each j, so the sieve condition places every image vertex in that piece. Fullness then places the entire simplex there. For cosieves, choose the piece containing z(0) and use arrows 0->j. This proves the union in every simplicial degree, including degree zero; the intersection follows from full inclusions.

The endpoint condition is essential. Arbitrary full subcategories covering objects can miss simplices that cross between their object sets.

## Role in the source argument

Makes certain strict-category attachment covers accessible to ordinary simplicial gluing.

## Antecedents and comparison

This is a classical nerve-cover argument and monomorphic homotopy gluing. No new-priority claim or general cover theorem is asserted.

## Checks and limitations

All 17,131 ideal-cover pairs in tested naturally labelled posets through five elements contain each of their 154,679 nondegenerate chains in a single piece.

Finite-poset chains test ordinary nerves; general higher orientals and the full model-transfer proof are outside the computation.

## Possible applications

Reducing a homotopy comparison to two pieces when directed closure forces each simplex into one piece.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
