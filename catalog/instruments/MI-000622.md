# Transfer an integer density to squarefree squareclasses

[Family 006](../families/openai-math-006.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000391.md) · [Batch B065](../../batches/B065/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let a property of nonzero signed integers be invariant under multiplication by positive integer squares. Write A(X) for its count among 0<|n|<=X and S(X) for its count among squarefree such n. If A(X)=2 delta X+o(X), then S(X)=2 delta X/zeta(2)+o(X), giving relative density delta among signed squarefree integers.

The unique decomposition n=d m² gives A(X)=sum_(m<=sqrt X) S(X/m²). Mobius inversion gives S(X)=sum_m mu(m) A(X/m²). For fixed M, the first M terms have the claimed asymptotic. The remaining terms have absolute value at most 2X sum_(m>M) m^(-2), uniformly in X, since A(Y)<=2Y. First send X to infinity and then M to infinity. This avoids an unjustified exchange of a growing summation range with a nonuniform little-o term.

## Role in the source argument

Moves density conclusions from all twists to their squarefree representatives.

## Antecedents and comparison

This is elementary Mobius inversion with a summable majorant. The source applies it to analytic ranks, but the extracted lemma does not establish the input rank densities.

## Checks and limitations

Exact signed counts for several squareclass-invariant properties check both convolution identities. A noninvariant property supplies a negative control.

Finite counts check the identities, not the existence of an asymptotic density for analytic ranks.

## Possible applications

Transferring arithmetic statistics between all integer parameters and squarefree representatives.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
