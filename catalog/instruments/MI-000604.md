# Recover a maximal-abelian expectation by refining centralizer pinches

[Family 300](../families/openai-math-300.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000373.md) · [Batch B062](../../batches/B062/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let phi be a faithful normal state and A a maximal abelian subalgebra contained in its centralizer. For a finite partition of unity Q by projections in A, define C_Q(z)=sum_(q in Q)qzq. This is unital completely positive, preserves phi, and contracts ||z||_phi=phi(z*z)^(1/2), also for z*. On GNS space it is the orthogonal projection z xi -> sum q z q xi. Refining Q decreases these projections, so the associated vectors converge.

The operators stay uniformly bounded. Every ultraweak cluster point commutes with all projections of A, hence belongs to A. Moreover E_A C_Q=E_A, so that cluster point must be E_A(z). The Hilbert-space limit is therefore E_A(z)xi. Apply the same argument to z* to obtain convergence in the two-sided state seminorm. On bounded sets, convergence on xi implies strong convergence because M prime xi is dense; applying it to adjoints gives strong-star convergence. One may use a refining sequence when its partitions generate A; otherwise the statement is a net.

## Role in the source argument

Approximates the conditional expectation using finite diagonal compressions before paving.

## Antecedents and comparison

This is the classical Hilbert-projection proof of state-preserving pinching convergence. The source proof was read in full; no new general conditional-expectation theorem is claimed.

## Checks and limitations

Nontracial diagonal states and refining partitions verify state preservation, orthogonal-projection identities and convergence to diagonal expectation. A rotated projection control shows why centralizer membership matters.

Finite partitions in matrices do not verify the later uniform paving number or arbitrary infinite-dimensional paving theorem.

## Possible applications

Replacing an abstract conditional expectation by finite state-contracting compressions.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
