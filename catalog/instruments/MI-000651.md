# Transfer jet surjectivity from weighted initial terms

[Family 039](../families/openai-math-039.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000420.md) · [Batch B068](../../batches/B068/README.md)

## Mathematical content

A finite-dimensional power-series space has a row-echelon basis with distinct least exponents; their number equals its dimension. For multiplicatively graded, additionally labelled spaces W_(k,gamma), the exponent-label pairs form a graded semigroup because the least exponent of a product is the sum. This statement does not require a common lift for all graded pieces.

Choose a positive weight lambda that makes every selected least exponent e_j uniquely minimal in its series f_j, with leading coefficient c_j. The normalized functions c_j^-1 s^(-lambda.e_j) f_j(s^lambda Z) U^gamma_j converge to Z^e_j U^gamma_j locally uniformly, together with every fixed derivative. A convergent majorant on a larger polydisc gives uniform convergence; Cauchy estimates give derivatives. Unscaled Laurent coordinates U remain in the compact-domain and derivative conditions.

If a prescribed finite jet matrix of the limiting monomials is surjective, one maximal minor is nonzero and remains so for sufficiently small positive s. Undoing the invertible row rescalings and coordinate scaling gives jet separation by the original space at the correspondingly scaled tuple. The tuple need not stay fixed.

## Role in the source argument

Connects graded monomial combinatorics to analytic jet separation in mixed local/Laurent coordinates.

## Antecedents and comparison

[Ito, Okounkov bodies and Seshadri constants](https://arxiv.org/abs/1202.6662), Proposition 5.7 and its complete proof on PDF pages 16-17, gives the antecedent degeneration principle. The source spells out weighted derivative convergence and labelled Laurent directions. This is an extension of an established interface, not a newly discovered degeneration principle.

## Checks and limitations

Exact weighted polynomial families check derivative limits, nonzero jet minors at small scales and least-exponent addition in products.

Finite polynomial tests do not establish the source large-scale packing or maximal Seshadri constants.

## Possible applications

Lifting an explicit monomial interpolation design to an analytic section space.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
