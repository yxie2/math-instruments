# Control shifted collisions and Fourier energy by positive bin masses

[Family 073](../families/openai-math-073.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000199.md) · [Batch B032](../../batches/B032/README.md)

## Mathematical content

For finite positive measures eta,zeta on the line, let B_epsilon(eta)=sum_j eta([j epsilon,(j+1)epsilon))^2. Same-bin pairs give B_epsilon(eta)<=eta tensor eta{|s-s'|<=epsilon}. For fixed C>=1, every shifted interval |s-s'-u|<=C epsilon meets only boundedly many bin offsets. Cauchy-Schwarz gives sum_j b_j b_{j+h}<=sum_j b_j^2, so its pair mass is at most C' B_epsilon(eta), uniformly in u.

Similarly, summing the integrable offset weights (1+|h|)^{-2} yields

$$\iint(1+|s-s'|/\epsilon)^{-2}\,d\eta(s)d\zeta(s')\le C'\sqrt{B_\epsilon(\eta)B_\epsilon(\zeta)}.$$

Choose K(t)=(sin(bt)/(bt))^2 with b small enough that K is bounded below on [-C,C] and its nonnegative Fourier transform is supported in [-1,1]. Fourier inversion then bounds the unshifted collision by C' epsilon int_{|r|<=epsilon^{-1}} |hat eta(r)|^2 dr, with the frequency convention absorbed into constants. No densities are assumed; atoms and singular measures are allowed.

Converts positive distance collisions into the Fourier quantities entering the source recursive states.

## Antecedents and comparison

This uses classical binwise Cauchy-Schwarz and the triangular Fourier transform of squared sinc. It is not a new restriction estimate.

## Checks and limitations

Eighty atomic measure models verify bin, shifted-collision and mixed-kernel bounds; 160 bounded-frequency Fourier comparisons use exact integration of exponential pairs.

Finite atomic checks do not establish the source oscillatory estimates or nonlinear recursion.

## Possible applications

Collision energies and singular-measure estimates with uniform control over translated differences.
