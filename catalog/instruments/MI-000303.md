# Recover an integer sign from the first escape of a rounded CRT phase

[Family 141](../families/openai-math-141.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000170.md) · [Batch B024](../../batches/B024/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Suppose residues of an integer X are available modulo distinct primes p, and their product Pi satisfies Pi>6|X|. For k>=0 set c_(p,k)=2^k X (Pi/p)^(-1) mod p in {0,...,p-1}. Then

$$\sum_p c_{p,k}/p\equiv2^kX/\Pi\pmod1.$$

No full product is needed to compute a cofactor modulo p: multiply the other primes modulo p. Choose a precision B with (#primes)2^(-B)<2^(-10), and form

$$w_k=2^{-B}\left(\sum_p\left\lfloor2^B c_{p,k}/p\right\rfloor\bmod2^B\right).$$

Its circular distance from 2^kX/Pi mod one is less than 2^(-10). Find the first k with 1/8<=w_k<=7/8. If X=0, no flag occurs. Otherwise let k_0 be the first k with 2^k|X|/Pi>=1/6; this value is below 1/3. A flag has occurred by k_0. At the first flag, the true phase has not wrapped, and rounding cannot move a near-zero phase into the flagged interval. Hence w_k<1/2 means X>0, and w_k>1/2 means X<0. An arbitrary later flag need not have this meaning.

The source supplies a finite search budget from |X|<=2^m and primes lambda<p<=Y, where Z=max(16,m,lambda), Y=2Z^4. A central-binomial estimate gives enough primes that log2 Pi>=3Z; also log2 Pi<=Y ceil(log2 Y). These bounds supply both Pi>6|X| and a bounded doubling search. Turning the modular operations into a fixed counting-hierarchy level requires separate closure results.

## Role in the source argument

Makes sign tests available without reconstructing enormous integers or their full CRT modulus.

## Antecedents and comparison

[Allender, Buergisser, Kjeldgaard-Pedersen and Miltersen, Theorem 4.2](https://www.cs.rutgers.edu/~allender/papers/slp.pdf) already determines signs through approximate Chinese remaindering. Its proof compares approximated CRT fractions for shifted nonnegative integers. The source uses a first-escape phase test with constant absolute precision; novelty of that formulation was not established.

## Checks and limitations

1,675 exact integer examples, up to 125 bits, pass with residue-only recovery. The largest circular rounding error is below 0.000254. The harness stores Pi solely for validation. X=Pi-1 demonstrates failure without the magnitude guarantee.

The finite tests assume the product-size guarantee; they do not certify the asymptotic prime budget, hierarchy closure levels or the main real-sentence theorem.

## Possible applications

Sign queries for implicitly represented large integers when small-prime residues are accessible.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
