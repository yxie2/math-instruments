# Pay coordinate-scaling costs before summing accumulating local perturbations

[Family 334](../families/openai-math-334.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000188.md) · [Batch B029](../../batches/B029/README.md)

## Mathematical content

For a local chart Phi_n(x)=c_n+lambda_n R_n x, a covariant two-tensor eta_n pushes forward as

$$\widehat\eta_n=\lambda_n^{-2}R_n(\eta_n\circ\Phi_n^{-1})R_n^T.$$

Its m-th derivatives therefore cost at most C_m lambda_n^{-m-2}. Choose each local perturbation only after these fixed chart costs are known, imposing

$$\|\widehat\eta_n\|_{C^m}\le\epsilon2^{-n}\qquad(0\le m\le n).$$

There are finitely many conditions at each stage. For each fixed derivative order m, the tail from n>=m converges uniformly. Thus the tensor series is smooth across its accumulation sets. Choosing epsilon below half the background metric's uniform lower eigenvalue keeps the sum positive.

Disjoint supports preserve each local modification exactly. If every summand vanishes near a point, uniform derivative convergence makes the full sum flat there even when the neighborhoods shrink with n. The same construction applies to the curvature bumps. Combining it with the curvature ODE preserves the entire Euclidean Taylor jet at the origin.

The source uses two accumulation levels: local patches approach boundaries of negative-curvature disks, and the disks approach the origin. This assembly only transfers local properties already established for the patches; it cannot supply the local obstruction itself.

## Role in the source argument

Combines arbitrarily small local obstructions into one smooth positive metric germ while retaining disk and origin jets.

## Antecedents and comparison

This is the standard diagonal seminorm method for smooth summation. Explicitly accounting for covariant tensor scaling prevents treating the patch size as harmless.

## Checks and limitations

3,320 exact derivative-budget inequalities, 192 geometric tail bounds and a symbolic tensor pushforward/pullback identity pass.

The verification assumes the local perturbations can be made arbitrarily small in each requested finite set of seminorms. That source obstruction theorem remains unaudited.

## Possible applications

Building smooth global or germ-level examples from countably many shrinking local constructions.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
