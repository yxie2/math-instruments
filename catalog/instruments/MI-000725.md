# Forget an uneven positive core under small average interface loss

[Family 216](../families/openai-math-216.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000489.md) · [Batch B077](../../batches/B077/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let (X_i,m_i) be probability spaces and 0<t_i(u,v)<=1 satisfy integral(1-t_i) d(m_i tensor m_(i+1))<=xi<1/64. Fix 0<g<=1 almost everywhere on X_1. Under density proportional to g(u_1) product_i t_i(u_i,u_(i+1)), the last-coordinate density is eventually at most 2, without a positive essential lower bound on g.

Write L_i f(v)=integral t_i(u,v) f(u) dm_i(u). Propagate q_1=1 with q_(i+1)=L_i q_i/beta_i and beta_i=integral L_i q_i. Induction gives q_i<=2, beta_i>=1-2xi, and q_(i+1)<=(1-2xi)^(-1)=M<2. Reversed kernels give the same bound on backward densities. Propagate f_1=g using these same beta_i, and set c_i=integral f_i, E_i=f_i-c_i q_i. Choose delta>0 with m_1(g<delta)<1/4. Since any backward density is at most 2, c_i>=delta/2 uniformly.

For mean-zero E, L_i E uses kernel t_i-1, with L2 operator norm at most sqrt(xi). The exact centered recurrence gives ||E_(i+1)||_2<=3 sqrt(xi)/(1-2xi)||E_i||_2, a strict contraction, and ||E_(i+1)||_infinity<=3/(1-2xi)||E_i||_2. Therefore f_N/c_N=q_N+E_N/c_N is eventually bounded by 2. The backward lower mass bound prevents normalization from undoing contraction. The threshold may depend on the fixed core g.

## Role in the source argument

Prevents a fixed multiplicative error from accumulating at every interface while controlling the last guard law.

## Antecedents and comparison

The proof combines a rank-one averaging perturbation with backward density estimates. These are established operator tools; exhaustive priority of this precise positive-core, average-loss formulation is not established.

## Checks and limitations

Weighted finite spaces compute forward and backward propagation, the exact centered recurrence and both contraction bounds, including cores with extremely small minimum weights. A high-loss identity-like kernel gives a persistent concentrated-marginal control.

Finite positive cores have positive minima, so the absence of a uniform lower bound in general rests on the proof. The tests do not supply the source XY small-loss estimates or scaling limits.

## Possible applications

Transferring average interface estimates to marginals influenced by a fixed interior boundary condition.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
