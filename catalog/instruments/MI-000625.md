# Clear rational denominators using local density and a full lattice

[Family 018](../families/openai-math-018.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000394.md) · [Batch B065](../../batches/B065/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let V be a finite-dimensional rational vector space and M an additive subgroup containing a full lattice Lambda. Suppose M is dense in V tensor Q_p for every prime p. Then M=V.

For x=p^(-j) lambda, choose a in M with a-x in Lambda tensor Z_p. Choose an integer m coprime to p, congruent to 1 modulo p^j, and divisible by all away-from-p denominators of a in a lattice basis. The Chinese remainder theorem allows this choice. Then ma-mx is integral at p by approximation and at every other prime by denominator clearing, so b=ma-mx belongs to Lambda. Also c=(m-1)x belongs to Lambda. Therefore x=ma-b-c is in M. Inverting each prime and using Bezout gives every rational lattice vector.

This extracts the final denominator-clearing step of the source additive-span proof. Its earlier assertion that the relevant group-generated additive subgroup contains a full lattice and is dense at each p is a separate input, not established here.

## Role in the source argument

Turns local spanning information into exact rational additive generation.

## Antecedents and comparison

This is classical local-to-global arithmetic for lattices, proved by a concrete CRT certificate. No novelty is claimed for the abstract criterion.

## Checks and limitations

Exact rational-vector examples construct m, b and c and verify x=ma-b-c with b,c integral for several primes and denominator powers.

The examples assume the required local approximation; they do not verify it for the source algebraic groups.

## Possible applications

Converting p-adic approximation at all primes into an exact additive generation statement.
