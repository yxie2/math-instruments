# Average the diagonal spectral measure through an independent cavity

[Family 261](../families/openai-math-261.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000503.md) · [Batch B081](../../batches/B081/README.md)

## Mathematical content

For H_s=[[s,b*],[b,H_c]] with bounded self-adjoint H_c, let mu_s be the spectral measure of the first coordinate. Suppose s has density rho and is independent of H_c,b. Conditional on the cavity, Schur complementation gives
<e_0,(H_s-z)^(-1)e_0>=1/(s-M(z)), M(z)=z+<b,(H_c-z)^(-1)b>, Im M(z)>0.
The integral over s of Im[1/(s-a-iv)] is pi. Hence integrating against rho is at most pi ||rho||_infinity. Integrating in energy against continuous compactly supported test functions, taking the Poisson approximation limit and using regularity yields integral rho(s)mu_s(B) ds <= ||rho||_infinity |B| for every Borel set B.

No cyclicity of e_0 is required. This is a statement about the averaged measure, not the spectral type at almost every parameter: already the one-dimensional operator H_s=s has mu_s=delta_s, although its average has density rho.

## Role in the source argument

Obtaining an expected local spectral bound while keeping its distinction from almost-sure spectral type explicit.

## Antecedents and comparison

[Marx, Continuity of spectral averaging, arXiv:1009.3694](https://arxiv.org/abs/1009.3694), Theorem 1.1 on PDF page 1, was read for the established Kotani rank-one averaging identity. The displayed bounded-density estimate is a direct classical consequence, not a new result.

## Checks and limitations

Finite block matrices check the resolvent Schur formula and Poisson integral. Numerical integration of Poisson-smoothed spectral measures checks the corresponding density bound; the scalar atomic family controls the invalid inference of almost-sure absolute continuity.

Averaging alone does not prove localization or absolutely continuous spectrum for a fixed realization.
