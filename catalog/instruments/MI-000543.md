# Eliminate high modes using coercivity in a form norm

[Family 275](../families/openai-math-275.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000312.md) · [Batch B055](../../batches/B055/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let H be a lower-bounded closed quadratic form, Pi an orthogonal projection preserving its form domain, and E_Pi the infimum of its compression. For u=w+q, w=Pi u, suppose the shifted complementary form obeys H[q]-E_Pi||q||^2>=alpha||q||_V^2, alpha>0, and the mixed form obeys |H(q,w)|<=beta||w||||q||_V. Then

H[u]-E_Pi||u||^2>=alpha||q||_V^2-2beta||w||||q||_V>=-(beta^2/alpha)||w||^2.

Consequently E_Pi-beta^2/alpha<=inf spec(H)<=E_Pi. The upper bound uses compressed trial states; the lower bound applies to every normalized form-domain vector, so attainment of the full spectral infimum is unnecessary.

The source builds V from the many-particle H1 norm. A one-particle gap gives sum_l(I-P)_l>=I-Pi. Combining this gap with a small fraction of a kinetic lower bound gives complement coercivity of order 1/m. Positive electron-electron repulsion retains the diagonal lower bound, while Hardy and nuclear-replacement form estimates bound the coupling by a polynomial factor times lambda^(-2/3). Its square therefore gives a polynomial factor times lambda^(-4/3). These continuum estimates are hypotheses of the selected elimination step, not consequences of finite-dimensional compression.

## Role in the source argument

Controls all spin sectors and all continuum states outside the localized orbital space in the source energy comparison.

## Antecedents and comparison

This is the classical variational square-completion form of a Schur-complement estimate. The full selected proof was read; its use of a form norm avoids requiring a bounded operator coupling on the entire continuum Hilbert space. No new general perturbation theorem is claimed.

## Checks and limitations

Weighted finite block forms compare their exact lowest eigenvalues with the beta-squared-over-alpha bound, including nontrivial form metrics. Two-level models check the error scale, and removing the complement gap produces a failing control.

The tests do not verify the source continuum Hardy, nuclear-replacement or orbital-gap estimates.

## Possible applications

Justifying an energy compression when the high-mode coupling is bounded only relative to a stronger form norm.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
