# Describe a noninjective mapping torus by its direct limit

[Family 250](../families/openai-math-250.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000356.md) · [Batch B061](../../batches/B061/README.md)

## Mathematical content

For any group endomorphism f:Q->Q, form L=colim(Q,f), with [q,j]=[f(q),j+1]. The stage shift alpha([q,j])=[q,j+1] is an automorphism, with inverse beta([q,j])=[f(q),j]. Then

<Q,t | t^(-1)qt=f(q)> is isomorphic to L semidirect_alpha Z,

where conjugation by t acts as alpha. The map from the presentation sends q to [q,0]. In the other direction [q,j] maps to t^j q t^(-j). The transition relation makes this well-defined, and the maps are inverse on generators. If Q has a finite presentation, only one stable-letter relation per generator is needed, so the torus remains finitely presented even if f is noninjective. The kernel of Q->L is the union of the iterated kernels of f.

The source uses this to discard an independently established kernel. Abstractly, if pi:Q->E is surjective, pi f=F pi, F is injective, and f kills ker(pi), then colim(Q,f)->colim(E,F) is an isomorphism: a class in its kernel already has pi(q)=1 and dies after one more transition. This reduction does not prove that the source Steinberg transition actually kills its kernel.

## Role in the source argument

Retains a finite presentation while replacing a stage group by a faithful limit quotient.

## Antecedents and comparison

Direct-limit descriptions and ascending normal forms are classical. [Geoghegan, Mihalik, Sapir and Wise](https://math.ivanovo.ac.ru/tg-seminar/archives/Geoghegan-Mihalik-Sapir-Wise/2001a.pdf), Lemma 3.1 on printed pages 296-297, gives the related injective ascending-HNN normal-form criterion; its proof was read. It is an antecedent, not a claim that the displayed noninjective formulation originates there.

## Checks and limitations

Finite cyclic endomorphisms verify eventual kernels, stage equivalence, the inverse shift and the orientation of the stable-letter relation.

Finite cyclic models do not verify the source algebra construction, Steinberg kernel theorem or two-transitivity.

## Possible applications

Passing from an endomorphism to an automorphic direct limit while keeping finite presentation at the mapping-torus level.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
