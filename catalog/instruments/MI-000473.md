# Solve a nonresonant recurrence by choosing the tempered time direction

[Family 339](../families/openai-math-339.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000255.md) · [Batch B047](../../batches/B047/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Suppose the invertible cocycle L has a single exponent gamma!=0, with the two-sided tempered Lyapunov estimates stated in the source, and the forcing e_n is tempered. The recurrence q_(n+1)=L_n q_n+e_(n+1) has a unique tempered solution on each regular two-sided orbit.

For gamma<0, sum the past: q_n=e_n+sum_(j>=1) L_(n-1)...L_(n-j) e_(n-j). For gamma>0, sum inverse transport from the future: q_n=-sum_(j>=0) L_n^(-1)...L_(n+j)^(-1)e_(n+j+1). Slow regularization bounds a summand by a tempered prefactor times exp(2 delta |n|) exp((-|gamma|+3 delta)j). Choose 3 delta<|gamma|, and then let delta be arbitrarily small to get convergence and temperedness. Measurable partial sums give measurability.

Two solutions differ by a homogeneous orbit. A nonzero homogeneous orbit grows exponentially in the future for gamma>0 or in the past for gamma<0, so cannot be tempered. The argument also applies to finite triangular blocks sharing the same exponent, provided their diagonal Lyapunov estimates and tempered off-diagonal bounds give the stated product estimates.

At gamma=0 uniqueness can fail: for L=1 and e=0 every constant solution is tempered.

## Role in the source argument

Removes nonresonant coefficients in the source finite-level normal coordinates.

## Antecedents and comparison

Classical Green-series resolution of a hyperbolic recurrence. [Kalinin-Sadovskaya](https://doi.org/10.3934/jmd.2017014) supplies related nonuniform normal-form context; the present extraction does not assert a new normal-form theorem.

## Checks and limitations

Exact rational truncations test both orientations with alternating forcing; triangular matrix examples check the recurrence and summation order. A zero-exponent constant solution is a uniqueness control.

Truncation checks cover finite examples, not general measurable convergence, the multiplicative ergodic theorem or the source geometric normal forms.

## Possible applications

Cohomological equations and finite-jet normalization when forcing and norm comparisons are only tempered.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
