# Recover an integral lattice from projection-transfer residues

[Family 059](../families/openai-math-059.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000438.md) · [Batch B070](../../batches/B070/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Retain free abelian groups D,H,E, maps p:D->H and t:H->D with pt=k id, K=ker p, and tau:E->Z/k. Write pr(l)=l-k^-1 t p(l). In the source k=4. The supplied page exact sequence has kernel K and exterior coordinate z in H tensor E, with obstruction in H/p(D); its exterior subgroup identifies with H tensor ker(tau). For an integral page class x set u=kx-x_ext(kz) in K. The supplied signed cap/Bockstein calculation says u is congruent to -t(b) modulo kD, where b=(1 tensor tau)z in H/kH.

Under these inputs the page lattice in K_Q direct sum (H tensor E)_Q is exactly

Lambda={(pr(l),z): l in D, z in H tensor E, p(l) mod kH=(1 tensor tau)z}.

Choose b_H lifting b. The congruence makes l=(u+t b_H)/k integral, p(l)=b_H and pr(l)=u/k. Conversely an allowed pair has a page lift with the same z by the exact sequence. Its numerator differs from kl-tp(l) by an element of K intersect kD, which equals kK because H is torsion free. Adjust by that integral element of K to obtain the desired rational coordinate. Replacing b_H by b_H+kh changes l by th but leaves pr(l) unchanged. Thus the residue relation, not merely the rational decomposition, retains the integral gluing data.

## Role in the source argument

Makes explicit which rationally split classes are actually integral and where the sign of the interface cap enters.

## Antecedents and comparison

The mechanism is classical integral gluing by congruences and the saturated-kernel identity K intersect kD=kK. The source-specific projection, transfer and signed Bockstein data are essential inputs; their geometric validity is not established by the algebraic extraction.

## Checks and limitations

Exact transfer matrices with k=4 compare both lattice descriptions, lift independence and saturated-kernel intersections, including examples with fractional inner coordinates and a sign-sensitive residue control.

The checks do not verify the topological tube, the preceding signed Bockstein calculation, the filling forms or the ambient-homeomorphism claim.

## Possible applications

Preventing rational orthogonal decompositions from losing integral extension information.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
