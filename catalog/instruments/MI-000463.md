# Turn uniform small packing constants into a fixed exponent saving

[Family 183](../families/openai-math-183.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000250.md) · [Batch B044](../../batches/B044/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Assume that for every eta>0, once N/b>=R_eta, every generic N-point sweep at every rank has at most eta(N/b)^(4/3) step-disjoint intervals of activity at least b. Set a=4/3, eta=1/(4*2^a), and fix one integer q>=max(4,R_eta).

For N>=4q take d=floor(N/q), b=d-1, and greedily end blocks when activity reaches b. A step changes at most two memberships, so each block involves at most d<=N/q points. The packing hypothesis bounds completed blocks by q^a/4; adding the remainder gives at most q^a/2 blocks.

Within a block, all nonactive memberships are fixed. Restriction to active points is therefore a sweep at one fixed local rank, whose complexity is bounded by the same all-ranks function F. Hence F(N)<=sum_j F(t_j), with t_j<=N/q and at most q^a/2 terms. For epsilon=log(2)/(2 log(q)) and beta=a-epsilon, induction yields F(N)<=C N^beta, since (q^a/2)q^(-beta)=1/sqrt(2). The finitely many sizes below 4q are absorbed in C.

Uniformity over local ranks and hereditary restriction are essential. A nonquantitative R_eta gives existence of epsilon, not an explicit numerical saving.

Converts the source uniform activity theorem into a genuine power improvement.
