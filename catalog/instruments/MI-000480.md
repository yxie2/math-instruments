# Find an interior balancing zero by projecting a strictly inward field

[Family 088](../families/openai-math-088.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000259.md) · [Batch B048](../../batches/B048/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let A range over positive semidefinite symmetric matrices of trace one. Assume a continuous assignment of nonzero seminorms g_A, where g_A(x) depends only on the projection of x to range(A). Fix k>0 and define the continuous traceless symmetric field F(A)=E[g_A(u)^k uu^T]/E[g_A(u)^k]-I/n on the unit sphere.

For singular A and a unit e in ker A, Gaussian independence gives E[g_A(u)^k(u.e)^2]/E[g_A(u)^k]=1/(n+k): the Gaussian numerator factors, while radial moments have ratio 1/(n+k). Reflection kills the mixed range-kernel block. Thus F(A) restricted to ker A equals -k I/[n(n+k)], so A-epsilon F(A) is positive definite for sufficiently small epsilon>0 and still has trace one.

Apply Brouwer to A -> projection_D(A-F(A)), where D is the compact convex trace-one positive cone. At a fixed point, the nearest-point inequality reads inner(F(A),B-A)>=0 for every B in D. The admissible choice B=A-epsilon F(A) forces F(A)=0. The kernel formula excludes a singular zero. Consequently the weighted second moment is I/n and the degree-two spherical harmonic component of g_A^k vanishes.

The source uses k=1 or 2 and supplies g_A through a variational construction. The argument above isolates what that construction must provide. A further scaling rule g_(aA)=a g_A permits determinant-one normalization without changing the balancing condition.

## Role in the source argument

Selects an affine position for one variational measure before the principal source bilinear inequality.

## Antecedents and comparison

Classical Brouwer and the nearest-point variational inequality, with elementary Gaussian radial moments. The extracted interface is the explicit boundary-kernel calculation; no new fixed-point theorem is claimed.

## Checks and limitations

Exact Gaussian moment formulas test the kernel field for quadratic seminorms and every rank in dimensions 2 through 8. Numerical nearest-point projections verify the variational inequality. A zero-k control removes the strictly inward conclusion.

The continuity and existence of the source minimizer assignment are hypotheses here. These checks do not establish Petty projection-volume rigidity.

## Possible applications

Producing isotropic positions when a continuous normalization is available on the closed positive cone and its boundary kernel moments can be computed.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
