# Combine exact integer roots with a Chebyshev filter while budgeting coefficients

[Family 274](../families/openai-math-274.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000309.md) · [Batch B055](../../batches/B055/README.md)

## Mathematical content

For integers m>=2r>=2, let ell=ceil(2sqrt(mr)log m), F(x)=product_(j=1)^r(1-x/j), and

p(x)=F(x) T_ell((m+r-2x)/(m-r))/T_ell((m+r)/(m-r)).

Then p(0)=1, p vanishes at integers 1,...,r, and |p(x)|<=2m^(-3r) on [r,m]. Indeed the Chebyshev denominator is at least exp(2ell sqrt(r/m))/2>=m^(4r)/2, while |F|<=m^r there. No smallness is asserted between zero and the first roots at noninteger points.

For W_N(p)=sum_k |[x^k]p|N^k, coefficient convolution is submultiplicative. The root product costs at most (1+N)^r. The affine argument has W_N at most 3+2N, so the Chebyshev recurrence gives W_N(p)<=(1+N)^r[11(1+N)]^ell and degree at most r+ell. These bounds also hold when m>N.

Taking r=ceil(N^a), m=ceil(N^b) with 0<a<b yields degree O(N^((a+b)/2)log N) and log W_N=O(N^((a+b)/2)(log N)^2). For every fixed u>(a+b)/2, both are at most N^u eventually, while |p(k)|<=exp(-N^a) at every integer 1<=k<=m. The coefficient norm pays for expansion of operator powers into many product terms.

## Role in the source argument

Supplies an integer count filter whose expansion cost can be included in the source localization induction.

## Antecedents and comparison

[Sherstov, Approximate inclusion-exclusion for arbitrary symmetric functions](https://web.cs.ucla.edu/~sherstov/pdf/incl-excl.pdf), Lemma 3.9 and its complete proof on manuscript page 17, multiply an approximate selector by an exact-root polynomial. That passage was read. The selected source tracks an additional W_N coefficient budget and explicitly allows m>N; no new general Chebyshev principle is claimed.

## Checks and limitations

Exact rational polynomial coefficients check roots, normalization, integer error, degree and weighted coefficient bounds in finite parameter examples, including m>N.

Finite examples do not establish the source asymptotic localization recurrence or quantum parity theorem.

## Possible applications

Spectral filters for integer-valued counts when later operator expansion requires coefficient control in addition to approximation error.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
