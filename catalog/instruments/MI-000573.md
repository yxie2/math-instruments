# Control all small components by a projection boundary budget

[Family 243](../families/openai-math-243.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000342.md) · [Batch B059](../../batches/B059/README.md)

## Mathematical content

For a nonempty vertex set R in the side-L three-dimensional box with |R|<=L^3/2, let delta R be its edge boundary and c=1-2^(-1/3). Then |delta R|>=c|R|^(2/3).

If the three coordinate-pair projections have sizes A,B,D, two applications of Cauchy-Schwarz give |R|^2<=ABD. One projection has size at least |R|^(2/3), indexing that many nonempty coordinate lines. At most |R|/L of those lines are full. Every other line contains a distinct boundary edge. Thus |delta R|>=|R|^(2/3)-|R|/L, which gives the constant c.

Delete an edge set T. Summing the boundary estimate over components R of size at most half the box gives

sum_(small components) |R|<=c^(-3/2)(2|T|)^(3/2),

since each deleted edge is counted at most twice. In the source |T|<=6 floor(L^(7/4)), so this mass is O(L^(21/8))=o(L^3). For sufficiently large L a unique component therefore has more than half the vertices. Under further deletions within the same regime, the new majority component is contained in the previous one. This monotonicity follows from component containment and the majority sizes, without making a noncanonical choice among small components.

## Role in the source argument

Keeps a defect away from a bounded set of assigned coordinates during the source counting-equivalence construction.

## Antecedents and comparison

The projection inequality is the classical three-dimensional discrete Loomis-Whitney inequality, proved directly in the selected source by Cauchy-Schwarz. The component estimate is an elementary boundary summation. Retrieval of the original 1949 AMS PDF was blocked; no full-text audit of that historical paper is claimed.

## Checks and limitations

282 subsets verify projection and boundary estimates. Forty-eight edge-deletion models verify the explicit small-component mass bound and nested majority components when present.

The small numerical boxes are not claimed to satisfy the asymptotic 6 floor(L^(7/4)) guarantee. The checks do not prove the witnessed-choice separation.

## Possible applications

Maintaining a canonical large connected region throughout a sequence of sparse deletions.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
