# Fixed-root Schur equations are equivalent to a polynomial identity over truncated free coefficients

[Family 201](../families/openai-math-201.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000149.md) · [Batch B019](../../batches/B019/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let T_N=E<s1,s2>/(s1,s2)^(N+1), with E central. Suppose W in M_d(T_N) has constant term W0=sum_h lambda_h pi_h, where the lambda_h are distinct scalars and the pi_h are nonzero pairwise orthogonal algebraic idempotents summing to I. Put q(t)=product_h(t-lambda_h), f_h=I-pi_h and C_h=f_h(W-lambda_h I)f_h. Its constant term is invertible on the complement, so C_h has a finite geometric-series inverse there. Define

$$S_h=\pi_h(W-\lambda_h I)\pi_h-\pi_hWf_hC_h^{-1}f_hW\pi_h.$$

Then q(W)=0 if and only if every S_h=0, despite noncommutativity of T_N.

If all Schur equations vanish, K_h u=u-C_h^(-1)f_hWpi_h u is a graph map into ker(W-lambda_h I). The assembled K=sum_h K_h pi_h has constant term I and is invertible; WK=KW0, hence q(W)=0.

Conversely, q(W)=0 gives polynomial idempotents P_h=product_(j!=h)(W-lambda_j I)/(lambda_h-lambda_j). All factors commute because they are polynomials in the same W with central coefficients. P_h reduces to pi_h. Normalize its graph by the invertible corner pi_h P_h pi_h; the complementary kernel equation uniquely determines that graph as -C_h^(-1) f_h W pi_h. Its remaining component is S_h=0.

Applying this at every N identifies vanishing Schur equations through word degree N with the same fixed polynomial identity through degree N. Word order must be retained in every inverse and product.

## Role in the source argument

Turns a family of spectral correction conditions into one algebraic relation that can be retained at successive stages.

## Antecedents and comparison

Nilpotent-ideal inversion, polynomial spectral projectors and Schur graph coordinates are established algebraic tools. This card records their finite free-algebra combination, without claiming a new spectral theorem.

## Checks and limitations

Read both directions. Exact arithmetic in truncated two-letter free algebras checks graph conjugation, polynomial idempotents and Schur equations, including perturbations that break the relation.

Repeated roots invalidate the denominators and change the statement. Centrality of scalar roots is needed. Commutative polynomial tests alone would miss word-order mistakes.
