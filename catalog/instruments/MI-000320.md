# Project a compact jump field to a decaying divergence-free field

[Family 366](../families/openai-math-366.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000179.md) · [Batch B027](../../batches/B027/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let A be compact of planar area zero and h be compactly supported in W^{1,2}(R^2\A). Extend its ordinary gradient density by zero on A, obtaining e in L2. Let Z be the L2 closure of gradients of smooth compactly supported functions and Q the orthogonal projection onto Z.

Local Poincare inequalities identify Qe with grad(phi), with phi in W^{1,2}_loc. Orthogonality gives Delta phi=div(e). Outside a disk containing the support, phi is harmonic with finite Dirichlet energy. Its exterior Fourier expansion contains neither a logarithm nor growing modes. Subtract its constant mode to obtain the unique normalization phi->0 at infinity, with phi=O(|x|^{-1}) and k-th derivatives O(|x|^{-1-k}).

Set F=h-phi off A and p=e-grad(phi). Then div(p)=0 on the whole plane, grad(F)=p off A, curl(p) is supported on A, and Delta p=grad-perp(curl p). Also F=O(|x|^{-1}) and p=O(|x|^{-2}) outside a large disk.

The projection removes the distributional divergence without erasing the allowed jump. Treating e as the whole-plane distributional gradient of h would incorrectly discard the jump contribution.

## Role in the source argument

Provides a canonical field for moving a compact crack while controlling the error at infinity.

## Antecedents and comparison

The Hilbert-space projection and exclusion of growing or logarithmic exterior modes are classical. The reusable packaging is adapted to a gradient defined only away from a compact jump set.

## Checks and limitations

An exact unit-circle jump model verifies harmonicity, matching normal flux, energy splitting and orthogonality. Thirty-six exterior multipole energy identities pass.

These are explicit models. General Sobolev projection, distributional support and existence arguments are supplied by the inspected written proof, not by finite tests.

## Possible applications

Localizing singular variational fields while keeping a canonical far-field normalization.
