# Pair two potential gradients before estimating a localized virial term

[Family 370](../families/openai-math-370.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000181.md) · [Batch B027](../../batches/B027/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Assume n>=3, A,B>-2 and positive locally bounded functions with exact Newtonian representations u=K*g, v=K*f, where f=|x|^B u^q, g=|x|^A v^p and K(z)=|z|^{2-n}/((n-2)|S^{n-1}|). The exact potential identities are inputs here. Define d pi(x,y)=f(x)g(y)K(x-y) dx dy. Its marginals are fu dx and gv dy; its total mass need not be finite.

For a compact radial cutoff eta, write X=x eta, w=-x.grad(eta), a=1/(q+1), b=1/(p+1), E_f=int eta fu and W_f=int wfu, with analogous g terms. Then

$$a(n+B)E_f+b(n+A)E_g-aW_f-bW_g=(n-2)\int\frac{(X(x)-X(y))\cdot(x-y)}{|x-y|^2}\,d\pi.$$

First justify the separate gradient integrals: near x=0, the region |x-y|<=|x|/2 contributes O(|x|^{2+A}) after multiplication by |X(x)|; on its complement |X(x)|<=2|x-y| reduces the bound to u(x). Swap the two sources for the other term. Weighted integration by parts outside B_rho has an inner boundary contribution O(rho^{n+B}) or O(rho^{n+A}), which vanishes. Substituting grad K and interchanging the second pair of variables then produces X(x)-X(y).

The source chooses eta=[(1-(|x|-1)_+^3)_+]^N with a sufficiently large integer N. This matches two derivatives at both cutoff interfaces and retains quantitative powers of distance to the outer boundary.

## Role in the source argument

Provides a compact interaction identity for the subsequent pressure estimate while preserving the weights at the origin.

## Antecedents and comparison

This is a potential-kernel formulation of the established Rellich-Pohozaev method. The retained weights, convergent separate integrals and cutoff vanishing order are the extracted details; a literature-wide novelty claim was not established.

## Checks and limitations

The weighted divergence identity is checked symbolically and 140 finite paired-kernel sums verify the sign and factor n-2.

The finite sums avoid the kernel singularity. They do not verify the potential representation, analytic integrability for every solution, pressure estimate or nonexistence conclusion.

## Possible applications

Localization of weighted nonlocal interaction identities without assuming finite total interaction energy.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
