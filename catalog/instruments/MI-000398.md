# Recover a free full-rank lattice from height-one localizations

[Family 168](../families/openai-math-168.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000218.md) · [Batch B036](../../batches/B036/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let C be a polynomial ring over a field, E a finite free C-module and M a free submodule spanning E over K=Frac(C). Inside E tensor K,

$$M=\bigcap_{\operatorname{ht}\mathfrak p=1}M_{\mathfrak p}.$$

Choose a C-basis of M, also a K-basis of E tensor K. Membership is the condition that every rational coordinate belongs to C. A reduced coordinate with a nonconstant denominator fails to belong to the localization at an irreducible factor of that denominator. Hence height-one membership forces all denominators to be units. If C is a field, full generic rank directly means M=E.

Freeness cannot simply be dropped: the ideal (x,y) in k[x,y] is full rank and becomes the whole ring at every height-one localization, but does not contain 1. Thus its intersection of height-one localizations is larger than itself.

## Role in the source argument

Converts verified codimension-one module containments into a global containment, provided the target lattice is free.

## Antecedents and comparison

This is the classical intersection property of a UFD, applied in a free module basis. No novelty claim.

## Checks and limitations

Eighty exact rational-coordinate models detect denominator-prime obstructions; the nonfree ideal (x,y) is a negative control.

The test does not establish freeness of the source sheaf kernels or its rank-two local containments.

## Possible applications

Gluing divisibility or containment statements checked at irreducible hypersurfaces.
