# Upgrade truncated rank tails to uniform integrability

[Family 006](../families/openai-math-006.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000392.md) · [Batch B065](../../batches/B065/README.md)

## Mathematical content

On a dyadic parameter block of size O(X), let the nonnegative integer statistic a satisfy a<=B_X=O(log X). Assume T_X(k)=#{a>k}<=C X/k² for a fixed lower threshold <=k<=K=floor((log X)^(3/5)). For a fixed sufficiently large integer R<=K, layer-cake summation gives

sum_(a>R) a <= R T_X(R)+sum_(k=R)^(K-1) T_X(k)+B_X T_X(K)
 <= C' X/R+O(X (log X)^(-1/5)).

The final term handles values beyond the proved tail range; simply summing the tail estimate to infinity would be invalid. Summing over dyadic blocks, separating the finitely many small blocks and then taking a limsup, gives normalized tail mass O(1/R). Thus the statistics are uniformly integrable. Together with limiting mass one-half at each of 0 and 1, this implies limiting mean one-half. Density information alone does not control rare ranks whose size diverges. The source obtains the individual logarithmic bound through Jensen's formula and its tail count from separate analytic arguments; both are assumptions in this extracted transfer.

## Role in the source argument

Isolates the additional analytic estimate needed to pass from a density theorem to a mean theorem.

## Antecedents and comparison

This is the standard uniform-integrability mechanism, with the source-specific cutoff K=(log X)^(3/5) producing the error exponent -1/5. No Goldfeld or mean-rank theorem is independently certified.

## Checks and limitations

Exact layer-cake checks on finite heavy-tail arrays test truncation bookkeeping; a vanishing-frequency large outlier demonstrates that limiting rank frequencies need not determine a mean.

The arrays do not verify the analytic tail estimate for elliptic-curve twists.

## Possible applications

Upgrading distributional arithmetic information to moment information when estimates stop at a finite threshold.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
