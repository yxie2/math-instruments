# Combine an Artin-Schreier residue barrier with a distinguished uniformizer norm

[Family 209](../families/openai-math-209.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000328.md) · [Batch B057](../../batches/B057/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let zeta be a primitive fifth root of unity, L=Q_5(zeta), V its valuation ring and pi=1-zeta. The projective V-curve defined by

(X-Z)^5-XY^4+Y^5+zeta Z^5=0

has special fiber X^5-XY^4+Y^5=0. Away from m=[0:0:1], one has Y nonzero and alpha=X/Y satisfies alpha^5-alpha+1=0. Frobenius sends alpha to alpha-1, so its orbit has length exactly five. Thus the residue field of every closed specialization away from m contains F_(5^5). Properness extends every generic closed point to its valuation ring, giving residue degree divisible by five away from m.

On Z=1 put F=(x-1)^5-xy^4+y^5+zeta. The ambient local ring V[x,y]_(pi,x,y) is regular of dimension three, and F has initial linear term -pi. Its quotient A is therefore a regular local domain of dimension two, with maximal ideal (x,y). Expanding the relation shows pi lies in (x,y)^2; the model is ramified rather than smooth over V.

The divisor y=0 gives S=V[t]/((t-1)^5+zeta). Its polynomial is t^5-5t^4+10t^3-10t^2+5t-pi, Eisenstein at pi because 5 is a unit times pi^4. This ring is a DVR with uniformizer t and residue field F_5; its fraction field D has degree five and is totally ramified over L. The constant term gives N_(D/L)(t)=pi, while eta=1-t satisfies eta^5=zeta. This supplies a distinguished generic point with residue degree one and a prescribed norm, separated from the residue-degree divisibility at every specialization away from m.

## Role in the source argument

Creates the geometric and arithmetic contrast later used by the source norm detector to distinguish a supported class.

## Antecedents and comparison

The ingredients are classical Artin-Schreier orbit calculations, the regular-hypersurface criterion and the norm formula for a root of a monic polynomial. The complete source model proof was read. The specific equation is recorded as source construction data, without a priority claim for its use in K-theory.

## Checks and limitations

Exact finite-field remainders check ten Frobenius iterates and the residue-degree divisibility pattern. Symbolic expansion verifies the cyclotomic and local equations, the ambient linear term, the divisor relation and the determinant giving norm pi.

No higher K-group, integral lift or nonzero Gersten kernel class is computed. The source global detection proof is a separate requirement.

## Possible applications

Designing a local geometric test in which one distinguished point has norm data incompatible with residue-degree restrictions away from it.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
