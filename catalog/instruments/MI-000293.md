# Fill refinement capacities with particles that retain their birth levels

[Family 085](../families/openai-math-085.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000165.md) · [Batch B023](../../batches/B023/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Unfold a finite positive refinement graph into its full path forest. For a prefix xi=(q1,...,qk), let b_xi be the product of its edge coefficients and M_xi=b_xi ||h_qk||. Vector refinement gives the capacity inequality M_xi<=sum_(eta child of xi)M_eta.

There is a positive measure on terminal paths together with birth levels B such that, at every prefix xi of level k,

$$\mu\{B\le k,\ \text{terminal path has prefix }\xi\}=M_\xi.$$

Create M_xi mass at each root. If S_xi is the sum of child capacities, send the fraction M_eta/S_xi of every existing population type to child eta. This fills M_xi M_eta/S_xi<=M_eta of its capacity; create the remaining mass there with birth level k+1. If S_xi=0, the parent and all child masses are zero. Existing populations continue with conserved mass to terminal descendants.

A newborn particle receives the entire ancestral prefix, even across zero-mass ancestors, but contributes to occupancy only at levels at or after its birth. Column-normalized refinement implies that summing over all prefixes ending at q gives occupancy ||h_q||, and total particle mass equals the total terminal vector mass. This handles shared descendants by path weights rather than identifying different histories.

When the source's consecutive centers move by at most r_j/2 and r_(j+1)=r_j/2, every terminal point stays within r_s of its level-s ancestor. Thus pathwise bounds can be integrated back to cellwise sums with the correct mass and locality. The construction itself is independent of the spatial direction-label process later attached to the paths.

## Role in the source argument

Realizes vector-mass budgets as an actual positive path measure on which intrinsic direction changes can be counted.

## Antecedents and comparison

This is a finite transport construction with births whenever child capacity exceeds parent mass. The source retains pre-birth history to interact with a separately evolving label law. The elementary transport mechanism is not claimed as novel.

## Checks and limitations

Read the full proof. Eighty vector-refinement models check 8,320 prefix occupancies and conservation of total terminal mass. An opposite-child example shows that ignoring the birth condition incorrectly assigns positive occupancy to a zero parent.

These numerical finite models do not verify the spatial label law, switching costs or analytic packing theorem. Exact occupancy in the mathematical statement includes the condition B<=k.

## Possible applications

Representing increasing multiscale norm budgets by positive trajectories while preserving history and avoiding fictitious early mass.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
