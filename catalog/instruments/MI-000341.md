# Construct relative area primitives and preserve moment faces in a Moser deformation

[Family 343](../families/openai-math-343.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000189.md) · [Batch B029](../../batches/B029/README.md)

## Mathematical content

On a compact connected oriented surface with boundary, a smooth parameter family of two-forms supported in a fixed interior compact set and of integral zero has compactly supported primitives chosen by one fixed linear operator.

Locally write eta=h dx wedge dy, let h_0(y)=int h(x,y) dx, and choose a fixed compactly supported rho of integral one. Put A(x,y)=int_{-infinity}^x[h(v,y)-rho(v)h_0(y)]dv and B(y)=int_{-infinity}^y h_0(v)dv. Then alpha=A dy-rho B dx is compactly supported and d alpha=eta. A partition of unity and chains of overlapping disks move all residual bump masses to one common bump; their total coefficient is zero. This preserves smooth parameter dependence.

For toric horizontal Gamma on a surface times a toric fiber V of complex dimension m,

$$ (\omega_V+d\Gamma)^{m+1}=(m+1)\omega_V^m\wedge d_C\Gamma.$$

Extra mixed terms vanish because they contain too many moment differentials. Thus positive d_C Gamma gives nondegeneracy.

For a path Omega_lambda with derivative d alpha_lambda, solve i_Y Omega=-alpha. If alpha is horizontal, vanishes near the base boundary, and i_{X_mu}Omega=-d mu near each outer moment face, then d mu(Y)=0 there. The flow fixes the base boundary collar and preserves every outer inequality simultaneously, including nonsimple corners. This is the relative Moser deformation needed by the source.

## Role in the source argument

Changes base-area distributions without disturbing the fiber truncation faces needed later in the packing construction.

## Antecedents and comparison

The zero-integral condition is the classical compact-support cohomology obstruction in degree two, and the flow equation is Moser theory. The explicit primitive and horizontal face condition make the support and boundary bookkeeping reusable.

## Checks and limitations

The local primitive formula is symbolic. Seventy-five finite two-form models verify the determinant/area relation and zero moment components of the horizontal Moser field.

No global packing or flow is constructed by the script. The written proof supplies compactness and support arguments; the source Hamiltonian comparison remains separate.

## Possible applications

Relative symplectic deformations with fixed base collars and simultaneous preservation of several moment inequalities.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
