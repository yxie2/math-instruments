# Obtain two conformal splitting directions while preserving parity and gauge

[Family 361](../families/openai-math-361.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000296.md) · [Batch B053](../../batches/B053/README.md)

## Mathematical content

On a smooth two-sphere with normalized conformal area density rho_w=exp(2w)/integral exp(2w), the Dirichlet form is conformally unchanged. For a repeated positive Laplace eigenvalue lambda and a real orthonormal pair u,v, the first-variation cluster matrix in direction dot(w) is

dot(M)_ij=-2lambda integral u_i u_j [dot(w)-integral dot(w)] dmu_w.

The subtracted mean contributes only a scalar matrix. Its two traceless entries are represented by U_1=u^2-v^2 and U_2=2uv, both of mean zero. These functions are linearly independent. Otherwise a nonzero traceless real quadratic form would vanish on (u,v) everywhere. Its zero set is two lines. Near a point where (u,v) is nonzero, continuity forces it into one fixed line, so a nonzero linear combination of u and v vanishes on an open set. Unique continuation contradicts orthonormality.

The Gram matrix of U_1,U_2 is therefore positive definite, and these two smooth conformal directions span the traceless two-by-two perturbations. If the metric is antipodally even and u,v have the same parity, both directions are even and remain admissible. Finally a parameter-dependent fixed-area coordinate change adds a commutator to the operator variation. Its compression to a scalar lambda eigenspace vanishes, so the splitting matrix is gauge independent. This gives local two-direction control; it does not prescribe an entire global crossing program.

## Role in the source argument

Supplies a local perturbation mechanism for the source three-dimensional companion to separate or retain selected eigenvalue coincidences.

## Antecedents and comparison

[Greilhuber-Kepplinger, On Arnold's Transversality Conjecture for the Laplace-Beltrami Operator](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2312.16939), Theorem 1.1 and Proposition 2.3 with its proof, identify conformal transversality failure through quadratic relations among eigenfunctions and attribute the criterion to Besson. These passages were read. The selected two-function argument is a concrete instance, with an explicit same-parity and coordinate-gauge observation.

## Checks and limitations

Exact sphere polynomial integrals compute the two-direction Gram and splitting matrices for a degree-one eigenfunction pair. Rational finite matrices verify that commutator compression vanishes on a repeated eigenvalue block.

These examples do not prove unique continuation, isolate every required crossing or establish the companion harmonic-dimension counterexample.

## Possible applications

Controlling a two-dimensional spectral cluster under conformal perturbations while maintaining a symmetry class and a fixed volume coordinate system.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
