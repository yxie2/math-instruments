# Push overlapping lifted tests down by their Euclidean norm

[Family 375](../families/openai-math-375.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000185.md) · [Batch B028](../../batches/B028/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Suppose a compactly supported Lipschitz amplitude is defined on a lifted tube whose map to physical space is a local diffeomorphism. At a physical point let xi_j be its finitely many nonzero inverse branches and define xi=(sum_j xi_j^2)^{1/2}.

Compact lifted support and local invertibility make the branch sum locally finite. The norm is Lipschitz even where it vanishes. Almost everywhere,

$$\sum_j|\nabla\xi_j|^2-|\nabla\xi|^2=\min_b\sum_j|\nabla\xi_j-\xi_j b|^2\ge0.$$

For sum xi_j^2>0 the minimizing vector is b=(sum xi_j grad xi_j)/(sum xi_j^2). On the common zero set all branch gradients vanish almost everywhere. Potential terms add exactly, regardless of their sign. Thus the physical quadratic form equals the lifted quadratic form minus the integral of this nonnegative variance.

For the normal map Phi(z,s)=z+s nu(z), Jacobian J=det(I+sA), and amplitude G(s)f(z,s), with G=1-tanh^2(s/sqrt(2)), the equation G''=(3g^2-1)G and normal integration by parts leave

$$\int G^2J|\nabla f|^2+f^2[-J_sGG'+3(v(\Phi)^2-g^2)G^2J].$$

The area formula counts all branches, so a globally injective normal tube is unnecessary. Caps can only be removed after establishing domination of the displayed terms.

## Role in the source argument

Transfers ambient stability to a lifted surface calculation while retaining an overlap correction that can later improve an error budget.

## Antecedents and comparison

The variance identity is the elementary least-squares formula underlying differentiation of a Euclidean norm. The source applies it to immersed normal tubes with exact potential accounting.

## Checks and limitations

One hundred sixty exact rational branch models verify the variance identity and potential additivity for positive and negative potentials. The normal Jacobian integration-by-parts identity is checked symbolically.

Finite branch algebra does not verify the global geometric hypotheses, domination for cap removal or the later curvature and reach estimates.

## Possible applications

Combining overlapping local tests into one physical test without paying an uncontrolled multiplicity factor.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
