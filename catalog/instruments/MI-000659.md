# Transport complementary bundles across an existing rational tangent box

[Family 052](../families/openai-math-052.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000428.md) · [Batch B069](../../batches/B069/README.md)

## Mathematical content

For a holomorphic splitting T_X=E1 direct sum E2, define nabla^i_xi e=P_i[xi,e] for xi in E_j, j!=i. The bracket identities give tensoriality in xi and the connection Leibniz rule in e without assuming either summand integrable.

Suppose a global tangent box F:P1 times P1->X is supplied, with F on the diagonal equal to g:P1->X and dF in factor j valued in E_j. Pull back the partial connection along the fibers of pr_i. In local frames its coefficient is the original connection coefficient composed with F, multiplied by the components of dF of the vertical vector. This construction uses no inverse differential and remains valid at critical points of F.

Each P1 fiber carries a nonsingular holomorphic connection, hence is flat because its holomorphic two-forms vanish. Simply connectedness gives path-independent transport from the diagonal. Holomorphic dependence of solutions of linear ODEs on parameters makes these identifications a holomorphic bundle isomorphism pr_i*(g*E_i)~=F*E_i, equal to the identity on the diagonal.

## Role in the source argument

Separates pulled-back summands after a tangent box has already been constructed.

## Antecedents and comparison

Projected-bracket partial connections and holomorphic ODE transport are classical. The inspected proof explicitly handles critical points and diagonal normalization. It does not itself supply the global tangent box.

## Checks and limitations

Symbolic vector-field brackets check both connection rules in a nonintegrable splitting; gauge-transformed ODE models check normalized transport, composition and critical-map pullback.

Local ODE models do not prove existence of a global box or automatic integrability.

## Possible applications

Comparing vector bundles across a product map even where that map is not immersive.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
