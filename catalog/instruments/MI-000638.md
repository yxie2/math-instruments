# Transfer a polynomial generalized inverse to canonical height spaces

[Family 016](../families/openai-math-016.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000407.md) · [Batch B067](../../batches/B067/README.md)

## Mathematical content

For polarized abelian varieties G,G' over the algebraic numbers, real homomorphisms act on both Lie spaces and H(G)=G(Qbar) tensor R, with canonical-height norm. For an integral homomorphism basis f_j, ample domination gives ||sum t_j f_j(x)||<=(sum |t_j| sqrt(C_j))||x||, uniformly over fields of definition. Thus convergence of real homomorphisms gives operator-norm convergence on height spaces.

The Lie action is faithful and the polarization adjoint lies in the real homomorphism space. For f, choose a real polynomial p with p(0)=0 and p(t)=1/t on the positive spectrum of f* f. Then f^dagger=p(f* f)f* is again a real homomorphism, and the Lie identities f^dagger f=P_(ker f)^perp and f f^dagger=P_im f become identities of real homomorphisms. They therefore hold on height spaces as well. For fixed f, ||f(x_i)||->0 implies ||P_(ker f)^perp(x_i)||->0 by boundedness of f^dagger. These real Lie subspaces need not be tangent spaces of abelian subvarieties; rationality is not inferred from real functional calculus.

## Role in the source argument

Allows normalized points at different height scales to be projected using limiting real homomorphisms.

## Antecedents and comparison

[Milne, Abelian Varieties](https://www.jmilne.org/math/CourseNotes/AVc.pdf), section 14, defines the Rosati adjoint and states its positivity; the definition, compatibility and positivity statement were read. Polynomial pseudoinverse construction is standard finite-dimensional spectral calculus. The arithmetic uniformity comes from the inspected source height-domination proof.

## Checks and limitations

Finite-rank matrices with exact prescribed singular spectra check polynomial inverse identities, projector transfer and the fixed-inverse estimate; varying singular values illustrate why a fixed map is required.

Matrix models do not verify canonical heights or the source Zilber-Pink sequence theorem.

## Possible applications

Transferring a finite-dimensional faithful representation identity to another normed representation of the same homomorphism algebra.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
