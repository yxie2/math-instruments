# Propagate uniform local compactness to properness of a disc-chain distance

[Family 058](../families/openai-math-058.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000436.md) · [Batch B070](../../batches/B070/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let U be a connected smooth complex manifold bounded in affine coordinates, with a cocompact biholomorphic group action. Its holomorphic-disc-chain distance d is finite, induces the ordinary topology and is proper. Bounded coordinate functions and Schwarz give d(x,y)>=c||x-y||; local chart discs give the opposite local topological control. A compact set K whose translates cover U has a uniform r>0 such that every closed r-ball centered in K is compact. Isometric translates give the same r everywhere.

If a closed R-ball is compact, cover it by finitely many r/4-balls. A disc chain to a point at distance <=R+r/4, with total length less than that distance plus r/4, can be cut at accumulated length R. Its remaining length is <r/2, placing the endpoint in one of finitely many compact r-balls. Thus the closed (R+r/4)-ball is compact; induction proves properness.

For a continuous deformation of closed analytic discs in an ambient chart, if all boundaries stay in U and one whole initial disc is in U, no disc escapes. Near a limiting parameter, a fixed boundary collar lies in a compact subset of U. Schwarz contraction bounds the distance of each interior point from that collar; properness keeps its limiting image in U. The allowed parameter set is therefore open, closed and nonempty. No extension of the discs past their boundary circles is needed.

## Role in the source argument

Gives a no-escape principle before making any fine assumptions on boundary regularity.

## Antecedents and comparison

This is a classical intrinsic-distance and cocompactness argument. The source writes the uniform-radius-to-properness step explicitly and then applies it to closed-disc deformations. Semialgebraicity is not used in this extracted step.

## Checks and limitations

Unit-disc automorphisms check distance invariance and compact-ball radii; finite path models check the R+r/4 covering budget, while an open-interval metric control shows why local compactness without a uniform radius is insufficient.

The examples do not establish smoothness of an initially singular affine set or the claimed symmetric-domain conclusion.

## Possible applications

Turning cocompact local metric control into a global continuation barrier.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
