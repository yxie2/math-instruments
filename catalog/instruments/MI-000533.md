# Convert cubic information savings into a flow of bounded nonlinear potential

[Family 229](../families/openai-math-229.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000302.md) · [Batch B054](../../batches/B054/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let 0<r<1. If nonnegative finite labels on a locally finite rooted tree satisfy z_v<=sum_(w child of v) r z_w/sqrt(1+z_w^2), choose 0<=d_vw<=r/sqrt(1+z_w^2) so that z_v=sum_w d_vw z_w. Set ell_root=1, ell_w=ell_v d_vw and theta(v,w)=ell_w z_w. This is a conserved nonnegative flow of root mass z_root.

With g_v=r^(-depth(v))ell_v, one has g_w^2(1+z_w^2)<=g_v^2. Consequently (r^(-depth(w))theta(v,w))^2<=g_v^2-g_w^2. Every finite path potential is at most one by telescoping, and so is every infinite-ray potential. For r=lambda^2 this is the source L^3 capacity normalization with edge resistances lambda^(-2 depth).

The useful source interface starts from 0<=m_v<=3, at most B children, b_w=r m_w, S=sum_w b_w and D=max_(w!=w') b_w b_w'(b_w+b_w'), taking D=0 for fewer than two children. Assume m_v<=S-cD. The elementary bound S^3<=sum_w b_w^3+B^3D implies, for 0<K<=c/B^3 and y_v=m_v+K m_v^3,

y_v<=r sum_w y_w-Kr(1-r^2)sum_w m_w^3.

Since y_w<= (1+9K)m_w, set delta=K(1-r^2)/(1+9K)^3. Then y_v<=r sum_w y_w(1-delta y_w^2)<=r sum_w y_w/sqrt(1+2delta y_w^2). Thus z=sqrt(2delta)y meets the flow recursion. The r-r^3 saving handles a one-child vertex even when the original pair saving is zero. The moment-saving hypothesis remains an external input.

## Role in the source argument

Turns positive posterior information into positive nonlinear capacity in one direction of the source proposed four-state criterion.

## Antecedents and comparison

[Pemantle-Peres, The critical Ising model on trees, concave recursions and nonlinear capacity](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0503137), Lemma 3.1, gives the general nonlinear capacity recursion; its complete proof on PDF pages 6-7 was read. The flow step here is its p=3 instance. The selected cubic change of variable was checked in the source; its required four-state moment-saving theorem was not independently certified.

## Checks and limitations

Exact rational models check the mixed-cubic bound and renormalization, including one-child cases. Finite trees check conservation and telescoping path potentials at high precision.

Finite trees do not prove an infinite-tree reconstruction theorem, the source moment-saving certificate or the converse capacity implication.

## Possible applications

Translating a branch-interaction information loss into a conserved flow with uniformly bounded nonlinear path potential.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
