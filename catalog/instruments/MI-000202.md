# Self-duality converts a mixed packing-covering witness into a full independent cover

[Family 185](../families/openai-math-185.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000122.md) · [Batch B012](../../batches/B012/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let N0,N1 be matroids on the same ground set F, each equal to its dual on these labels. Suppose F=P disjoint union C, there are disjoint S0,S1 contained in P spanning N0 restricted to P and N1 restricted to P, and I0,I1 contained in C cover C and are independent in the respective contractions onto C.

Contraction onto C is (Ni dual restricted to C) dual. Self-duality therefore implies Ti=C minus Ii spans Ni restricted to C. Since I0 union I1=C, T0,T1 are disjoint. Put Ai=Si union Ti. Restriction closure, cl_(Ni|X)(S)=cl_Ni(S) intersect X, shows Ai spans all of Ni; the Ai are disjoint. Complements F minus Ai are independent in Ni dual=Ni and cover F.

Thus a self-dual pair with no independent covering cannot have any packing/covering partition. Conversely an independent covering itself gives the partition P empty, C=F. The argument works for infinite matroids using the maximal-extension axiom and the usual spanning/dual-complement equivalence; no subtraction of infinite ranks is involved.

## Role in the source argument

Reduces the source counterexample task to excluding one simpler global covering condition.

## Antecedents and comparison

The mixed restriction/contraction formulation and its duality framework are established in [Bowler and Carmesin](https://www.math.uni-hamburg.de/spag/dm/papers/PackingCovering.pdf). This is a useful specialization to a self-dual pair, not a claim of a new general duality theorem.

## Checks and limitations

Read the complement and closure steps. Exact enumeration of small self-dual uniform and partition matroids checks the conversion for every tested mixed witness, including different matroids on the same labels.

Isomorphism to a dual after relabeling is insufficient for these same-set complement identities without transporting the witnesses. Finite examples verify the reduction, not an infinite counterexample.

## Possible applications

Simplifying obstruction constructions when duality can make packing and covering interchangeable.
