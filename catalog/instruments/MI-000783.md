# Exclude critical mass loss below a local Sobolev threshold

[Family 337](../families/openai-math-337.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000518.md) · [Batch B085](../../batches/B085/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let q=2m/(m-2)>2 and let Q on H^1_0(U), U relatively compact, be a nonnegative quadratic energy with positive Dirichlet coefficient and bounded potential. Suppose local chart estimates give (Y_m-epsilon)||v||_q^2 <= c||grad v||_2^2+C_epsilon||v||_2^2. If Y=inf_{||v||_q=1}Q(v)<Y_m, it is attained.

Use a squared partition sum chi_i^2=1. The identity sum |grad(chi_i v)|^2=|grad v|^2+v^2 sum |grad chi_i|^2 and the triangle inequality in L^(q/2) patch the Euclidean threshold with only a lower-order error. Zero extension and finitely many charts give compact L^2 inclusion without requiring regularity of the boundary of U.

A normalized minimizing sequence converges weakly in H^1 and strongly in L^2. Put t=||psi||_q^q. Brezis-Lieb splitting and the local threshold imply Y>=Y t^(2/q)+Y_m(1-t)^(2/q). Strict threshold and strict concavity force t=1, also when Y=0. Lower semicontinuity gives attainment.

## Role in the source argument

Identifying exactly which strict inequality prevents a minimizing sequence from concentrating.

## Checks and limitations

Symbolic squared-partition identities and scalar mass-splitting inequalities are checked, including Y=0 and the failure of the exclusion at threshold.

The numerical scalar check does not prove weak compactness or Brezis-Lieb. The source geometric nonnegativity of Q is an input, not certified here.
