# Reflect to unique high-dimensional fillers without changing low cells

[Family 312](../families/openai-math-312.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000220.md) · [Batch B037](../../batches/B037/README.md)

## Mathematical content

In the source free coherator convention, each generating operation of output dimension j has arity of height at most j. The q-coskeletal reflector R_q keeps all cells through dimension q and, successively above q, inserts exactly one filler for each parallel boundary.

Interpret operations in their original free cellular order. For output j<=q the arity bound ensures every input tuple already comes from X, so retain its old operation. For j>q the previously interpreted faces specify a boundary and its unique filler defines the operation. This establishes a model and the universal factorization into any q-coskeletal model. It does not reorder operations by dimension or bound the arities of composite operations. For k<q, R_k R_q is naturally R_k.

Reflection sends every boundary attachment in dimension j>k to an isomorphism. Since it preserves pushouts and transfinite colimits and its unit preserves cells through k, such higher attachments preserve all cells through k before reflection too. A source-face expansion in dimension j factors into boundary attachments in dimensions j and j+1, giving the corresponding statement. For a homotopy test in dimension k, choose a cutoff at least k+1 to retain the joining cells as well.

## Role in the source argument

Justifies reducing a finite-dimensional homotopy question without assuming the elementary-expansion result being proved later.
