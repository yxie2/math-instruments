# A double commutator upgrades low-energy compression to a moment bootstrap

[Family 225](../families/openai-math-225.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000096.md) · [Batch B006](../../batches/B006/README.md)

## Mathematical content

The source’s energy argument has the following abstract form. Let E be nonnegative self-adjoint, D bounded self-adjoint, and $B_a=\mathbf1_{\{E\le a/R\}}$, with R>0. Assume the relevant commutator identities are valid on the operator domain, $\|B_2DB_2\|\le A$, and $\|[D,[E,D]]\|\le C/R$. Then

$$\|DB_1\|^2=\|B_1D\|^2\le2A^2+C/2.$$

Indeed, setting $m=\|DB_1\|$, a unit vector in the range of B1 satisfies

$$\langle Du,EDu\rangle=\tfrac12\langle u,[D,[E,D]]u\rangle+\operatorname{Re}\langle Du,DEu\rangle\le(C/2+m^2)/R.$$

Its component above energy 2/R has squared norm at most C/4+m-squared/2; the low component is bounded by A. Taking the supremum absorbs the remaining half of m-squared.

If additionally $E\Omega=0$, $\|\Omega\|=1$, and the local structure gives

$$\langle D^k\Omega,ED^k\Omega\rangle\le(c_k/R)(1+\|D^{k-1}\Omega\|^2),$$

splitting at B1 yields the recursion $\|D^k\Omega\|^2\le c_k+(2A^2+C/2+c_k)\|D^{k-1}\Omega\|^2$. Thus all fixed moments are controlled whenever these constants are uniform.

In the six-vertex application $D=\sum_lg_lZ_l$. The conditions $\sum|g_l|\le AR$, $\sum|g_{l+1}-g_l|\le A$, and $\sum|g_{l+1}-g_l|^2\le A/R$ provide the needed bounds. Neighboring spin exchange preserves $Z_l+Z_{l+1}$, so the double commutator costs squared coefficient differences. The source separately verifies domain preservation on its local-spin subspace.

## Role in the source argument

Turns estimates for spectrally compressed current operators into ordinary equal-time moments, which can then control neutral height averages.

## Antecedents and comparison

Double-commutator energy estimates and spectral projection arguments are classical. The closer six-vertex transfer and reflection framework is [Duminil-Copin, Kozlowski, Lammers and Manolescu](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.06268v1). This card gives an explicit abstraction of the source’s absorption step, not a claim that its general ingredients are new.

## Checks and limitations

Checked the double-commutator sign, the factor two in the projection cutoff, and the power-moment recursion. Finite Hermitian examples test the stated bound; a two-level example shows that a compression bound alone does not control the one-sided norm.

The bounded commutator and domain hypotheses must be established in each application. The power-energy estimate is additional input; the initial compression estimate alone does not imply uniform moments.

## Possible applications

Obtaining ordinary moments of spatial observables from low-energy operator estimates in lattice and transfer-matrix models.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
