# Preserve flux forms while computing the exact conformal constraint deficit

[Family 260](../families/openai-math-260.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000502.md) · [Batch B081](../../batches/B081/README.md)

## Mathematical content

In dimension three let 2 mu=R+(tr K)^2-|K|^2 and J=div(K-(tr K)g). Keep the closed flux forms alpha_i=i_(X_i)dV_g fixed while setting g'=u^4 g and K'=u^2 K, u>0. Then X_i'=u^(-6)X_i, u^4 mu'=mu-4u^(-1)Delta u, and u^2 J'=J+4K(grad log u, .).

For |Y|_g<=1 put I_g(Y)=|X_1|^2+|X_2|^2+2<X_1 cross X_2,Y> and D_g(Y)=mu+J(Y)-I_g(Y). Cauchy-Schwarz gives I_g(Y)>=0. Direct substitution gives
u^4 D_(g')(u^(-2)Y)-D_g(Y)=4u^(-1)(-Delta u+K(grad u,Y))+(1-u^(-4))I_g(Y).
For u>=1 this is bounded below by 4u^(-1)(-Delta u-|K||du|). Rotating the pair of flux forms by a constant SO(2) matrix preserves I and D. A surface null expansion theta_sigma=H+sigma tr_S K transforms as theta_sigma'=u^(-2)(theta_sigma+4 partial_nu log u).

The proof uses the scalar-curvature formula and the connection difference 2(df(A)B+df(B)A-g(A,B)grad f), f=log u. The trace-gradient terms cancel in the transformed momentum constraint. Retaining the flux forms, rather than the vector components, is essential.

## Role in the source argument

Designing deformations with an explicit margin for the dominant-energy condition and boundary expansion.

## Antecedents and comparison

These are classical conformal geometric identities arranged to track the electromagnetic matter deficit and boundary expansion simultaneously. No new conformal transformation law is claimed.

## Checks and limitations

Direct pointwise tensor contractions check the transformed momentum, curvature deficit, electromagnetic scaling and SO(2) invariance; a radial sphere checks the expansion formula.

These identities do not solve the elliptic deformation problem, provide global end control or prove a Penrose inequality.

## Possible applications

Designing deformations with an explicit margin for the dominant-energy condition and boundary expansion.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
