# Differentiate an endpoint Schur complement without assuming endpoint order

[Family 344](../families/openai-math-344.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000256.md) · [Batch B047](../../batches/B047/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Write a smooth symmetric block matrix A(t) as [[V(t),C(t)],[C(t)^T,H(t)]], and suppose H'(t)>0 on the relevant intervals. For distinct endpoints with invertible Delta H=H(y)-H(x), put R=Delta C (Delta H)^(-1), Q=Delta V-Delta C (Delta H)^(-1)Delta C^T, and S(a)=V'(a)-C'(a)H'(a)^(-1)C'(a)^T.

Block congruence gives det(Delta A)=det(Delta H)det Q and Q(y,x)=-Q(x,y). For either endpoint a, the derivative with the appropriate sign is

partial_y Q at a=y, or -partial_x Q at a=x
= S(a)+(R-C'(a)H'(a)^(-1))H'(a)(R-C'(a)H'(a)^(-1))^T >= S(a).

This square uses positivity of H'(a), not positivity of Delta H. It therefore remains valid when the actual endpoints are reversed. Near an ordinary diagonal, Q(x,y)=(y-x)S(x)+O((y-x)^2).

In the source, additional geometric identities give Q(u,u+D)=0, with each moving endpoint staying inside one smooth chart interval. Starting or ending at these zeros and integrating yields Q(u+iD,v+jD)>=integral_u^v S(t+jD)dt and >=integral_u^v S(t+iD)dt for 0<u<v<D and i,j in {0,1}. These positive lower bounds require the cut-pair zeros and positive S; they do not follow from the derivative identity alone.

## Role in the source argument

Handles the four folded endpoint configurations without reversing a lower bound when x>y.

## Antecedents and comparison

[Boyd-Vandenberghe, Convex Optimization](https://web.stanford.edu/~boyd/cvxbook/bv_cvxbook.pdf), Appendix A.5.5, gives the classical block determinant and quadratic minimization formulas; that section was read. The source packages their differential square with its geometric zero anchors.

## Checks and limitations

Exact rational noncommuting block examples verify the square and determinant identity for positive and negative Delta H. A negative H prime supplies a sign control.

The finite matrix checks do not validate the source Lagrangian construction, cut-pair intersection dimension, or chart-pole avoidance. The anchored inequalities are conditional on those separately stated inputs.

## Possible applications

Matrix comparison estimates where endpoint order changes but the local derivative block remains positive.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
