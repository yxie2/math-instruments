# Collision counts control exceptional fibers as well as exceptional values

[Family 024](../families/openai-math-024.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000141.md) · [Batch B017](../../batches/B017/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let F:T -> W be a map of finite sets, T=G disjoint-union D. Assume every w outside E subset W is covered. Write r_w=|G intersection F^(-1)(w)| and K=sum_w r_w(r_w-1), counting ordered collisions among good representations. Then

$$-|E|\le |T|-|W|\le |D|+K/2.$$

Define E*=E union F(D) union {w:r_w>=2}. One has

$$|E^*|\le |E|+|D|+K/2,\qquad |F^{-1}(E^*)|\le |E|+2|D|+K.$$

Outside these exceptional fibers, F is a bijection onto W minus E*. Coverage gives the lower count. For r>=1, r-1<=r(r-1)/2 bounds the excess of good representations. For r>=0, r<=1+r(r-1)/2 bounds their total within E*; add the discarded set D. These calculations include uncovered exceptional values and fibers mixing good and discarded representations.

The preimage bound is essential when later arguments sum over representations. A single exceptional value may have an arbitrarily large fiber; its value count alone cannot bound the weight of its representations. Arbitrary weighted versions require further control on representation weights and are not implied by these cardinality formulas.

## Role in the source argument

Turns estimates on prime-prefix tuples into estimates on distinct totient values while controlling all discarded preimages.

## Antecedents and comparison

This is elementary fiber counting via the second factorial moment. The explicit two-level exceptional bookkeeping is useful, but the card makes no claim of a new counting principle.

## Checks and limitations

Read the complete proof. Exhaustive small maps, good/discarded partitions and covering exception sets check every inequality and the resulting bijection; high-multiplicity controls distinguish value and preimage counts.

The required pair-collision estimate K and coverage outside E must be proved in the application. A bound on collisions in G does not control an unbounded discarded set D.

## Possible applications

Passing from parametrizations to distinct objects when both exceptional objects and their representations must remain negligible.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
