# Transfer nearby metrics while protecting a prescribed subspace

[Family 251](../families/openai-math-251.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000346.md) · [Batch B059](../../batches/B059/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let A,B be positive Hilbert-space operators between I/2 and 2I, with A-B=h Gamma, Gamma self-adjoint, ||Gamma||<=1 and 0<=h<=1/8. Let P commute with B, let R=PE, and assume ||P Gamma P||<=eta<=1. There is an explicit invertible S_R satisfying S_R^* A S_R=B, S_R(R)=R, ||S_R-I||<=Ch and ||(S_R-I)P||<=Ch eta, with dimension-independent C.

Whiten B and write B^(-1/2)AB^(-1/2)=[a b; b^* d] relative to R and its original orthogonal complement. Put E_0=d-b^*a^(-1)b and

Q_R=[a^(-1/2), -a^(-1)b E_0^(-1/2); 0, E_0^(-1/2)],
S_R=B^(-1/2)Q_R B^(1/2).

Multiplication proves the metric identity, and the zero lower-left block preserves R. The spectral bounds give ||a-I||<=2h eta, ||b||<=2h, E_0>=1-2h and ||E_0-I||<=2h+4h^2/(1-2h). Functional calculus yields the two error scales. In particular eta=0 makes S_R exactly the identity on R. For nested protected subspaces R contained in R', S_(R')^(-1)S_R is B-unitary, O(h) globally and O(h eta) on R. No preservation of the original complementary subspace is asserted.

To fix an almost-fixed subspace exactly, let U be unitary with ||U-I||<=t<1/2 and ||(U-I)P||<=e<=1/2. In block form [a b;c d], replace U by I on R and the polar factor d(d^*d)^(-1/2) on its complement. The compression d is invertible because ||d-I||<1. Since d^*d=I-b^*b, the polar correction costs at most e^2, and the discarded blocks cost at most 2e. The resulting unitary fixes R and differs from U by at most 3e.

## Antecedents and comparison

Schur-complement elimination and positive square roots are classical. [Higham, Computing the Polar Decomposition with Applications](https://eprints.maths.manchester.ac.uk/694/1/high86p.pdf), Section 2.1 and Lemma 2.1 on printed page 1161, records the polar formulas used for the final correction; that section was read. The source combines these ingredients with a designated-subspace error scale. Earliest priority of that precise interface is not established.

## Checks and limitations

18 complex metric pairs, including nine zero-compression controls and zero/full-subspace endpoints, verify transfer identities, protection and nested B-unitarity. Thirty-six unitary examples verify exact fixing and the 3e correction bound. Maximum metric residual is below 4e-15.

Observed finite norm ratios do not establish a universal constant; the dimension-independent estimate rests on the inspected spectral argument. The global Ulam-stability construction is not tested.

## Possible applications

Changing inner products while preserving a designated subspace and repairing a nearly fixed subspace exactly.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
