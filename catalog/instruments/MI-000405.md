# Derive finite generation from finite mod-p homotopy and derived completeness

[Family 313](../families/openai-math-313.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000221.md) · [Batch B037](../../batches/B037/README.md)

## Mathematical content

For a derived p-complete spectrum X, all groups pi_j X are finitely generated over Z_p if and only if all pi_j(X/p) are finite, with no connectivity assumption.

The cofiber sequence gives 0 -> pi_j X/p -> pi_j(X/p) -> (pi_{j-1}X)[p] -> 0. Both ends are killed by p, but the middle need not be. Exact triangles relating X/p^a, X/p^{a+1} and X/p show that every pi_j(X/p^a) is a finite p-group. Decreasing images in a finite target stabilize; the Milnor sequence and derived completeness then identify pi_j X with their inverse limit.

This makes M=pi_j X a compact pro-p Z_p-module, with pM closed and M/pM finite. Lift a basis of M/pM to a_i. Successively write z_l=sum_i b_{i,l}a_i+p z_{l+1}, with digits 0<=b_{i,l}<p. The digit sums converge in Z_p. The residual p^L z_L tends uniformly to zero in every fixed finite target because that target has bounded p-exponent. Hence the resulting Z_p-linear map Z_p^d -> M is surjective, not merely dense.

Conversely, finite generation makes both ends of the displayed cofiber sequence finite. This lemma supplies only the completion step; finiteness of the mod-p chromatic input remains a separate theorem.

## Role in the source argument

Converts a finite mod-p calculation into abstract finite generation without assuming homotopy-group completeness in advance.

## Checks and limitations

One hundred twenty finite p-power digit-lifting models check exact reconstruction from generators modulo p, through eight precision levels.

No spectrum or chromatic mod-p finiteness input is constructed by the tests.

## Possible applications

Completion arguments in stable homotopy where finiteness is first accessible after a mod-p cofiber.
