# Ordinal slices assemble reverse injections from a classified seed

[Family 244](../families/openai-math-244.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000157.md) · [Batch B021](../../batches/B021/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Work in ZF. Assume a nonempty seed A satisfies: every nonempty X is a surjective image of A times an ordinal; every ordinal-indexed family of nonempty sets has a choice function; and every nonwellorderable surjective image of A is bijective with A. Then every surjection f:X->Y admits an injection Y->X.

For Y nonempty choose s:A times eta->X onto. Put B_i=s[A times {i}], Y_i=f[B_i] without union_(k<i)f[B_k], and S_i=B_i intersect f^(-1)[Y_i]. The Y_i partition Y, the S_i are pairwise disjoint, and f restricted to S_i maps onto Y_i. The S_i need not cover X.

Define J_i as the set of injections from Y_i into S_i before making any choices. If Y_i is empty, the empty map belongs. If it is wellorderable, ordinal-indexed choice chooses one preimage in each fiber. If it is nonwellorderable, so is S_i. Every nonempty subset of an image of A is itself an image of A: for that one subset, send all points outside it to one fixed member. Consequently S_i and Y_i are both bijective with A, so an injection between them exists.

These are pointwise existence proofs, not a simultaneous choice of wellorderings, default elements or bijections. Only after all J_i are formed and shown nonempty, apply ordinal-indexed choice to select j_i in J_i. Their union is an injection because both the target slices and source slices are disjoint.

In the nonwellorderable case the injection need not respect f's fibers. The conclusion is the Partition Principle, not a right inverse for every surjection.

## Antecedents and comparison

[Ryan-Smith, Propositions 3.17 and 3.20](https://eprints.whiterose.ac.uk/id/eprint/224344/15/s10474-025-01533-3.pdf) uses ordinal slicing and choice from sets of candidate injections. The source assumes the stronger classification of nonwellorderable seed images. Neither mere quotient injections nor an unproved injective presentation may replace its stated hypotheses.

## Checks and limitations

Read the complete proof and prior local-to-global statement. Finite models check disjoint slice construction, union injectivity and examples where the chosen reverse injection is not a section.

Every finite set is wellorderable. The finite models cannot verify the nonwellorderable branch, ordinal-choice preservation or existence of the source symmetric model.
