# Optimize a Gauss valuation along a smooth factor

[Family 037](../families/openai-math-037.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000417.md) · [Batch B068](../../batches/B068/README.md)

## Mathematical content

For an N-dimensional klt germ and a positive multiple v of a divisorial valuation, write A=A(v)>0 and sigma=vol(v). On its product with a smooth curve, use the Gauss valuation w(sum f_i t^i)=min_i(v(f_i)+ia), a>0. Its discrepancy is A+a. The colength of its level-k valuation ideal is the sum over i<k/a of the original colengths at level k-ia. The resulting Riemann sum gives vol(w)=sigma/a.

Taking a=A/N minimizes (A+a)^(N+1)/a. With density defined as normalized volume divided by dimension to that dimension, this yields d((x,0),X times A^1)<=d(x,X): at the chosen a, (A+a)^(N+1) sigma/(a (N+1)^(N+1))=A^N sigma/N^N. Passing to the infimum uses the established divisorial approximation of normalized volume. No reverse inequality is needed or proved by this calculation.

## Role in the source argument

Allows a lower-dimensional strict density bound to obstruct a higher-dimensional equality case.

## Antecedents and comparison

Gauss extension and optimization are standard valuation techniques. The inspected source uses this precise normalization for its dimension reduction. No new general product formula is claimed.

## Checks and limitations

Exact monomial colength convolution, weighted simplex asymptotics, and symbolic minimization check the volume normalization and optimum added weight.

Monomial models do not prove divisorial approximation or the headline singularity classification.
