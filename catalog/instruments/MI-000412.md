# Lift anti-invariant two-forms to closed forms by an elliptic inverse

[Family 342](../families/openai-math-342.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000225.md) · [Batch B038](../../batches/B038/README.md)

## Mathematical content

On a closed almost-Hermitian four-manifold let R project two-forms onto the J-anti-invariant subbundle, contained in the self-dual subbundle. The operator L=R d d* on anti-invariant forms has principal symbol |xi|^2/2 times identity and satisfies <La,b>=<d*a,d*b>. Its kernel is smooth, coclosed and self-dual, hence closed.

Let H project onto this kernel and let G be the generalized inverse of L, of order -2. Then

$$B=dd^*G+H,\qquad dB=0,\qquad RB=I.$$

B is an order-zero closed lift. For closed alpha, (I-BR)alpha is closed and invariant, with [alpha]=[(I-BR)alpha]+[HR alpha]. The second summand is represented by a harmonic anti-invariant form. Its intersection pairing is positive definite, and it is orthogonal to invariant classes. Thus H^2 splits into the invariant classes and H_J^-.

For exact alpha, harmonic orthogonality gives HR alpha=0, so both BR alpha and (I-BR)alpha remain exact. Positivity is not a property of this linear projection.

## Role in the source argument

Supplies a controlled linear correction before the nonlinear positivity/current argument.

## Antecedents and comparison

[Draghici and Zhang, Proposition 2.1 in the arXiv version](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1111.7287) gives the exact-form correction phenomenon. [Draghici, Li and Zhang, Theorem 2.3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0812.3680) establishes the cohomology decomposition. The source gives an explicit order-zero lift; no new-priority claim for the decomposition.

## Checks and limitations

One hundred exact nonzero Fourier-symbol models on a flat Hermitian four-torus verify closedness and RB=I; the harmonic correction is checked separately.

Symbol calculations do not independently construct the global pseudodifferential inverse or establish positivity of a corrected form.

## Possible applications

Removing an anti-invariant component while preserving closedness, and preserving exactness when the harmonic obstruction vanishes.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
