# Multiply normalized projection-body volumes using Cartesian products

[Family 088](../families/openai-math-088.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000260.md) · [Batch B048](../../batches/B048/README.md)

## Mathematical content

For a full-dimensional polytope P, let v_F=area(F) times its outward unit normal. Cauchy's projection formula gives Pi P=sum_F[-v_F/2,v_F/2]. For full-dimensional A in R^r and B in R^s, r,s>=2, the product facets have vectors (|B|v_F,0) and (0,|A|v_G). Hence Pi(A times B)=(|B|Pi A) times (|A|Pi B).

Define R_d(P)=|Pi P|/|P|^(d-1). The volume exponents cancel exactly, giving R_(r+s)(A times B)=R_r(A)R_s(B). Affine covariance Pi(LP)=|det L| L^(-T)Pi P makes R_d invariant under invertible affine changes.

For the standard simplex T_d, its projection body is a translate of ([0,1]^d+[0,(1,...,1)])/(d-1)!. Extrusion along the diagonal adds volume d to the unit cube, so |Pi T_d|=(d+1)/((d-1)!)^d and R_d(T_d)=c_d=(d+1)d^d/d!. The product comparison is therefore

c_r c_s/c_(r+s)=[(r+1)(s+1)/(r+s+1)] binomial(r+s,r) r^r s^s/(r+s)^(r+s).

At r=s=10 it is 22355476/22020096>1, with exact positive numerator difference 335380. This reproduces the source finite-dimensional product witness. Smaller products need not exceed the simplex value; multiplicativity alone does not imply an excess in every dimension.

## Antecedents and comparison

[Saroglou, On the shape of a convex body with respect to its second projection body](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1409.4347v2), Section 1, states Cauchy projection and affine invariance; these pages were read. The product and simplex calculations were read in the source and checked algebraically. A targeted antecedent comparison is not an exhaustive search for earlier product witnesses.

## Checks and limitations

Determinant enumeration independently computes low-dimensional simplex projection-zonotope volumes and products. Exact rational arithmetic reproduces the dimension-20 ratio and detects subcritical smaller equal-dimensional products.

No formal proof build or independent human review was performed. The separate principal Petty theorem and the companion asymptotic exponential-excess argument are outside this extraction.

Testing proposed affine-invariant extremizers against products whose factors have explicit facet descriptions.
