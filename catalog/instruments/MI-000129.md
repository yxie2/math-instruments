# Antipodal row and column incidences force quadratic exact coordinate dimension

[Family 094](../families/openai-math-094.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000087.md) · [Batch B003](../../batches/B003/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Fix $1<p<\infty$, $p\ne2$, and integer $k\ge2$. In $\ell_p^{k^2}$ let $r_i$ and $c_j$ be the indicator vectors of the rows and columns of a $k\times k$ grid. The $4k+1$ points

$$\{0\}\cup\{r_i,-r_i:1\le i\le k\}\cup\{c_j,-c_j:1\le j\le k\}$$

require at least $k^2$ coordinates in any isometric ell_p embedding, even an unrestricted nonlinear one. Translate and rescale a distortion-one map to fix zero. Strict convexity forces preservation of antipodes. The p-parallelogram defect

$$\|u+v\|_p^p+\|u-v\|_p^p-2\|u\|_p^p-2\|v\|_p^p$$

vanishes exactly when the supports are disjoint: every nonzero scalar defect has the sign of $p-2$. The images of different rows therefore have disjoint supports, as do different columns. Every row–column pair has defect $2^p-4\ne0$, so their image supports intersect. A coordinate can witness at most one of the $k^2$ incidences. For arbitrary $n\ge9$, padding gives the lower bound $\lfloor(n-1)/4\rfloor^2$.

## Role in the source argument

Explains the discontinuity between exact coordinate realization and the source’s approximate dimension reduction, including even integer exponents greater than two.

## Antecedents and comparison

The support test is classical: [Lamperti, Corollary 2.1](https://msp.org/pjm/1958/8-3/pjm-v8-n3-p09-s.pdf). The source credits Ball’s earlier quadratic-order sharpness for 1<=p<2. The candidate extracted device is the finite antipodal incidence configuration extending this argument across p>2; no claim of exhaustive novelty verification is made.

## Checks and limitations

Checked the scalar-sign proof and strict-convexity step. The bundled finite calculation verifies all row/column defects and support incidences for several grid sizes, with p=2 as a negative control.

Exact equality is essential; the proof gives no comparable dimension lower bound at fixed distortion greater than one. Strict convexity excludes p=1 from this argument, and the defect vanishes identically at p=2.

## Possible applications

Encoding combinatorial incidence constraints into exact metric realizations, and constructing small certificates that a proposed coordinate compression cannot preserve all distances.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
