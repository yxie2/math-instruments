# A norm ultraproduct preserves spectral minima with unbounded representation dimensions

[Family 292](../families/openai-math-292.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000144.md) · [Batch B018](../../batches/B018/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let x_n(g) be unitaries in a nonzero unital C*-algebra B with ||x_n(g)x_n(h)-x_n(gh)||->omega 0 for each fixed g,h in Gamma. Let pi_n be any sequence of nonzero finite-dimensional unitary representations, with no bound on dimension. For a finite S with representation gap kappa, put R=4|S| and

$$P_n=\sum_{s\in S}(x_n(s)\otimes\overline{\pi_n(s)}-I)^*(x_n(s)\otimes\overline{\pi_n(s)}-I).$$

Form the norm ultraproduct of C_n=B tensor B(conjugate V_pi_n). The class g->[x_n(g) tensor conjugate pi_n(g)] is a genuine representation because its multiplicative defects are exactly those of x_n(g), tensor a unitary. Its Laplacian has spectrum in {0} union [kappa,R]. Since 0<=P_n<=RI and quotient norms are ultralimits,

$$\min\operatorname{spec}[P_n]=R-\|RI-[P_n]\|=\lim_\omega\min\operatorname{spec}P_n.$$

Consequently the limit lies in {0} union [kappa,R]. If the coordinate minima are below a fixed a<kappa on an omega-large set, the limit is zero.

The entire sequence pi_n is inserted into one ultraproduct. This permits a contradiction argument selecting a different bad representation at every coordinate; proving convergence separately for each fixed representation would not supply that uniformity. No countable intersection of ultrafilter-large sets is used.

## Role in the source argument

Makes small spectral matches uniformly small even when the matching representations grow in dimension.

## Antecedents and comparison

The positive-element spectral formula and quotient norm identity are standard C*-algebra facts. The extracted candidate is their application to a varying representation sequence; no new general ultraproduct theorem is claimed.

## Checks and limitations

Read the complete proof. Finite positive matrices of varying sizes check the spectral-minimum formula. Perturbed cyclic-group representations with varying multiplicities check the gap and dimension-independent continuity estimate for the Laplacian.

Finite perturbation checks cannot certify an ultrafilter limit. Exact coordinate group relations are unnecessary, but asymptotic defects for every fixed pair are an essential input. Nuclearity is not needed for this isolated lemma.

## Possible applications

Uniformizing a spectral exclusion over a family whose dimensions cannot be bounded in advance.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
