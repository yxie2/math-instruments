# Convert formal integration coefficients into a nonzero first column

[Family 116](../families/openai-math-116.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000464.md) · [Batch B074](../../batches/B074/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Over a characteristic-zero field, define J_i(a)=integral_0^z a(t)/(t+i) dt for positive integers i. This raises vanishing order and therefore acts on F[[z]]/(z^d). In the monomial basis its strictly lower triangular matrix is (T_i)_(r,q)=(-1)^(r-q-1)/(r i^(r-q)) for 0<=q<r<d, and zero otherwise. Expansion of 1/(z+i) and termwise integration prove the formula.

If pi_d retains the first d coefficients, pi_d J_i=T_i pi_d. Thus for a word w=i1...ik, T_i1...T_ik e0 is the truncated coefficient vector of J_i1...J_ik(1), with the rightmost operator acting first. Constants use scalar identity matrices and the empty word is included. For any polynomial f, f(T)e0=pi_d(h_f), so h_f!=0 with order below d implies f(T)!=0.

The source invokes separate independence and multiplicity inputs to bound order(h_f)<2ns^2 for its formula class, taking d=2ns^2. That bound is a dependency of the hitting theorem, not proved by this coefficient identity. The entries have directly controlled rational bit length and can be constructed by integer powers and products.

## Role in the source argument

Makes a formal-series witness into an explicit rational matrix test.

## Antecedents and comparison

Formal integration and jet representations are established constructions. The selected step is the explicit coefficient formula and its first-column detection interface. The source multiplicity input is not independently audited in this card.

## Checks and limitations

Exact rational matrices are compared with independently expanded and integrated series for words and commutators; tests check multiplication order, constants and truncation.

No universal hitting theorem follows from finite examples or from the jet identity alone.

## Possible applications

Turning a proved order-of-vanishing bound into an explicit finite-dimensional evaluation.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
