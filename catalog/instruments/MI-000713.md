# Keep boundary terms in the finite van der Corput estimate

[Family 154](../families/openai-math-154.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000480.md) · [Batch B076](../../batches/B076/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Extend u_1,...,u_N in a complex Hilbert space by zero. For an integer H>=1, H sum_n u_n=sum_(m=1)^(N+H-1) sum_(j=0)^(H-1)u_(m-j). Cauchy-Schwarz and expansion of the window norms give
||N^(-1) sum_n u_n||^2 <= (N+H-1)/(N^2 H^2) [H sum_n ||u_n||^2 + 2 sum_(h=1)^(H-1)(H-h) Re sum_(n=1)^(N-h) <u_(n+h),u_n>],
where an empty inner sum is zero.

For a uniformly bounded infinite sequence with bound M, suppose each normalized fixed-lag correlation has a limit gamma_h and gamma_h ->0. First let N grow with H fixed. The limsup of the squared mean is at most M^2/H+(2/H) sum_(h=1)^(H-1)|gamma_h|. Then let H grow. The Cesaro means of |gamma_h| vanish, proving convergence of the averages to zero in norm. Interchanging these limits without uniform correlation estimates is unjustified.

## Role in the source argument

Provides the norm-convergence component used to identify an already existing pointwise limit.

## Antecedents and comparison

This is the classical Hilbert-space van der Corput argument. Its explicit finite window identity makes boundary and normalization errors testable; no novelty is claimed for the criterion.

## Checks and limitations

Complex vector arrays check the exact window expansion and inequality, including H>N. Constant vectors test sharp equality at H=1 and the necessity of correlation decay.

No correlation decay or mixing hypothesis for the source multiple products is established by the finite check.

## Possible applications

Verifying finite correlation estimates before passing to an asymptotic ergodic statement.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
