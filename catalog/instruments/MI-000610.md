# Flatten model distance while keeping its upper Hessian bound

[Family 356](../families/openai-math-356.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000379.md) · [Batch B063](../../batches/B063/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Assume the source local distributional Hessian comparison for q=d(p,.)²/2 on a radius R*. Let s_kappa(r) be the model sine, c_kappa=s_kappa', and m_kappa(r)=integral_0^r s_kappa. Choose 0<a<b<R*, with b<pi/(2sqrt(kappa)) if kappa>0. For a smooth nonincreasing cutoff theta equal to one through a²/2 and zero after b²/2, define chi(0)=0, chi'(q)=theta(q)s_kappa(sqrt(2q))/sqrt(2q), and F=chi(q).

Then F is bounded and globally Lipschitz, agrees with m_kappa(d(p,.)) on B_a(p), and is constant outside B_b(p). The weak chain rule gives H_F(Z,Z)(h)=H_q(Z,Z)(h chi')+integral h chi'' <grad q,Z>². The comparison coefficients in radial and transverse directions become A(r)=theta c_kappa+r theta' s_kappa and B(r)=theta c_kappa. Since theta'<=0 and s_kappa>=0, A<=B. Multiplication by chi'>=0 preserves any nonpositive singular Hessian part. Therefore G=theta(q)c_kappa(r) is a bounded continuous upper Hessian coefficient. On B_a, G=1-kappa F. No global L² assumption on the constant tail of F is required.

## Role in the source argument

Makes a local distance comparison admissible for a theorem requiring bounded global functions.

## Antecedents and comparison

The mechanism is classical radial chain-rule calculus. The source carefully retains the sign of the singular Hessian measure. The local distributional Hessian comparison is an input, not proved by this cutoff.

## Checks and limitations

Positive, zero and negative model curvatures check the differentiated radial formulas, A<=B, the inner model equation and the constant outer tail.

Smooth model checks do not prove the underlying RCD Hessian comparison or its transfer to every geodesic.

## Possible applications

Globalizing local semiconcavity estimates without introducing a positive cutoff error in the radial direction.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
