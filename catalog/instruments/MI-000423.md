# Remove cyclic base change by a continuous seam correction

[Family 064](../families/openai-math-064.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000230.md) · [Batch B039](../../batches/B039/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Suppose f_t admits, after t=u^e, a jointly continuous tube trivialization H_u:T_0 -> T_(u^e), with continuous inverse, H_0=id, fixed central section and f_(u^e) H_u=f_0. The tubes for u and zeta u coincide, where zeta=exp(2 pi i/e). Set J_s=H_(zeta s)^(-1) H_s; this is an automorphism preserving f_0 and J_0=id.

For t=r^e exp(i theta), 0<=theta<=2 pi, define

$$\Phi_t=H_{r\exp(i\theta/e)}\circ J_{r\theta/(2\pi)}.$$

At theta=0 this is H_r, and at theta=2 pi it is H_(zeta r) J_r=H_r. Thus the angular seam closes, as does the inverse seam. Joint continuity at u=0 makes both factors tend to identity uniformly in the angle on compact sets, so the central circle collapses continuously. This descends the given trivialization without requiring a deck-equivariant resolution. A finite disk base change can first be normalized to u^e by absorbing its nonvanishing unit.

## Role in the source argument

Removes a ramified base change after the difficult geometric lifting and tube trivialization have already been obtained.

## Antecedents and comparison

Explicit continuous gluing using a path of fiber automorphisms. No independent priority claim or assertion that arbitrary discontinuous transports descend.

## Checks and limitations

Three hundred twelve linear transport models with moving fiber functions verify right equivalence, inverses and the seam for e=1,...,6; maximum seam error is about 1.6e-16.

The tests use full linear spaces, not closed-ball Milnor tubes. They do not construct the required geometric trivialization or certify the source headline theorem.

## Possible applications

Descent of already constructed local trivializations through finite disk covers.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
