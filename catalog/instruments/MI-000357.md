# Generate commuting edges from sparse markers and propagate a height window

[Family 259](../families/openai-math-259.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000197.md) · [Batch B031](../../batches/B031/README.md)

## Mathematical content

For a Bernoulli action with an infinite-order element t, the set {x:x(1)<p} meets almost every t-orbit. Add N_p={x:x(t^n)>=p for all integers n}; this invariant exceptional set is null because the probability of avoiding p in N independent coordinates is (1-p)^N. The resulting C_p has measure p and meets every orbit.

Keep all t-edges and restrict each of d generators j commuting with t to C_p. To realize j at z, move by t^n to C_p, apply j there, then return by t^{-n}; commutation gives zt^n j t^{-n}=zj. The graphing therefore has cost 1+dp and generates the full product-factor relation.

In the source height extension modulo M>100, d=100. Add the a-edges at heights 0 through 98, costing 99/M. Its defining word relation implies that availability at 99 consecutive source heights supplies the next height. Induction reaches every height, including edges ending back at height zero. The cost is at most 1+100p+99/M; letting p decrease to zero gives the source upper bound 1+99/M.

## Role in the source argument

Constructs the explicit action of small cost used in the source comparison between actions.

## Antecedents and comparison

The commuting-marker argument appears in [Gaboriau 2000, Proposition VI.23](https://perso.ens-lyon.fr/gaboriau/Travaux-Publi/Cout/Cout.pdf). The source gives explicit Bernoulli markers and a finite-height word propagation.

## Checks and limitations

One thousand five hundred finite commuting-marker paths and twelve height-window inductions are checked.

The finite models do not verify the full group presentation, freeness of its action or the separate larger-cost lower bound.

## Possible applications

Sparse generating graphings for commuting actions and finite cyclic extensions.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
