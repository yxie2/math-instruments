# A finite flat product map realizes prescribed cohomological roots over every base ring

[Family 194](../families/openai-math-194.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000147.md) · [Batch B019](../../batches/B019/README.md)

## Mathematical content

For distinct integers r1>...>rn, there is a vector bundle V of rank n! on P^n over Z such that, over every commutative ring A, V(a) has no cohomology if a equals any r_j. Otherwise its only cohomology is a finite free A-module in degree v(a)=#{j:r_j>a}, with Euler characteristic product_j(a-r_j).

Use the multiplication map f:(P1)^n->P^n taking an ordered collection of binary linear forms to their product. It is proper with finite geometric fibers, hence finite. On every field fiber, equal-dimensional smooth source and target give flatness by the Cohen-Macaulay/regular dimension criterion. Fiberwise flatness over Z gives finite flatness globally. A generic characteristic-zero form has n! ordered factorizations, so the constant rank is n!.

Set V=f_* O(-1-r1,...,-1-rn). Finite flatness gives a vector bundle and arbitrary base-change compatibility. The projection formula identifies the cohomology of V(a) with that of O(a-1-r1,...,a-1-rn) on (P1_A)^n. On P1_A, O(m) has free H0 of rank m+1 for m>=0, free H1 of rank -m-1 for m<=-2, and zero cohomology for m=-1. These integral cohomology complexes split, so their tensor product gives the claimed single degree, vanishing roots and product Euler polynomial. For n=0 use the rank-one bundle on P0.

An additive module length with lambda(A)=mu then gives Euler characteristic mu product_j(a-r_j). This last statement concerns the free cohomology modules; transfer to another geometric space requires the source's additional comparison lemma.

## Antecedents and comparison

The construction is expressly adapted from [Eisenbud-Schreyer, Theorem 6.1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0712.1843v3). The source records integral flatness and arbitrary base change for its auxiliary-ring application. No novelty is claimed for supernatural cohomology bundles themselves.

## Checks and limitations

Read the complete construction. Exact tensor-product cohomology counts check vanishing at every chosen root, the unique cohomological degree and Euler polynomial for varied root lists and twists.

Finite rank calculations do not verify finite flatness of a morphism over every base. The bundle rank n! need not be minimal. The separate exceptional-divisor comparison is not audited here.
