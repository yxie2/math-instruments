# Symmetric and exterior squares turn rank loss into a product-free Choi range

[Family 272](../families/openai-math-272.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000082.md) · [Batch B002](../../batches/B002/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let $L:M_4(\mathbb C)\to M_4(\mathbb C)$ be completely positive and PPT, meaning that its output transpose is also completely positive. Suppose twenty distinct projective directions $[x]$ satisfy $\operatorname{rank}L(xx^*)<4$, every nonzero homogeneous quadratic vanishes on at most nine of them, and $L(yy^*)$ is positive definite for some $y$.

Let $U:\mathbb C^{10}\to\operatorname{Sym}^2\mathbb C^4$ and $V:\mathbb C^6\to\bigwedge^2\mathbb C^4$ be the standard isometric embeddings into $\mathbb C^4\otimes\mathbb C^4$. Write $\widehat x=U^*(x\otimes x)$ and let $K$ be the signed complementary-pair map on exterior two-vectors. Define

$$S(A)=V^*(L\otimes L)(UAU^*)V,\qquad R(A)=KS(A)^T K^*.$$

For the real coordinate inclusion $E:\mathbb C^6\to\mathbb C^{10}$, set $\Phi_1(A)=ES(A^T)E^*$ and $\Phi_2(B)=R^*(E^*BE)$, where $R^*$ denotes the Hilbert--Schmidt adjoint. These are PPT maps. Their composite has a nonzero positive Choi matrix $Z$ whose range contains no nonzero product vector.

The mechanism is explicit: rank loss puts $\widehat x\otimes\widehat x$ in $\ker Z$ because $\bigwedge^4\operatorname{ran}L(xx^*)=0$. A product vector in $\operatorname{ran}Z$ would force two nonzero quadratics to have zero sets covering all twenty directions, contrary to $9+9<20$. The positive definite output guarantees $Z\ne0$.

## Role in the source argument

Converts a rank-loss certificate for a small PPT map into nonseparability of a composition on a larger matrix algebra. The source then supplies a separate integer-pencil certificate for the twenty directions.

## Antecedents and comparison

The product-exclusion argument builds on [Bennett et al., Unextendible Product Bases and Bound Entanglement](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/9808030v2), [Pittenger](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0208028v1), and [Parthasarathy](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0405077v1). Exterior complementary-pair duality is also established. The candidate contribution is their assembly with a PPT rank-loss map, not the product-exclusion principle by itself. Targeted searches for the PPT/Veronese/twenty-direction combination did not identify an independent earlier instance.

## Checks and limitations

Checked the 10- and 6-dimensional tensor spaces, the Choi input-transpose cancellation, the conjugation required for exterior orthogonality over complex subspaces, and the zero-set counting step.

This is a conditional construction until the hypotheses on L are supplied. The arithmetic certificate was not rerun in this batch. Product-free range is asserted for the dimension-ten composite Choi matrix; it does not persist for every larger flagged completion.

## Possible applications

Potential use: search for other PPT maps with controlled projective rank-loss sets, or replace quadratic evaluation by another explicitly verified interpolation condition.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
