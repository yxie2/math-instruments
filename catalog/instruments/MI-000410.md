# Translate positivity of a three-form frame into taming of an almost-complex structure

[Family 341](../families/openai-math-341.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000224.md) · [Batch B038](../../batches/B038/README.md)

## Mathematical content

For a definite pair of real two-forms (A,C) on an oriented four-manifold, put x=AC/A^2, y=sqrt(C^2/A^2-x^2)>0 and T=(C-xA)/y. Then AT=0 and T^2=A^2, so Omega=A+iT satisfies Omega^2=0 and Omega conjugate(Omega)=2A^2>0. Its complex kernel defines an almost-complex structure J; conjugating Omega gives -J. The anti-invariant two-forms are exactly span(A,C), and Omega trivializes the canonical line.

A third real two-form B completes a positive wedge-product triple precisely when its invariant part B^(1,1) has positive square. In complex dimension two that means its two Hermitian eigenvalues have the same strict sign. Thus B tames one of J or -J, with a fixed choice on each connected component. Its anti-invariant part vanishes on every complex line.

A real two-plane is Lagrangian for both A and C exactly when it is a complex line without a chosen orientation: Omega(u,v)=0 is equivalent to complex dependence of two independent real vectors.

## Role in the source argument

Converts a positive-triple deformation question into the choice of a taming representative for a varying almost-complex structure.

## Antecedents and comparison

Classical four-dimensional two-form and Hermitian linear algebra. No first-priority claim.

## Checks and limitations

One hundred twenty exact wedge models check decomposability, the normalization identities and equivalence of positive triple with a definite invariant Hermitian part.

Pointwise identities do not supply closed representatives or a global deformation.

## Possible applications

Reformulating positivity constraints on triples of two-forms as familiar taming-cone conditions.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
