# Avoid all bounded-degree polynomial equations over one fixed coefficient field

[Family 001](../families/openai-math-001.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000239.md) · [Batch B042](../../batches/B042/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let K be the fixed discretely valued characteristic-zero coefficient field in the source, containing Q_p and with v(p)=1 and value group (1/e)Z. For r,b>=1, choose N>b and B=b sum_(i=0)^(r-1) N^i. Choose a prime q>max(B,e) and theta with theta^q=p.

The value 1/q has order q modulo (1/e)Z, so [K(theta):K]>=q; the defining polynomial gives equality. Set x_i=theta^(N^i). Substituting X_i=T^(N^i) maps distinct monomials of total degree at most b to distinct powers of T, by base-N uniqueness, all of degree at most B<q. No nonzero such polynomial over K can vanish at this point.

This avoids all bounded-degree polynomials over K simultaneously, regardless of their cardinality, using coordinates in Q_p(theta). It is not algebraic independence: higher-degree equations remain, and allowing coefficients in K(theta) would already permit X_i-x_i.

## Role in the source argument

Makes all relevant bounded-degree transversality minors nonzero over one finite extension.

## Checks and limitations

Twenty monomial models check injectivity for 451 monomials and that every encoded degree lies below the chosen extension degree.

The tests model coefficient-basis independence over Q; they do not construct the source p-adic comparison field or filtration.

## Possible applications

Simultaneous generic choices when infinitely many conditions have a common degree bound and coefficient field.
