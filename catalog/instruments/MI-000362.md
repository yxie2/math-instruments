# Order thermal forms using positive Fourier coefficients and a covariance square

[Family 075](../families/openai-math-075.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000200.md) · [Batch B032](../../batches/B032/README.md)

## Mathematical content

For A(y)=sum_s lambda_s(1-cos(2 pi s y)), lambda_s>=0, let q_A(u) be the Fourier coefficients of exp(-A), F(A)=-log q_A(0), and K_A=exp(F(A)-A). Expanding each positive and negative character exponential shows q_A is a nonnegative even probability mass function on the integers.

Under K_A, every covariance of cos(2 pi u y) and cos(2 pi v y) is nonnegative. The two-copy change (x,z)->(x+z,x-z) converts that covariance into a sum of squares of integrals of sin(2 pi ux)sin(2 pi vx) times products of cosines, with nonnegative series coefficients. Differentiation with respect to lambda_v then gives coefficientwise monotonicity of q_A(u)/q_A(0).

Consequently Delta_A(u)^2=2(1-q_A(u)/q_A(0)) decreases under adding a thermal form; Delta is subadditive by the L2 triangle inequality. F increases, while F(A+D)-F(A) decreases as A increases in form order. For P=sum P_j and P_lo<=P<=P_hi in this order, arbitrary shifts obey

$$\left|\int\chi_u(y)\prod_j e^{-P_j(z_j-y)}dy\right|\le q_P(u)\le e^{-F(P_{lo})}\int\chi_uK_{P_{hi}}.$$

The first bound follows by discarding phases in the convolution of nonnegative Fourier coefficients.

## Antecedents and comparison

The source explicitly uses the Griffiths-Ginibre positive-correlation method. [Benassi, Lees and Ueltschi](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1611.06019), Theorem 1.1 and Corollary 1.2, provide established correlation/monotonicity context; the source supplies the displayed harmonic calculation.

## Checks and limitations

Sixty periodic quadrature models check finite coefficient windows, all tested covariances, monotonicity, diminishing increments, triangle bounds and shifted envelopes.

Quadrature is not a proof for all forms or Fourier modes. None of the later Fourier-convergence machinery is certified.

## Possible applications

Positive Fourier kernels with monotone metrics and controllable shifted convolutions.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
