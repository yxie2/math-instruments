# A smooth profile with fixed L1 and L2 norms and arbitrarily small first moment

[Family 026](../families/openai-math-026.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000159.md) · [Batch B022](../../batches/B022/README.md)

## Mathematical content

For any lambda,beta>0 there is a nonnegative g in C_c^infinity((0,infinity)) such that integral g^2=1, integral g=sqrt(lambda), and integral u g(u)^2 du<beta.

Choose a smooth phi_R equal to 1/u on [1,R], supported inside [1/2,2R], and bounded between zero and 1/u. Put A=integral phi_R, B=integral u phi_R^2, and N^2=integral phi_R^2. Then A>=log R, B<=log(4R), and for R>=2 the quantity N^2 is bounded above by 2 and below by 1/2. Normalize g0=phi_R/N. With c=lambda/(integral g0)^2, set g(u)=c^(-1/2)g0(u/c). Dilation preserves the squared norm, fixes the L1 norm at sqrt(lambda), and gives

$$\int u g(u)^2\,du=\lambda B/A^2\le\lambda\log(4R)/(\log R)^2.$$

The last bound tends to zero. For each fixed choice the support and all moments are finite; they are not asserted to remain uniformly bounded as beta decreases. In the source application, first fix the profile, then the product dimension, and only afterward let the arithmetic scale tend to infinity.

## Role in the source argument

Allows the product weight to suppress the right-block first moment without shrinking the prescribed left-block mean.

## Checks and limitations

Read the full proof. Numerical quadrature checks 12 explicitly smoothed compact profiles across four support scales and three values of lambda, including the dilation identities and first-moment bound.

Quadrature is not proof of smoothness or an asymptotic sieve estimate. The symbolic smooth cutoff and dilation argument gives the stated existence result.

## Possible applications

Separating a weighted location moment from fixed mass and energy constraints in product trial functions.
