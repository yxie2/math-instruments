# Correct an equivariant height to constant positive complex Laplacian

[Family 060](../families/openai-math-060.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000252.md) · [Batch B046](../../batches/B046/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let X be a compact connected complex surface with no holomorphic one-forms, and let Y -> X be a cyclic cover with smooth real h_0 satisfying h_0(Ty)=h_0(y)+1. Choose a Gauduchon metric with fundamental form omega, and let L=Lambda_omega dd^c with subharmonic sign.

The kernel of L on X is the constants; elliptic index zero and integral_X L v=0 identify its cokernel with constants. Since L h_0 is periodic, let c be its mean and solve L v=c-L h_0 on X. Then h=h_0+v composed pi has Lh=c and the same deck increment.

If c=0, the descended exact real (1,1)-form eta=dd^c h is primitive, hence anti-self-dual. Stokes gives 0=integral eta wedge eta=-||eta||^2, so eta=0. Then partial h is a nonzero descended holomorphic one-form, contradicting the hypothesis. Reverse T and h if needed to get c>0. All positive-order derivatives are periodic and bounded. A compact set whose deck translates cover Y shows that preimages of bounded closed height intervals are compact.
