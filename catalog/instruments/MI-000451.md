# Control every positive power by spectral tails without a reducing range

[Family 282](../families/openai-math-282.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000244.md) · [Batch B043](../../batches/B043/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let A,B be positive selfadjoint operators on Hilbert spaces H,K, and let J:K -> H be a contraction. Assume E_A((t,infinity)) J E_B([0,t])=0 for every t>=0. Then for every s>0 and psi in Dom B^s, J psi belongs to Dom A^s and ||A^s J psi||<=||B^s psi||.

Indeed E_A((t,infinity))J psi=E_A((t,infinity))J E_B((t,infinity))psi, so its norm is at most the B-tail norm. Integrate the squared inequality against 2s t^(2s-1) dt. No invariance or reduction of ran J is used. In the source, spectral-strip comparison after disintegration provides this stronger input for the scalar-copy embedding.

Ordinary A<=B is insufficient for all powers: A=diag(1,0), B=A+[[1,1],[1,1]] are positive with B-A>=0, while B^2-A^2 has a negative eigenvalue.

## Role in the source argument

Transfers fractional-power domains to a distinguished embedded copy without assuming it reduces the ambient operator.

## Checks and limitations

One hundred embeddings with nonreducing ranges satisfy all tested band conditions and ten power inequalities each; an ordinary-order counterexample is included.

Finite tests do not establish unbounded domain statements or the source spectral-strip hypotheses.
