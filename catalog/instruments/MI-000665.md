# Recover a differential line from ratios of sections

[Family 057](../families/openai-math-057.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000434.md) · [Batch B070](../../batches/B070/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let i:L->Omega_V^p be nonzero on a connected smooth projective complex manifold. For sections s0,...,sr of one positive power L^m with s0 nonzero, every ratio satisfies d(sa/s0) wedge i(L)=0 generically. Consequently kappa(L)<=p. If equality holds, p algebraically independent ratios from one section map have nonzero wedge and that wedge spans i(L) generically.

To prove the differential relation, adjoin simultaneous mth roots of the sections in total spaces of L. Choose a reduced component dominating V and resolve it projectively. The root sections map to holomorphic p-forms alpha_a upstairs. These forms are closed. Since alpha_a=(t_a/t0)alpha_0, differentiating gives d(t_a/t0) wedge alpha_0=0; taking the mth power of the ratio and using generic injectivity of characteristic-zero differential pullback gives the original ratio relation.

If r independent covectors wedge to zero with a nonzero p-form, each nonzero basis monomial of the form contains all r covectors. Thus r<=p; for r=p the form is a multiple of their wedge. Generic differential rank equals the section-map image dimension in characteristic zero.

## Role in the source argument

Identifies a maximal-dimensional differential line with the section map it defines, for later descent arguments.

## Antecedents and comparison

This is the classical closed-form/root-cover mechanism behind differential-line Kodaira-dimension bounds. The source gives an explicit simultaneous-root proof. No new general Bogomolov bound is asserted.

## Checks and limitations

Exact exterior-algebra kernels check the dimension restriction and equality direction; symbolic section-root ratios check the differentiated closed-form relation.

The tests do not prove compact Kahler closedness, resolution existence or the source conditional abelianity theorem.

## Possible applications

Reading a geometric foliation direction directly from a line bundle section map.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
