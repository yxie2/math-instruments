# Glue consistent bag laws into a global cut metric

[Family 089](../families/openai-math-089.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000262.md) · [Batch B048](../../batches/B048/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let bags B_t cover a finite set V along a tree, and require the bags containing each vertex to form a connected subtree. Give each binary assignment space {0,1}^{B_t} a nonnegative measure mu_t of the same mass m>0. Suppose adjacent bags have identical marginals on their intersection.

Choose a root, sample its assignment from mu_root/m, and then sample every child conditionally on the parent-child overlap. Previously assigned vertices in that child are exactly the overlap by the running-intersection property. Induction gives each bag marginal mu_t/m. Multiply the global probability law by m to obtain rho.

Where all separator factors are positive, rho(omega)=product_t mu_t(omega|B_t) / product_(ts) mu_(B_t intersect B_s)(omega|overlap). A full assignment with a zero separator mass has zero mass; one does not divide by zero. The product formula shows root independence. Complement symmetry of all bag laws gives complement symmetry of rho, and separator labels along any bag path form a Markov chain.

The functions v -> [omega -> omega(v)] embed the cut pseudometric D(u,v)=rho{omega(u)!=omega(v)} into finite-dimensional real L1. If each graph edge is in a bag and its bag separation mass is at most U times its length, the triangle inequality extends this to D(u,v)<=U d(u,v). No lower distortion bound follows merely from gluing.

## Role in the source argument

Assembles local cut measures in the bounded-treewidth source without changing their bag marginals.

## Antecedents and comparison

[Chlamtac-Krauthgamer-Raghavendra](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1006.3970v2), Section 3.2, Lemmas 3.3-3.4, give the conditional sampling and root/traversal invariance. Their statements and proofs were read directly. The source records the finite-mass product formula and cut-metric interpretation.

## Checks and limitations

Exact rational enumeration of small bag trees compares conditional sampling from every root with the product law, verifies all marginals, and includes zero-mass separators. Pairwise-consistent anti-correlated laws on three cyclic bags supply an obstruction when the tree hypothesis is dropped.

The check does not prove the source transport-of-uncertainty or its lower-distortion bound. Global assignments may have many different laws with the same bag marginals; canonical here refers to this Markov gluing.

## Possible applications

Consistent local probability models, cut metrics and dynamic programs on tree decompositions.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
