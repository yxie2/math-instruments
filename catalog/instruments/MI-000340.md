# Rearrange convex functions increasingly without losing convexity on a downward polytope

[Family 343](../families/openai-math-343.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000189.md) · [Batch B029](../../batches/B029/README.md)

## Mathematical content

For a continuous convex function f on [0,L], its increasing rearrangement at h in [0,L] is

$$g(h)=\min_{0\le a\le L-h}\max\{f(a),f(a+h)\}.$$

Convex sublevel sets are intervals, so the formula is the quantile by sublevel length, including flat sublevels and both endpoints.

Now let Delta={p>=0:b_nu.p<=c_nu} be compact and full dimensional, with every b_nu coordinate nonnegative. Rearrange a continuous convex F along the j-th coordinate interval [0,L(y)], where L(y)=min_nu(c_nu-sum_{i!=j}b_nu,i y_i)/b_nu,j over positive denominators.

The resulting R_jF is continuous and convex: convex combinations of two minimizing intervals are admissible intervals on the intermediate slice, and convexity bounds their endpoint values. Slice distributions and therefore the full integral are preserved. The minimum formula preserves order and constants; these imply ||R_jF-R_jG||_infinity<=||F-G||_infinity.

It is nondecreasing in coordinate j and preserves monotonicity in any other coordinate. Lowering another coordinate enlarges the allowed interval set because the face normals are nonnegative. Consequently R_m...R_1F is coordinatewise nondecreasing. No rationality or simplicity of the polytope is required.

## Antecedents and comparison

Increasing rearrangement is classical. The source packages convexity, contraction and preservation of earlier coordinate monotonicity on a downward polytope. The [Siegel-Yao primary preprint](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2312.13224) supplies the broader packing conjecture context, not a proof of the present global source claim.

## Checks and limitations

Forty quadratic slice models preserve their integrals; 400 simplex models verify convexity and coordinate monotonicity. Maximum integral error is below 1.2e-16.

These models do not certify the general rearrangement proof or the separate Hamiltonian realization of a rearrangement.

## Possible applications

Converting convex data into monotone data while retaining its integral and controlling uniform approximation error.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
