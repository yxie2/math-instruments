# A forbidden label pattern bounds the clauses one bit away from acceptance

[Family 132](../families/openai-math-132.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000117.md) · [Batch B011](../../batches/B011/README.md)

## Mathematical content

For M>=3 set k=2M^2+1, r=ceil(sqrt(M)) and t=16. A regular tournament on k rows has M^2 outgoing edges per row. Its edges admit labels a(i,j) in [r] with no t-set I and positions m_j such that a(i,j)=m_j on every internal edge i->j. Independent uniform labels give a union bound k^t r^(t-binom(t,2))<=3^16 M^(-20)<1.

Let children carry nested predicates P_1>=...>=P_Q. For parent index q in {1,...,Q-1}, clause i requires P_Q=1 on all r children of row i and P_{Q-q}=0 on child (j,a(i,j)) for each outgoing edge i->j. The parent is the OR of its clauses. Every condition of a fixed clause uses a distinct child.

At a rejecting input, any raw-bit flip that causes acceptance repairs a clause with exactly one failed condition. Fix these candidate lists before choosing the flip. Let T contain clauses with one failed target and no failed gates; let G contain clauses with one failed gate and all targets true. If i,j belong to T and i->j, the passing gate from i forces its selected child in j to be j's unique failed target. Thus T would realize the forbidden label pattern if |T|>=t, so |T|<t.

For i,j in G, an edge i->j is a failed gate of i. Each induced outdegree is at most one, so |G|(|G|-1)/2<=|G| and |G|<=3. At most one such vertex has induced outdegree zero. These candidate counts are uniform over all inputs, not just the block-sensitivity witnesses.

## Role in the source argument

Limits how many distinct recursive clauses can be repaired by single-bit changes at a rejecting input.

## Antecedents and comparison

The architecture adapts [Meiburg, Block Sensitivity can exceed Spectral Sensitivity Squared](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.00851v1), particularly the tournament-gated construction. The source adds this forbidden destination-label condition for ordinary sensitivity. The union bound itself is classical.

## Checks and limitations

Verified the probability exponents and candidate-list argument. Finite labeled tournaments with an exhaustively computed forbidden-set threshold test the target and gate counts across nested child states, including label collisions.

Random-label existence does not give an efficient construction at the source parameter sizes. The candidate-count argument needs nested predicates and distinct children within each clause. A spectral-sensitivity separation alone would not imply the ordinary-sensitivity claim.

## Possible applications

Bounding local repair possibilities in recursively gated Boolean constructions and distinguishing single-bit effects from block witnesses.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
