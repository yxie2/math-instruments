# Force prime torsion using linked finite idempotent diagrams

[Family 250](../families/openai-math-250.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000358.md) · [Batch B061](../../batches/B061/README.md)

## Mathematical content

For ell=2 or 3, let prefix and deletion operators on infinite binary sequences act on the free F_ell-vector space on those sequences. Write E_v for projection onto sequences starting with v. The three prefix-free lists (00,01,1), (000,01,1), (001,01,1) give three copies of M_3(F_ell), with shared projections A=E_00, A0=E_000, A1=E_001, B=E_01, C=E_1. Their identities need not coincide, but A=A0+A1 and I=A+B+C.

Let P be the finite union of their actual idempotents, identifying operators that occur in more than one copy. Suppose z_E in an arbitrary abelian group satisfies z_(E+P)=z_E+z_P whenever the three operators occur in the diagram and EP=PE=0, including cross-copy sums. Then ell z_I=0.

Within M_3(F_ell), write x(u,lambda)=z_(u lambda), where lambda u=1. For v in ker(lambda), the increment x(u+v,lambda)-x(u,lambda) is independent of u. If u'-u and v are independent, choose mu with mu u=mu u'=0 and mu v=1. The identity u lambda+v mu=(u+v)lambda+v(mu-lambda) expresses the same rank-two idempotent as two orthogonal sums and gives the same increment for u and u'. For dependent differences, compare through a third affine vector using the two-dimensional kernel; this works also over F2. Summing ell equal increments around u,u+v,... gives ell times each increment zero. Transposition supplies the analogous covector comparison, without assuming z_E=z_(E transpose).

Consequently all diagonal units in each copy have equal ell-multiples. Shared projections make ell z_A, ell z_A0, ell z_A1, ell z_B and ell z_C equal to q. The cross-copy relation A=A0+A1 forces q=2q, hence q=0 and ell z_I=0. If one common value satisfies both the ell=2 and ell=3 conclusions, it vanishes. The cross-copy split is indispensable; ordinary rank on a single matrix algebra would not force this conclusion in a torsion-free target.

## Role in the source argument

Creates a finite additive obstruction later used in controlled homological fillings.

## Antecedents and comparison

The source explicitly shares this diagram with its simple-overgroup companion. Rank-one idempotent identities and coprime torsion cancellation are elementary. Targeted searching did not establish external priority for this exact three-copy diagram, so it is recorded as a source mechanism without a novelty certification.

## Checks and limitations

Exact arithmetic over F2 and F3 verifies all selected affine-vector comparisons, both orthogonal decompositions, signed relation identities and complete prime-length telescoping cycles. Prefix-cylinder membership verifies the cross-copy split.

The finite matrix checks inspect the proof identities; they do not verify the later realization by group homomorphisms or uniform chain fillings. The operator model is infinite-dimensional, not a finite-dimensional representation of binary prefix relations.

## Possible applications

Designing finite diagrams whose additive invariants must be annihilated by specified primes.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
