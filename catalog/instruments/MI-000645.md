# Compute rank-one p-curvature by cyclic orbits of set partitions

[Family 035](../families/openai-math-035.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000414.md) · [Batch B067](../../batches/B067/README.md)

## Mathematical content

In a commutative characteristic-p ring with derivation partial satisfying partial^p=0, multiplication by a obeys (partial+a)^p=a^p+partial^(p-1)(a), where the right side is a multiplication operator. The left side commutes with every multiplication operator, since its commutator is the p-fold iterated commutator partial^p, so it is determined by its value at 1. Iterated Leibniz expansion indexes that value by set partitions of p labels: a block of size j contributes partial^(j-1)(a). Cyclic permutation has p-element orbits except for the discrete and single-block partitions. All other contributions cancel modulo p, leaving exactly the two terms.

For a retained p-basis x_i with commuting coordinate partials, a closed form alpha=sum a_i dx_i with Cartier(alpha)=alpha has partial_i^(p-1)(a_i)=-a_i^p. This follows from alpha=dc+sum b_i^p x_i^(p-1)dx_i, Cartier(alpha)=sum b_i dx_i, and (p-1)!=-1. Thus D_i=partial_i+a_i satisfy D_i^p=0, [D_i,D_j]=0 and [D_i,x_j]=delta_ij. The local algebra statement does not require finite type once the p-basis and Cartier formula have been supplied.

## Role in the source argument

Produces the nilpotent commuting operators used for horizontal expansion in Laurent formal neighborhoods.

## Antecedents and comparison

[Katz, Nilpotent connections and the monodromy theorem](https://web.math.princeton.edu/~nmk/old/nilpconn.pdf), section 5, defines p-curvature, states and proves Cartier descent in Theorem 5.1, and uses Jacobson identities in Proposition 5.2. Printed pages 190-192 were read. The selected source gives a direct rank-one set-partition proof; no new Cartier theorem is claimed.

## Checks and limitations

Exact operator matrices over finite truncated p-basis algebras check the p-th power identity for arbitrary a, its Cartier-fixed consequence for logarithmic units, commutators and the two exceptional cyclic partition orbits.

Truncated algebras test the universal identities, not the existence of the source retained Laurent p-basis or its global formal line bundle.

## Possible applications

Verifying zero p-curvature locally without expanding all noncommutative words individually.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
