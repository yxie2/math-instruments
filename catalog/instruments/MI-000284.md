# Pay a shared conditioning entropy once before averaging sampled coordinates

[Family 028](../families/openai-math-028.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000161.md) · [Batch B022](../../batches/B022/README.md)

## Mathematical content

There are k pairs of finite coordinates, with pair j taking values in groups of size p_j. Let S choose s distinct pairs uniformly and choose one sign in each pair by independent fair bits. Require S to be independent of the joint data (Y,H). Put Lbar=(1/k)sum_j log p_j, and let F_S(Y) be the sampled coordinate tuple. Then

$$\frac1{2k}\sum_{j,\pm}\bigl(\log p_j-H(\phi_j^\pm(Y)\mid H)\bigr)
\le \overline L-\frac{\mathbb E_S H(F_S(Y))}{s}+\frac{H(H)}s.$$

For each fixed S, joint conditional entropy is at least H(F_S(Y))-H(H) and at most the sum of its coordinate conditional entropies. Average this inequality. Each coordinate appears with probability s/(2k), giving the displayed bound after division by s. Independence ensures the distributions being averaged are the same fixed distributions of Y and H, not distributions biased by how S was selected.

Thus if the average sampled entropy is at least (1-epsilon)s Lbar and H(H)<=kappa s Lbar, the mean coordinate deficit is at most (epsilon+kappa)Lbar. No independence among Y's coordinates or between Y and H is required. Conditioning each coordinate separately and charging H(H) each time would lose the useful factor 1/s.

## Role in the source argument

Keeps the continuation-data entropy budget small enough for the later coverage estimates.

## Antecedents and comparison

This is a direct combination of the Shannon chain rule and conditional subadditivity, with the uniform inclusion probability made explicit. The source packages it for shared continuation data; no novelty is claimed for the underlying entropy inequalities.

## Checks and limitations

Read the complete proof. Thirty correlated finite joint distributions are checked for each of three sample sizes, with exact enumeration of the uniformly sampled index/sign sets and numerical entropy evaluation.

The tests do not establish the source lower bound on sampled entropy, the size of its conditioning data, or the coverage-transfer theorem. Adaptive sampling requires a new analysis.

## Possible applications

Avoiding repeated information costs when many coordinate conclusions use one shared description.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
