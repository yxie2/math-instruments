# Transitive blowups amplify integer subset deficits with bounded block error

[Family 173](../families/openai-math-173.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000119.md) · [Batch B012](../../batches/B012/README.md)

## Mathematical content

For a finite oriented graph D on n vertices let F(S) be the raw union of out-neighbors of S, and F squared its iteration. Replace every vertex by a transitive tournament of order m, with all external arcs prescribed by D. For a subset U of the blowup, let S be its nonempty-block support. Internal image f in a transitive block satisfies f squared(T) subset f(T), |T|-|f(T)|<=1 and |T|-2|f(T)|+|f squared(T)|<=1.

The resulting graph G satisfies the unconditional accounting bound

$$|U|+|F_G^2(U)|-2|F_G(U)|\le m\bigl(|F_D^2(S)\setminus F_D(S)|-|F_D(S)\setminus S|\bigr)+n.$$

Indeed blocks in F_D(S) are entirely filled by the first image. For the second image, blocks in F_D(S) union F_D squared(S) may be upper-bounded by full blocks, while the remaining supported blocks contribute their internal second image. Their net residual is at most one per block.

Consequently, if D has positive indegree at every vertex and every proper nonempty S has |F_D squared(S) minus F_D(S)|<|F_D(S) minus S|, then taking m>n gives |U|+|F_G squared(U)|<2|F_G(U)| for every proper nonempty U. Proper support gives an integer deficit at most minus one. Full support gives F_G(U)=F_G squared(U)=V(G), so strictness follows directly from |U|<nm. These are conditional hypotheses; this card does not supply a graph satisfying them.

## Role in the source argument

Turns a hypothetical minimal counterexample into a graph with a uniform strict raw-image inequality.

## Antecedents and comparison

The preceding subset-deficit reduction is related to [Seacrest, Lemma 4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1808.06293v3), explicitly cited by the source. The selected mechanism is the transitive-block accounting and its bounded additive error, not that earlier reduction.

## Checks and limitations

Read the internal-block identities and support split. Exhaustive small blowups check the unconditional bound for arbitrary oriented base graphs, without assuming the hypothetical strict-deficit graph exists.

Raw images can contain original vertices and first neighbors. Replacing them by strict second neighborhoods invalidates the displayed accounting. The strict conclusion also requires positive indegrees in the full-support case.

## Possible applications

Amplifying discrete set-expansion defects while keeping all internal-block errors uniformly bounded.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
