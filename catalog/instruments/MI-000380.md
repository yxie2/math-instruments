# Prove algebraic tensor independence using a central support projection

[Family 299](../families/openai-math-299.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000209.md) · [Batch B034](../../batches/B034/README.md)

## Mathematical content

If M is a von Neumann factor on H with unit I and B is a unital subalgebra of M', multiplication M algebraic-tensor B -> B(H) is injective. For independent b_1,...,b_l in B, let S be the weakly closed space of rows (a_i) with sum a_i b_i=0. Matrices whose rows lie in S form a weakly closed left ideal J in M_l(M), so J=M_l(M)p for its right support projection p.

Right multiplication by diagonal unitaries from M preserves J. Hence p commutes with all such unitaries, and its entries are central in M, therefore scalar. Its rows are scalar relations among the independent b_i; consequently p=0 and J=0.

Induction gives injectivity for any finite family of commuting factors. In an irreducible representation of a maximal tensor product of unital C*-algebras, the weak closures of the represented legs, or of any grouping into blocks, are factors: a central element of one block commutes with the whole image and is scalar. Neither faithfulness on each original algebra nor spatial decomposition is required.

## Role in the source argument

Prevents representation-dependent cancellation when independently constructed tensor labels are multiplied.

## Antecedents and comparison

This is the classical relation-module/support-projection proof of algebraic independence for commuting factors. It does not identify a completed tensor-product norm.

## Checks and limitations

Nine finite spatial-factor basis calculations and a nonfactor negative control where multiplication kills a nonzero algebraic tensor.

Finite spatial examples do not cover nonspatial representations; the support-projection argument supplies that generality.

## Possible applications

Testing nonvanishing in irreducible representations without assuming a tensor decomposition of the Hilbert space.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
