# Linearize weighted formal coordinates without diagonalizing Jordan blocks

[Family 014](../families/openai-math-014.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000403.md) · [Batch B066](../../batches/B066/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Consider a complete free graded-commutative algebra in characteristic zero generated by V* in degree 0 and R* in degree -1. Let a continuous graded automorphism have linear eigenvalues of absolute weights -1 on V* and -2 on R*, relative to q>1. At generator order n>=2, errors lie in Hom(V*,Sym^n V*) or Hom(R*,R* tensor Sym^(n-1)V*). The conjugacy operator, target action times inverse source action, has all eigenvalues of modulus q^(-(n-1)/2), strictly below one. Its difference from the identity is invertible even on generalized eigenspaces.

Solve at each order and take the generator-adic limit to obtain a coordinate change tangent to the identity conjugating the automorphism to its linear part. If a minimal differential commutes with it, the image of R* has no constant or linear term and an order-n term would compare weight -2 with -n. Only n=2 survives. When a reductive group is normalized by the automorphism, the correction operator and its inverse preserve the equivariant jet subspaces, giving equivariant corrections. This is a formal construction; no analytic radius of convergence is asserted.

## Role in the source argument

Removes higher relations in a local deformation model while retaining equivariant data.

## Antecedents and comparison

[J. P. Pridham, The deformation theory of representations of the fundamental group of a smooth variety](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0401344), Theorem 2.11 and Corollary 2.12, uses Frobenius-compatible finite jets and weights to obtain quadratic hulls. Pages 14-16 and the complete theorem proof were read. The extracted calculation is an explicit graded nonresonance interface, not a new global Langlands theorem.

## Checks and limitations

Exact homological operators with nontrivial Jordan blocks check invertibility at orders 2-5; a finite formal conjugacy is solved coefficient by coefficient. The quadratic resonance is retained as a control.

Finite jets do not certify a derived geometric presentation or any global categorical equivalence.

## Possible applications

Explaining why weight constraints force quadratic relations without a semisimplicity assumption.
