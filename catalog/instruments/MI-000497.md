# Repair squared-distance triangles by integrating local Gaussian kernels

[Family 117](../families/openai-math-117.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000276.md) · [Batch B050](../../batches/B050/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Partition a finite set into charts. Within each chart let delta be a nonnegative multiple of an L1 distance, and put delta=infinity between charts. Suppose Hilbert vectors P_v satisfy <P_v-P_z,P_y-P_z>>=-min{r,delta(v,z),delta(y,z)} for r>0.

Define a Gram matrix <q_v,q_w>=4 integral_0^r exp(-delta(v,w)/l) dl, interpreting exp(-infinity)=0. An L1 distance is a squared Hilbert distance, and Gaussian kernels on Hilbert points are positive semidefinite. Block sums across charts and integration preserve this property, so q_v exist.

For a=delta(v,z), b=delta(y,z), the triangle inequality implies 1-exp(-a/l)-exp(-b/l)+exp(-delta(v,y)/l)>=(1-exp(-a/l))(1-exp(-b/l)). On 0<l<min{r,a,b}, the right side is at least (1-e^(-1))^2. Thus <q_v-q_z,q_y-q_z>>=4(1-e^(-1))^2 min{r,a,b}>=min{r,a,b}. Appending q_v eliminates every negative angle. Consequently d(v,w)=||P_v-P_w||^2+||q_v-q_w||^2 satisfies all triangle inequalities and is of negative type.

The correction distance equals 8 integral_0^r [1-exp(-delta(v,w)/l)] dl. It is at most 8r globally and at most 8t[1+log(r/t)] when t=delta(v,w) lies in (0,r]. At t=0 it vanishes. This follows by bounding the integrand by min{1,t/l}. The local defect hypothesis is what permits this vanishing short-distance correction.

## Role in the source argument

Repairs the companion gap construction without overwhelming its small within-chart distances.

## Antecedents and comparison

[Schoenberg, Metric Spaces and Positive Definite Functions](https://pages.stat.wisc.edu/~wahba/stat860/pdf1/schoenberg.1938.pdf), printed pages 524-525 and equation (4), proves Gaussian positive definiteness; those scanned pages were visually inspected. The scale integral and defect compensation are the selected source argument. No exhaustive novelty claim for this combination.

## Checks and limitations

Finite chart models numerically check the Gram spectrum, angle compensation, triangle inequalities and the logarithmic local bound. An initial squared-distance example violates a triangle before correction.

Numerical quadrature is not a general positivity certificate. The source proof supplies the analytic argument; the rest of the integrality-gap construction is not certified.

## Possible applications

Repairing nearly metric squared-Hilbert distances when defects decay with a known chart metric.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
