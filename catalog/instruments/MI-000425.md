# Amplify bilinear anti-concentration without a dimension loss

[Family 105](../families/openai-math-105.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000231.md) · [Batch B040](../../batches/B040/README.md)

## Mathematical content

A law on a binary vector space is quarter-balanced if every nonzero linear functional assigns probability at least 1/4 to each bit. If g,h are independent quarter-balanced draws and B is a nonzero bilinear form, then Pr(B(g,h)=b)>=1/16 for both bits. Indeed g lies outside the proper kernel of g -> B(g,.) with probability at least 1/4; conditional on such g, quarter balance of h supplies the second factor. Thus |E(-1)^B(g,h)|<=7/8.

For R independent products, the parity bias is at most (7/8)^R. Taking R so this is at most 1/2 restores quarter balance for recursive use. More generally, in a hidden sum X=sum_(v in W minus 0) v tensor h_v with independent fresh buckets, every nonzero tensor character sees a nonzero functional in at least one bucket. Its bias is bounded by that bucket's bias, since all other factors have magnitude at most one.

The dimension does not enter. Revealed information must leave the hidden bucket tapes independent; success or slice conditioning is not automatically permissible.

## Role in the source argument

Separates the scalar bias budget from the dimension and branching used elsewhere in the sampler.
