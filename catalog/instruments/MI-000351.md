# Push approximate invariance to a subgroup and extract a finite Folner set

[Family 253](../families/openai-math-253.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000194.md) · [Batch B030](../../batches/B030/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let H be a subgroup of a countable group J. Choose right-coset representatives t for Ht and write x=ht uniquely. Then pi(x)=h satisfies pi(kx)=k pi(x) for k in H. Pushing a probability nu forward to p=pi_*nu contracts every translation error: ||kp-p||_1<=||k nu-nu||_1.

Choose finite E with p(E) close to one and normalize q=p 1_E/p(E). The exact truncation error is ||q-p||_1=2(1-p(E)), so the sum of errors for a finite set K grows by at most 4|K|(1-p(E)). Starting from sufficiently small errors makes sum_k||kq-q||_1<epsilon.

For the finite superlevel sets A_t={q>t}, layer cake gives int |A_t| dt=1 and int sum_k |kA_t symmetric-difference A_t| dt=sum_k||kq-q||_1. Consequently some nonempty finite A_t has summed boundary less than epsilon |A_t|. For empty K any singleton works. The source obtains the input probability from uniform shape-preserving matchings under a separately proved partition coupling.

## Role in the source argument

Converts the source approximate random matching invariance into finite Folner sets, including inheritance by subgroups.

## Antecedents and comparison

This is the classical Reiter-to-Folner layer-cake argument, with an explicit right-coset projection and truncation budget. It is not a new amenability criterion.

## Checks and limitations

Sixty rational probability models use a nonnormal subgroup of S3 and verify equivariance and contraction. Thirty exact finite distributions on the integers verify both layer-cake identities.

The tests do not produce the source almost-invariant partition laws, nor verify finite presentation or simplicity.

## Possible applications

Constructive extraction of finite approximate invariants from countably supported measures.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
