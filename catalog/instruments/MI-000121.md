# A thermal block decomposition preserves the scalar entropy term

[Family 276](../families/openai-math-276.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000084.md) · [Batch B002](../../batches/B002/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let $X,Y$ have the same finite rectangular shape and satisfy $\operatorname{Tr}XX^*=\operatorname{Tr}YY^*=1$. Let $\alpha,\beta,\eta,\delta\ge0$ sum to one and satisfy $\alpha\delta-\beta\eta=K^2$ with $K\ge0$. Put

$$T=\begin{pmatrix}\alpha XX^*+\beta YY^*&KXY^*\\KYX^*&\eta XX^*+\delta YY^*\end{pmatrix},\quad U_0=\alpha+\beta,\quad U_1=\eta+\delta,\quad u=U_0U_1-K^2.$$

Writing $h$ for binary entropy in nats and $g(u)=h((1+\sqrt{1-4u})/2)$, the source proves

$$S(T)\ge(\alpha+\eta)S(XX^*)+(\beta+\delta)S(YY^*)+g(u),\qquad 0\le u\le\tfrac14.$$

For $u>0$, the coefficient relation is the line $U_1\beta+U_0\eta=u$. Its endpoints are

$$ (\alpha_1,\beta_1,\eta_1,\delta_1)=(K^2/U_1,u/U_1,0,U_1),$$
$$ (\alpha_2,\beta_2,\eta_2,\delta_2)=(U_0,0,u/U_0,K^2/U_0),$$

with weights $w_1=\beta U_1/u$ and $w_2=\eta U_0/u$. Both endpoint matrices factor through a two-by-two block matrix with one zero block, and both have the same scalar term $g(u)$. Entropy concavity therefore preserves that term in the mixture. The source separately handles $u=0$. Equality holds when $X=Y$.

## Role in the source argument

Extends the triangular block inequality to simultaneous thermal excitation and damping, without requiring a pure channel output.

## Antecedents and comparison

[Tang, Zhu, Bai and Wang](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28592v1) is the source explicitly cited for the zero-block theorem and ordinary-damping additivity. [Fang](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.14621v1) studies a converse for the generalized channel. The scalar one-use optimization has older antecedents. The candidate device here is the common-scalar endpoint decomposition; neither triangular entropy nor the binary optimizer is attributed to this extraction. A targeted phrase search found no independent prior instance of that decomposition.

## Checks and limitations

Checked the line equation, both weights, all coefficient recoveries and equality for X=Y. The bundled checks use exact rational coefficient samples and finite-dimensional noncommuting complex Gram matrices.

The determinant relation is a hypothesis; arbitrary four-coefficient block states are not covered. The equality case and finite numerical checks do not prove the general entropy inequality. Its analytic dependency is the triangular block theorem.

## Possible applications

Potential use: extend a zero-pattern matrix inequality along a coefficient segment when every endpoint has the same scalar contribution.
