# Detect splitting of a sign-action lattice extension modulo two

[Family 018](../families/openai-math-018.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000393.md) · [Batch B065](../../batches/B065/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let Gamma be finite, M a torsion-free lattice with Gamma action, and c a central involution acting as -1. An extension 1->M->E->Gamma->1 determines a class beta in H¹(Gamma/<c>, M/2M), and the extension splits if and only if beta=0.

A lift z of c satisfies z² in M and z² fixed by c, hence z²=1. Choose lifts s_g with s_1=1 and s_c=z. Write s_g s_h=f(g,h)s_(gh) and z s_g z^(-1)=d_g s_g in additive lattice notation. Comparing products gives d_(gh)=d_g+g d_h+2 f(g,h). Modulo two this is a cocycle, with d_c=0, descending to the quotient. Changing s_g by a_g changes d_g by -2a_g; changing z by a changes d_g by (1-g)a. Thus the cohomology class is well defined.

If beta=0, change z to make every d_g even and then change s_g by d_g/2. All lifts commute with z. The centralizer of z maps onto Gamma and has kernel M^c=0, hence gives a complement. Conversely a complement supplies compatible lifts with zero class. If an involution r has an involutory lift, its d_r also obeys (1+r)d_r=0. This is a criterion for an existing extension, not a statement that every cohomology class is realizable.

## Role in the source argument

Replaces a lattice-extension splitting problem by a finite-coefficient obstruction.

## Antecedents and comparison

The argument is elementary extension and cocycle algebra. The source identifies a corresponding sign computation in its number-field companion. No new classification of global normal subgroups is inferred.

## Checks and limitations

Explicit extensions of C2 times C2 by Z, with presentation t²=u^a and ztz^(-1)=t^(-1), test the cocycle, lift changes and parity obstruction for several integers a.

Finite parameter tests illustrate the algebra; they do not establish the source global cohomological inputs.

## Possible applications

Testing when a finite quotient admits a compatible complement in a lattice extension.
