# Exclude a critical Jacobian equality by a growing harmonic barrier

[Family 072](../families/openai-math-072.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000386.md) · [Batch B064](../../batches/B064/README.md)

## Mathematical content

For a holomorphic locally univalent F on the upper half-plane, put q=-iy F''/F' and G=F-iyF'/k. Its normalized real Jacobian satisfies k²J=k(k-1)+(2k-1)Re(q)+|q|². At k=3/4, J identically zero would force |q+1/4|²=1/4, hence |F''/F'|>=1/(4y). This pre-Schwarzian has no zeros, so u=log|F''/F'| is harmonic and u>=-log4-log y.

No finite harmonic u on the upper half-plane can obey that lower bound. On [-1,1] times [epsilon,1], compare it with

b_epsilon(x,y)=-log4+log(1/epsilon) cos(pi x/2) sinh(pi(1-y)/2)/sinh(pi/2).

This function is harmonic. On the sides and top its added term is zero, and on the bottom that term is at most log(1/epsilon), so b_epsilon<=u on the boundary. The minimum principle gives the same inequality inside. At (0,1/2) the positive coefficient of log(1/epsilon) is fixed, forcing u to infinity as epsilon decreases, a contradiction. This rules out exact global equality; passage from an equality law to a uniform exponent gap needs the source's separate compactness and transport arguments.

## Role in the source argument

Provides a deterministic obstruction at the proposed equality threshold.

## Antecedents and comparison

The proof uses the classical harmonic minimum principle and an explicit separated-variable rectangle barrier. The source specializes it to a pre-Schwarzian Jacobian equality. No independent novelty or integral-means gap certification is asserted.

## Checks and limitations

Symbolic Laplacian and Jacobian calculations, boundary comparisons and increasing center values check the barrier and the equality algebra.

The checks do not establish the weighted probability law, measurable pairing or strict integral-means theorem.

## Possible applications

Ruling out an equality case that would impose uniform logarithmic blowup along a boundary interval.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
