# A state bijection fixes directed-cycle boundary weight at three

[Family 180](../families/openai-math-180.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000120.md) · [Batch B012](../../batches/B012/README.md)

## Mathematical content

In the colored sphere triangulation with an outer black triangle and a state r as above, choose in every other black face t one edge directed from r(t) to another vertex of t. Give an oriented edge weight delta(v,w)=+1 if its black incident face lies to the left, and minus one otherwise.

Every simple directed cycle of selected edges avoids the roots. Its delta-sum is minus three when counterclockwise and plus three when clockwise. Its bounded disk contains no root. If h is the boundary length, I the number of interior vertices, B,W the interior face counts, and l the number of boundary edges with their black face inside, then

$$B+W=2I+h-2,\qquad 3(B-W)=2l-h,\qquad B=I+l.$$

The first two equalities are Euler and signed side counting. The third uses the state: each interior vertex is matched to an interior black face; a boundary vertex is matched to the black face of its selected outgoing edge, inside exactly l times. Conversely each interior black face is matched to an interior or boundary vertex, without duplication. Eliminating I,B,W gives 2l-h=-3. Reversing orientation reverses the signed sum.

The proof of this identity uses the state bijection and the outer-root convention; it does not need the no-separating-triangle hypothesis used to guarantee two states.

## Role in the source argument

Controls cycle weights in the cancellation argument and in the later forest construction.

## Antecedents and comparison

Planar state matchings and local clock moves are established in [Hine and Kalman](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1808.06091v1). Euler and face-side counting are classical. The extracted addition is the state-forced equation B=I+l and its exact cycle-weight consequence; priority has not been certified.

## Checks and limitations

Read the bijection argument. Exhaustive states and edge selections on small even bipyramids check the signed cycle sum, and arbitrary cycles provide negative controls for dropping the state condition.

An arbitrary orientation of the triangulation is not a state selection. The sign depends on traversal orientation, and the bounded disk must be interpreted using the specified outer face.

## Possible applications

Controlling weighted cycle cancellations and detecting forbidden cycles from an incidence matching.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
