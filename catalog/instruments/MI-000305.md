# Preserve an integral pairing by repeating a mod-two unit

[Family 306](../families/openai-math-306.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000171.md) · [Batch B025](../../batches/B025/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let f:C->C' be a map of finite complexes of finitely generated free abelian groups, inducing a quasi-isomorphism modulo two. Its mapping cone has zero mod-two homology. Universal coefficients imply that each finitely generated cone homology group localized at two has zero quotient by (2); Nakayama makes that localized homology zero.

Consequently f induces an isomorphism after localization on the free quotients L=H(C;Z)/Tor and L'=H(C';Z)/Tor. In integral bases its matrix has odd determinant, so it is invertible modulo 2^N for every N>=1. This does not assert an integral inverse.

When L=L' has rank r, all such reductions lie in the finite group GL_r(Z/2^N). A common exponent E depending only on r,N makes every E-th power the identity. If an integral cap pairing v(u)=q is nonzero, choose N with q nonzero modulo 2^N. Then v(f^(kE)u) remains congruent to q and hence nonzero, for arbitrarily large k.

The same common exponent works for several pads and a repeated product of maps, even if the maps change when external choices change. Within each repetition, however, the local data must be literally periodic so that the operation is a power of one matrix. An arbitrary long sequence of unrelated units need not be the identity. Torsion is removed only after passing to integral homology, not by discarding part of mod-two homology.

## Role in the source argument

Reconciles rational nonvanishing with mod-two handle units while allowing arbitrarily long geometric repetitions.

## Antecedents and comparison

[Nakayama, Stacks Project Lemma 10.20.1](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/00DV) supplies the local vanishing step. The adjugate formula and finite-group exponent are classical. The source application carefully retains torsion until the free integral quotient is formed.

## Checks and limitations

All 1,638 matrices in GL_2(Z/2^N) for N=1,2,3 satisfy the group-order recurrence. A two-term complex containing even torsion, odd torsion and free homology checks the mod-two cone calculation and a free map of determinant 3.

These are algebraic models, not verification that the source instanton maps are units or that the geometric repetition and pairing formulas hold.

## Possible applications

Forcing nonvanishing after arbitrarily many repetitions when invertibility is known only at one prime.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
