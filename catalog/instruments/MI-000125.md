# A moment-coordinate tensor square decomposition gives summandwise variance control

[Family 091](../families/openai-math-091.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000086.md) · [Batch B003](../../batches/B003/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let the smooth diffeomorphism $x=\nabla\varphi(z)$ send the probability $e^{-\varphi(z)}dz$ to $Z^{-1}e^{-V(x)}dx$, with $\tau=D^2\varphi>0$. For a compactly supported smooth real test $f$, set $M=\tau^{-1}$, $H=D^2V$, $B=\tau D^2f\,\tau$, $W=\delta_\mu B$ and $u=\delta_\mu W$, where $\delta_\mu$ is negative weighted divergence and acts rowwise on matrices. The source obtains

$$\mathbb E u^2\ge\mathbb E\operatorname{tr}(MBHB)+\mathbb E W^THW.$$

The key intermediate term is $\operatorname{tr}((D_xW)^2)$, not $\|D_xW\|_F^2$. After the integrations by parts and a constant coordinate normalization at a point, the remaining cubic tensors satisfy

$$2\|S\|^2+4\langle S,J\rangle+\|J\|^2+\langle J,J^{(12)}\rangle
=2\|S+\operatorname{Sym}J\|^2+\tfrac16\|J-J^{(12)}\|^2.$$

Here $S$ is fully symmetric, $J_{ijk}=h_{ia}C_{ajk}$, $h=D^2f$, $C=D^3\varphi$, and $J^{(12)}$ swaps the first two indices. A further matrix-square remainder is nonnegative.

For $n\ge2$, even integer $q\ge2$, $\epsilon>0$, nonzero $p_i$ and positive $h_i$, take $\psi_i(x)=(\langle p_i,x\rangle/h_i)^q$ and $V=\epsilon|x|^2/2+\sum_i\psi_i$. Combining the tensor estimate with density of the even tests gives, for all real $b_i$,

$$\operatorname{Var}_\mu\!\left(\sum_i b_i\psi_i\right)\le\sum_i b_i^2\mathbb E_\mu\psi_i.$$

The $p_i$ need not span: the positive quadratic term ensures integrability.

## Role in the source argument

Two complementary Cauchy–Schwarz estimates use the gradient and Hessian of each homogeneous summand. Their coefficients add to exactly the required budget. Differentiating the log partition function in logarithmic slab widths then gives concavity, followed by the soft-slab and Wulff limits.

## Antecedents and comparison

[Fathi, Stein kernels and moment maps](https://arxiv.org/abs/1804.04699v3) supplies the moment-map Stein-kernel viewpoint. [Kolesnikov–Livshyts–Rotem](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.12099v3) already connect strengthened variance inequalities to local Brunn–Minkowski problems. The source-specific candidate is the separate budget for each slab summand through the displayed tensor remainder; the underlying transport and Bochner methods are established.

## Checks and limitations

The finite check verifies the tensor identity exactly for symmetric rational tensors, including equality and nonsymmetric negative controls. The two Cauchy–Schwarz steps and homogeneity factors were checked from the text.

The finite check does not validate global moment-map regularity, all integrations by parts or the entire volume theorem. The tensor identity alone cannot be applied to an arbitrary vector field: W must have the specified double-divergence origin.

## Possible applications

A candidate route to variance estimates retaining individual convex penalties, and a diagnostic for finding the precise nonsymmetric tensor error that must be paid in other transport arguments.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
