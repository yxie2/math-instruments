# Retain extra tensor-category morphisms in a coefficient algebra

[Family 053](../families/openai-math-053.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000229.md) · [Batch B039](../../batches/B039/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Suppose F is a symmetric tensor functor from the split semisimple rational symplectic representation category, with its stated odd-standard parity convention, to a rational rigid tensor category C. Put H(X)=Hom_C(1,F(X)). For simple representations X_lambda define the coefficient algebra

$$B=\bigoplus_\lambda H(X_\lambda)\otimes X_\lambda^\vee.$$

Semisimplicity reconstructs H(X) as (B tensor X)^Sp. Tensor products of target morphisms define multiplication on B; the super-dual evaluation sign gives supercommutativity. Rigidity then identifies

$$\operatorname{Hom}_C(FX,FY)\simeq
\operatorname{Hom}^{\rm even}_{B,\mathrm{Sp}}(B\otimes X,B\otimes Y).$$

Thus additional target morphisms are retained in B even if an ordinary realization would kill them. Neither fullness of F nor scalar-only endomorphisms of the target unit is assumed. Multiplicity spaces are ordinary coefficient spaces; the representation dual carries the parity convention.

## Role in the source argument

Provides a faithful coefficient-enriched model for the source selected Chow tensors, conditional on its tensor-functor construction.

## Antecedents and comparison

[O Sullivan](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0908.0626), Lemma 2.3.1(i), states a general factorization of a tensor functor from a semisimple category through a commutative algebra in its ind-completion and a fully faithful functor. That statement was checked as a direct antecedent; its full proof was not re-audited.

## Checks and limitations

One hundred exact exterior-algebra toy models test super signs, associativity and coefficient retention, including an even nilpotent. An augmentation control loses information. The tests do not model Sp or construct the categorical equivalence.

No actual Chow class, Pfaffian relation or counterexample is verified here. The source functor and category hypotheses remain inputs.

## Possible applications

Keeping algebraic correspondences visible when a cohomological realization is not faithful.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
