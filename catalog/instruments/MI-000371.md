# Pass from trace to log-determinant derivatives through a Sobolev boundary sequence

[Family 051](../families/openai-math-051.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000204.md) · [Batch B033](../../batches/B033/README.md)

## Mathematical content

For N as in the determinant identity, put T=tr N. Pointwise inside the disc,

$$T_r-(\log\det N)_r=\operatorname{tr}((I-N^{-1})N_r).$$

The boundary trace N=I and radial W^{1,2} regularity imply ||N(r)-I||_2=o((1-r)^{1/2}). Integrability of ||N_r||_2^2 supplies radii r_n increasing to 1 with (1-r_n)||N_r(r_n)||_2^2 bounded. The circular mean of the displayed error therefore tends to zero along this sequence. No boundary value of N_r is assumed.

For the source centered variation F with parameter derivatives F_{t_j}=z b_j, closedness of h gives L h(F_z,F_z)=partial_z partial_barz(|z|^2 T), where L=sum_j partial_tj partial_bartj at zero. On radius r the normalized integral is r^2<T>_r+(r^3/2)<T_r>_r. The determinant curvature identity replaces the second term along the sequence, yielding

$$L A_h(F_t)=n-A_{R_h}(f).$$

The integral on the left has a sequence-independent limit, so the final identity does not depend on the selected radii.

## Role in the source argument

Justifies a boundary flux step in the maximizing-disc Hessian calculation with the actual Sobolev regularity.

## Antecedents and comparison

Cauchy-Schwarz on radial Sobolev lines and subsequence selection are classical. The extraction isolates the regularity needed to exchange trace and log-determinant fluxes.

## Checks and limitations

Four radial matrix models verify the area Hessian identity; sixteen nonsmooth boundary examples with exponent between 1/2 and 1 test decay despite divergent individual boundary derivatives.

These examples do not prove the general Sobolev trace theorem or construct the centered holomorphic variations.

## Possible applications

Variational identities where the metric has a fixed boundary trace but boundary normal derivatives are unavailable.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
