# Retain a noncommutative square in integrated trace duality

[Family 262](../families/openai-math-262.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000307.md) · [Batch B055](../../batches/B055/README.md)

## Mathematical content

For R>0, D>=0 and t>0, the exact identity

Delta_D(t)=||R^(-1/2)-R^(1/2)(tI+D)^(1/2)||_HS^2
=tr(R^(-1)+R(tI+D)-2(tI+D)^(1/2))>=0

requires no commutation. The cross terms cancel adjacent inverse square roots, and cyclicity handles the remaining product. Equality is equivalent to R=(tI+D)^(-1/2).

The source takes X_s=(sI-B)^2+V>=bI uniformly in real s, with Hermitian B,V and b>0, and R(t)=pi^(-1)integral_R (X_s+tI)^(-1)ds. Set P=t^(-1/2)I-R, beta=sigma-1/2, 0<sigma<1, N=c_sigma integral_0^infinity t^beta P(t)dt, with c_sigma normalized so the scalar integral at V=1 equals one. Write p=1+sigma and q=p/sigma.

Its half-line boundary matrices S,T give L=(S+T)/2=R^(-1), Z=(S-T)/2 and LZ+ZL+i[B,L]=0. The logarithmic determinant integral supplies the weighted balance needed for

tr(NV)-tr(N^q)=c_sigma integral_0^infinity t^beta [tr(RZ^2)+q Delta_(N^(1/sigma))(t)]dt,

provided N>=0. The scalar power term comes from maximizing tr(ND)-tr(D^p)/p at D=N^(1/sigma). Both defects are nonnegative. The positivity assumption is on the entire symbol X_s; V itself may be indefinite. In the commuting case Z=0, R=(tI+V)^(-1/2), N=V^sigma and the deficit vanishes.

## Role in the source argument

Preserves an explicit noncommutative equality defect in the source trace estimate, which later feeds its matrix action inequality.

## Antecedents and comparison

The Hilbert-Schmidt square and scalar-power trace duality are classical. [Benguria-Loss](https://archive.intlpress.com/site/pub/files/_fulltext/journals/mrl/2000/0007/0002/MRL-2000-0007-0002-a005.pdf), Lemma 3, provides an earlier matrix Riccati method in this subject. The complete source trace proof was read; the particular integrated deficit is recorded without a claim of earliest priority or certification of the surrounding sharp spectral theorem.

## Checks and limitations

Noncommuting positive matrices test the exact square and its equality condition. Numerical resolvent integration tests the half-line Riccati algebra; scalar quadrature checks normalization and the integrated power-duality term.

The finite tests do not construct the source global matrix field, connecting paths or infinite-line spectral bound.

## Possible applications

Tracking equality and coercivity in a matrix trace comparison without replacing products by unjustified commuting expressions.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
