# Construct correlated letters with fixed marginals and a controlled noisy-sum cost

[Family 075](../families/openai-math-075.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000200.md) · [Batch B032](../../batches/B032/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let S be finite and nonempty, T a positive integer, and H a strictly positive kernel on TS-TS with values at most one. Assume that for every probability lambda on S, independent T-letter sums U,U' satisfy E H(U'-U)>=exp(-E). With c=-log H, there is an exchangeable law of X^T and Gamma, each X_i uniform on S and Z=sum X_i+Gamma in TS, such that

$$D(X^T\Vert U_S^{\otimes T})+I(X^T;Z)+E c(\Gamma)\le E.$$

Minimize T H(U_S)-H(X^T|Z)+E c(Z-sum X_i) on the finite polytope of prescribed marginals. Conditional entropy gives convexity and compactness gives attainment. The relative-interior marginal point supplies finite dual multipliers a_i. For fixed Z=z, Gibbs minimization gives minus the logarithm of sum_x exp(sum_i a_i(x_i))H(z-sum x_i); optimizing the output selects the largest such partition sum.

Permutation averaging permits a_i=a. With lambda proportional to exp(a), the dual value becomes -T D(U_S||lambda)-log max_z E_{lambda^T} H(z-sum X_i). The maximum dominates the average over an independent sum z, so the moment hypothesis bounds this by E. Symmetrize a primal minimizer to obtain exchangeability. The identity T H(U_S)-H(X^T|Z)=D(X^T||U_S^T)+I(X^T;Z) retains both information costs.

## Role in the source argument

Supplies the correlated-letter input for the source entropy compression while preserving every one-letter marginal.

## Antecedents and comparison

This is finite Gibbs variational duality with linear marginal constraints. The useful feature is that divergence and noisy-output information are controlled together; neither may simply be discarded in later conditioning arguments.

## Checks and limitations

Six binary examples solve the primal constrained optimization and the symmetrized dual independently, with maximum gap below 1.3e-9, and verify the combined-cost identity.

The tests do not establish the hypothesis quantified over every lambda for the source kernels, nor the subsequent compression theorem.

## Possible applications

Designing correlated finite inputs under exact marginal constraints and a joint information/error budget.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
