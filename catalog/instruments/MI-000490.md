# Build one center that controls every affine slack

[Family 114](../families/openai-math-114.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000269.md) · [Batch B049](../../batches/B049/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let Q be a nonempty bounded rational polytope of affine dimension d. Choose affinely independent vertices v_0,...,v_d and barycentric affine coordinates lambda_j on aff Q. If some vertex x has |lambda_j(x)|>2, replace v_j by x. In fixed affine coordinates the absolute simplex determinant increases by |lambda_j(x)|>2. Linear optimization of both signs of each lambda_j finds a violating vertex if one exists.

At termination, |lambda_j(x)|<=2 for all x in Q. With c=(v_0+...+v_d)/(d+1), every affine function L nonnegative on Q satisfies L(x)=sum lambda_j(x)L(v_j)<=2 sum L(v_j)=2(d+1)L(c). Thus one center controls all nonnegative slacks, including slacks not enumerated in the construction. Dimension zero is immediate.

With a polynomial vertex-encoding bound and a polynomial vertex optimization oracle, all nonzero determinants lie between 2^(-B) and 2^B for a polynomial B. There are at most 2B+1 exchanges. An affine basis can be found by optimizing both signs of directions orthogonal to the current affine span. This gives a polynomial oracle construction under the stated encoding assumptions. The source states the weaker ambient-dimension constant 4(n+1); the factor 2(d+1) displayed here follows directly from its termination criterion.

## Role in the source argument

Produces simultaneous slack in the polymatroid source without enumerating every defining inequality.

## Checks and limitations

Exact rational finite vertex sets test determinant growth, termination and simultaneous slack bounds, including lower-dimensional affine embeddings.

The tests are finite vertex models and do not implement the source separation-to-optimization machinery or prove its complexity bounds.

## Possible applications

Finding a rational interior point with uniform slack relative to all valid affine inequalities.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
