# Rule out an open zero-height region using finite-radius frequency control

[Family 346](../families/openai-math-346.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000375.md) · [Batch B063](../../batches/B063/README.md)

## Mathematical content

On a connected open subset of R^m, let nu be a nonzero nonnegative Radon measure, f a vector measure and beta a symmetric matrix measure, with [[nu,f transpose],[f,beta]] positive semidefinite. Put d0=tr(beta). Assume grad(nu)=2f, div(f)=d0, and integral div(X) dm0=integral DX:dbeta. Also assume 2m0<=d0 and |m0|<=C d0 locally. For p>=4 set phi_l=l^(-m)(1-|y-x|²/l²)_+^p and psi_l=2p l^(-m)(1-|y-x|²/l²)_+^(p-1). Define H=int psi_l dnu, A=l² int phi_l dm0, D=l² int phi_l dd0 and R=int psi_l dbeta[y-x,y-x]. Dots denote log-radius derivatives. Integration by parts and matrix Cauchy-Schwarz give

A dot=2A-D+R, H dot=2D, D²<=RH, 2A<=D.

Then H>0 at every positive interior radius. Otherwise choose x outside the support at distance l0>0 with a larger interior ball. On t=log(l)>t0, H>0 and P=A/H,n=D/H,q=R/H satisfy n>=0, n>=2P, q>=n² and P dot>= (n-1)(n-2P). Thus P is bounded above when moving inward. Since A tends to zero at t0 and (exp(-2t)A) dot>=-exp(-2t)H/4, monotonicity of H also bounds P below by -(exp(2(t-t0))-1)/8. Hence P dot>=n²/2-C on this finite interval. Integration bounds integral n², so n is integrable down to t0. But (log H) dot=2n then prevents H from tending to zero, a contradiction. All stated measure identities are inputs, not consequences of positivity alone.

## Antecedents and comparison

The source explicitly attributes this finite-radius continuation step to the failed-fit analysis in its almost-everywhere-regularity companion. Its ingredients are classical frequency and Riccati inequalities. No independent novelty claim is made for the abstract measure formulation.

## Checks and limitations

Harmonic-polynomial height, flux and stress models verify the radial kernel identities and inequalities by exact polynomial integration. Random admissible frequency parameters check the algebraic lower bounds.

Polynomial models do not construct limiting measures from a varifold or prove the source singular-set dimension theorem.

## Possible applications

Establishing positivity of a normalization from coupled stress identities without assuming it at every center.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
