# Correct positive separators while distinguishing existence from a fixed class

[Family 342](../families/openai-math-342.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000225.md) · [Batch B038](../../batches/B038/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Using a closed lift B with RB=I, suppose P is a positive current annihilating anti-invariant forms. Normalize P(F)=1, where F is the fundamental form. Positivity bounds P by the uniform norm, so it has order zero and a representation as a probability measure on unit complex line bivectors.

Define T(alpha)=P((I-BR)alpha) and Q(alpha)=-P(BR alpha). Then T=P+Q and Q is anti-invariant/self-dual. If P annihilates all closed invariant forms, T annihilates all closed forms. This is the separator obtained when the entire linear space of closed invariant forms misses the positive cone.

For a specified invariant cohomology class a, separate its affine representative space instead. The separator annihilates only its direction space, the exact invariant forms, and is nonpositive on its representatives. Because I-BR preserves exactness, T is a closed current with dual class c(T) orthogonal to H_J^- and c(T) dot a<=0. It need not annihilate every closed form. Similarly, ordinary affine separation says a class b is taming exactly when it evaluates positively on every nonzero closed positive current.

The distinction between a linear subspace and an affine class is essential; the former gives zero pairings with all closed tests, the latter a sign on one class.

## Role in the source argument

Sets up the precise current obstruction required for an existence theorem or for a cone/class theorem.

## Antecedents and comparison

Classical Hahn-Banach separation and positive-current duality, explicitly credited by the source to Sullivan and Harvey-Lawson. The exact-form and cohomology ingredients have the antecedents recorded in the companion lift card.

## Checks and limitations

One hundred exact finite-dimensional linear/affine models verify the dual correction, support of Q and the distinction between total annihilation and a class-specific sign.

Finite linear models do not construct distributional separators or audit the source estimates needed to eliminate them.

## Possible applications

Avoiding an incorrect strengthening from existence of a positive representative to realization of an arbitrary prescribed class.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
