# Turn fourth-moment control into a bounded spectrally filtered witness

[Family 290](../families/openai-math-290.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000372.md) · [Batch B062](../../batches/B062/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let p_i be probability weights, z_i bounded operators, phi a vector state with vector xi, and T a bounded Hilbert-space operator. Put A=sum p_i z_i*z_i and B=sum p_i z_i z_i*. Assume sum p_i||T z_i xi||²>=gamma>0 and phi(A²)+sum p_i phi(z_i*Bz_i)<=C. For independent unit-circle phases epsilon_i set z=sum sqrt(p_i)epsilon_i z_i. Then

E||Tz xi||²>=gamma,
E(z*z)²=A²+sum p_i z_i*Bz_i-sum p_i²(z_i*z_i)².

Polar clipping z_K=u min(|z|,K) gives ||z_K||<=K and E||(z-z_K)xi||²<=C/K². Suppose a linear filter F has operator-norm bound C1, acts on GNS vectors as a contraction, fixes every z_i xi and has the desired output spectral support. For v=F(z_K), E||v xi-z xi||²<=C/K² and

(E||Tv xi||²)^(1/2)>=sqrt(gamma)-||T||sqrt(C)/K.

Choosing K>=2||T||sqrt(C/gamma) yields a realization with ||v||<=C1 K and ||Tv xi||>=sqrt(gamma)/2. In the source, the filter keeps a narrow band fixed and enlarges its half-width by a factor of two. Its construction and the preceding modular averaging are additional inputs. The source obtains moment control using limits with n fixed; no convergence rate uniform in potentially unbounded ||y_n|| is assumed.

## Role in the source argument

Converts unbounded spectral witnesses into uniformly bounded ones once a suitable mixed fourth moment is controlled.

## Antecedents and comparison

[Haagerup and Musat, On the best constants in noncommutative Khintchine-type inequalities](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0611160), equation (2.10), Lemma 2.6 and Remark 2.8, already use fourth moments, functional-calculus truncation and the Steinhaus diagonal correction. PDF pages 5-10 were read, including the truncation proof. The source adds its modular averaging and band-filtering interface; these selected finite checks do not establish that entire interface.

## Checks and limitations

Exact enumeration of fourth-root phases checks the noncommutative moment formula for nontracial states, clipping error and a finite Schur spectral filter. A Rademacher control detects the extra fourth-moment terms when the phase law is changed.

Finite matrix models test the conditional moment-to-witness lemma. They do not realize a nontrivial finite-dimensional algebra with scalar state centralizer or verify the global modular recovery theorem.

## Possible applications

Recovering a bounded test operator from averaged Hilbert-space detection without requiring a uniform operator bound on the original witnesses.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
