# Use a maximal section field to remove horizontal poles and force rank one

[Family 068](../families/openai-math-068.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000443.md) · [Batch B071](../../batches/B071/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let Z be smooth projective of positive dimension, L,M line bundles, D effective integral, and alpha a nonzero movable class with L.alpha=D.alpha=0 and M.alpha<=0. Suppose M+m_i L has nonzero sections s_i for strictly increasing m_i tending to infinity. Among nonempty systems uL+vM+wD with nonnegative integer coefficients, choose B0 of maximal image dimension. Every such system has dimension below dim Z: a generically finite system is big and would pair positively with alpha.

Let K be the relative algebraic closure of the section field of B0 in C(Z), and choose a smooth projective model S. Every ratio within any allowed system belongs to K, since a transcendental ratio would increase the joint image dimension after multiplying sections by those of B0.

Set k=m2-m1 and rho=s2/s1. The ratio s_i^k s1^(m_i-m2)/s2^(m_i-m1) lies in K. At a horizontal prime Q it yields k(ord_Q s_i-ord_Q s1)=(m_i-m1)ord_Q rho. Regularity of all s_i and unbounded m_i force ord_Q rho>=0. On the prepared model, the Jacobian section times s_D rho^j gives a nonzero generic adjoint section for every j>=0. Any two generic sections can have their vertical poles cleared by a common base twist: the moving base line of B0 is big, and its fixed divisor restores an allowed system downstairs. Their ratio belongs to K, forcing generic rank exactly one, including j=0. If S is a point, rho itself descends to a nonzero section of kL.

## Role in the source argument

Produces a lower-dimensional base and rank-one adjoint family from infinitely many twisted sections.

## Antecedents and comparison

The section-field maximality and pole-clearing tools are classical. The source combines them with an explicit interpolation identity and retains the zeroth adjoint degree. Exact priority of this package is not established.

## Checks and limitations

Exact line-bundle exponent identities and horizontal order sequences test the interpolation. Negative slopes reach a finite contradiction with regularity; nonnegative slopes and base-ratio toy models check the rank-one mechanism.

The tests do not establish analytic transfer, semipositivity descent or anticanonical nonvanishing.

## Possible applications

Reducing infinitely many section spaces to one controlling field and one rational section.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
