# Bound a squared omission delay by frozen killing hazards

[Family 235](../families/openai-math-235.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000499.md) · [Batch B080](../../batches/B080/README.md)

## Mathematical content

For independent clauses C_1,...,C_M let V be the last satisfiable prefix index capped at M. Let V^(-i) omit C_i without renumbering later positions. Then V^(-i)>=V and is independent of C_i. Martingale differences of V equal the corresponding differences of V-V^(-i), so conditional Jensen and orthogonality give Var(V)<=sum_i E(V^(-i)-V)^2.

For m>=i let G_m be the punctured prefix and q_m its probability of being killed by a fresh clause, set to zero when already unsatisfiable. The event A_m={G_m satisfiable, G_m and C_i unsatisfiable} is exactly {V<m<=V^(-i)}, including m=M. Thus the omission delay is sum_m 1_A_m. Conditional on the punctured past, C_i is independent of future clauses. Survival to ell requires every intervening clause to avoid individually killing the CURRENT solution set, giving P(A_m and A_ell | past)<=q_m(1-q_m)^(ell-m).

Expanding the square and summing the geometric series gives E(V^(-i)-V)^2<=2 sum_(m=i)^M E min(M q_m,1). Future clauses can cooperate to kill a formula, so the frozen-hazard survival relation is an upper bound, not generally an equality.

## Role in the source argument

Reducing fluctuation control of a monotone stopping time to a one-step hazard summed over time.

## Antecedents and comparison

The arbitrary-omission variance method is classical Efron-Stein/Steele theory; the source cites Boucheron-Bousquet-Lugosi-Massart for that form. That separate article was not reread. The source proof fixes the constant and time convention explicitly.

## Checks and limitations

Exhaustive small clause processes verify the capped delay identity, variance inequality and pivotal-pair bound, including prefixes surviving exactly to the cap and cooperative killing.

The finite checks do not give the required integrated hazard estimate for all n or prove convergence of threshold locations.

## Possible applications

Reducing fluctuation control of a monotone stopping time to a one-step hazard summed over time.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
