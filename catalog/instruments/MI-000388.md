# Telescope weighted curvature when parent and child masses do not conserve

[Family 121](../families/openai-math-121.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000213.md) · [Batch B035](../../batches/B035/README.md)

## Mathematical content

On a finite rooted tree, let each nonterminal have parent mass m>0, child masses m_i>=0, W=sum m_i>0, displacements z,z_i, and z_r=sum z_i. Assume |z|<=m, |z_i|<=m_i, |z_r-z|<=e<=m. Interpret 0/0 as zero. Define R=m-W, a comparison gap G>=0, and slack h>=0 such that R+G<=h. Then

$$K=\sum_i z_i^2/m_i-z_r^2/W=\sum_{m_i>0}(z_i-(z_r/W)m_i)^2/m_i\ge0.$$

Writing E=z^2/m, the local estimate is K<=sum E_i-E+3e+2R_-, because the displacement perturbation costs at most 3e and the denominator discrepancy costs at most 2R_-. If C is the total terminal mass, mass cancellation gives sum R_-<=sum h+C and sum G<=C+2sum h. Since terminal E<=mass, summing yields

$$\sum(K+G)\le4C+3\sum e+4\sum h.$$

The source uses approximate edit costs as masses. Exact additivity is unnecessary, but dropping the mass-discrepancy charge would invalidate the energy argument.

## Role in the source argument

Bounds the total cost of exceptional alignment-tree nodes with high curvature or a large comparison gap.

## Antecedents and comparison

The weighted variance identity and energy telescope are classical. The reusable refinement is the explicit two-sided accounting for nonconserved estimated masses.

## Checks and limitations

One hundred exact rational nonconservative tree models verify variance, local hypotheses and the complete structural bound.

The abstract models do not validate the alignment geometry or source warmup estimates that supply the hypotheses.

## Possible applications

Multiscale analyses where available weights approximate costs rather than forming an exact conserved flow.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
