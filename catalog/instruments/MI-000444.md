# Track ordinary depth additively through the Ihara bracket

[Family 008](../families/openai-math-008.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000241.md) · [Batch B042](../../batches/B042/README.md)

## Mathematical content

In the free Lie algebra on x,y, define ordinary y-depth as the least number of y letters in a nonzero associative word. Let D_psi(x)=0 and D_psi(y)=[y,psi], with Ihara bracket {psi,phi}=D_psi(phi)-D_phi(psi)+[psi,phi].

If psi has depth at least p and phi at least q, every derivation term replaces one y by a word with at least p+1 y letters, increasing the count by at least p. The ordinary commutator also adds counts. Hence {psi,phi} has depth at least p+q.

A homogeneous Lie polynomial of weight greater than one has depth at least one, because the Lie algebra on x alone has no such component. Thus every bracket word of length at least two in these elements has zero depth-one component. This can distinguish a new generator from old brackets. The source B-count filtration after reduction is a different filtration and cannot be substituted without proof.

## Role in the source argument

Separates prospective depth-one generators from the rational span of old bracket words.
