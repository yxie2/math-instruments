# Force positive entropy in almost every invariant component by domination

[Family 015](../families/openai-math-015.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000405.md) · [Batch B066](../../batches/B066/README.md)

## Mathematical content

For an invariant probability mu on a standard Borel dynamical system, suppose every invariant probability xi<=c mu, for any finite c, has entropy at least h_0>0 for a fixed transformation T in the acting group. In the group-ergodic decomposition mu=integral nu_z d tau, the component space is fixed by T. Entropy is measurable and obeys the integral formula over this fixed partition, even if nu_z is not T-ergodic. If the zero-entropy components had weight w>0, their normalized mixture xi would satisfy xi<=mu/w but h_xi(T)=0, a contradiction.

The source obtains the domination-stable bound from ordinary balls: on SL_n lattice space, mu(xB(r))<=C_Omega r^n on compact sets. For a regular diagonal flow with minimum exponent gap lambda, its two-sided thin tube is covered by O(r^(-(n-1))) balls of radius Cr, where r=exp(-lambda t). Thus every dominated xi has compact-uniform tube mass O(exp(-lambda t)). The cited two-sided entropy criterion gives h_xi>=lambda/3. This extraction assumes the ball estimate and that the limit is a probability; positive entropy of mu alone would not exclude zero-entropy components.

## Role in the source argument

Ensures that measure classification applies to almost every component, not merely to the averaged entropy.

## Antecedents and comparison

This is the classical restricted-component argument using affinity of entropy. The source attributes its ordinary-ball method and entropy criterion to ELMV. Those external entropy papers were not re-audited in this batch; the source proof and its explicit hypotheses were read.

## Checks and limitations

Finite mixtures of Bernoulli components check the entropy integral and restriction-by-weight calculation; mixed positive and zero entropy provides a negative control to the weaker averaged claim. Exact dimension arithmetic checks the tube decay exponent.

Finite probability models do not prove the geometric ball estimate, entropy criterion or torus-packet equidistribution.

## Possible applications

Upgrading a lower bound stable under bounded density changes to an almost-everywhere component conclusion.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
