# Four diagonal-form comparisons interpolate to a thermal hyperbolic weight

[Family 273](../families/openai-math-273.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000083.md) · [Batch B002](../../batches/B002/README.md)

## Mathematical content

On coordinate Hilbert spaces $\mathcal H_j$, $0\le j\le k$, define $Q_{X,j}(Z)=\sum_\alpha X_j(\alpha)|Z_\alpha|^2$. Let $\mathcal T\subset\mathcal H_0$ be linear, $L_i:\mathcal T\to\mathcal H_i$ linear, and $w_i>0$. A weight family satisfies comparison when

$$Q_{X,0}(Z)\ge\sum_{i=1}^k w_iQ_{X,i}(L_iZ)\quad(Z\in\mathcal T).$$

At each coordinate let $m>0$ and $x,y\in\mathbb R$, and put $f(x)=x/\sinh x$, with $f(0)=1$. Assume every source test has finitely many parameter values $(m,x,y)$ in its support, and $\mathcal T$ is invariant under scalar functions of that tuple. If comparison holds for all four weights $mf(x)e^{\pm x}$ and $mf(y)e^{\pm y}$, it holds for

$$F=m\frac{f(x)f(y)}{f(x+y)}.$$

No commutation between $L_i$ and the multipliers is assumed. Finite parameter support allows infinitely many nonzero coordinates with the same parameters.

The additional scalar function is defined by $B(f(x)^2)=x\coth x$ for $x\ge0$. The source proves that $-B'$ has a positive Stieltjes representation. For $s=f(x)^2,t=f(y)^2$, this constructs the comparison-preserving weight $E=m(t-s)/(2(B(s)-B(t)))$, with $E=-m/(2B'(s))$ at $s=t$. A quadratic minimization in an auxiliary coordinate then gives exactly $F$.

## Role in the source argument

Combines the variance and entropy-Hessian comparisons at regularized minimizers into the metric used for a thermal-balance defect. It is a standalone comparison theorem, distinct from the full bosonic entropy inequality.

## Antecedents and comparison

Parallel sums and positive operator means are classical: [Anderson--Duffin](https://doi.org/10.1016/0022-247X(69)90200-5) and [Kubo--Ando](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01371042). [Guha--Erkmen--Shapiro](https://arxiv.org/abs/0710.5666v2) supplies the EPnI problem, not this interpolation formula. Targeted searches for the four-weight and Stieltjes/EPnI formulations found no independent earlier match; this limited search does not establish priority. The source-specific Stieltjes function and exact target weight are the candidate new combination.

## Checks and limitations

Read the parallel-sum variational argument and scalar minimization, including x=y, x=-y and x=y=0. The numerical checks bundled with this batch compare the independent minimization and target formulas on a finite grid.

The finite-parameter and multiplier-invariance hypotheses are essential to the stated proof. Numerical agreement does not prove the Stieltjes representation or its unbounded-form extension. The source Hessian bound is asserted at its minimizers, not for every quantum channel and state.

## Possible applications

Potential use: transfer several elementary diagonal metric inequalities to a sharper thermal metric when the source test space satisfies the stated invariance conditions.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
