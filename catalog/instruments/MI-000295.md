# Group a multilinear expansion by its two earliest increments and retain the remainder

[Family 086](../families/openai-math-086.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000166.md) · [Batch B023](../../batches/B023/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Consider a four-linear form H and approximations to inputs F_0,...,F_3 updated one position at a time in a fixed cycle. At update t, let Delta_t=g_t-g_(t-4), with an absent earlier approximation interpreted as zero. At a finite cutoff T, expand each input into all its increments and its terminal residual.

Group terms containing at least two increments by their earliest update levels u<t. They necessarily occupy distinct positions i,j. Summing every later choice in each complementary position e gives F_e-g_(e,prev(t)), where prev(t) is the last update of that position before t. Thus the grouped term is H with arguments Delta_u, Delta_t and those two later residuals in their respective positions.

The exact finite identity is the sum of these grouped terms plus a remainder consisting of H of all four terminal residuals and four terms with one whole terminal approximation and three terminal residuals. The remainder need not vanish for arbitrary approximations; dropping it at a finite cutoff is incorrect.

The source obtains a limiting expansion by supplying residual estimates e^(-k_t), approximation complexity/supremum bounds exp(C k_t^d), and a stagger k_(t+1)=k_t^A with A>d. When comparing two approximations, replace the more accurate one first. Its smaller error absorbs the earlier approximation's larger supremum bound. Subsequent comparison with actual inputs gives an error of the form C(e^(-k_h)exp(C k_l^d)+e^(-k_l)). In full differences or residuals, the actual-input terms cancel. For sufficiently late updates this yields the source pairwise form-norm decay after allowing a reserve in the exponent.

The algebraic grouping and the analytic decay are separate. Detection, inherited fits and the claimed accuracy bounds are substantive inputs to the latter.

## Role in the source argument

Organizes the source four-linear form so that two structured increments coexist with cancellation in later residuals.

## Antecedents and comparison

Multilinear telescoping and grouping terms by first differing scales are classical. The source uses the two earliest updates with a rapidly staggered accuracy schedule. A targeted inspection did not establish a novelty claim for the exact organization.

## Checks and limitations

Read the entire proof and preceding approximation construction. Ninety exact integer four-tensor models check 2,625 grouped terms at six cutoffs; every tested arbitrary approximation has a nonzero terminal remainder, which the identity retains.

The finite algebra tests do not verify the source residual norm, complexity bounds, vanishing of terminal remainders or the trilinear Hilbert-transform estimate. The faster accuracy must dominate the earlier approximation complexity in the correct replacement order.

## Possible applications

Reorganizing multilinear approximation errors to expose two distinct accuracy scales without losing finite-cutoff terms.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
