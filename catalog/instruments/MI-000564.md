# Diagonalize an arbitrary node-order pairing on two triangles

[Family 165](../families/openai-math-165.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000333.md) · [Batch B058](../../batches/B058/README.md)

## Mathematical content

For 2s distinct real nodes, s>=2, label them in any prescribed order. Let P_(a,b) denote polynomials of separate degrees at most a,b. On

L_s={(p,q):1<=q<p<=s} union {(p,q):s<p<q<=2s},

evaluation is an isomorphism for both P_(s-1,s-2) and P_(s-2,s-1). To prove this simultaneously, the two central rows force two first-variable factors in the first space. Removing those rows leaves the second degree rectangle at s-1. The outer two columns give the converse induction. Dimensions agree at s(s-1), and s=2 is immediate.

Put G(t)=product_p(t-u_p) and S(p,q)=1/2 for p<q, -1/2 for p>q, zero otherwise. Define M(A,B)=sum_(p,q) S(p,q)(u_p-u_q)A(u_p,u_q)B(u_p,u_q)/(G'(u_p)G'(u_q)). For A,B in the two respective rectangles, the product (u-v)AB has separate degrees at most 2s-2. Lagrange interpolation therefore annihilates every added row term r_p or column term c_q in S.

Replace S off the diagonal by S-1/2+1_(p>s). This is -1 on the first triangle, +1 on the second and zero elsewhere. In the two evaluation bases, M is diagonal with nonzero weights. It is thus nonsingular regardless of the numerical order of the nodes. In particular, A in P_(s-2,s-2) vanishes if M(A,B)=0 for every B in P_(s-1,s-1).

## Role in the source argument

Turns cyclic-order data into algebraic detection of a polynomial test without requiring ordered numerical coordinates.

## Antecedents and comparison

The annihilation identity is the leading-coefficient comparison in classical Lagrange interpolation. The complete source induction and diagonalization were inspected. Targeted searches did not identify an earlier exact even-node formulation; this is not a proof of novelty.

## Checks and limitations

24 exact rational models with shuffled distinct nodes and s=2,3,4 check both interpolation determinants, the full and reduced pairing matrices, nonsingularity and all required Lagrange moments.

The tests do not construct drawings or establish the source conversion of crossings into these pairings.

## Possible applications

Recovering polynomial information from signed order data after quotienting out row and column contributions.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
