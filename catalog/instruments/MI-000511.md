# Control percolation sprinkling by a positive connection-kernel series

[Family 214](../families/openai-math-214.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000286.md) · [Batch B051](../../batches/B051/README.md)

## Mathematical content

For independent Bernoulli bond fields with parameters p and a, their union has parameter p'=p+(1-p)a. Let T_p(x,y) be the connection probability and Adj the graph adjacency matrix. Then T_(p')<=sum_(j>=0) a^j T_p(Adj T_p)^j entrywise.

A finite simple union-open path splits at its j ordered second-field edges. The intervening first-field legs have disjoint open witnesses. Independence and the disjoint-witness inequality bound their joint probability by a^j times the product of their connection probabilities. Summing over intermediate vertices and j gives the series; extra walks in that sum only overcount.

If the maximum degree is d, T_p is bounded on counting-measure l2, and a d ||T_p||<1, the series converges in operator norm. It gives ||T_(p')||<=||T_p||/[1-a d ||T_p||]. Entrywise domination implies this bound because both kernels have nonnegative entries: apply the dominating form to absolute values of test vectors. No positive-semidefinite ordering between the two kernels is needed. Consequently, bounded T_(pc) with pc<1 implies boundedness at a strictly larger parameter and hence pc<p_(2-to-2). The difficult input is boundedness at pc itself.

## Role in the source argument

Converts the source proposed critical operator bound into an open interval above pc.

## Antecedents and comparison

[Hutchcroft, Percolation on Hyperbolic Graphs](https://d-nb.info/1187112097/34), Lemma 2.4 and Corollary 2.6, state and prove the same connection-kernel comparison; PDF pages 9-10 were read. The source explicitly cites this antecedent. This extraction is a reusable classical instrument, not a novelty claim.

## Checks and limitations

Exact enumeration of bond states on small graphs computes connection matrices at rational parameters and verifies domination by the rational Neumann resolvent. Norms are checked numerically against the scalar bound.

Every finite graph has bounded connection operators; these tests do not establish a nontrivial infinite-graph threshold gap or the source critical boundedness theorem.

## Possible applications

Extending a known connection-operator bound to nearby percolation parameters.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
