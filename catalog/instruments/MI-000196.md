# An augmented matching cover charges rectangular conflict pruning to corner sets

[Family 173](../families/openai-math-173.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000119.md) · [Batch B012](../../batches/B012/README.md)

## Mathematical content

Let X be finite with any binary relation. Let R and C be separate copies of subsets of X times X. Declare (p,j) in R and (i,s) in C conflicting when p points to i and s points to j. Let Z contain all intermediate corners (i,j) of conflicts, and H all opposite corners (p,s). A row member (p,j) covers (i,j) if p points to i; a column member (i,s) covers it if s points to j.

There exist conflict-free survivors R',C', retaining every originally conflict-free member, with

$$|R\setminus R'|+|C\setminus C'|\le |H|+|\{z\in Z:z\text{ is uncovered by }R',C'\}|.$$

Adjoin copies Z_L,Z_R to the conflict bipartite graph and join original members to the opposite copy whenever they cover a corner. A vertex cover of size at most |Z|+|H| gives the inequality: for every covered corner at least one of its two copies must belong to the cover. Originally conflict-free members are isolated and can be retained.

The cover bound uses a maximum matching minimizing its number k of original-to-original edges. Its R-to-Z_R and Z_L-to-C parts have maximum sizes ell and t in those respective subgraphs. Introduce generic arrow coefficients a_pi,b_sj and maps L:R to Z, N:Z to C, B:R to H, A:H to C, with entries a_pi,b_sj,b_sj,a_pi along the corresponding corners. They satisfy NL=AB. A common real specialization realizes the needed matching minors and maximum ranks of every local matrix (b_sj).

The elementary kernel fact is that if D has maximum rank among matrices supported on a fixed pattern T, then x in ker D and y in ker D transpose satisfy x_j y_s=0 for every allowed position (s,j). Otherwise perturbing that one entry increases rank. Apply it to the local blocks: kernel vectors of L supported on an original matched member plus the ell matched row members have independent images under B, all lying in ker A. Thus

$$|H|\ge k+\operatorname{rank}A\ge k+\operatorname{rank}(NL)\ge k+\ell+t-|Z|.$$

The matching has size k+ell+t; the bipartite matching-cover theorem finishes the charge. The use of maximum rank is local to a prescribed support, not a claim that an arbitrary numerical matrix has this kernel property.

## Role in the source argument

Deletes incompatible row and column witnesses while preserving enough coverage for the source contradiction.

## Antecedents and comparison

The generic-rank ingredient is [Edmonds, Theorem 1](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/jres/71B/jresv71Bn4p241_A1b.pdf). Matching-cover duality is classical. The particular augmented graph and commuting-square charge are the selected candidate combination; targeted searches did not establish an earlier identical lemma.

## Checks and limitations

Read the matching refinement, simultaneous specialization, local kernel step and rank charge. Finite checks construct minimum covers of augmented graphs and check the survivor inequality, including relations with loops and opposite arrows.

The finite tests do not prove the all-size rank argument or the source headline theorem. Shared variable assignments must satisfy all required nonzero minors simultaneously.

## Possible applications

Deleting incompatible product-indexed witnesses when lost coverage can be balanced by an opposite-corner rank budget.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
