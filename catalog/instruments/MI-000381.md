# Choose tensor labels that remain independent under every rank-two quotient

[Family 299](../families/openai-math-299.md) · [Source manuscript](../papers/P-000209.md) · [Batch B034](../../batches/B034/README.md)

## Mathematical content

For a real m-dimensional space V, m>=2, and r>=1, choose n with 2^n>2mn+r-1. There exist x_1,...,x_r in V^{tensor n} whose images are independent under P_1 tensor ... tensor P_n for every tuple of real maps of rank at least two.

Reduce to surjections P_j:V->R^2. For a unit coefficient vector u in S^{r-1}, the equation (tensor P_j)(sum u_i x_i)=0 imposes 2^n independent conditions on x, because differentiation in the x variables is surjective. The bad incidence dimension is 2mn+r-1+r m^n-2^n<r m^n, so its projection is null. The noncompact parameter space is handled by a countable atlas.

For a unital C*-algebra D without characters, finitely many commutators generate its unit ideal. Their selfadjoint entries span a finite real V that contains a noncommuting pair in every unital representation, hence has represented rank at least two. The labels above are selfadjoint; real independence therefore survives complexification. Factor tensor independence then makes the labels independent in every irreducible representation of D^{tensor n} maximal-tensor B, for arbitrary nonzero unital B.

## Role in the source argument

Creates enough labels that survive all represented tensor legs, including nonspatial maximal-product representations.

## Antecedents and comparison

The construction is a finite-dimensional Sard dimension argument combined with the standard commutator-ideal characterization of absence of characters. No novelty claim beyond the source-specific organization is made.

## Checks and limitations

Seventy-five sampled tuples of rank-two quotient maps verify label independence in three dimension regimes satisfying the source inequality.

Random sampling cannot establish the universal quantifier over all quotient maps; that conclusion uses the read incidence proof.

## Possible applications

Representation-uniform noncancellation in tensor constructions when every represented factor retains at least two real directions.

These are proposed uses; no subsequent application is recorded for this instrument.
