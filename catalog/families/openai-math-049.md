## Dependencies and antecedents

## Review and formalization scope

Read the full polynomial presentation and stabilization proofs, and the full lifting proof with the universal polynomial definition. Both manuscripts receive extractions. The graded obstruction, bundle rigidity and noncoordinateness are not certified.

The scope and all three Comparators were read in full. AbhyankarSathaye targets an existential noncoordinate affine-space quotient in every dimension n>=4. CommutingDerivations targets n-1 independent commuting locally nilpotent derivations with the specified common kernel and ordinary extra partials. StableCoordinateFour targets the explicit degree-five polynomial, one-variable stabilization, noncoordinateness and all nonrectifiable affine-three-space fibers. Each target has a sorry body. These are target declarations, not checked separate solutions.

The stabilization is over a characteristic-zero field. The lifting lemma allows zero divisors but requires the stated unit ideal. Neither calculation proves noncoordinateness.
